# Kunst-Stoffe Materialmarkt Pankow

Tags: #material-supply #urban-mining #reuse #interview-source #priority-medium

---

## Type

Physical material market for rescued and reusable materials in Berlin-Pankow.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick

Good source for supply reality at the everyday operational level: inconsistent stock, mixed materials, low standardization, and highly variable documentation.

## What This Source Can Contribute

- Stock visibility in practice
- How availability changes over time
- Pricing, turnover, and material categorization approaches
- What a planner cannot know from current listings

## Research Relevance

Direct relation to:

- Supply-side constraints on early-stage design decisions
- Information gaps between physical stock and digital catalogue
- Mismatch between design timing and material availability
