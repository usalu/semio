## Type

Material market embedded in the Haus der Materialisierung circular ecosystem.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick

Useful because it shows that supply is not only transactional — it is embedded in networks of repair, education, community use, and experimentation.

## What This Source Can Contribute

- How material exchange sits inside a broader reuse ecosystem
- What kinds of users interact with these markets
- Which material records are sufficient for circulation but not for design

## Research Relevance

Direct relation to:

- Ecosystem thinking around supply and reuse infrastructure
- Distinction between community reuse and professional design reuse
- Data gaps that emerge at the hand-off to design teams
