## Type
Applied research and experimentation environment for circular construction — tests solutions at real scale with architects, engineers, craftspeople, and sustainability experts.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick
Tests circular solutions under real conditions — not just discussed but built and iterated. Design bottlenecks and missing digital tools become particularly visible here. Strong for workflow mapping and bottleneck identification.

## What This Source Can Contribute
- Where existing tools fail in real implementation
- What interdisciplinary workflows actually look like
- Which digital support is needed during prototyping and testing
- How circular building ideas move from concept to practice
- How design processes change when circular solutions are tested at real scale
- Which iterations arise from material and construction constraints
- How architects navigate uncertainty and limited data
- How interdisciplinary collaboration shapes design decisions

## Research Relevance
Direct relation to:
- Workflow mapping across disciplines
- Bottlenecks between digital tools and physical reuse
- Integration of circular strategies into early design phases
- Design bottlenecks in reuse-based projects
- Missing digital support in early concept phases
