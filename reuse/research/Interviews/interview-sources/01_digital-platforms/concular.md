## Type
Circular construction platform combining consulting, urban mining, pre-deconstruction audit, marketplace logic, and digital documentation.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick
Sits across multiple categories at once: digital inventories, component valuation, pre-demolition audit, and environmental assessment. Stock data, deconstruction planning, material valuation, and reuse all converge here. One of the best hybrid interview sources for the project.

## What This Source Can Contribute
- How reusable components are documented before demolition
- Which datasets are needed for resale and reuse
- How clients use digital circularity services
- Where planners still lack direct tool support
- How reusable components are identified before deconstruction begins
- Which data is actually collected in condition surveys
- Which information is critical for later design integration
- How audit data becomes usable for planning, evaluation, and handover
- Where the largest gaps between stock documentation and design tools lie

## Research Relevance
Direct relation to:
- Workflow gaps between marketplace and design tool
- Component metadata from pre-demolition audit to listing
- LCA-oriented evaluation integration
- Provenance, condition, and component capture
- Data flow from urban mining into component catalogue
- Requirements for structured stock data
