## Type

Research project on reuse of structural systems from secondary materials.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick

Bridges urban mining and engineering — not just material recovery, but diagnosis, preparation, and integration of structural systems into design. One of the best sources for the source-to-verification-to-design interface.

## What This Source Can Contribute

- How secondary materials are assessed before structural reuse
- Which logistics and value-chain steps matter
- Where technical bottlenecks appear between sourcing and design use
- What information must travel with a reclaimed structural element

## Research Relevance

Direct relation to:

- Condition assessment and provenance documentation
- Information flow from urban mining to structural verification
- Design experiments with non-standard structural stock
