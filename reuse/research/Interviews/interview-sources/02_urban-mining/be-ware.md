## Type
Research project on reuse of structural systems from secondary materials — spans urban mining, engineering verification, and design integration.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick
Bridges urban mining and engineering: damage diagnosis, material recovery, logistics, and structural reuse all converge here. One of the best sources for the source-to-verification-to-design interface.

## What This Source Can Contribute
- How secondary materials are assessed before structural reuse
- Which logistics and value-chain steps matter
- Where technical bottlenecks appear between sourcing and design use
- What information must travel with a reclaimed structural element
- Which engineering data is actually needed for reused components
- How uncertainty in secondary materials is handled structurally
- How technical assessment connects to deconstruction and logistics
- What kinds of data would feed into digital plausibility checks

## Research Relevance
Direct relation to:
- Condition assessment and provenance documentation
- Information flow from urban mining to structural verification
- Design experiments with non-standard structural stock
- Structural verification with reused components
- Early structural plausibility requirements in design process
