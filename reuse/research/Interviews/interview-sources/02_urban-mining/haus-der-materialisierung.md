# Haus der Materialisierung

Tags: #urban-mining #material-supply #design #reuse #interview-source #priority-high

---

## Type

Circular economy hub and infrastructure for material cycles, workshops, repair, education, and reuse.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick

Best source for understanding the local, physical, and social infrastructure behind reuse. Shows that reuse depends on actors, access, storage, and community networks — not only objects and datasets.

## What This Source Can Contribute

- How material cycles are organized locally
- Which actors need to collaborate for reuse to function
- What information is available at the moment of material recovery
- How formal and informal reuse infrastructures differ

## Research Relevance

Direct relation to:

- Upstream workflow and material sourcing conditions
- Actor network dependencies in circular supply
- Community-embedded reuse vs. transactional marketplace models
