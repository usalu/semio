# Haus der Materialisierung

Tags: #urban-mining #material-supply #design #reuse #interview-source #priority-high

---

## Type
Circular economy hub and infrastructure for material cycles, workshops, repair, education, and reuse — and the community ecosystem in which reuse-based design takes place.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick
Best source for understanding the local, physical, and social infrastructure behind reuse. Shows that reuse depends on actors, access, storage, and community networks — not only objects and datasets. Makes visible the material and organizational environment in which reuse-oriented design actually happens.

## What This Source Can Contribute
- How material cycles are organized locally
- Which actors need to collaborate for reuse to function
- What information is available at the moment of material recovery
- How formal and informal reuse infrastructures differ
- How material availability influences creative design decisions
- Which opportunities and constraints arise from local circular systems
- Why platforms need to represent contexts, not just components
- What translation is needed between material supply and design use

## Research Relevance
Direct relation to:
- Upstream workflow and material sourcing conditions
- Actor network dependencies in circular supply
- Community-embedded reuse vs. transactional marketplace models
- Process side of design within a circular ecosystem
