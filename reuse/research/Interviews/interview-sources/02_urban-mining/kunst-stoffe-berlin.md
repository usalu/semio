# Kunst-Stoffe Berlin

Tags: #urban-mining #material-supply #reuse #interview-source #priority-high

---

## Type

Material reuse center and network operating physical material markets in Berlin.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick

Deals with real material heterogeneity. Reveals what kinds of materials are actually available, how inconsistent supply can be, and how limited documentation typically is at the point of reuse.

## What This Source Can Contribute

- Material variability in practice
- Typical gaps in documentation and technical information
- How local reuse markets handle stock and turnover
- Why material availability rarely matches design timing

## Research Relevance

Direct relation to:

- Raw supply conditions and metadata gaps
- Constraints imposed on design by actual stock reality
- Missing information at the point of transfer to design teams
