## Type

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick

Combines assessment, logistics, condition measurement, and real component reuse in a completed building. Useful as a project-based engineering case.

## What This Source Can Contribute

- How reused timber and façade elements are checked and verified
- How logistics and stakeholder coordination affect engineering decisions
- What minimum data is needed to move forward with structural reuse
- How condition measurement translates into design input

## Research Relevance

Direct relation to:

- Condition data requirements for timber and façade reuse
- Information flow from sourcing through preparation to integration
- Cross-disciplinary coordination in a reuse project
