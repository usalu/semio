# ZRS – CRCLR House

Tags: #engineering #design #reuse #interview-source #priority-high

---

## Type
Architecture and engineering practice with a built circular project using reused structural steel components. Reuse is embedded in the architectural logic here — not added as an afterthought.

## Why This Is a Strong Interview Pick
One of the strongest sources for both engineering and design sides. Shows how architectural and structural thinking meet in a reuse project — and how concept design starts from found components rather than idealized new ones.

## What This Source Can Contribute
- How reused structural elements are assessed and integrated
- How uncertainty is managed in engineering decisions
- What kinds of proofs or plausibility checks are possible with secondary stock
- How concept design is shaped by found components rather than idealized new ones
- How circular detailing and Design for Disassembly are implemented
- How design decisions change when components are already given
- Which information architects need in early design phases
- How architects and engineers co-develop a reuse-based structural concept

## Research Relevance
Direct relation to:
- Component-level reuse decisions in engineering and design
- Coordination between architects and structural engineers
- Workflow changes compared with new-build structural design
- Integration of discrete existing components into concept phase
- Requirements for design tools in a reuse context
