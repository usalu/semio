local `DagDocumentDsl` mirror struct (`#[derive(dsl::DslDocument)]`) because DagNodeSpec's `kind`
field needs boxing that the real domain type deliberately doesn't carry (see the file's own
`🔖️DslMirror` region doc comment). Confirmed by inspection before writing anything. Hand-implemented
`impl vcs::DocumentPack for DagDocument` analogously, delegating to
`<DagDocumentDsl as vcs::DocumentPack>::{encode_pack_with,decode_pack_with}` via the existing
`dag_document_to_dsl`/`dag_document_from_dsl` converters (DagDocumentDsl gets DocumentPack for free
from its own derive). Placed directly after the existing manual DocumentDsl impl, same region
(`🔖️DslMirror`).
