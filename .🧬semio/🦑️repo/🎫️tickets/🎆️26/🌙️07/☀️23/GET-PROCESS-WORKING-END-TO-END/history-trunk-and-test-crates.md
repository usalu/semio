Fixed; this made the birth, new-local-admission and manual-protocol laws pass.
- Final: lib 48/48 pass (including both fold laws and the drafts law), `fold_work` 3/3 pass, spr `fold_falls_back_to_positional_mutation_ids_and_zero_clock_without_meta` passes (`semio-os-history` lib).
