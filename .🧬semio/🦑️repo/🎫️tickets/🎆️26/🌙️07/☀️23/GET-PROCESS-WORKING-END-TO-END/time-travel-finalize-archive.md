# Time-travel finalize, archive load and inert retirement (tests-07-2)

## Causes found
1. **Undo/redo/alternative undo: "lost its matching Session stage" and head not restored.** `commit_report` (undo, redo) moves the Report into the publisher over several turns, so `TimeTravelStoreState::finalizing()` is already true while `commit_stage==0`. `drive_time_travel_turn` then ran `StepCommit` first, whose stage 0 admits from `self.finished` (absent for an authored Report): "completed replay source is absent" -> `Failed` outcome -> the Session had no Finalizing stage to fault. The authoring step that owns the admission never ran.
2. **Remote edit overwrite ends on the wrong head.** Test side: `historyEditCommit` is multi-turn under the original model; the law asserted the head right after the verb. It now pumps until the finalize retires (as `commit_scenario` does).
3. **Inert retirement (item 7).** `close_inert` retired with `retire_step` only; owners that `requires_original_control()` (controlled retirement) need `retire_step_controlled` under `with_native_retirement`, as the instance does.
4. **Pure lane archive load (item 5).** Not fixed: the fault is `admit_original_construction` ("construction requires its original empty candidate"), raised while a document-archive replacement initializer builds. `AppDocumentStoreOwnerProducer` already uses `persisted_document_store_owners`; the remaining suspect is the persisted-hydration mutation receiving (`store/document/history/hydration/mutation`, re-entering the construction admission with the candidate slot still occupied). Needs a backtrace run; not reached.

## Fixes
- `time-travel/store-state/finalize`: `admitting_report()` (stage 0, not closing, publisher or resident held).
- `time-travel/instance/turn-driving-and-replay-steps`: `StepCommit` is skipped while an open authoring owner is still admitting its Report (`authoring` open and `admitting_report()`), so `step_supersede_authoring` advances the admission. A first attempt keyed on `commit_request.is_none()` stalled the overwrite path (admit takes the request); the authoring condition fixes that.
- Tests: `opened-instance-edit-drafts-and-withdraw` pumps until the finalize retires; `inert-label-op-and-hostile-input` `close_inert` uses the controlled retirement when required, and `inert_wire` uses `ArtifactOperationWire::canonical()` (the inert op has no `SemanticMutation`).

## Round 2 (evidence from run `p`, builds paused at the coordinator's request)
- Inert wire: `inert_wire()` is `install_persisted_receiving(canonical().with_batch_digest()).with_original_target(whole_document_target)` over the op's `artifact_persisted_encoded_binary_operation!`; it is the persisted wire the issuers agent wanted (the `semantic::<TestSnapshot>()` variant does not compile, `InertLabelOp` has no `SemanticMutation`). The two inert-label laws are not re-run by me after the change; run them with the next build.
- `undo_and_redo_of_a_finalize...`: verified, passes (4.5 s) with the `history_verb` pump.
- Item 7: logged owner state after `close_inert`: `pending=true replaying=false finalizing=false control=false original_sources_close_pending=true`. A cancelled replay or finished replay is retired by `CloseOriginalSources` driver turns (as the instance's `drive_time_travel_turn` does first), never by `retire_step`. Fix prepared (not yet run): `close_inert` first loops `owners.run(.., CloseOriginalSources { grant: mounted_policy.close }, Some(cx))` under renewed turns until `original_sources_close_pending()` is false.
- Item 5: backtrace of the fault: `ArtifactStoreReplayInitializer::seed_history` (store/initialization/replay) -> `built` -> `admit_original_construction`; the `built` call in `seed_history` is one of the unit-page call (`unit_out`) or the identity-seed call (`phase_out`). In `admit_original_construction` the error fires when the candidate output is already `Some` with the source released and `admission.complete == false`: a previous turn's construct closure stored the candidate but the native child poll was not `Complete` (`ready = output.is_some() && child.is_some()` false, so the admission cleared the source without completing). The next call then sees an occupied candidate. Candidate cause: `seed_history` alternates between the `unit_out` branch and the `phase_out` branch on `demand.capacity_bytes != 0` while one shared `self.construction` admission is still mid-attempt for the other output. Next step (needs a build): log `output.is_some()`, `child`, `frame.phase()` at the `ready=false` branch and make `seed_history` select its branch from the admission's pending output (`unit_out.is_some()` or `phase_out.is_some()`) instead of the recomputed demand.
- Long history: `[DEBUG]` counters show the Report replay advances at a constant ~190 driver turns per operation (about 70 microseconds per turn, fuel 5 per turn): 3061 operations need roughly 580k turns, about 40 s, so the 30 s pump deadline is the symptom. Separately, `done` freezes at 1064 from turn ~193k on (fuel still burns 5 per turn, no `Unfunded`) until the fuel runs low. Two causes to chase: (a) which `step_original_report_replay` phases each take a whole turn (read issuer, history source, input advance, replacement select, preparation birth, step), why a single operation needs ~190 of them; (b) the stall at operation 1064 (a phase returning `Pending(progress)` without a receipt or work, for example the `deadline()` or `maximum_work <= ORIGINAL_INVOCATION_WORK` early return, or a replacement or target capture phase that never completes). Next step needs a build: per-turn phase tag in `step_original_report_replay` and its early returns.

## Round 3 (builds `q` to `t`)
- Item 7 verified: `an_unfunded_replay_step_defers...` passes with the `CloseOriginalSources` loop in `close_inert`; both inert-label laws (`a_blocking_mutation...`, `an_inverse_refusal...`) pass with the persisted `inert_wire`.
- Item 5 cause (from `[DEBUG]` at the admission: `occupied complete=false child=None`, plus a preceding `not ready output=false child=Some(ok)`): `ArtifactStoreReplayInitializer::seed_history` chose between its page-unit attempt (`unit_out`) and its identity-seed attempt (`phase_out`) by recomputing `dag` demand (`capacity_bytes != 0`). The two attempts share one `OriginalConstructionAdmission`. While the unit construction was mid-attempt (output not yet stored) the demand dropped to zero, so the identity-seed call took over the admission, cleared the unit's source and child, and the next turn the unit call found its candidate occupied: "construction requires its original empty candidate". Fix: new `unit_attempt` flag in the initializer (set on entering the unit branch, cleared on completion or failure) keeps the unit branch selected until its attempt settles. Not yet verified: run `t` (the two laws) was stopped by the coordinator's pause.
- Long history, per-phase trace (`pending tags`, 100000 pending returns): 92 percent were the snapshot-read lease denial in `step_original_report_replay` (`snapshot_read_leases.try_issue` returned `Err`), 3.4 percent history-input advance, 2.2 percent history-source close. Cause: every replayed operation takes a registry lease on the previous post root, and the returned lease keeps a whole document root alive until the maintenance rotation's returned-read pump reclaims it. The pump reclaims one returned read per maintenance rotation, so a replay that folds one op per turn outruns it (about 190 turns per operation) and finally exhausts the 1,024-lease registry (the freeze at operation 1064). The tool-run publication already gates on `reclaim_document_snapshot_read_returns` for this reason; the Report replay had no such gate. Fix: `drive_time_travel_turn` now, while replaying and returned reads or the document disposer are outstanding, spends the turn on `advance_document_snapshot_read_return` before stepping the replay. Effect in run `s`: operations prepared in 30 s rose from 61440 to 92580 and the freeze at 1064 is gone; the law still did not finish in 30 s, and the per-phase trace of that run (period lowered to 10000) was the stopped run `t`.
- Open: whether the remaining time is per-turn cost or more pump alternation. Next measure: rerun `the_undo_of_a_finalize_over_a_long_history` with the tag trace (re-add `dbg_tag` at the `return Ok(ReplayStep::Pending(progress))` sites of `step_original_report_replay`, module-scope statics) and count turns per operation.

## Round 4 (builds `u` to `y`) and final state
- Whole target `cargo test time_travel_tests_1` (semio-plugin-tests-07-2): **29 pass, 1 fail** (run `y`, no `[DEBUG]` left). Only `the_undo_of_a_finalize_over_a_long_history_lands_through_the_driver` fails.
- Item 5 fixed. The `unit_attempt` flag alone was not enough: `[DEBUG]` at the failing call showed `edit=63 lane=0 unit_out=false phase_out=true cap=0`, i.e. the identity-seed (`phase_out`) attempt was mid-flight (its reset not yet paid) when the recomputed demand became non-zero and the unit-page branch took over the shared admission; the unit call then reset it, and the phase call came back to an occupied candidate. Fix in `store/initialization/replay`: both attempts carry a flag (`unit_attempt`, `phase_attempt`); a branch stays selected until its attempt settles, and the unit branch never preempts a phase attempt. `a_pure_lane_hydrates...` passes (the archive load of 240 edits takes over 60 s under machine load; a separate performance question).
- Long history, what is fixed: the lease-exhaustion livelock. The replay now drains returned document snapshot reads (`while returned_snapshot_read_count() != 0`, each step under the turn's remaining grant, until the turn should yield) before stepping the replay. Gating on `reclaim_document_snapshot_read_returns` (which also requires the disposer to be terminal) livelocked at the end because the disposer re-arms per returned lease; gating on the returned count does not. Prepared operations in 30 s: 61440, then 92580, then 99314.
- Long history, what is not fixed: the law still does not leave `Replaying` within its 30 s pump deadline (`processed` 99314 of an unknown total, no `Pending` returns from `step_original_report_replay` beyond 10000 in the run, so the time is spent per driver turn, not in the phase machine). A turn-level profile (what one `advance_typed_operation_publication` turn costs while replaying; the pump drain at 1 returned lease per op, the retained-root retirement per lease at about 9 steps) is the next step. Candidate algorithmic causes, unmeasured: per-op root retirement (`ArcRootRetirement` per returned lease), and the per-turn driver overhead (history refresh, patch) paid for every one of ~3000 turns. `an_interior_undo_over_a_long_history...` (similar size) passes.
