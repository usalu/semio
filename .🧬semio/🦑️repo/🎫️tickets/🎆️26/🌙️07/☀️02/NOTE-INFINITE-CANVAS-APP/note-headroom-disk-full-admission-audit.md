# Note compiler-volume admission under disk-full pressure

Attempt 49 stopped after 717 seconds of queued compiler-image admission at 0.0 GiB free. The terminal error identified `code: ENOSPC`, `syscall: open` and the exact `headroom.lock` in the unit-cache lock directory. The allocation had already recorded a waiting row. The initial registration path also returned an unlimited lease whenever it could not create its directory or waiting row, even when the image had been measured and was full. [Headroom admission](../../../../../../../🧰️framework/🛍️products/🦑️repo/🔨️modules/📚️library/⚡️caching/🦀️cargo/🗃️unit-cache/🧹️gc/🏦️headroom/🟦️.ts)

The measured path now defers disk-full waiting-row creation and exact disk-full claim opening after the existing ballast attempt. It preserves the original queue arrival time, reports when it is waiting to write admission metadata, and uses existing paced retries and single-flight reclamation. Both kinds of deferral are bounded by the existing claim-attempt policy. The lease is granted only after the real exclusive claim and successful running-row write. An unmeasured volume retains its prior policy. Foreign-path errors, claim body failures and cancellation still propagate.
