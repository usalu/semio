It reports 5,493 missing prefixes, 3,402 duplicate-sibling findings, 173 multiple-emoji names, 34 generic emojis, 219 presentation mismatches, six independent Unicode-oracle disagreements, and no emoji-prefixed reserved names.

This is not a completion audit. The inventory was captured before the newest Stdio scaffolding, Architect, Graph and development output changes finished. Its findings include ignored/currently generated material and require owner-specific classification; a tool-required literal name is not permission to exempt its whole surrounding tree.

The largest remaining scopes are Stdio (5,707 findings), OS (643), Norm (469), FEM (425), Puzzle (225), CAD (200), Mit-Bestand (187), repository product (172), Note (152) and Block (150). Counts are findings, not necessarily distinct files. Concurrent additions can increase counts, so they are not compared as a clean before/after score.

Individual decisions and verification stay in their scoped reports. The exact diagnostic snapshot is `🗑️generated/current-global-emoji-audit.json`; no naming mutation was run by this audit.
