# Content v2 report NW-H3 (new humans)

Assets (folder `S/🗂️catalog/👤️humans/<id>/🔣️.json`): `standing-phone-user`, `courier-with-hand-truck`, `pregnant-person`, `runner-with-blade-prosthesis`.
Scratch: `T/🧰️scripts/NW-H3/` (rig scaling `h3lib.py`, gait generator `gait.py`, per-asset `a_*_rig/poses/geom.py`, builders `build_*.py`, local rule mirror `h3check.py`,
`check.sh`/`probe.sh` wrappers for the shared-machine official check). Everything is generated by the builders from hand-entered station tables; no simplification algorithm.

## Method

- Rig: the archive human template scaled per asset (de Leva male/female segment fractions, own proportions, 1.66-1.82 m), with asset bones added
  (phone; truck-lift prismatic float + hand-truck + parcels; belly; blade-r + blade-tip-r replacing foot/toe-r).
- Poses are solved by IK (legs to planted ankles, arms to grips/backs); walking and running clips are generated from planted heel/ball/blade-tip paths
  (stance feet fixed to the millimetre, checked by FK), 30 fps keys with a closing key; the runner clip uses authored per-frame key poses with explicit ballistic phases.
- Physical scenario: layered clothing regions (ISO 9920 clo, 1 clo = 0.155 m2K/W, vapour resistance R/(0.0165 x 0.38)), DuBois skin area, Mifflin-St Jeor basal metabolism,
  Gagge defaults, respiration patterns with nose/mouth emitters on the head, plume emitter, exhaustive pose bindings. Anything not in `references-physiology.md` as verified is tagged `authored-estimate`.
- Representations: four individually drawn scales per asset (no `unavailable`), station tables per bone; 1:500 merged masses (arms/hand/toe merged, thin parts as strokes), 1:200 separated masses,
  1:100 articulated clothing with restrained hatch, 1:50 full articulation; far limbs coincide with near limbs in static poses so that hidden-line removal leaves no slivers.
