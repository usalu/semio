## Known simplifications
Vertical-axis motions (slewing, steering, door swing) are not elevation joints. Cylinder pin closure is exact at keys and approximate (centimetres) between them. Crane pendants are drawings only. Inertial structure is used for car, bus and forklift where motion is uniformly accelerated; clips without acceleration are quasi-static. The 3-D roll balance of the riderless bike is outside the model. Rope pieces of the crane are exact at key times (linear interpolation) and deviate marginally with Catmull-Rom easing. The continuous-rotation loop rule (closing key +360 deg k) is not needed: no vehicle clip loops a wheel rotation (the excavator dig-cycle loop has no unlimited joint).
