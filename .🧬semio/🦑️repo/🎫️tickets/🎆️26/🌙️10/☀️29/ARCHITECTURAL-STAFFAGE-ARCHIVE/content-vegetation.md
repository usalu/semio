## Honest tooling disclosure
Every number was chosen by hand (rig, masses, loads, silhouettes, lobes, texture marks), but not typed as final JSON:
scratch tools in `T/🧰️scripts/vegetation/` (inputs under `design/`) expand compact notation. They never invent content.
- `w2l.ts`: design shapes are authored in WORLD rest coordinates and converted rigidly to bone-local coordinates; it also expands the
  notations `taper` (tapered limb outline from two endpoints, widths, bow), `cloud` (scalloped foliage outline from centre, radii and a
  per-lobe radius list) and `marks` (short curved strokes from x, y, angle, length) into plain `path` shapes. The shipped JSON contains only `path`/`poly`/`ellipse` shapes.
- `texmass.py`, `frond.py`, `blades.py`, `mkloads.py`, `mkrig.py`, `mkc.py`, `sway.py`, `gusttracks.py`, `diurnal.py`: formatting of
  hand-chosen values. Leaf-texture mark sets are a few hand-placed templates (round / flame / plate / pendulous / curtain) mirrored and offset per foliage mass;
  palm leaflets and miscanthus blade fans are laid out along each rachis / bundle bone from the rest pose; sway keys sample a
  hand-chosen amplitude/phase table; diurnal leaf and bark temperatures are the engine's own `solveThermal` results at hourly solar levels
  (so the keyed values are consistent with the thermal view); wind loads come from `wind.py` (F = ½ρ·cd·A·v²).
- Stale-risk: the final JSON is regenerated by `build.sh <id>` + `assemble.sh`; do not hand-edit it without updating `design/`.

## Common physics
- Wind: ρ = 1.25 kg/m³ (EN 1991-1-4), power-law profile v(z) = v10 (z/10)^0.22, frontal area per foliage mass (ellipse × depth factor, stated
  in each pose description), cd 0.45 / 0.35 / 0.22 (calm / fresh breeze 8 m/s / gale 22 m/s; Vogel 1989 reconfiguration, Mayhead 1973 for conifers),
  stem drag as `distributed` load. Loads kind `wind`, direction +x. Gust clip: `load:*` tracks 0.05 → 2.75 → 0.05 (EN 1991-1-4 peak pressure, Iv 0.25).
- Structure: `dynamics: "quasi-static"`, fixed `base-support` anchor at the root bone listed in `structure.supports`; bone masses (wood = volume × green density,
  Wood Handbook; foliage `authored-estimate`), solid circular sections on stem/limb bones. Base moments from the check: oak ≈ 41 kN·m (fresh breeze) / ≈ 100 kN·m (gale), see views.
- Thermal: foliage regions `leaf` (stomatal conductance, leaf size) with source `literature` (Körner et al. 1979 ranges) or `authored-estimate` where scaled by species/shade;
  bark `passive`; transpiration emitters `carries: "vapour"`, bound explicitly in every pose. Conductance is constant over the day (the solver has no night closure),
  documented in every asset; night appears only in emitter strength. Latent totals at noon: oak ≈ 40 kW, poplar ≈ 32 kW, willow ≈ 55 kW, pine ≈ 8 kW, palm ≈ 15 kW,
  birch ≈ 12 kW, hedge ≈ 3 kW, miscanthus ≈ 0.4 kW (order of magnitude: single-tree sap flow of 0.1 to 1 m³/day).
- No winter-bare poses (shapes cannot be toggled). Third poses are gales; fourth poses (wet snow / ice / snow on plates / snow on hedge top / wet snow on blades) exist where
  physically sensible (oak, birch, pine, hedge, miscanthus). No `key: true` poses are used (clips override bones on the named `windy` pose).
- All clips use `ease: "smooth"`; sway clips loop and are periodic (first key = last key); gust is non-looping; diurnal-transpiration loops over 24 s (1 s = 1 h).

### english-oak
Rig: root-plate (fixed) → trunk → trunk-upper → leader → leader-top; 4 limbs, 8 branches, 8 foliage-mass bones; limits ±2.5° (trunk) widening to ±12° (foliage).
Silhouette: short massive bole (d 0.8–0.9 m), buttress flare, four thick spreading limbs, dome crown of 8 overlapping scalloped masses with small gaps showing limbs/twigs; bark fissures and knots at lod 2–3, twig tufts lod 2, leaf marks lod 1–3.
Physics: oak-wood 950 kg/m³, E 8.1 GPa, MOR 57 MPa (Wood Handbook); leaf-sun gs 3.5 mm/s, leaf-shade 2.2 mm/s, leaf size 0.08 m; snow 10 kg/m² on foliage plan area.
Simplification: one rigid body per pose; foliage 450 m² one-sided leaf area.

### lombardy-poplar
Rig: 4 stem segments, 8 ascending tier branches, 8 foliage flames + spire. Silhouette: single straight stem, flame-shaped masses overlapping into a dense column (bare stem only below 2.7 m). gs 5.5 mm/s, leaf 0.06 m. Largest base moment per weight (slender, tall).

### multi-stem-birch
Rig: three separate stems (each lower + upper bone, so they sway with different phases), 6 branches, 9 foliage clouds. White stems with lenticel dashes (lod 2–3), dark base bark, pendulous twig strands (lod 1–3). gs 6 mm/s, leaf 0.05 m. Ice pose bends outward under 0.64–0.92 kN per cloud (order of magnitude after EN 1991-1-3).

### scots-pine
Rig: bole in 3 segments + leader, 2 dead stubs, 6 limbs, 7 needle-plate bones. Two-tone bole (grey-brown plated bark below 7 m, orange flaking above), flat-topped crown of horizontal plates from 12 m. Needles: leaf size 0.002 m, gs 2.5 mm/s (shaded 1.8), projected needle area 150 m². Wind loads only on the crown plus lower-bole line load (upper-bole drag neglected, stated).

### date-palm
Rig: root, 3 stem segments, crown shaft, 8 fronds × (proximal + distal) = 16 bones, 2 fruit-bunch bones. Ringed stem with diamond leaf-base texture, shaggy shaft, pinnate fronds (blade, rachis, leaflets at lod 2/3). Fronds fold to the lee (CW) in wind; limits ±18° / ±30°. Hot dry environment (32 °C, 30 % RH, 800 W/m²); gs 2.5 mm/s (authored estimate), leaf 0.1 m. Fruit bunches are always drawn (shapes cannot be toggled). Stem stiffness is an authored estimate (no reference).

### weeping-willow
Rig: root, 2 trunk segments, 6 arching limb segments, 2 caps, 8 hanging curtain pendulums (each its own bone with 35 kg and revolute ±40°). Lee swing is a drag/weight equilibrium (≈ 9° at 8 m/s, ≈ 30° at 22 m/s); wind sway clip uses pendulum periods ≈ 5 s. gs 9 mm/s, leaf 0.02 m, 330 m² leaf area (highest transpiration of the set).
Known simplification: the cap masses are flat-looking domes; no individual strands move.

### hornbeam-hedge
Rig: row base (fixed, 3 m along +x), 6 stem bundles, 6 clipped blocks. Overlapping blocks with scalloped clipped tops; seams between blocks read as clip panels; stems visible below 0.35 m at 1:50. Snow on the flat top (150 N per block). gs 4.5 mm/s (core 2.5), leaf 0.07 m.

### miscanthus-grass
Rig: crown, 4 culms (lower, arching tip, plume), 6 blade bundles (rising + arching bone). Blades drawn as fans of narrow tapered pieces, plumes as feathery tapers with strand marks. In wind the arching blade segments rotate CCW (drag on a drooping blade lifts or lowers the tip as derived), keeping tips above ground. C4 grass gs 6 mm/s, blade width 0.015 m.
