## Common physics (all six)
Operating keys with named levels (en/de); regions with model, geometry, sky view, layers, heat capacity; `operating.heatW` released at level 1 calibrated from v1 steady rises with total film coefficients 10 to 22 W/(m2 K) (so warm-up is a transient with minutes time constants because heat capacity is the active shell mass); exhaust/vent emitters with `volumeFlowM3S`, `temperatureRiseK`, `vapourKgPerKg`, `operatingKey`; pose bindings exhaustive (sun fraction, exposure, tyre/track/ballast ground contact); clip tracks and ignition/shutdown events. Sources: `authored-estimate` for machine data, `literature` only for material properties (Incropera 2011, ASHRAE 2021, Heywood 2018, Limpert 1999, Wong 2008 as references; added reference `incropera-2011`). Representations "50" reuse the v1 detail drawing (lod dropped, hatch only on glazing); 500/200/100 redrawn.

## Per asset
- **city-car** (petrol): keys engine-load (off/idle 0.2/part 0.5/full), rolling, braking. Tailpipe 0.08 m3/s, +280 K, 0.075 kg/kg vapour at full load (derived from rho_a eta_v V N/2, Heywood). Regions: panels, roof, bonnet, tailgate, glazing, engine bay 1.1 kW, exhaust 2.3 kW, tyres, brake discs 36 kW. Clips: drive-by (ignition), park-and-open-hatch (shutdown). 500: body silhouette without arches + greenhouse + panels + wheel discs; bonnet/tailgate drawn thicker (0.14 m) with body cut back, so they still hinge. 200: arches, roof band, two panes, engine-bay slot, exhaust, rim/disc. 100: + door gaps, lamps, mirror, 5 spokes, windscreen. No unavailable scale.
- **city-bus** (BEV): keys traction, rolling, braking, hvac; no exhaust; roof heat-pump `vent` emitter (1.5 m3/s, +22 K, dry); body, roof, doors, glazing are `conditioned` (21 C, double glazing). New clip layover-shutdown, ignition event in depart. 500: box + 3 glass bands + 2 door openings with 4 leaves + pods + wheel discs; 200: individual panes, roof band, door leaves; 100: + pillar lines, lamps, mirror, rim holes (brakes), handrails.
- **cargo-bike**: keys assist (30 W motor, 9 W battery), rolling, braking; no emitters (riderless). Tubes 0.022 to 0.06 m cannot be printed as outlines at 1:500/1:200: drawn as centre-line strokes (500: 0.13 mm = thicker than real, stated; 200/100: strokes at about real width); wheels solid discs at 500/200. Steer rod added at all scales (v1 omitted it visually).
- **excavator** (diesel): keys engine-load, hydraulic; stack +360 K, 0.45 m3/s, 0.04 kg/kg; new clip start-and-idle (ignition), shutdown in dump-and-stow. 500: belt, house (engine+exhaust merged), cab glass, counterweight, boom, stick, bucket, boom cylinder only; 200: + belt circles, hood, stack, all three cylinders; 100: + rollers, pin bosses, slew ring, seat.
- **tower-crane**: keys hoisting, slewing (renamed from hoist); ventilation emitter on hoisting; cab conditioned. Lattice decision by printability: 1:500 composed toned silhouettes (chords 0.1 m / diagonals 0.06 m are 0.2/0.12 mm); 1:200 individual members as strokes of 0.25 to 0.5 mm (real width); 1:100 members 0.5 to 0.8 mm plus horizontals; 1:50 v1 members. Ropes/pendants (0.02 m) kept as 0.13 mm lines at 500 (exceeds tolerance, stated). Symbol `site-plan-reach` (plan, true-extent, 500/200). Events: ignition in lift-and-traverse, shutdown in unload.
- **forklift** (electric): keys traction, lifting (renamed from lift), rolling, braking; motor-vent `vent` emitter; ignition event in lift-pallet, shutdown in drive-with-load. 500: body, guard as line frame, mast bars, pallet block, wheel discs; 200: + dash electronics, tilt cylinder, rim/hub; 100: + seat, steering wheel, controls, rim holes.
