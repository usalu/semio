# Horse (animals) notes

- Fragments: `🧰️scripts/animals/horse/` (00-head, 10-rig, 20-anchors, 30-geometry, 40-poses, 50-clips, 60-thermal). Scratch: `horse-ik.py`, `horse-emit.py` (IK poses), `horse-clips.py`, `horse/gen-*.py` (rig/geometry coordinate conversion). Output `🗑️generated/animals/horse.json`; check prints a check mark.
- Dimensions: height 2.37 m (ears, head high in standing-alert), width 2.9 m, depth 0.62 m, 550 kg; withers 1.6 m. Rig: 36 bones (pelvis, lumbar, thorax, 3-bone neck, head, jaw, 2 ears, 4-bone tail; per side scapula, humerus, radius, cannon, pastern (fetlock), hoof; hind thigh, gaskin, cannon, pastern, hoof).
- Poses (named): standing-alert, standing-rest (near hind rested on its toe, not in contact set), grazing-head-down. Key poses (`key: true`): walk-phase-0..7.
- Clips: walk-cycle (0.95 s, lateral sequence near hind, near fore, far hind, far fore, duty 0.64, plant, advanceM 1.25, about 1.3 m/s), graze-and-swish (6.4 s loop, chewing 75/min, two tail swishes, ear flicks, snort; contacts planted).
- Sources: Hildebrand 1965, Muybridge 1887 (gait); Buchner et al. 1997 (mass fractions, authored-estimate); Back and Clayton 2013 (joint ranges); Spector 1956 Meeh k = 9.0 gives 6.04 m2 (the regions sum to 6.04 rather than 5.9); Scholander 1950, Cena and Monteith 1975, Gebremedhin and Wu 2001 (coat insulation, authored-estimate); Hoyt and Taylor 1981, Kleiber 1947, Hodgson and Rose 1994 (metabolism, respiration).
- Thermal: activity met is rest 1.5, grazing 1.9, alert 1.7, walk 4.2 (about 1.5 kW). This is deliberately above the suggested 1 and 2.5-3 because a 550 kg horse emits about 1 W/kg (about 90 W/m2 of coat), i.e. 1.5 met in the 58.15 W/m2 unit; baseMet 1.5. Breath 12/min (period 5 s), 3.6 m/s exhale, 30 C, vapour; separate snort emitter. Sweat as wettedness override 0.08 on trunk, neck and limbs in walk poses.
- Loads: lead-rope equipment load at the halter ring in walk poses. Supports: none declared; contacts come from poses and clip spans.
- Simplifications: 2D side view, only vertical-plane joints; hoof abs angle fixed so soles stay flat in stance; scapula rotates about its upper end (limits IK reach to +-0.4 m hoof excursion); no suspensory apparatus or tendon model; hindquarter thigh/gaskin outlines are boxy at 1:50; torso segment seams visible as lines.
