- NOT the environment: the committed recipe produces the same commit ids (`45a608ff…`) with git 2.54 under a quiet and under a hostile ambient (signing on, CRLF, latin1, other identity, other TZ, GIT_CONFIG_COUNT, bogus GIT_DIR). I also brute-forced timezone offsets, committer identity, message newline and CRLF/emoji-path variants against `20839f5b…`: none reproduces it.
