# Result spr: granted multi-record History decoder (`RetainedHistoryDecode`)

Executor Sonnet 5.5, foreground, no sub-agents, no cargo/rustc build, no modifying git command, no `package.json`/`bun.lock` edit, nothing under frozen storage/revision paths. Rust was checked statically only (rustfmt parse + patched differential). Nothing Rust was compiled or run.

## Finding

The red law `OS/📡️spr/📜️history/🛬️decode/🧪️tests` (0/1 in tst2/law2/law4) is a text law over the old single-file `📜️history/🦀️.rs`. Since the monolith carve (`0c3e7894`) the granted, borrowed-range receiving frontier already existed as `RetainedHistorySourceDecode` (`🛬️decode/🗂️file`, with `RetainedHistorySourceFrames`, `RetainedSourceDictionary`, edit/transition/conflict/identity children, paged indexes) and the store `MemberHistoryReceiving` used it. What stayed red:

1. the legacy whole-frame `RetainedHistoryDecode` (`step(bytes, maximum_bytes, maximum_records)`, `decode_frame`, `take_auxiliary_owners`, `RetainedSprLimits`) was still the public decoder and had four production holdouts (archive load, maintenance ladder, window config pack load, document load) plus tests;
2. the law anchored on shapes (`pub fn demands(`, `RetainedWireFieldDecode`, `original_source_ranges`) that cannot hold in one file after the carve.

The Member operation law (`🏪️store/…/🛬️receiving/🟦️.ts`) already forbids `RetainedHistoryDecode::new_persisted_document`, so the direction was fixed: replace the legacy decoder by a driver over the granted engine and migrate every holdout (greenfield: no bridge, no compat).

## What changed

### Production

- `📡️spr/📜️history/🦀️.rs`: the legacy decoder (about 280 lines) is deleted. `RetainedHistoryDecode` is now the granted driver over `RetainedHistorySourceDecode`: one cumulative `NativeForwardedDecodeReceipt` ledger, one `NativeDecodeRetirementRecipient`, per-turn rebinding of the progress and allocation ports (`NativeForwardedDecodeControl::rebind` / `detach`), `step(source, turn, grant, &mut Option<HistoryLog>)`, `close_step(source, turn, grant)`, `demands(body)`, `take_receipt()`, `source_identity()`, `consumed_end()`, `fault()`, `terminal_is_empty()`; fault and partial owners are retired through the same paid birth/close protocol as Member (local `box_demands` / `birth_demands` / `admit_owned` / `close_box`, because the history crate cannot depend on `os-store-read`). New: `RetainedHistoryTurn` (caller authority: `should_yield`, `is_cancelled`) and `RetainedHistoryUninterrupted`. The turn grant is the only capacity ceiling of a turn; no scalar fuel, no record count parameter.
- `📡️spr/🦀️.rs` (facade): exports `RetainedHistoryOpMetaFault`, `RetainedHistorySourceStep`, `RetainedHistoryTurn`, `RetainedHistoryUninterrupted`; `RetainedHistoryDecodeStep` is gone.
- `🔌️plugin/🗃️document/🗄️document-archive-load/🦀️.rs` + `🗃️document/🦀️.rs`: archive History read policy (`HistoryReadLimits` from the archive ceilings), `document_archive_history_decoder`; `RetireParentHistoryAuxiliary` phase and `retained_pending` removed (dictionary and edit ids are borrowed ranges and actual edit buffers now, so there is no auxiliary owner); `history_custody` is the decoded log staging for its paid retirement; `retirement_demands` and `close_step` drive the decoder to terminal before the log and the physical archive retire.
- `🔌️plugin/🧵️instance/📄️docs/📥️staging/🪜️maintenance-ladder/🦀️.rs`: `DecodeParent` quotes `history.demands(body)`, steps with `StepTurn` (the actual `StepContext` answers yield and cancellation), returns the paid receipt; ready log moves straight to hydrate or merge.
- `🔌️plugin/🧵️instance/🔌️plugin-app-impl/🦀️.rs` + `🗃️document-load/🦀️.rs`: construction through `document_archive_history_decoder`.
- `🔌️plugin/🪟️window/🎚️config/📥️retained/📦️typed-pack-load/🦀️.rs`: window config History policy (`window_config_history_limits`), `advance_history` over the driver, `LoadOriginal::{HistoryAndAuxiliary, Auxiliary}` removed, close drives the decoder; `retirement_demands` quotes `owner.demands(body)`.
