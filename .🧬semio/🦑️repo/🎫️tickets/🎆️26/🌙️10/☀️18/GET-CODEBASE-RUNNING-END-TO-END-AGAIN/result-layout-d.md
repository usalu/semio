## Design
Unfunded allocations of measure/arrange before: `MountedLayoutJob.child_scratch` (Vec<usize> refilled per step), `FlexTree.main`/`cross` (Vec<f32>), and the local `taffy::TaffyTree` of every `Wrapped`/`Grid` container (not priceable: taffy allocates internally).
- children: `Siblings` cloneable cursor over the admission sibling links (`📐️text-admission-and-measure-steps`); `measure_one`/`arrange_one` take `impl Iterator<Item=usize>+Clone`.
- Flow arrange: base main/cross parked in each child's own `resolved` slot (overwritten by the final rect); arithmetic identical.
- Wrapped: owned solver (line breaking like taffy `collect_flex_lines`, per-line grow/shrink/justify, align-content stretch, line-cross stretch); max-content measure = single-line measure.
- Grid: owned CSS track sizing for span-1 items (init, maximise, expand fr, stretch auto; justify/align fallback as taffy), tracks live in a funded paged `UiFixedList<TrackSize, FLEX_TRACK_CREDITS>`; `FlexTree::fund_scratch` admits one page per turn through the grant (`fund_worker_slot` MeasureLayout/SolveLayout arm), close releases the pages (`close_granted`, one owner per grant). `UiFixedList::try_reserve_capacity_one_funded` added (contract crate).
- taffy: removed from the shipped crate (`semio-framework-ui-wgpu-layout` dependency -> dev-dependency with `grid`), it is the oracle in `🧪️tests/🔬️targets-wgpu-flex-unit` (`the_owned_wrapped_solver_agrees_with_taffy_across_the_corpus`, `the_owned_grid_solver_agrees_with_taffy_across_the_corpus`, 1500 random cases each).
- laws: `every_layout_step_allocates_only_through_its_own_receipt` (heap == receipt for every admit/shape/measure/arrange/collect/close step over a tree with row, wrapped, grid, text, nested containers) and `a_four_thousand_node_surface_lays_out_inside_its_budget_in_every_dialect` in `🧪️tests/🔬️targets-wgpu-mounted-layout-unit`.
