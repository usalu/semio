# Audit: Beat2_GridRandomSampling (sampling clip)

Scope: read-only audit of the 33 English stills and 11 German stills (`de-parameters`, `de-run_2`, `de-budget`, `de-grid_2`, `de-growth_2`, `de-random_3`, `de-gaps`, `de-reveal`, `de-compare`, `de-limits_2`, `de-limits_3`) in `🗑️generated/frames/Beat2_GridRandomSampling/`, the scene source (`🎲️sampling/🐍️.py`, modified 09:48, frames captured 10:20, so the stills reflect the current code), and the two layout reports. Reference stills: `🗑️generated/energy-frames/` (`Beat1_LastZuerstSenken-natural`, `Beat5_Verschattung-raffstore`, `Beat1_LeistungUndEnergie-jahr`, `Beat3_IsolateAirflow-compare`, `Beat8_Hauptgleichung-chain`).

## Verdict

The clip is a coherent, physically plausible sampling story and is at or above the depth of the energy reference stills in most frames. One room, one colour ramp, one currency (kWh/a), and every plane point is linked to a room section. Sun altitudes are right for Zurich (19 degrees winter, 66 degrees summer). Summer overhangs block rays, small windows admit fewer rays, heating and lighting fall and cooling rises with window share, and the stacked bars sum to their totals in all frames but one (a 1 kWh/a rounding gap). The compare and reveal screens are the strongest part.

The main defects are: (1) the orientation glyphs are rotated by 180 degrees, so every compass label shows the glazed side on the wrong wall (high); (2) several collisions where value chips or labels sit on the sun, the rays, the contour labels, the dimension lines or other tags; (3) stale state from the random set carried into the Latin hypercube clause and a room that is unrelated to the reveal surface; (4) plane tags and Delta values without units or with arithmetic that does not add up on screen; (5) secondary text below the 14 px floor, and dimension labels at about 9 to 10 px. Two screens are abstract or sparse (the random table with no rooms, and the untranslated letter formula `N = m × m × … × m = m^k`). The layout reports say "none" for all 66 keys, but the overlaps listed here show that the checker misses label-on-label and label-on-sun collisions.

Severity count: high 1, med 20, low 22 (43 rows).

## Strengths to keep

- One room and one colour ramp across plane tags, thumbnails, bars, tiles and contours; the set shapes (square grid, diamond random, triangle Latin hypercube) stay the same in every frame.
- Physics reads correctly: winter sun at 19 degrees and summer at 66 degrees for Zurich; the summer overhang blocks the rays; a small window (random_3, 18 %) shows a thin ray bundle; heating falls with window share, lighting falls with window share and rises with overhang, cooling rises with window share and falls with overhang. The bars sum to their totals in every frame except evaluate_2.
- The budget story is clear: nine pips, a 3 × 3 sweep with room, sun and bar per run (grid_2), the ring on the best (grid_4), and the 3 · 3 · 4 · 3 = 108 token line with "12 × budget".
- The random table, the dashed empty box, and the closest pair with Δ WWR, Δ d and Δ kWh/a explain coverage concretely.
- Latin hypercube: nine strata with live strip chips ("WWR 41.1 % – 48.9 % · 1 point"), "9 of 81 cells occupied" and the vertical and horizontal marginals are a clear, concrete demonstration.
- Reveal and compare: the dense-grid heat map with contours and star, the ≤ 5 % band, and the four compare tiles with Δ in kWh/a and % on one scale are the strongest screens in the clip.
- German: decimal commas and thousands dots are correct (522,1; 1.681), the scoreboard words are right (Lauf, bester, Stufen, Dämmung, Vordach, Raster, Zufall), and the orientation letters are translated (O, S, W, N).
- Captions are short (two lines or fewer) and describe what is on screen in most clauses (run_2, budget, grid_2, random_3, gaps_2, marginals, cells, compare_2).
