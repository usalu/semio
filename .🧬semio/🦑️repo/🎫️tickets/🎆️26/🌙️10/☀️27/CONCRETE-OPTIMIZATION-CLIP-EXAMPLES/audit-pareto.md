# 🔍 Audit: Beat5_ParetoFront (Pareto Optimality)

Scope: all 32 English frames in narration order, 12 German frames (`sets`, `approximate`, `dominated_2`, `rule_2`, `walk_2`, `payback`, `knee_3`, `third_2`, `third_3`, `weights`, `decision`, `front_2`), 2x zoom crops of the label-collision spots (scratch crop in `C:/Users/Ueli/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-Ueli--claude-spares-spare-7DbrPU/28a619a6-7ba6-4c8d-9c28-99595879210c/scratchpad/zoom_pareto.png`). Calibrated against the energy stills in `🗑️generated/energy-frames/`. Frame folder: `🗑️generated/frames/Beat5_ParetoFront/`.

## Verdict

The clip meets the core linking rule better than most of the set. One shared tracker drives the tile on the plot, the façade section, the cost chip, the stacked electricity bar and the cards, and every visible number is internally consistent. Checked arithmetic: 21,696 - 19,210 = 2,486 CHF; 724 - 649 = 75 kWh/a; 192 / 65 = 3.0 CHF per kWh/a; 3.0 / 0.25 = 12 a; 8.4 / 0.25 = 34 a; 21,346 - 19,210 = 2,136 CHF; 776 - 649 = 127 kWh/a; 241 + 219 + 73 = 533 kWh/a; 533 / 48 m² = 11 kWh/(m²·a). The weak spots are (1) the third objective, daylight, never reaches the room, so the lamps glow identically from WWR 30 % to WWR 80 %; (2) the walk step does not show what changed (overhang 0 to 0.6 m buys the 65 kWh/a); and (3) a hard-coded English "vs" in the German daylight row. The layout reports say "none" for every clip, but they miss the recurring collisions of the "6 m" and "1 m" dimension labels with check rows, chips and thumbnails. The layout check is blind to dimension labels and needs to cover them.

## Strengths to keep

- One shared tracker set (`s.focus`) drives tile, façade section, cost chip, stacked bar and cards. Every frame I checked has the same design in all of them (for example `extremes`, `walk_2`, `knee_3`, `weights_2`).
- Sun rays are blocked by the overhang at d 0.6 m (`grid_2`, `extremes`), so the shading consequence of the overhang is shown with the energy-clip rays.
- Dominance is drawn as a quadrant with signed legs in CHF and kWh/a (`dominated_2`), and the check rows give the numbers (`rule`, `third_2`).
- The sweep (`front`) greys dominated tiles, rings the kept ones and moves the room to each kept design, which links the plot and the façade.
- The "not in the grid" ghost and the "no façade" cross at the ideal corner are good concept devices.
- The payback chart colours each step green (within lifetime) or red (beyond), with a dashed lifetime line and the chip for tariff. The knee moves correctly between `knee_2` (0.25 CHF/kWh, step 1) and `knee_3` (0.40 CHF/kWh, step 2).
- The weight slider, iso-line and pick (`weights`, `weights_2`) are internally consistent: the pick chip, the room and the marked tile agree.
- German numbers follow the German convention (19.210, 0,25, 3,0, 6,4 %). Captions fit in one or two lines in both languages, with no clipping at frame edges or caption-band overlap.

## Coverage notes

- Layout reports (`en-layout.md`, `de-layout.md`) list "none" for all 32 clauses in both languages. The overlaps above are not in them, so the layout check needs the dimension-label and chart-label cases.
- Calibration against `energy-frames/`: the energy stills use one drawing per clip, a short caption box and a title chip, and they are sparser than these Pareto frames. The Pareto frames are richer in linked values; their weak point is the link from a value to its physical cause (daylight, overhang, insulation), not screen depth.
