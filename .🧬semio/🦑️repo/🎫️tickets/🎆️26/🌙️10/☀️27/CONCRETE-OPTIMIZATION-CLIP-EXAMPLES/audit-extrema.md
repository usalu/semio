# Audit: `Beat1_LocalGlobalExtrema` (topic `📉️extrema`)

## Verdict

Not ready to render. The architecture is right: one θ tracker drives the plan, the winter/summer section, the stacked bar and the plot marker, every number carries a unit, and the bar totals reconcile (43 + 503 + 135 = 681; 181 + 803 + 147 = 1,131; 311 + 407 + 164 = 882 kWh/a; S = 1,131 − 681 = 450 kWh/a; +201 kWh/a = +29.5 %, +50 CHF/a). The `formulate_2` strip, the `negate` reflection, the `constraint` band and the θ* versus E(θ*) split are clear and match the reference depth. Three clauses, however, break the link rule or contradict their captions: the first plotted point (θ = 90°) is never drawn; the `local` clause ends with the room already at 360° N while its caption describes the 315° NW start, a state no frame ever shows; and the summer section draws a full low-sun bundle at 360° while the caption says little direct sun enters. The "Reference" label is also applied to the north ghost bar in `global` and collides with the design row on six frames. Both layout reports say "none" for every clause, so they cannot be used as sign-off. Frames are end states only; motion was not judged. Reviewed: all 27 English frames and 10 German frames (`formulate_2`, `response_2`, `domain`, `sweep_3`, `local`, `global_2`, `maximize`, `constraint_2`, `feasible_2`, `scope_2`), plus four energy reference stills (`Beat1_LeistungUndEnergie-summe`, `Beat8_Hauptgleichung-chain`, `Beat1_LastZuerstSenken-envelope`, `AnlagenVerluste-sum`). Line numbers refer to `C:/git/semio/🎓️teaching/🏛️architecture/🎯️optimization/🎬️clip/📉️extrema/🐍️.py`.

## Strengths to keep

- One θ tracker drives the plan, the section, the stacked bar and the plot marker; the marker chips ("θ 270° · 1,131 kWh/a") and the bar always agree.
- Bar totals reconcile with the plot at every clause; S, +201 kWh/a, +29.5 % and +50 CHF/a are consistent across `global_2`, `negate_2` and `feasible_2`.
- `formulate_2`: the compass unrolled into the bearing strip (East 90° … East 450°) explains the design variable clearly in both languages.
- `negate`: the E curve reflected into S(θ) with "min E ⇔ max S" and the "S = 0: east façade" tag is the strongest conceptual screen.
- `constraint` and `constraint_2`: the compass sector and the plot band use the same 90° to 270° infeasible range; the cross on the excluded square and the unchanged curve make "a constraint changes no value of E" visible.
- `argument` and `argument_2`: θ* (direction) and E(θ*) (kWh/a) are separated with two chips and unit tags.
- `compare` and `compare_2`: the two valley cards and the descent route from 315° make "a search can end in the wrong valley" concrete.
- `scope`: the Direction, Domain and Scope rows are legible at 22 px and state the optimality claim with its bounds.
- Captions fit two lines in every reviewed frame in both languages; German number formatting (1.131 kWh/a, +29,5 %, 1.200 ticks) and the German labels (Ostfassade, Hofseite, Straßenlärm, zulässiges Optimum) are correct; no truncation was found in the ten German frames.
