# 📊 Audit: Beat4_NormalizedIndex (⚖️index)

Scope: all 34 English frames viewed in narration order, 9 German frames (variants, units_2, ruler_2, matrix, criteria_2, weights_2, sweep_2, range_3, limits), both layout reports, two upscaled crops (range leaders, range_3 row border, weight chips, cost elevation, winner room), and the index, share, and rank arithmetic re-derived from the printed values. Reference stills: `Beat1_LeistungUndEnergie-summe.png`, `Beat8_Hauptgleichung-chain.png`, `AnlagenVerluste-sum.png`, `Beat1_LastZuerstSenken-reserve.png`, `Beat5_Verschattung-raffstore.png`.

## Verdict

The clip is structurally sound. One example runs through every screen, and the numbers reconcile: unit sums, normalized values, index bars, the sensitivity and range re-weighting, and the room bars (181 + 281 + 39 = 501 kWh/a, 501 / 48 m² = 10.4) all check out. The German track is complete with correct decimal formats, and the layout is clean except one collision. It is not yet at energy-clip depth, though. The opening and index screens are sparse cards and tables. A room drawing appears in only 16 of 34 English frames. Two clauses end in a wrong or stale state (sensitivity, range_2), and the constraint idea of limits is never shown on screen. The cost elevation draws a slab for d = 0.0 m and draws punched windows as a ribbon. Verdict: rework the 6 high items before this meets the quality bar; the medium items are the next pass.

## Strengths to keep

- The numbers reconcile across frames. Unit sums (21,964 to 26,625; 37.7 to 44.5) equal the printed parts. Index values (0.48, 0.40, 0.52, 0.70, 0.65) follow from weights and normalized values. The 40/30/20/10 and 23/60/11/6 re-weightings reproduce. Room bars sum correctly (181 + 281 + 39 = 501; 121 + 567 + 37 = 725). WWR 25 % of 24 m² = 6.0 m², 70 % = 16.8 m².
- Ruler plus focus ring plus room (ruler_2, winner, sweep): moving the focus moves the room and the room bars, which is the core of the visual principle.
- Energy palette reused consistently: heating red, cooling teal, lighting yellow in the room bars. Criteria colours stay fixed: electricity teal, cost orange, daylight yellow, carbon red.
- The units beat (units to units_3) shows the unit dependence concretely: the teal electricity segment grows from invisible to the largest part once the sum is taken in kCHF and t.
- range_2 ghost dots at the old positions show the re-normalization clearly. range_3 shows the order flip with per-row rank change (Shaded 3 to 2, Punched 2 to 3).
- The sweep and sweep_2 close-call logic is tied to the live winner's room, and the Shaded/Ribbon crossing at 0-11 % is visible.
- Thumbnails sit in evenly spaced slots with leader lines, and exact values are carried by the dots and their labels, so overlapping thumbnails are avoided.
- German numbers use decimal commas in the rulers and matrix, and dot thousands in the units and criteria cards. Caption lengths fit the two-line band in both languages.
- Captions match the picture in most clauses, for example "cost ruler: cheapest becomes zero" (cost), "Zero is the best of these five" (mapped), "Strengths elsewhere outweigh its weakest criterion" (winner_2).
