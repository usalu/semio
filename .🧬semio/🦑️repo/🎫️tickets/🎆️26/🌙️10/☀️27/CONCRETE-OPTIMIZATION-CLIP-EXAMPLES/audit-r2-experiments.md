Read-only audit. ## 1. Verdict

The clip is well past the first audit: every number on screen matches the model (61 of 62 string checks OK, the one mismatch is a sign), the old "427 − 252 = +176" arithmetic, the 0 kWh/a curvature bar and the thumbnail-only fraction are fixed, and the 2 × 2 row of live rooms is a good teaching device. It is still far from the owner's bar: the interaction is printed with the opposite sign of its own narration, the ray legend and blocked-sun chain do not match what is drawn (all 7 rays of the 70 % room are crossed while the model blocks 59 % of that solar gain), and the model, curvature and repeat beats rely on a waterfall, unlabelled curves and bars on a kWh scale instead of rooms that move with the values. About 20 labels still collide with lines or with each other, and the depth stays well below the energy clips (no substituted formulas with units in large type, no hero room per clause, no leader-linked colour matching), so the clip is not deliverable until the blocker and the majors in section 3 are fixed.

## 4. Fix proposals

Each fix names the value to bind, the element to move, and the target region in the 1280 × 720 still. Bind every number to `evaluate` or to `FACTORIAL`, `FRACTION` and `CENTRE`, not to literals.

- D01 (blocker): Rename "saving" to "overhang effect" everywhere in the strip and chips, and print the two signed effects: "overhang effect at 70 % = −278.9 kWh/a", "at 30 % = −122.3 kWh/a", then "A·B = ½ × (−278.9 − (−122.3)) = −78.3 kWh/a". Change the narration of difference and difference_2 (EN and DE) to "half the difference of the two overhang effects". Keep the chip "Interaction (A·B) = −78.3 kWh/a" in the same colour. Check the sign convention against `_factorial()` effects["ab"] (already −78.3).
- D02: Replace the legend text (top right, x ≈ 690–1245, y ≈ 100–110) with "1 ray = 0.3 m of glass: 204 kWh/a solar gain, 122 kWh/a of it direct and blockable". Bind 204 to solar_summer(r3) ÷ 7 and 122 to 204 × direct_share_summer. Keep the 30 % saving (122) in its own colour so the two numbers are not mistaken for each other.
- D03: Draw each ray as two strands: the direct beam (yellow, crossed when shaded) and the diffuse 40 % (faint, always passes to the floor). Scale the crossed direct strand by shading (0.977 at 70 % + 1 m) so one ray is only 98 % crossed. Chain at 70 %: "blocked direct sun 837 of 1,428 kWh/a (59 %)". Remove "7 × 122" logic.
- D04: Write the chain as a change, not a division: "Blocked direct sun 837 kWh/a → cooling electricity avoided 279 kWh/a (chiller EER 3.0)". Use the model difference (278.9) for the plot arrow and state "≈ 837 ÷ 3.0". Drop the minus sign on the chain; keep "−" only on the plot arrows labelled "change".
