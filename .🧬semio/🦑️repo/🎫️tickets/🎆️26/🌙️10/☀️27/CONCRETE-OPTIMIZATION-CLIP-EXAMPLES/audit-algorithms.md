# Audit: Beat6_EvolutionarySearch (Algorithms and Evolutionary Search)

Scope: read-only audit of the English frames (all 44 keys, in narration order), 10 German frames spread over the clip (task, gradient_2, overshoot_2, pattern_2, population, crossover, tournament_2, survival, stopping, tools_2), both layout reports, and the energy reference stills (Beat1_LeistungUndEnergie-summe, Beat8_Hauptgleichung-chain, AnlagenVerluste-sum, Beat5_Verschattung-raffstore, Beat1_LastZuerstSenken-reserve). Frames: `🗑️generated/frames/Beat6_EvolutionarySearch/`.

## Verdict

The clip has the right architecture: one south-façade seminar room, a map that is fogged and revealed, a room section and a stacked bar that follow the current design, and a run tape with a live counter. The probe chips, the map minimum star and the final result chips mostly agree with each other. It is not ready to ship. Three problems block it. First, every search frame shows a cooling number in the room (for example 1,237 kWh/a) that is about three times the cooling segment of the bar on the same screen (613 kWh/a total); the room shows cooling demand and the bar shows electricity, with no label saying so. Second, the genetic beats show values the solver has not produced or that contradict the cards on screen: "best 497 kWh/a" appears at 0 of 50 simulations, "best 563 kWh/a" appears beside a card at 548 kWh/a, and the elite stars sit on 587 and 598 kWh/a while better cards are visible. Third, the y tick labels 0.0 to 0.9 run past the left frame edge in every map frame (148 layout items, English and German). Depth is below the energy clips: rooms are small with 9 to 10 px dimension labels, chips and cards are about 12 to 13 px, the update rules are never written as a formula with live numbers, and four clauses (compare_2, generations_2, population_3, close) show no visual change from their predecessor. German text does not truncate in the 10 frames checked; one German counter disagrees with English (see row 6).

## Strengths to keep

- One shared design study across the clip: the start (75 %, 0.10 m, 745 kWh/a), the map minimum (495 kWh/a at about 66 % / 0.99 m) and the run counts (1,681; 50; 41 and 16 to 1 %) agree from frame to frame, and the chips match the bar deltas (for example "Change -132 kWh/a" equals 745 to 613).
- Map, room and bar move together in the search beats: the dot on the map, the window and overhang in the room, and the bar total update in gradient_4, pattern_3 and pattern_5.
- Probe chips carry units and signed slopes: "windows 74.3 % · 741 kWh/a" and "Slope windows 5.0 kWh/a per %" show that more window raises and more overhang lowers electricity.
- The run tape and counter with the "50 simulations · 3 %" sliver against 1,681 in proof make the cost of a simulation visible.
- The fog metaphor works: the heat map dims, only simulated points are painted in their map colour, and the star marks the map minimum with its label.
- Genetic crossover and mutation are clear: the two parent façades with gene chips, the curved arrow into the child, the dashed mutation step, and the before and after chip (583 to 563 kWh/a).
- The stopping and compare charts use the same colours and the same budget, and the step curves for the three methods read well.
- German: numbers use decimal commas and dot thousand separators, chips and captions fit in both languages, and no German caption truncates in the 10 frames checked.
- Captions stay at two lines or fewer and clear the caption band; the stage ends above the caption band in all frames.
