# Early Two-Boiler Evidence: Source and Chronology R92

SAB2024p231 explicitly attributes two Idéal boilers,44,800 and16,800calorías, not combined, to a31May1932 report by Compagnie de Chauffage Central par le Vide (FLC H1(12)102 and147). This is strong evidence of TWO early physical units by1932. The accessible content is the scholarly report attribution; the primary report sheets themselves have not been directly inspected. The reproduction does not establish a per-hour unit, a separate function for each boiler, installation date, or second location.

The same account mentions sheet-metal smoke conduits and four elbows connected to a cement chimney stated20cm diameter and12m height. Do not turn this into four elbows per unit, a recovered bore diameter, or a drawn route without the original report.

Visually reviewed FLC U1(14)063/064/065 reproductions (book230–232): these are1965 original-trace heating drawings, not contemporaneous1930 surveys. No readable pair of independently located boiler footprints can be extracted from retained raster content. Author basement plan44 likewise gives no labelled two-unit placement. Book230 explicitly notes known errors in the1965 trace (kitchen radiator and omitted parents-bath radiator), so it is evidence of network intent, not infallible original geometry.

BL-1 currently represents one physical boiler. A passing BL-1 native test does not represent the attested1932 pair. However, forcing a second boiler into the1930–31 occupied model would invent a location and possibly chronology. Record an unlocated second early unit, capacities/date and unresolved function, and seek the primary102/147 sheets or a labelled early cellarplan/photo before physical placement. Presence by1932 is strongly attested; exact presence/location in the selected1930–31 target remains unresolved.

## Official inventory follow-up

The [Fondation’s direct Savoye archive inventory](https://www.fondationlecorbusier.fr/archive/architecture-savoye-villa-poissy-france-1928/) is now inspected. It confirms H1-12 holdings, grouped76–144 as1930–31 correspondence and145–155 as1934 heating correspondence; these are broad inventory groups, not dates of individual sheets102/147. Therefore they neither replace the report date reproduced by SAB231 nor secure original installation chronology. The page provides catalogue descriptions, not direct102/147 boiler facsimiles. It separately lists original heating/sanitary estimates underH1-13/1–11 (1928), and later TRACE-origin heating sheets underU1-14. No observed inventory entry supplies a second plantXY or distinct function. The official source supports continued archival retrieval, not fabricated geometry.
