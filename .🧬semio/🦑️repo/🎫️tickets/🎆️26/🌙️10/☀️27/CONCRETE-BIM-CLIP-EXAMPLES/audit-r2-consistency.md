## 1. Verdict

The clip has left the bare box behind: it shows the real Villa Savoye (axonometric, section, roof plan), the values are read from the model, and the arithmetic reconciles (146 − 105 = 41 kN; 11.28 m² × 12.92 kN/m² ≈ 146 kN; 0.20 + 0.10 + 0.06 + 0.02 = 0.38 m, and 0.58 m after the event). It is still clearly below the bar: about half of the ten beats carry their concept as a table or a label rather than as a drawn part of the villa, and the recap and limits beats do not show what their narration says. Two defects block release: the clip shows two different "roof loads" (12.92 and 11.78 kN/m²), and the recap narrates "separate models" while the frame shows one assembled villa; layout and depth are also well behind the energy clips (about ten text overlaps and four clipped labels in English, the same in German, a plan too small to read, and no formulas, reference chips or numbers in captions).
