## Garden Cornice: Do Not Invent Replacement Datums

Annex 6 unmistakably draws a shaped concave cap at the open garden edge and a +6.77 leader at that top detail. The cap/body silhouette differs from the ordinary adjacent roof field. Figure 10 also shows the special edge beam and its post connection. This establishes a missing shaped water-cap/cornice detail in a plain rectangular representation.

It does not establish a defensible new full-body bottom/top pair from this raster. The existing nominal 16×32cm and inferred 6.19–6.51m register must remain labelled inferred. In particular, simply replacing it by 6.45–6.77m would assume that the +6.77 weathering/cornice top is the structural rectangular beam top. The image does not justify that assumption. Resolve the reinforcement-body line separately from cap, render and gutter before changing the full beam envelope. Annex 6 and Figure 10 are mutually necessary; neither supports treating +6.77 as the general roof structural top.

## Aerodynamic Posts 16 and 22

Article §6.2 explicitly distinguishes three sections along each post: engagement with the parapet, exposed zone, and cornice support. The visible Figure 10 cross-section drawings are materially different contours, not a single ellipse repeated over the full height. The article further distinguishes the originally proposed 14cm, handwritten reduction to 12cm, and measured finished exposed width of 15cm. These are design-versus-built states, not interchangeable exact sections.

A single 15cm-wide, 23cm-long profile remains an inferred teaching trace. The article does not attest 23cm as a measured major dimension. The present raster does not allow a confident transcription of every section station, major dimension or concrete-versus-plaster boundary. Do not substitute a 12cm structural profile for the measured 15cm finished exposed width without explicit surface ownership. At minimum preserve the three-region semantic distinction and record missing dimensions/stations honestly; historical precision requires the original higher-resolution FLC-H1-13-310 or a measured book drawing.

## Facade Wall and Curtain-Lintel Omissions

The inspected source has W-S, W-E, W-N and W-W each using an assumed 0.30m thickness and TY-WALL. Many internal/service walls retain an assumed 0.15m. These are authored assumptions, not survey dimensions. The parental suite author is replacing the affected local partitions; this audit makes no competing edits.

Primary book p96, as retrieved by the parent, identifies 20×22cm lintels at cantilever facades and 16×22cm elsewhere, with finished facade totals of 29cm and 26cm respectively. It describes an 8cm outer leaf, 5.5cm inner leaf, 2.5cm exterior render and 1.5cm interior plaster. These solid layers alone do not sum to either total; retain the source-specific cavity/structural relationship instead of forcing the aggregate 30cm slab into a fictitious single masonry material. The excerpt does not justify reducing every exterior wall globally to one of these two totals without mapping the facade families.

The verified 2022 article Figure 6 independently identifies the hanging L lintel, an inner-leaf U metal support, small RC parapet posts, and a 6cm RC shelf. Its accompanying text explains that the free facade needs auxiliary concrete stiffening not represented in the main structural plans. Consequently the completed primary beam register does not establish complete facade structure. Current source has no separately identifiable curtain-lintel/corner-hanger/inner-leaf support products or corresponding material parts in the audited named entries. Parent-retrieved p96 further establishes 11×11cm finished corner hangers. Map those to actual facade segments before authoring, and keep inferred placement separate from sourced section size.

## Frames and Moving Glazing

TY-RIBBON presently has MAT-GLASS and no physical frame sections, original/restored frame distinction, glazing rebate, curtain recess or operable module schedule. RW-S/E/N/W and RW-W-UPPER reference that type. GL-TERRACE is a glass-filled opening. CW-1 records glazing paths. These establish apertures and glass extents, not complete historic window assemblies.

Therefore neither a window object count nor a passing opening subtraction test certifies frame fidelity. Required primary-detail work includes original timber ribbon-window frames versus later replacements, metal service/ramp glazing sections, fixed/sliding module divisions, salon sliding mechanism and shutter assembly, condensation drainage and curtain lintels. The author should consult the book's carpentry chapter and cited original FLC details before giving these a measured profile. No exact module widths or frame depth were verified in this audit.

## Remaining Attested Architectural and Service Work

The R15 book p69 evidence identifies two cellar projections as coal hopper and garage hoist hatch. The audited source still exposes no explicitly identified hoist/hopper products or evidence metadata; the existing cellar projections alone do not implement the pivoting jib, hatch leaf, opening, or hopper throat. Their mapping remains the R15 inference until the primary plan is correlated to the garage. Do not turn the opposite projection into an invented service shaft.

The service fixtures are not categorically absent: the source includes BL-1, salon RAD-1, two servant radiator fixtures, multiple taps/basins, laundry and kitchen fixtures. However BL-1 is located at ground datum and remains a simplified teaching equipment body; these entries do not constitute verification of the original boiler location, full heating distribution, flues, original chimney state, fixture support construction or plumbing details. Broad statements that all services are either complete or absent are both unsupported.

Parental alcove implementation and identification of posts 27/32 are actively owned by the model author. This report deliberately does not reintroduce the stale absence finding from R11. The new suite topology needs a fresh post-publication audit against the primary p110/p124 evidence, actual support footprints, doors, storage and bath separation.

## Acceptance Consequence

No source-supported exact garden-beam datum correction was found. The defensible result is a narrower uncertainty: the shaped cap and three post zones are attested, their complete metric geometry is not. The facade build-up and auxiliary lintel/frame omissions are concrete and remain independent of successful structural ownership, foundation, schema or drawing tests. Those tests validate authored geometry; they cannot establish missing historical construction details.
