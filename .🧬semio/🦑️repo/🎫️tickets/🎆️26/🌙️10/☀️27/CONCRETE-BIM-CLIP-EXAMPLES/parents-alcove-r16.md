## Topology Defect

The current SP-PARENTS begins at18.8m while the actual alcove begins beside post27 at17.277m. W-PARENTS crosses the alcove. W-PARENT-BATH-N seals the source-attested open bath. The repair must remove those false crosswalls and make the main suite entrance/private dressing corridor distinct from the public route to the son's bedroom. A loose bed added to the existing northern rectangle would hide the defect.

The CMN detailed plan also shows that the shared bathroom is a shallow band and the son's entry has a short leg beside the parents bathroom. The current deep shared-bath rectangle and generic15cm partitions force an artificial public route through the parents dressing area. The offline candidate in parents-alcove-r16-input.json records a thin-partition layout and resulting room masks for independent testing; it is not published or declared a measured plan. Nominal8cm internal partitions are inferred from CMN line proportions, not a sourced construction specification. The proposed repair must test0.8m physical public passage and every existing fitting before publication.
