# Ramp Wearing Capacity Controls R95

The current exterior recipe declares 40 mm concrete paving over 20 mm sand. At the RF-2A birth, the registered walking datum is Z=3.17 m and the high F-13-14 beam cap is Z=3.14 m. The beam's actual high portion extends to Y=8.355 m from Y=8.23 m. Its 30 mm initial vertical capacity cannot contain the nominal paver, even before bedding; the source finish uses normal depth, which requires a slightly larger vertical offset. Beam dimensions and ramp axes must remain unchanged.

This requires an explicit local unresolved wearing policy, rather than an ordinary paver silently clipped thinner. The existing roof policy provides the material identity and schema precedent: `maximum_reference_capacity`, `depth`, and `material`, using MAT-PAVING-UNRESOLVED for a qualified 15 mm wearing region.

The ramp equivalent should derive a band from actual declared structural upper facets and the original affine walking plane. For each upward structural facet below the walking plane, its projected footprint and the exact affine capacity inequality define the local affected region. No box, hull, global material mask, nominal structural replacement, or increased tolerance supplies this region. A band applies only where the wearing layer would exceed the available normal capacity. Guards above the walking plane remain ordinary explicit material blockers; they do not imply a thin paving region.

Within the affected region, the qualified wearing stock retains the original walking plane and the explicitly declared depth/material. Bedding occupies the released construction space down to the original reference bottom, then actual declared structural material is subtracted. Outside the region, the original wearing/bedding construction remains. A minimum qualified depth that itself does not fit is a construction error, not permission to clip a named product further. This follows the roof policy without inventing a rebate, shim, or surveyed hidden joint.

Required language-neutral controls include a sloped walking plane over a horizontal cap, sufficient-capacity unchanged stock, a strict 2 nm capacity crossing, a non-overlapping tall guard, a hollow/disconnected cap footprint, and rejection of a qualified wearing depth that cannot fit. Independent GEOS checks the complete projected affected region; SciPy checks actual closed-cell volumes and conservation. Production proof must retain full nominal references, inspect actual RF-2A/F-13-14 material depths, and recheck every declared guard/flight/beam interface.

This file is a design checkpoint only. The ramp-local band schema, implementation, source qualification, and actual acceptance have not been applied. Production slab/finish reservation integration remains the immediate dependency.
