# Hall Source Corrections R31

[Official UVa book, p145](https://uvadoc.uva.es/bitstream/10324/68427/1/9788413202839.pdf) identifies ground column15 as cylindrical,28 cm, beside the spiral and13 cm downpipe. Current TY-SQUARE22 assignment contradicts this. Do not move its axis.

[Indexed reproduction of the same authored book, p206](https://es.scribd.com/document/1072987232/9788413202839) states that exterior leaves, explicitly main entrance and ground service doors, are timber. Main double leaves are recessed behind insulated sheet-metal side wings and sit in a larger translucent-glass surround. It references FLC19505 and19453. This is indexed reproduced text, not recovered original-detail pixels. Exact wing dimensions remain unresolved. CCA's opaque open leaf corroborates the distinction. Current D-ENTRY glazed-leaf type is wrong.

Primary-plan overlay and fit uncertainties are documented separately in ground-registration-r31.md. No historical frame/member dimension is inferred to be measured from a photographic silhouette.
