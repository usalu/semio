Newly retrievable primary text: [Zaparaín, Savoye as Built, printed page69](https://uvadoc.uva.es/bitstream/10324/68427/1/9788413202839.pdf). The indexed passage identifies the two concrete cellar projections as a coal hopper and a garage hoist hatch. This resolves the former inventory restriction against assigning these functions without evidence. The exact mapping is still a geometric inference: in the registered Annex1, the projection toward the garage (model positiveX, nearY12) is the hatch candidate; the opposite outer projection nearY16 is the coal hopper candidate. Inspect and trace before authoring.

The same passage specifies independent footings beyond the cellar, foundation bearing datum−1.50m,35–45cm lime-concrete blinding,10cm reinforced pad bases, pyramidal transitions and square buried stems ending at−0.30m. Buried stems are22cm square, except numbers2/4 at25cm square for drainage. It also documents16cm-wide,25cm-high plinths below ground-storey masonry.

The current source needs an audit for these missing exterior foundations and for the hatch, coal hopper and pivoting metal jib. The source drawing is generated/archive-r8-page18.png (Annex1). Existing cellar footings do not establish completeness for the other structural supports. This report adds evidence, not a completed implementation claim.
