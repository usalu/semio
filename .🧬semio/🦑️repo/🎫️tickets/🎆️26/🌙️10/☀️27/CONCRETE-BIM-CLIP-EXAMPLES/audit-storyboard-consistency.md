# Audit: storyboard-consistency (Beat6_Consistency, "Identity and Consistency")

Read-only audit of `storyboard-consistency.md` against `research-ifc.md` (sections 2, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14, 15), `research-villa-savoye.md` (1.4, 1.13, 2.6, 2.11, 6.1, 7.4, 8.1, 8.5, 10.5, open questions 14), `audit-current.md` ("Prior Decisions To Keep"), `agent-rules.md` (quality bar) and `api.md`. No file was changed except this report.

Method: caption lengths counted with `wc -m` (UTF-8). Layout reference: the energy authoring visuals (`🎓️teaching/🏛️architecture/⚡️energy/🏗️authoring/🎨️visuals/🐍️.py`, lines 170-173: `SAFE_BOTTOM = -2.60`, a two-line caption reaches about y -2.66). Sibling storyboards agree: `storyboard-information.md` line 34 (caption band y < -2.62), `storyboard-representations.md` line 14 (caption to -2.66, drawing y -2.55 to 3.10), `storyboard-exchange.md` line 7 (caption band y < -2.6).

Severity: HIGH = factual error, unlabelled teaching scenario, or a collision with the caption band or stage. MEDIUM = overclaim, abstract visual, value not traceable to a model function, missing German, or missing dependency. LOW = wording or minor layout.

Summary: 9 HIGH, 27 MEDIUM, 17 LOW (53 rows).
