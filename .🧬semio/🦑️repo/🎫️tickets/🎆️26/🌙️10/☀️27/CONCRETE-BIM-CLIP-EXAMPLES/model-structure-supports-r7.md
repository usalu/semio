# Structural Support Catalogue R7

The CMN schematic establishes post-and-slab structure, hall-integrated tables and slender kiosk supports. The4.75m grid is source-attested. Exact coordinates, counts and diameters here are explicitly inferred teaching geometry, not a structural survey. The primary source and unresolved limitations are recorded in architectural-reference-r3.md.

The model contains18 ground and15 upper columns. Ground hall columns use0.24m diameter and stop at the first slab soffit. Kiosk posts use0.15m diameter and stop at SL-KIOSK soffit. Main frame columns use assumed0.30m diameter. These are separate types; new types mark fire resistance as not assessed rather than claim a rating.

## Canopy and Lights

SL-KIOSK covers only x14.2..18.7,y15.3..21.2 at roof level with assumed0.15m thickness; the remainder of the hanging garden remains open. KIOSK-C1 and KIOSK-C2 are actual first-storey support products related to that canopy, rather than decorating the open garden with unassigned columns.

Two garage rooflights at x14.65..15.35 and y8.85..9.55/y12.75..13.45 match real holes in SL-1 and the planter rim inner footprints. The upper guest rooflight is a separate feature. Each source detail and support has a stable tag/GUID and material/containment/disciplined model membership.
