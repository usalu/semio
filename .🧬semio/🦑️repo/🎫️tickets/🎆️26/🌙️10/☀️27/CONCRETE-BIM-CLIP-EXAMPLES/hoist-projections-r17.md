# Cellar Hoist and Coal Projections R17

This is offline preparation only. No hoist or hopper source products have been published.

The primary bookp69 and CMN2021§9 establish the garage-to-cellar pivoting lifting jib and hatch; the book also identifies the coal hopper. Annex1 preserves two side projections. Applying the same19m/734px structural registration as the numbered columns, the positiveX projection nearY11–13 reaches the garage and is the hoist candidate; the negativeX projection nearY15.5–16.4 is the coal candidate. This functional mapping is an inference from adjacency, not an explicit label read from the plan.

The raw pixels and derived outer/clear candidate polygons are preserved in hoist-projections-r17-input.json. Approximate reading uncertainty is0.04m. Existing source cellar walls and floor/ceiling slabs are plain rectangles and currently omit both projections: a generic jib added over the present solid geometry would not create a usable shaft or cellar access.

Implementation therefore needs real hollow construction, a floor/cover aperture and a connected opening through W-CELLAR-E; the coal projection likewise requires its actual wall connection. A horizontal native hatch contract must be designed rather than calling a vertical wall door a floor hatch. The concrete material is described as planned in the book, so it cannot silently become a measured as-built specification. Section levels, construction thicknesses and jib pivot/arm dimensions are unresolved and must remain explicitly inferred until additional details are inspected.

Parking must be revalidated against the actual numbered supports and the real unified garage portal. The older three-car static fixture predates that register; its fit is not an acceptable constraint for relocating the documented building or inventing a convenient jib position. The input lists physical clearance and material-ownership acceptance checks for the next implementation wave.
