# Villa Savoye: Sourced Facts for a BIM Teaching Model

Scope: research for six BIM teaching clips using the Villa Savoye (Le Corbusier & Pierre Jeanneret, Poissy, France) as the single example building. Read-only research; no repository file was edited except this report.

## Method and limits

- Read directly (text available): English Wikipedia (raw wikitext), French Wikipedia (raw wikitext), structurae.net, sites-le-corbusier.org (Association des Sites Le Corbusier), rostarchitects.com, lex.dk.
- Read only through search-engine snippets (no full page could be fetched): Cité de l'architecture technical sheet (PDF), Île-de-France heritage inventory (PDF), monumentum.fr mirror of the Mérimée record, ICON Eye, Designing Buildings wiki, Studio Matrx, Inman, Norman Foster Foundation. Snippet-only facts are rated lower.
- Not reachable: UNESCO whc.unesco.org/en/list/1321 (HTTP 403), Fondation Le Corbusier page (HTTP 404 on the URL tried), Centre des monuments nationaux page (HTTP 404 on the URL tried), villa-savoye.fr (blocked by the fetch tool), POP/Mérimée notice (connection error), Cité PDF and Valladolid/CSIC structural paper (binary PDF; no text extracted; CSIC landing page failed on TLS).
- Consequence: no official primary source was verified directly. The official dates and structural figures still need checking against the Fondation Le Corbusier archive or the Monuments historiques dossier.
- Correction to my own search trail: one search query contained an invented Mérimée ID ("PA78003"). It matched nothing. The ID found in mirrors is PA00087573 (see facts table).

## Open questions and conflicting sources

1. **Construction start year.** 1928 (EN and FR Wikipedia, ICON, lex.dk, sites-le-corbusier) against 1929 (Cité sheet, structurae). Design began 1928; the 1929 date is probably the start of construction. Verify against the Fondation Le Corbusier archive.
2. **Transfer to the State.** 1958 (EN Wikipedia lead), 1962 (FR Wikipedia, structurae), 1967 (IDF inventory snippet). The 1958 date is the expropriation by Poissy; the 1962 date appears to be the transfer to the State.
3. **Restoration dates.** 1963 start (Dubuisson/Debuisson) and 1985–1997 (Véret) are both documented; the IDF snippet gives 1983–1993 for main works and 1996–97 for paint. Véret's supervision ended 1992 (structurae) or the works ran to 1997.
4. **Reopening to the public.** 1997 (FR Wikipedia) against 1968 (IDF snippet). Possibly the first opening was limited.
5. **Footprint.** 21.5 m × 19 m (about 408 m²) versus 190 m² "prise au sol" (Cité). The likely reconciliation is that the 21.5 × 19 m box is the upper volume and 190 m² is the enclosed ground floor. Not verified.
6. **Piloti count.** 25 columns (lex.dk) against 15 cylinders (student slideshow). Neither is authoritative. Unresolved.
7. **Window frame material.** Steel sliding windows (Cité sheet) and "metal sash" (Rost) against "timber windows rather than metal" (EN Wikipedia). The EN sentence may be a general statement about Le Corbusier. Unresolved.
8. **Ribbon windows.** On all sides (Rost) against only the south façade (FR Wikipedia). Unresolved.
9. **Lodge.** 40 m² gross (Cité) and 30 m² living area (sites-le-corbusier). These are probably gross and net figures, not a conflict.
10. **Mérimée reference.** PA00087573 (monumentum.fr mirror, IDF snippet) is not confirmed on the official POP site. The date of the decree (16 December 1965) is from the FR Wikipedia text and the mirror.
11. **Letters and 1937 declaration.** The 1937 "uninhabitable" declaration (FR Wikipedia) is not confirmed by a primary source. The letter quotes on arch2o have no citation.
12. **UNESCO ID.** "1321-006" (Wikipedia) against "1321rev-006" (UNESCO map snippet). The UNESCO page itself was not reachable.
13. **Concrete finish.** The FR Wikipedia phrase "béton armé brut de décoffrage" may mean the building is exposed concrete. This conflicts with the white rendered/painted box. Unresolved.
14. **Missing values (no source found).** Storey heights, piloti diameter, slab thickness, concrete strength, ribbon window dimensions and sill height, ramp slope and length, turning radius, lodge orientation, U-values, insulation status, render specification and what the 1985–1997 restoration changed (windows, render, roof).
