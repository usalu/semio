## Inspected Evidence

The [author-hosted 2018 paper](https://www.fernandozaparain.com/_files/ugd/89d2e0_cff029a1a41d4f24bea997559ced3222.pdf), printed p107/PDF page6, was downloaded and its actual embedded pixels inspected. Object104 (847×453) is group[8]: twelve July1930 photographs, beginning FLC L2(17)006. Object112 (847×611) is group[10]: sixteen November1930 photographs. Its second-row second image shows the ramp, a low prismatic object at left and a tall narrow projection beside the curved right screen. Neither projection was assigned an outlet identity from appearance alone. Object100 (847×146), group[4], includes a construction photograph with a tall narrow stack in front of a broad wall; its building level and function were not resolved.

These reproductions establish visible morphology and contextual relationships. Their small images lack dimensioned roof coordinates, identifiable slab penetrations and duct clear sizes. No XY footprint, height, hollow thickness, cap or slab sleeve has become measured. A photographic projection beside a curved screen is not sufficient to assign a duct to salon, kitchen or boiler.

## Retrieval Method and Reproduction

Small PDF SHA256: 171ced1b332219e02d6bd0809b7ff0646b889f61819e9b7f46ac81d9f4b8f849. Retained input helper: roof-photo-r36.mjs. Generated inputs and images are under 🗑️generated and may be deleted after parent verification. The helper does not require a PDF renderer, worker, getOperatorList, shell or subprocess. It reads embedded JPX image streams directly, decodes using the existing third-party PDFJS standalone image decoder behind an authoring-only function, and encodes PNG through existing Sharp. Native WebAssembly compilation was disallowed; PDFJS automatically used its JavaScript fallback successfully. Returned pixels were grayscale despite the requested component count. Explicit negative-to-positive inversion restored inspected photographs. No product dependency or public product API was added.

Web screenshot returned citation-only data again, without image blocks. Both a bounded official full-book Range request and the26-page official companion request failed before response, with timeouts capped at15/18seconds; no full-book repeat download occurred.

## Authoring Boundary

The roof source remains at445elements. No terminal can responsibly be authored from these photographs alone. Register original FLC19572 roof canalisation against at least three model-grid controls, identify each outlet in a dated photograph, then derive a footprint and explicitly uncertain section dimensions. The source contains zero roof terminals; this pass does not claim that missing physical work is complete. Existing boiler trunk coordinates cannot supply an unrelated terminal's registration.
