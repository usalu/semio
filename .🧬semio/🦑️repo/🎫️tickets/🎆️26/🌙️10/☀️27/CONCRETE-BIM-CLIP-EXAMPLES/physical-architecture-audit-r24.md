Preserve that topology; next scope should register original module stations, author separate planar panes and metal members, and test each pane plane plus actual intermediate metal sections. CMN first-floor pixels and text attest the salon sliding bay and facade apertures, not measured historic module widths. The omission is joinery/module construction, not a claim that all current apertures are in the wrong place. Next scope requires source-bound stations and frame/sash/track contracts, with pane and member material ownership tests; do not invent an evenly divided historical schedule.
