# Handrail Construction Audit

The three current handrail products contain36/36/8 solid outer-envelope segments. A direct source-coordinate inspection corrects an earlier suspicion: the inclined XY end sections are ellipses, deliberately widened along the slope. Projecting their vertices normally to the segment axis gives17.5mm radius (sample stair GF17.499999686..17.500000302mm, FR17.499999721..17.500000293mm, ramp17.5mm). Thus horizontal end planes do not by themselves make the nominal35mm normal section wrong. No section resize is justified from that suspicion.

Real remaining gaps are hollow tube material and shared bend construction. Adjacent stair station ellipses can differ at the same Z plane: source vertex-to-next-ring boundary discrepancies reach21.193mm (GF) and19.278mm (FR). This is a station-profile mismatch; it is not proof of positive3D overlap by itself, since adjacent parts end at the same Z. Original TUBE-ST-GF part30 also has a proven positive STAIRS-RETURN-L column intersection in the old whole audit (0.00004675888m³); actual current source must be rechecked after the final stair/structure changes.

A source-preserving reconstruction should retain the registered centreline and nominal normal diameter, use common miter end rings and a qualified inferred wall gauge, keep a real open flow core, and partition any actual RC contacts once. For a polyline, normalized axis-bisector planes at joints permit common elliptical sections. Propagating each circle generator along the current segment to the next joint plane keeps shared rings exactly identical and corresponding side planes planar, including spatial bends; independent NumPy/SciPy/GEOS should verify normal radii, station continuity, closed positive sectors, material volume and core negatives. Do not copy independent end ellipses at each segment, delete intersection facets or move structural supports. Original gauge, exact welds/end plugs/brackets are unmeasured and must stay explicit inference or unlocated.

Ramp product topology contains separate rails/posts, so its T joints require actual outer-stock air reservations and material ownership, not treating all8segments as one continuous polyline. No handrail source edits have been made during this audit.
