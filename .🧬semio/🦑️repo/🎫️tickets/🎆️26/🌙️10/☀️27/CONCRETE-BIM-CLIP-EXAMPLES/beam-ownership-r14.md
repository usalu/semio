# Beam and Slab Ownership R14

Primary beams intersect slab fields. Removing their plan footprints as architectural voids would create fictitious floor holes and invalidate support and surface calculations. The schema instead permits a slab region to name its member and material. Slab-owned concrete remains part of the complete floor reference surface. A beam explicitly lists embedded_slabs, exposes exclusive parts outside the slab, and resolves shared_parts from the referenced member-owned slab regions.

Drawings consume exclusive beam parts and all slab parts once. Beam representations and quantities combine exclusive and shared parts to describe its complete solid. Beam quantities also expose exclusive_volume and shared_volume, permitting aggregate takeoffs without adding the same concrete twice. Flush beams may have no exclusive parts, provided the source explicitly identifies an embedded slab. No compatibility aliases or fictitious beam voids were introduced.

Regional load derivation treats explicitly solid material regions as full-density material, rather than applying hollow field assumptions to concrete beam strips. Material densities remain assumed where the source marks them assumed. The teaching analysis remains an idealised slab-and-column calculation; exclusive beam self-weight and a complete beam-to-column finite-element network require explicit treatment if that analysis scope is extended.
