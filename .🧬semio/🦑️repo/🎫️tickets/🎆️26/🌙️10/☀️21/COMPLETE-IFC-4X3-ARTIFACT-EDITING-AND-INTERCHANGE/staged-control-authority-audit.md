## Shared bounded binding strategy

Intercept original UiIntent before uiIntentToActionDescriptor in the dedicated form host. Resolve its exact current completed source surface/revision/node/nodeKey and trigger, reject missing/replaced/disabled record, require the reserved staging action and current form token, and read the authored ActionBinding from that resident record. Ignore intent.args for authority. Do not accept an event-provided path/reset list/choice value or reconstruct fabricated StagedFormResponse.fields around arbitrary input.

The worker's current proposed shared StagedFormBinding contract is appropriate: current token, explicit layout-preserve/replace mode and a bounded edit list whose paths/fixed values come from the producer; text-edit slots accept only a separate raw string. Fixed set/append buttons carry their exact issued payload; remove carries a producer-issued path; selection either uses an individually rendered fixed choice binding or resolves a bounded issued option id through the exact completed record/provider. No whole choice inventory goes in UiValue or the small status extension. Constant/readonly nodes have no editable binding. Dependent resets originate in that same binding and invalidate the previous readiness generation.

Native and browser must use the same closed binding schema/reducer and validate resident binding before flattening. FullResponse.fields remains suitable for producer validation and existing direct tests, not an authority inventory available to the small status consumer. Structural readiness is regenerated by the original producer after a staged change; the status does not grant template semantic publication.

Four independent actual TS neutral calls preserve original args and produce the expected partial text, exact named choice, fixed u64 and reset outputs; staged-binding-independent-probe.ts/generated JSON retain evidence. Both implementations require exact status/binding token, one to eight edits, at most one raw-input edit, fixed-only bindings with no extraneous raw input, nonempty paths of at most64 segments and forbidden prototype-path rejection.
