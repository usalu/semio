# Rounded Rectangle Independent Math Reference

These are independent expected-math references for the unchanged approved 16-row input contract, not output from an IFC rounded-profile producer. No production module was imported. The retained external interfaces are `rounded-rectangle-three-reference.ts` and `rounded-rectangle-mpmath-reference.py`; raw owned observations are generated/rounded-rectangle-three-reference.json and rounded-rectangle-mpmath-reference.json. Installed Three EllipseCurve/ShapeUtils triangulation and mpmath 1.3.0 at 100 decimal digits supply separate checks.

The eight supported rows all form closed, oppositely directed indexed triangle edges. Default curved subdivision is N=72: ordinary perimeter M=76, capsule M=74, circular limit M=72. Logical coordinate/index entries are 18M−12 (1356,1320,1284), vertices 2M and triangles 4M−4. Exact cardinal arc endpoints and only consecutive exact join deduplication retain these counts. Three cap triangulation has M−2 triangles. The absolute 1e−5 m row uses N=316/M=320. The cap-four row needs N=4/M=8 and is refused by approved TOTAL-boundary-edge capacity, even though the circular sagitta alone fits. No cap override was introduced.

High precision computes the largest singular value of the two true profile-plane columns of mapping×solidTilt×profileRotation, not the largest world coordinate scale. The world sagitta upper is radius×SI×sigma×(1−cos(pi/N)). All admitted subdivisions meet max(absoluteTolerance, relativeTolerance×worldRadius). Sampling counts follow the existing conservative pi-square certificate as a reference policy; the report also records the independent ideal-sagitta minimum multiple of four. No claim of ideal-minimality for the conservative certificate is made.

Ideal area is XY−(4−pi)R². Polygon area is XY−4R²+(N/2)R²sin(2pi/N). Their positive difference equals (N/2)R²(theta−sin(theta)), theta=2pi/N, checked with an explicit 1e−90 relative numerical residual allowance (the initial overly strict default almosteq comparison on millimetre-scale cancellation was corrected in the owned oracle, not in runtime). Volume multiplies area by depth×abs(normalizedDirection.z)×abs(mapping determinant)×SI³. Profile translation/rotation acts before solid-local extrusion displacement, then solid tilt/translation and mapping. The combined oblique negative-Z/reflection case is included. Independent Three sampled volumes agree with high precision to below 6e−15 for all eight supported cases.

The large finite translation row retains formal ideal geometry but binary64 coordinates collapse; its zero mesh volume is a precision refusal observation, not a valid approximation. Six invalid-source/carrier/role rows are not meshed by this math interface. Source radius zero, missing radius, radius over half, INTEGER carrier, foreign profile placement and tangential extrusion cannot acquire geometry acceptance from formulas alone.

No Float32 GLB coordinate or normal accuracy certificate, primary schema validation, complete physical/backend agreement, native execution or our-producer success is claimed. Official bath 112-vertex/220-triangle reference and its tessellation/analytic difference remain separate. All tool processes are terminal; frozen inputs and production files were unchanged.
