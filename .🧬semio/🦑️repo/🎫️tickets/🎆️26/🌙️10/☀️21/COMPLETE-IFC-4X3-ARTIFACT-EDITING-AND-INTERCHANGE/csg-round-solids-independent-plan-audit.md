# Circular Solid Extraction Independent Plan Review

Reviewed csg-round-solids-plan.md against the frozen20-row data and current circle.ts as text only. No production import/process mapping or source mutation was used. No confirmed defect in the planned reservations or primary origin convention was found.

The cylinder reservations are exact for two n-vertex rings, two n−2 cap fans and 2n side triangles: vertices2n, triangles4n−4, logical values6n+3(4n−4)=18n−12. Cone needs n base vertices and one apex, n−2 base triangles and n side triangles: verticesn+1, triangles2n−2, values3(n+1)+3(2n−2)=9n−3. At72, values1284/645. Source capture and public cumulative output grants remain distinct from these private mesh reservations. Sampling must precede safe checked count arithmetic and growth.

Primary bottom-centre/+Z semantics agree with the cached cylinder/cone pages and actual declaration roles. The cone apex must be a single retained vertex; repeated tip vertices would change reservations and create degenerate cap/side triangles. For a positive local-Z cone, bottom fan (0,i+1,i) and side (i,next,apex) are outward. The same cap winding is already used by circle extrusion. Existing oblique and negative-Z extrusions must retain their actual normalized extrusion vector: translation is depth times that vector, not an invented vertical height. Their vector.z sign reverses local winding once. The central represented-frame determinant sign then reverses winding once independently. Negative extrusion plus reflected frame therefore produces two distinct justified reversals, with positive parity overall. A primitive cylinder/cone has no negative extrusion parameter; its dimensions remain strictly positive.

The world certificate should use only the composed circular-plane linear basis and SI radius, not point subtraction, extrusion direction, translation, height or an enclosing3D norm. Under an affine map, each cone cross-section is a scalar contraction of its base disk; linear taper bounds side chord deviation by the base-circle bound. This justifies the ideal-surface bound for the cone as well as cylinder. It does not certify binary64 transformed coordinates, Float32 GLB coordinates or transcendental rounding. The existing normal-SI radius/depth/transverse refusal and projected finite/nondegenerate triangle checks must survive extraction. In particular, a tiny positive source radius can be schema-valid while the selected geometric admission refuses it.

## Coverage and remaining consumer obligations

Frozen20 covers both primitives, integer/real numeric carriers, primitive rotation/tilt, millimetres, reflected nonuniform mapping, nonzero MappingOrigin, absolute tolerance tightening/coarsening, segment cap, nonpositive dimensions, wrong primitive Position role, string radius, subnormal radius and zero mapping scale. It is not a complete control/arity/precision test suite.

Before production, add actual public tests for missing/extra primitive attributes; missing/unset Position; malformed Axis2Placement3D axis/ref direction roles; foreign CsgSolid root reference; unsupported complex declarations; radius/depth overflow and nonfinite source words; translated coordinate collapse; singular/near-singular composed basis; exact one-short/equal private output grant; cumulative grant across distinct products sharing one representation; cancellation and original callback identity at sampling, ring creation, cap and side emission; and canonical mutation/inverse restoration. Existing circle extrusion regressions need oblique/negative-Z direction combined with reflection and profile offset/rotation, not only new primitive positives. These are supplemental obligations, not changes to the frozen20 corpus.

The independent Three mesh evidence measures actual Float32 positions/indices, bounds and volume. Closed-edge incidence identifies only known parametric seam/cap aliases in that constructor; it is not an independent general exact-welding or arbitrary-solid validator. Different centre-fan triangulation counts are permitted. IfcOpenShell full EXPRESS observations establish physical declaration facts; its unsupported primitive geometry backend is not a positive geometry oracle. Native and full-view claims remain absent.
