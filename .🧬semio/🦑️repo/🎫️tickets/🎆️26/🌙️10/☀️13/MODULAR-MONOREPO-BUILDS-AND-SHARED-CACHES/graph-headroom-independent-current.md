## Findings

1. P2: line 35 calls `readdirSync(directory)` before checking the 4096-entry protocol cap. The inventory cap therefore bounds subsequent authentication, but not directory enumeration allocation or synchronous elapsed time. Use incremental enumeration that stops at the first excess row, checks cancellation and yields periodically. Preserve the present fail-closed behavior for overlarge inventories; do not silently omit unknown entries from the count.
2. P2: line 38 admits a 512 MiB authority receipt, then synchronously reads it, decodes to a second large string, parses and recursively validates its observations. Cancellation is checked only after reading and before parsing. This has a finite byte ceiling but a potentially very large uninterrupted memory/time peak under image EX. The new streaming digest avoids that issue for snapshots; the receipt needs a suitably smaller authored metadata cap or a bounded incremental/controlled parser contract. The current test receipt is tiny, so it does not qualify worst-case cancellation or peak memory.

Neither observation is evidence of an unsafe deletion or a runtime failure. They are concrete bounded-resource/cancellation limits in the current implementation.
