## True Discharge Invariants

The actual order remains:

1. Actual child physical removal, sidecar unlink/durability, child kernel writer close and original child reader lifetime close are authenticated before parent charge retirement is authorized. Failure here cannot cause this path to append retirement. Existing durable charge remains unless some other independently authorized action changed it.
2. Under original parent reader/journal loan, verify exact predecessor, remove only the owned empty namespace and persist its parent; append retiring/retired through the original descriptor, fsync it, reread exact complete bytes and verify allocation absence, then sync journal directory.
3. source.settle records local reservation settlement only after those barriers. Final retained-parent cleanup close follows this discharge point.

A retained-parent cleanup close failure after step 3 cannot restore charge. The journal is retired and the local source is settled; the operation still rejects and its remaining cleanup/resource state requires truthful observation. This corrects the prior reader-close guarantee: preauthorization child kernel/original child reader closure is required; postdischarge retained-parent cleanup close is not a reversible veto.

A complete retired record may already be readable/replayable when later fsync, reread, directory sync or writer-close reports failure. Refusing local source.settle does not restore a charged journal. Before append, charge is retained. A retiring-only complete prefix remains charged. An incomplete retired suffix refuses complete replay. After a complete retired record, current readable replay may be retired even when local source settlement has not happened; crash/power-loss durability remains unknown until genuinely established. This slice adds no rollback, journal healing, missing-path adoption or durable partial-retirement recovery. Physical/claim unknowns before authorization remain unresolved; postappend outcomes must be classified from actual complete journal bytes and genuine owner recovery, never from a callback error alone.

The storage safety prerequisite remains that genuine private physical/claim/child closure proof precedes any retirement append. Late journal uncertainty does not recreate the already removed image.
