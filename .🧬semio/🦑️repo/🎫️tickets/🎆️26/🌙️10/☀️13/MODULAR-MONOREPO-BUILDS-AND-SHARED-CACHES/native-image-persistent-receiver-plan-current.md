# Persistent Native Receiver and Activation Transaction

This is a Source proposal, not an executed image or a grant. See native-image-cleanup-current.md. Fresh/inactive persistent operations currently refuse before effects.

## One Physical Owner with Explicit Roles

Capture temporary creator, fresh persistent preparation, existing inactive activation or existing mounted borrower before effects. Inactive activation accepts actual bounded existing receipt/backing/root under exact EX and genuine preaccepted cleanup. It cannot create/delete/adopt backing or receipt. Its first complete bounded observation must establish no active mapping before attach. Prior active/ambiguous mapping refuses BEFORE effects. Record activation attempt before real child birth; cleanup can undo only that newly accepted mapping, then reobserve and retain backing/receipt.

A mounted borrower never receives deactivation authority. Close seals births, drains real accepted operations and releases SH even after cancellation.

## Actual Receiver, Not a Boolean

Construct an actual persistent image object while preparation EX is retained: original base control, complete physical facts, real NativeStorage port, its operation ledger and guard state. Transfer the actual guard and physical record through one private transition. No user callback, copied object, descriptor, public registration or receipt write can acknowledge transfer.

The real receiver owns EX-to-SH transition, SH acquisition and final physical verification. Before acknowledgment, errors belong to preparation cleanup. After acknowledgment, failures belong to the receiver: drain accepted work, release the actual guard and leave legitimately published backing owned by the canonical durable image protocol. Never invoke temporary detach after publication. Concurrent legitimate retirement during the guard transition must be detected by final identity verification; failed open supplies no permission to recreate/adopt a replacement.

Publication therefore includes exact creator/activation record, captured persistent role, bounded actual receipt identity, real receiver constructed under original base authority, actual guard responsibility transfer and private acknowledgment before creator cleanup may skip physical settlement.

Use one coherent private physical owner containing creator and persistent receiver state. Do not add a structural receiver registrar between command owner and provider. Keep the closed consumer beneath that owner and pure state/schema platform neutral. If cross-file private sharing creates a cycle or registrar, consolidate the coherent private state into one actual owner module.

## Complete Operation Control and Lifetime

The approved NativeStoragePort.withOperation should supply actual carried operation control to its algorithm, rather than let waits retain a broader captured control. The owner authenticates base/refinement and synchronously enrolls a bounded private frame before effects. Accepted nested frames finish after top callback return; sampled remaining durations never become new clocks.

Core custody transactions must accept live per-operation control rather than close over an acquire-child control. Reservation identity remains bound to its original base. Each recheck/commit/retire enters its full owner operation and uses returned control for locks/progress/physical retirement. Acquire/recover/observe/checkpoint stay enrolled through full result, without retaining a dead acquire-child inside a returned reservation.

Private close/drain precedes canonical accepted-body retirement. Bare provided recheck/observe/maintenance calls enter the same owner frame. No new Original or generic registry is needed.

1. Enroll the whole actual opening operation under its already-carried opening phase before effects. Preaccept the existing finite Native cleanup token and shortened creator body from that phase. Acquire creation EX with that exact body control. The existing command owner captures the real proof before create/attach.

5. After acknowledgment, failure belongs to the receiver and cannot invoke temporary detach or delete the canonical publication. It drains accepted work and retains the exact durable/unresolved receiver record; any held guard is managed by that record. An EX competitor in the gap can legitimately retire/replace the image, but final equality must then fail and no alternate inode is adopted.

The carried opening phase is the existing options.control, with its original absolute/monotonic deadline. Creation uses its pre-funded D-minus-R body only until actual durable transfer. ### Existing Inactive Sequence
