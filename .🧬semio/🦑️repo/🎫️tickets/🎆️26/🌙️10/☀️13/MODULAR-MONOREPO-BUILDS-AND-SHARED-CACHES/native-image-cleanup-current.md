Before any creation/activation side effect, accept a finite cleanup reserve and retain the real directory/exclusive operation custody. Preserve the body control unchanged. Preserve the primary body failure plus all cleanup/settlement failures. Do not swallow cleanup errors or recursively remove a path merely because a local mount flag is false.
