## Settlement And Limits

The funded callback awaits the actual opener; an eventual returned child therefore cannot outrun this continuation. It catches body/open errors with a separate failed flag (preserving thrown undefined), then awaits actual child.close inside the funding callback before returning or rethrowing. The funding owner joins accepted reservation operations before settling. Cleanup starts only after that funding work has returned. Actual backend close remains the ordinary reader/descendant close; success does not call physical retirement and preserves the durable image and charge.

On failure, backend settlement follows accepted cleanup begin and joined ordinary constructor work. Unknown cleanup or unresolved backing records unresolved parent/child custody. Original proof refusal is not repaired with a replacement proof. Error, undefined, cancellation and late accepted work therefore do not authorize charge release merely because no callback image exists.

`🧰️framework/🔨️modules/🏃️process/📦️artifacts/🏗️native-build/💾️storage/🟦️.ts:159,190` explicitly separates never-entered unborn retirement from entered retention. Entered retention may close the privately captured parent cleanup reader after the backend settles; it does not append retirement or settle the retained allocation. This is conservative retained charge, not completed entered-child parent retirement. Source branch inspection supports that distinction; the neutral tests and ordinary fixture do not establish physical Mac/Linux/Windows behavior, restart recovery, whole-cache teardown or persistent-development-state retirement.
