All require exact source closure, negative probes, parser revision, authority and final receipt semantics to be retained. The best grounded candidate for further bounded measurement is duplicate physical source capture across the three semantic parsers, followed by repeated stat/exists probes attributed by path and caller within one snapshot. A candidate implementation must retain stamp-bound exact byte identity, racy-file handling, parser-specific rows, nested dependency observations, cancellation/checkpoint behavior and final validation. No implementation is proposed as the live-worker fix without an actual hot-stack or bounded attributable cost measurement.
