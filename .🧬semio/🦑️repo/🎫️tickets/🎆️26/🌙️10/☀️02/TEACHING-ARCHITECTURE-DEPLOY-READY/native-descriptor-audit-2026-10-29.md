The [upstream manpage](https://github.com/lsof-org/lsof/blob/master/docs/manpage.md) additionally states that missing search terms are not reported to stderr with -Q. The [upstream FAQ](https://github.com/lsof-org/lsof/blob/master/docs/faq.md) explains that +D recursively constructs a pathname search list, including the directory itself. This documents the mechanism consistent with the actual frontend closed-child probe.
