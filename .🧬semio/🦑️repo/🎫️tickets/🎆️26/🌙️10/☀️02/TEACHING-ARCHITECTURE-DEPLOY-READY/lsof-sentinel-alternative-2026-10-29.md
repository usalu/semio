## Primary Semantics

The upstream [selection documentation](https://github.com/lsof-org/lsof/blob/master/docs/options.md) specifies OR by default and global AND with -a. These combine selection sets; they do not remove individual requested file-search obligations. The [manual](https://github.com/lsof-org/lsof/blob/master/docs/manpage.md) also explains that -a applies to all selection options, not selected pairs.

Apple's [published main.c](https://github.com/apple-oss-distributions/lsof/blob/main/lsof/main.c#L1445-L1456) independently iterates every requested Sfile after observation. An unmatched entry sets rv=1; matching PID processing later cannot reset that failure. Thus an actually open external sentinel can satisfy its own term but cannot satisfy a different closed descendant term generated by +D. This is an implementation counterexample to guaranteed success through OR, not an assumption that some output means complete success. The already-established missing-Q Apple contract remains unchanged; no Darwin executable was run here.

## Why Other Sentinel Shapes Do Not Supply This Boundary

An exact sentinel name or own-PID selector added to +D still leaves the unmatched descendant term, as both Source and physical results show. Adding -a instead restricts eligible processes/files and cannot establish every foreign descriptor in the scope. Omitting +D and selecting only the owned PID or sentinel no longer discovers a different process holding a scoped file.

A complete unfiltered census already has no recursive pathname search terms, so adding a sentinel is unnecessary for its nonempty result and does not reduce its global output. Adding a PID/name selector to that otherwise unfiltered command selects that set rather than preserving an implicit all-process wildcard. The earlier actual unfiltered alternative exceeded the unchanged16384line ceiling (17348lines); this fixture does not repeat the host-wide census or claim it can now fit. Retaining complete raw-output budgets before filtering prevents that route from becoming a truncation bypass.

Any hypothetical sentinel-aware parser would additionally need exact owned PID/FD/type/access/name and held handle identity, reject a missing/duplicate/replaced sentinel, retain the actual readonly owner through observation and final checks, and account for all extra raw output before removing its record. Canonical path/alias/escape checks and original scope descriptor guards must remain. Those requirements cannot repair the prior nonzero status, so no parser feature is proposed.
