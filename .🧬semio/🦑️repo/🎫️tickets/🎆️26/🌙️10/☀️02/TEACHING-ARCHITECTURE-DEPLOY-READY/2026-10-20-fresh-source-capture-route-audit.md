## Concrete Reuse Gaps

Existing full transport writes fixed generated/private-source-capture-{manifest,receipt,...}.json names without exclusive fresh-suffix protection. Running unchanged would overwrite old retained evidence. It is also fixed to old prepared image/Bun pin and exact historical build-input JSON; current toolchain/image compatibility must be revalidated,not assumed. Capture cleanup force-removes its chosen container and removes unretained volume but does not fail/report actual cleanup status. It lacks the stronger exact immutable ID/token bracket of newer helpers. Therefore do not blindly invoke unchanged helper for fresh cohort: reseal fresh input/output namespace and bounded ownership/cancellation/cleanup contract through existing ports before action. No new helper was created by this audit.
