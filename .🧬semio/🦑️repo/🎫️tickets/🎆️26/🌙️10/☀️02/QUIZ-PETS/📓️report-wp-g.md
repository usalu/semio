### How it works

- **Survey.** Surfaces: elements matching `surfaces`, not inert or hidden, crossing the stage, at least 48 px wide →
  `{ id, x0, x1, y }` with one id per element for its lifetime (`WeakMap` counter), then the floor. Keep-outs: boxes of
  `keepouts` grown by 4 px, plus the focused element grown by 8 px. All numbers relative to the layer's own box (the
  viewport when the layer has no area). Reads only.
- **Pacing.** Accumulator over animation-frame timestamps, 15.625 ms per tick, at most 8 ticks per step. A step happens
  once `64 ÷ rate` ticks are due (rate 64: every frame; 32: every other; 16: every fourth) or at once after `wake()`.
  Rate 0: no frame; one timer when the pace names a wake tick; the step that ends the sleep carries every tick slept
  (the stage jumps over lulls in O(1), see E's `pass`/`lull`). `hide()` cancels frame and timer; `show()` restarts the
  clock without catching up.
- **Layer.** React renders `<div class="pet-layer" aria-hidden="true">` once. One effect starts the show (stage,
  watchers, pacer); a second effect hands changed props to it as events (`tuned`, `hushed`, `summoned`, a new survey).
  A step = read (survey when stale) → `advance(menagerie, stage, [ticked, surveyed, glanced, …waiting, summoned])` →
  `frameOf` → paint (one `depict` per species on stage, `paint` per frame, DOM order = frame order). Pointer events are
  coalesced to the latest. The rotation epoch advances every 60…120 s of stage time (`randomBetween([seed, 0xfffffffd,
  epoch], 60, 120)`). A fault inside a step ends the show silently (no console output, everything torn down).

## 3. Deviations from the design, and decisions

1. **Rate 0 is not only "still or empty".** E's stage also reports rate 0 for resting actors without an idle loop and
   jumps over lulls. The pacer therefore delivers every tick slept after a rate-0 rest (capped at 8 only while running);
   otherwise each sleep would advance the stage by 8 ticks only.
2. **Event order in a step is `[ticked, surveyed, glanced, waiting…, summoned]`**: time passes before what happened is
   folded in, so a long rest is jumped while the stage is still at rest.
3. **Surfaces and keep-outs beyond §6.3** (all in the vectors):
   - a surface and every ancestor of a surface are never keep-outs (a card that is itself a link, button or list item
     would otherwise block its own edge); a focused element that contains a surface is not a keep-out either (the skip
     link target `main`), a focused surface itself is;
   - surfaces are clipped by scrolling or clipping ancestors (never by `html`/`body`, whose overflow is the viewport's):
     an edge scrolled out of its pane carries nobody;
   - `[tabindex="-1"]` and `[contenteditable="false"]` do not count as controls.
4. **Staleness has two urgencies.** Scroll, resize and the root's size → survey with the next frame (pets ride a
   scrolling card frame by frame); mutations, focus, transition end → at most every 8 ticks while running. While
   actors travel (rate 64) the page is measured every 8 ticks anyway, while they only stand once a second (changes no
   event announces, such as inline-style glides). A survey equal to the last one is not dispatched. `focusout` is
   watched besides `focusin`. The first notification of the `ResizeObserver` about a sized root is passed over
   (it cost a second frame on every still mount).
5. **Pointer**: `pointerleave` is listened to on `documentElement`, not `window` (it does not bubble); a touch pointer
   leaving does not send `unpointed` (the tap stays worth a look until the stage forgets it after 4 s). Pointer events
   are dropped on a still stage and wake the pacer only when actors are on stage. `poked` needs the primary button.
6. **The show rests while the layer itself is inert or hidden** (I's open point): no frame, no timer, no survey, pets
   stay where they are behind a dialog and continue afterwards.
7. **Rotation** never advances on a still or quiet stage (a swap is motion); its span restarts when either ends.
8. **The first paint needs one animation frame** ("still paints once and schedules nothing" holds after that frame).
9. **Forced colours**: the static element is still rendered (the signature returns `ReactElement`), empty, and no show
   starts; the stylesheet hides it.
10. **Gallery on port 6198, not 6071**, so my run could not collide with F's own; harness sources live in
    `TK/wp_g_harness`, not under `🗑️generated`, because they are inputs.
11. The coordinator's amendment on `loop-seam` (rotation modulo 360°) concerns H1 and the validator; nothing in G
    depends on it and I changed nothing for it.
