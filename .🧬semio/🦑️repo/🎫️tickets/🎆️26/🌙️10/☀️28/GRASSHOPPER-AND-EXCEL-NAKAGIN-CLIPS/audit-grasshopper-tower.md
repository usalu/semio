# Audit: grasshopper_tower, Beat3_Tower (en and de)

Scope: all 20 keyframes per language (40 PNG, 1280x720) read with the Read tool. Narration (en and de) checked against the scene file `🎓️teaching/🏛️architecture/🦗️grasshopper/🎬️clip/🗼️tower/🐍️.py`, the Nakagin model `🏢️nakagin/🔣️.json`, the Grasshopper model `🦗️grasshopper/🎬️clip/🧱️model/🔣️.json`, `research-nakagin.md` and `research-grasshopper.md`. Strict findings in both `layout.md` files checked against the frames. Read-only: nothing edited except this report.

## Verdict

Not ready to render. Three blockers: the storeys clause shows contradicting counts (nowire), the capital elevation Z is not the value the caption claims (ontop), and the type pattern is wired so that it teaches a false link (types). Both languages carry the same defects, and German adds a list-separator problem. Several majors concern unlabelled or unsourced numbers (podium height, capital values typed into the scene, storeys label), and the clip never shows a typical mistake. Frames are otherwise clean: no clipped text, captions at most two lines, all text readable at 1080p.

Counts: 3 blockers, 14 majors, 19 minors (frame table, 36 rows); 10 fact findings; 8 didactic findings.

## Fact findings

1. Tower split and storeys. Research (Wikipedia): Tower A 11 storeys, Tower B 13 storeys. Research teaching model: 9 and 11 capsule floors, 7 capsules per floor, split 63 + 77 = 140. The model `🏢️nakagin/🔣️.json` uses 9 and 11 capsule floors with facades [0,1,2] (A) and [0,1,2,3] with an exception at storey 10 (B), giving 54 and 86. The clip's 54 / 86 match the model and sum to the historical 140. The model and the research teaching split disagree (54/86 versus 63/77). Choose one and record it. The label "Storeys" should be "capsule floors" (see table).
2. Capsules per floor. The generic formula storeys × 4 × 2 gives 8 per floor, which is the Wikipedia wording ("most floors had eight"). The research teaching model uses 7. The clip is consistent with the model but not with the research teaching choice; state which one the formula describes.
3. Podium height. Model: 2 storeys × 2.75 m = 5.5 m. Research: Base bounding box 7.5 m (IFC, medium confidence). The clip shows 5.5 m as the building's height with no teaching marker. Keep 5.5 m if it is the chosen teaching value and record the deviation from the research.
4. Capital. Model: height 2.75 m, overhang 0.25 m, taper 0.5 m, described as the machine room on top of the core. Research: IFC capital 5.5 × 11.5 × 5.5 m, the vertical axis unresolved, the real roof not confirmed (low confidence). The clip shows 4.00 m and 0.60 m mid-animation (see table). Mark the capital values as teaching placeholders.
5. Core, storey pitch, capsule. Core 5.5 × 5.5 m, storey pitch 2.75 m, capsule 2.5 m wide with 0.25 m gap (pitch 2.75), window 1.3 m: consistent with the research and the model. Model also staggers item 1 by 0.917 m (one third of the pitch); the clip shows side-by-side items only. Acceptable, but say so in the teaching notes.
6. Capsule type counts. Model: A 36, B 35, C 34, D 35 (sums to 140, used by the swap panel). Research table: 40, 36, 44, 20. The research table is out of date; update it or note the change.
7. Grasshopper names and ports. Spot-checked against the Grasshopper model JSON: Division (A, B to R), Negative (x to y), Radians (D to R), XY Plane (O to P), Unit Z (F to V), Merge (D1 to D4), Rectangle (P, X, Y to R, L), Center Box (B; X, Y, Z), Move (G, T to G, X), Extrude (B, D to E) exist with those names. Rotate (G, A, P to G, X), Orient (G, A, B to G, X), Series (S, N, C to S), Graft Tree (T to T), Multiplication, Addition, Value List, Panel and Number Slider match Grasshopper 1 / Rhino 8 names. Not verified: Tree Statistics outputs P, L, C (research marks it [CHECK]); the List Item output is printed as "i" on the canvas, Grasshopper names it "I". Check both in Rhino 8 before render.
8. Axes. Plane at storey 0, facade 0: origin (-12.475, 2.75, 5.5), X (1,0,0) along the north facade, Y (0,1,0) outward, Z up. Caption "X runs along the facade, Y outward, Z up" is correct.
9. Orient and Rotate semantics: Orient moves geometry from source to target plane; Rotate takes radians (Radians component present). Captions are correct.
10. "Teaching model": said once, in recap. Matches the storyboard. The hook says "a tower" (two towers, see table).

## Didactic findings

1. Arc. Good spiral: 140 hook, five parts, core, shaft, series, facades, axes, items, orient, types, count, nowire, capital, ontop, podium, why, towers, total, swap, recap. Each clause mostly changes one thing.
2. Typical mistake missing. The storyboard listed misconceptions (every capsule modelled by hand; planes are points; item 0 equals item 1; equal detail everywhere). The clip addresses only "more storeys means more modelling" (nowire) and "equal detail" (why). Add one short beat with a visible mistake and its fix, e.g. a capsule on the world XY plane facing the wrong way, corrected by the facade plane (a bridge to the casting clip).
3. Smallest example. Planes and items are introduced on the full tower B (11 storeys, 4 facades). Introduce them on one storey and one facade first (the series and facades steps), then scale up in count.
4. Plan-row gaps. "Capsule rotations" appear only as plane rotations; no capsule is shown turning per facade. "Capital on each core": only tower B is drawn in the capital and ontop steps. "Counts from the definition": met.
5. Weakest clauses. types teaches a false wiring (blocker); swap shows a recolour where a geometry change is promised (major); towers asserts a facade difference that is not on screen (major).
6. Count clause. The formula is the clearest teaching moment in the clip, but its literals 4 and 2 are typed and the slider it refers to shows a different value in nowire (blocker).
7. why clause. The explicit reason ("the podium is context, not the subject") is well placed; the bar chart numbers need fixing first (see table, why).
8. Terminology. "Storeys", "Capsule storeys", "Geschosse", "Storeys-Slider" and "Geschoss" are used for both storeys and capsule floors. Use one term for each: storeys (building levels) and capsule floors (levels with capsules).
