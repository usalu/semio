# Conservative Visibility Bounds And Area Priority

Schema and portable cases precede implementation. A raw convex-cover bbox plus1e−10 is not conservative at an acute expanded corner. The geometric bound uses the maximum effective normalized-plane offset divided by the minimum half-interior-angle sine, with explicit floating-roundoff allowance. Numerically unresolved angles leave the bbox unbounded and retain the exact half-plane path. Actual normalized half-planes +1e−10 remain authoritative.

Opaque candidates will be attempted deterministically by largest actual convex projected polygon area, then original Item index. The exact union fallback, strict3Dsupport separation, opacity and source geometry remain unchanged. No role/colour filter or geometry budget is introduced.

## Conservative bound

Let edge lengths be Lᵢ and epsilon=1e−10. The original actual half-planes divide cross products by max(Lᵢ,1e−15). Therefore their maximum physical outward offset is d=epsilon·max(max(Lᵢ,1e−15)/Lᵢ). For a convex corner with interior angle theta, uniform offset d moves its intersection by d/sin(theta/2). The half-angle sine is half the norm of the sum of the two adjacent unit edge directions. The implementation lowers its minimum by64 machine epsilons to bound normalization arithmetic; adds64 coordinate ulps to the resulting outward bbox margin; and rounds outward with nextafter. Zero-length edges or numerically unresolved acute angles produce an infinite bbox, so no bbox rejection is attempted. The same unchanged expanded half-planes still decide the sufficient cover proof afterward.

Independent NumPy pairwise line solves enumerate feasible expanded-plane vertices for all portable controls and prove they lie within the computed bound, including the acute-tip case extending about1e−6m beyond the raw bbox despite only1e−10 plane expansion. A raw bbox+epsilon would be wrong there. No tolerance or material geometry is widened by the optimization: the conservative box only avoids unnecessary half-plane work, and the authoritative original test is untouched. Prepared bounds are computed once per culling call; there is no new global drawing cache.

## Deterministic attempts and scope

Each Item priority is its largest actual convex projected polygon area; ties use original Item index. Its own convex blockers are attempted in descending actual polygon area. Every candidate still passes original opacity eligibility and GH-owned actual-plane strict separating support before a single cover can hide a target. If no single cover succeeds, the prior exact union subtraction path remains unchanged. No colour/role/size policy or geometry budget was added.

Owned production changes are only `_single_cover_bounds`, the bbox stage in `_single_cover`, per-call bound/area preparation and candidate attempt ordering in `_opaque_visible`. GH’s canonical-plane eligibility/blocker guards, source grouping, graph, singleton/faceting and fragment kernel are preserved and separately owned. GH’s subsequent progress/cancel-only `_section_order` save is outside this helper proof’s consumed paths; the exact verified whole-file snapshot is retained below rather than silently relabelled latest.
