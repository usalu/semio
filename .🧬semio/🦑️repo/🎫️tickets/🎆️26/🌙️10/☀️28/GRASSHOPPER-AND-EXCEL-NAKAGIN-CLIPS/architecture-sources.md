# Nakagin Architecture Sources

Read-only source audit, 2026-10-29. Evidence supports an architectural reconstruction; it does not establish an exact as-built model. No model or source files were changed by this audit.

## Primary and Institutional Evidence

- [MoMA exhibition](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/5830): 1972 completion, 140 prefabricated single-occupancy capsules attached to two concrete-and-steel cores. Restored A1305 is from the top floor. Original drawings/models were exhibited, but this page does not supply dimensioned drawings.
- [SFMOMA acquisition](https://www.sfmoma.org/read/a-towering-acquisition/): two concrete towers connected at the base; seventy capsules on each. Nominal capsule plan 8.2 × 13.1 feet, approximately 2.5 × 4 metres. Electronics were offered as options; avoid assuming identical equipment in every unit.
- [M+ A806 accession](https://www.mplus.org.hk/en/collection/objects/unit-a806-nakagin-capsule-tower-19701972-tokyo-japan-2024104/): restored 2022 artifact overall 255 × 270 × 420 cm. Materials include polymer-modified cement, glass, steel, PVC, wood, electronics. These are overall museum-object measurements and must not silently replace nominal module-envelope dimensions.
- [Preservation and Restoration Project](https://www.nakagincapsuletower.com/project): 23 capsules salvaged after 2022 demolition, restored under Kisho Kurokawa Architects supervision.
- [BDP reconstruction account](https://annualreview.bdp.com/stories/second-life-for-an-icon/): direct account of KKAA-commissioned digital model. Approximately 70 original record drawings; eight detailed design variants across FF&E, MEP and architecture. Fabrication photographs conflicted with structural-frame drawings, and installed blinds/privacy-screen locations differed from drawings. The most important evidence against claiming a perfect replication from generic dimensions alone. No public model download found on this page.
- [Tokyo Build hosted on-site Nikkei report, 2022-06-21](https://www.the-tokyo-build.co.jp/image/news/20220621/nikkei.pdf): contractor-assisted site visit during demolition. Page 1 explicitly identifies A as thirteen floors and B as eleven; 140 capsules total. Page 2 gives approximately three tons per capsule. Page 3 identifies steel reinforced concrete, inter-tower bridge, and roof above second-floor offices. Page 4 documents services under capsule floors and narrow inter-capsule gaps. Use as a witnessed building record; images are copyrighted and must not be copied into distributable assets without permission.

## Modeling Decisions and Uncertainty

High confidence: 140 capsules; A thirteen floors/B eleven; seventy capsules per tower; two structural circulation/service cores; prefabricated steel capsule concept; nominal 4 × 2.5 × 2.5 m envelope; shared podium/base and inter-tower bridge; multiple interior variants.

Use nominal exterior envelope separately from often-published interior clear dimensions 3.8 × 2.3 × 2.1 m. The latter was seen in secondary architectural/student material, not confirmed here by an accessible original dimensioned drawing. Likewise a 1.3 m porthole diameter is widespread but should be classified as a provisional nominal dimension until checked against drawing/scan.

Not established by these accessible sources: complete 140-unit position/orientation schedule, exact core center spacing and cross-section, floor and half-floor elevation sequence, actual capsule entrance clearances, bolt plate geometry, slab thicknesses, roof-tower profiles and heights, service riser routes, individual furniture dimensions, drainage details, and exact installed screen/blind inventory. Do not substitute a seeded packing algorithm or repeated generic interiors and label them exact as-built.

A floor count alone does not give building height. Generic fourteen-story claims should not override the witnessed A13/B11 identification. An overall height around 54 m appears in institutional skyscraper data, but roof/tower-top versus occupied-roof distinctions need checking before use.
