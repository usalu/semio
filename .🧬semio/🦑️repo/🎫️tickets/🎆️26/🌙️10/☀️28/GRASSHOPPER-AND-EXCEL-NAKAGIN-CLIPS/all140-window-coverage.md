# All 140 Visible Window Orientations: Final Source Coverage

The actual pre-demolition GLUON textured mesh has been projected and inspected per physical capsule face, not only per roof. All140 expected-or-corrected window-bearing walls have positive visible circular-rim evidence:107 unchanged expected walls plus33 observed corrections (13A,20B). This supersedes the earlier16/29/34 conservative dark-region counts, which were incomplete detector subsets and are not full visual coverage counts. Exact physical centres, yaw, family, phase, source image/cell and scope are stored per row in generated/gluon-all140-aperture-evidence.json.

The33 corrections preserve counts/IDs and authored floor Z/phase; they change canonical physical XY/yaw/family or the actual aperture wall. Every correction has positive rim evidence, no duplicate other occupied same-floor/phase body, and a retained-phase horizontal material score strongest among the three sampled phases. Production integration, whole140 collision/door/route tests and current captures are separate gates; this source coverage does not silently accept any untested implementation.

All140/140 is a visible aperture-facing/topological coverage claim. It is **not** a surveyed coordinate tolerance, proof of concealed entry handedness, exact original sanitary/media fitout of every resident capsule, or complete historical condition at all dates. Partial/damaged rims are admitted where their circular aperture and bearing wall are identifiable; empty/missing roof captures are never treated as absent capsules. Source orientation is expressed in authored model axes; geographic north is not established.

Evidence inputs: scan-contact-sheets.py (seven current-pose sheets), scan-alternative-sheets.py (four canonical alternatives sheets), scan-unresolved-sides.py (both side walls for formerly blank B fronts), scan-confirm-poses.py and scan-confirm-side-windows.py (phase/duplicate/rim corroboration). No capture geometry is reused at runtime. The33 exact corrections and phase scores are durably tabulated in all-storey-pose-evidence.md; generated JSON is detailed temporary research output.

## Window Height Convention

The model centre1.25m above outerfloor is equivalent to1.05m above the+.20m finished floor. Early analysis expected-height metadata mixed these baselines and was corrected. Using the registered raw roof/local ground and actual authored outerfloor,23 usable rim fits have world-centre residual−.097..+.054m (median−.0084m), and the nine added B side rims−.108..+.025m. No vertical window-centre edit is supported by those residuals. Weak automatic B11S1 fitting is excluded; its front rim is manually unambiguous and signed phase2 support is strong.
