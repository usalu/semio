# Reference Long Light And Fan Blind

The earlier audit matrix claimed a long light/privacy device without confirming their named source parts. At that earlier snapshot _reference_interior had only ceiling-light and smoke-detector. Named lamp and window-unit geometry are now saved; current material/pose findings and pending final acceptance are documented in window-unit-plan-audit.md and long-wall-luminaire.md.

## Original Interior Photograph

[Domus archive](https://www.domusweb.it/en/from-the-archive/2010/12/21/kurokawa-s-capsules.html), cached original composite domus-5.png, shows a shallow elongated wall luminaire above the grille on the entry-side portion of the media wall, below the high shelf/ceiling line. It runs along that wall; it is not the separate round ceiling luminaire. A housing and exposed linear diffuser/tube are visible. Exact length, section and fasteners are not dimensioned here and should be inferred explicitly. The photograph/axon also establishes integrated shelves/media and the porthole privacy assembly; no roller or Venetian assumption is justified.

## Direct Observed Window Operation

The [Window Research Institute field study](https://madoken.jp/en/series/20219/) documents A904 with outer fixed glazing, an inward-opening inner sash, and a radial folding-fan blind between them. It stows compactly and has quarter-turn settings. Upper/lower metal locks operate the inner sash. This is direct observed A904 specimen evidence with preservation-project assistance. The account does not explicitly date the inner sash to1972 or establish an original/unmodified installation; installation date and all140 prevalence remain unproven. That is not evidence of a retrofit. The original technical section independently documents fixed glass/fan hardware, not an opening second pane.
