## Independent Registration

The public GLB is one merged textured mesh without metric metadata. Metric calibration uses the clear A53.50m overall dimension, supported A roof apex rawY1.1445266 and a robust local ground plane. IRLS ground fitting over3029 horizontal triangles gives Y=.002517X+.001686Z+.001471, weightedRMS≈.000988raw (about4.6cm). At A the fitted ground is≈.001773; scale≈46.81675m/raw. Raw repeated pitch≈.05965845 becomes≈2.79301m, independently challenging the former2.90 read.

The flat podium plane rawY≈.1665 becomes≈7.71m. Its higher edge rawY≈.1746 becomes≈8.09m. The A sparse phase1 roof rawY.23875 becomes≈11.094m; authored7.72+2.80/3+2.50=11.153m differs≈.059m. The former8.12support interpretation missed by≈.459m. These are constrained derived choices with.15m regression tolerance, not millimetre survey claims.

The scan roof/underside separation of one sparse capsule is≈2.715m, whereas nominal shell height is2.50m. The model does not demonstrate a matching low hardware extension; this remains a real nominal/physical-envelope convention discrepancy. M+ publishes255×270×420cm without axis labels. Those original ordered values remain separate; assigning axes or stretching the shell to force agreement would exceed the evidence.

Window dark-disc fits are not a reliable metre oracle: several circles have low residuals but dark-region radii vary≈20% because reveals, blinds, reflections and damage differ. Selecting only fits that yield a preferred pitch would be circular. Full roof spans include overall hardware/scan edges and cannot silently equal the nominal4.00m shell.

## Source Search Boundary

The public Domus original is1280×1547; removing image transforms exposes no higher resolution. Alternate UPC, Arquia and University of Houston reproductions are smaller. The SCI-Arc image is a modern redraw without primary dimension labels. MoMA documents original42×63cm architect-authored sections but does not publish readable full-resolution dimensions in the inspected press material. The local bachelor thesis contains no Nakagin drawing. These searches do not turn unresolved digits or concealed construction into measured facts.

Portable dimension cases and NumPy actual-geometry regressions cover the authored chain, all140 phase elevations, separate head parameters/native roof ratios, actual core/slab/basement bounds and independent scan roof/overall agreement.
