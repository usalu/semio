## Reference Interior Reconstruction

The reference fitout follows the dimensioned plan and interior axon/photo in [Domus 520, April 1973, republished archive](https://www.domusweb.it/en/from-the-archive/2010/12/21/kurokawa-s-capsules.html). It is one documented furnished configuration, separate from the A–D program studies. The native CAD contains shell/porthole curves rather than sanitary fittings, so fixture dimensions and control counts remain explicitly assumed. The source supports a transverse tub, circular basin, angled toilet, curved wetroom door, integrated CRT/audio cabinet, grilles, wired telephone, shelves and shower plumbing.

The capsule.detail schema now records sanitary wall thicknesses, tube/shower dimensions, tessellation, grille pitch, seal width and hinge radius with provenance. The tub, basin and toilet are closed material meshes around genuinely open tapered cavities with continuous floors; the toilet seat is a through-open ring. Curved spouts, waste trap, shower hose/rose/nozzles, drain grates, valve stems and handles are physical meshes. The media assembly has a curved CRT face, bezel, tuning knobs, audio keys/dials, separated speaker grille wires, telephone dial/handset/earpieces and coiled cord. Entry hardware includes three hinges with leaves/screws, lock/rose/viewer, key slot, latch and rounded lever. Four continuous dark seals follow the long side-panel edges at the rounded shell transitions, without inventing a panel grid.

Five dedicated portable tests pass (2.667 s): NumPy solid-angle classification independently proves void/wall/floor samples and opening/grille gaps; Rhino mesh validity/closedness checks every new sanitary/plumbing/telephone part; dimensions, controls, curved surfaces, handed variants and study separation are checked. AJV accepts the authored model, rejects 29 malformed models, and confirms all 1277 quantity leaves reference declared source keys. The initial regression failed against the prior filled basin/toilet and missing detail meshes before implementation.

The reconstructed detail is a source-informed teaching model, not a measured preservation survey. Precise tap profiles, plumbing routing, electronic controls, seam/hinge dimensions and concealed sanitary geometry are inferred. Capsule dimensions, poses, thermal quantities and program-study geometry are untouched by this detail work. The reusable inspect-capsule-detail.py produces a generated research view; cached primary imagery remains generated research material, not a redistributed product asset.


### Final Detail Check

The expanded five-test fixture suite passes in 2.830 s, including Rhino closedness/validity for entry hardware, cladding seals, CRT meshes and grille wires in addition to sanitary ware. The handheld shower now has a grip, rose and individual jets attached to its hose. White cabinet/shelf finishes match the archived reference photo.
