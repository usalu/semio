## Script and precision memo lifetime

`📦️project-nodes/🟨️.mjs:97–108` creates one scripts cache in `createProjectInferenceEnvironmentV1`, and each `inferProjectBodyV1` uses the environment's scripts. The worker service `📦️project-nodes/🏊️project-farm/👷️service/🟨️.mjs` creates that environment once in `open` and passes it to each `produceProjectRecordV1` job; a recycled worker creates a new environment. The parent likewise constructs its scripts once before its project loop (lines 159, 224). Thus precision context keyed by scripts is reused across projects handled by the same live environment, not recreated for each job. Caches are not shared across OS worker processes or worker generations.

`🧭️script-inputs/🟨️.mjs` creates files/closures/commandDependencies Maps in that scripts object. `boundedScriptInputsV1` memoizes walks by workspace, roots, required-source, resource and selector digests, and memoizes per-file import analysis by workspace, file, mode, provider designation and selectors, using observed-source analyses. Late closure recipes are separately held in process-level `closureEntriesV1` by workspace and recipe and reused while observations hold; concurrent callers share the entry flight. Therefore same-key unchanged work is intended to reuse within a worker/parent process, but distinct keys, observation invalidation and new workers can recompute. The actual hit rate, AST implementation-level cache and dominant live cost were not measured; these facts do not prove the cause of the current delay.
