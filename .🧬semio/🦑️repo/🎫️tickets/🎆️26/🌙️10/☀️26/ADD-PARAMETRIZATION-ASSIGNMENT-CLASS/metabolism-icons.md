## Family Identification

The exact eight standard living capsule SVGs are 💊️capsules. The only other eight-variant capsule SVG family is 🥚️ellipsoids, named ellipsoid-capsule in both SVG and representation catalog. No cylindric-capsule SVG filename exists in the assets subtree. 🔋️cylindric-tambours contains four storey variants and is not the requested eight capsule variants. Treat ellipsoid-to-cylindric naming equivalence as unconfirmed; do not silently relabel the asset family.

Variants pair by suffix slash, backslash, z, p, s, L, q, J; these identify geometric connection/configuration variants rather than generic capsule sizes. Original Inkscape document indices map backslash=1, slash=2, q=3, p=4, J=5, L=6, z=7, s=8.

## Styles and Embedding

All examined SVGs use black strokes and fill=none; standard capsules use stroke-width 0.708661 and 1.41732. Ellipsoid capsules use 2.83465/3.9685 and on some shapes 5.66929 (q also has 0.24). They retain Inkscape/Sodipodi metadata and are authored line drawings, not generic pill silhouettes. Preserve SVG geometry; print conversion should scale preserving aspect ratio. Different family bounds require a common output box with keep-aspect fitting, rather than forced stretching. Dark-theme output needs a deliberate stroke treatment or light image panel; original SVG strokes are black.
