# Silhouette Reference Review

Visual review replaced geometric draft people with photo-derived architectural entourage. Selected references: [pitr, standing, CC0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Man_standing_silhouette.svg), [Mette Aumala, walking, CC0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Silhouette_of_a_walking_man.svg), [shokunin, runner4, Openclipart CC0](https://openclipart.org/detail/76993). [Openclipart licensing](https://openclipart.org/share) permits adaptation.

The inspected vectors were normalized into the existing language-neutral contour contract. Isolated standing trace specks were omitted. The running heat anchor follows the new torso; metabolic watt values remain unchanged. Seated and sleeping contours remain hand-authored, with natural profiles and existing floor anchors. No exact Le Corbusier image was reproduced.

Rejected candidates fused body and furniture or contained layers unsuitable for the production SVG renderer. Production has no references to those candidates. Attribution is retained beside the library. Review downloads and raster comparisons are temporary generated outputs.
