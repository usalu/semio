# Reusable Source Sequences

Canonical sources render once per script and scene. A lesson may select an inclusive range of narration clauses using semantic first/last keys. Generated voiceover trace marks determine exact cuts, so edited narration and timings cannot silently invalidate hard-coded timestamps. FFmpeg performs accurate video/audio cuts through the existing external-tool interface. All occurrences preserve curriculum order while sharing source rendering and selected clip cache artifacts.

Physics thermal mass is canonical: a 20 m² ceiling with 10 cm active depth weighs 4,800 kg and stores about 2.7 kWh at 2 K. Heating reuses the introductory storage explanation and winter application; cooling reuses day/lag and summer application. The later energy-balance chapter selects the canonical system-loss sequence from the earlier heating final calculation. Distinct applied calculations remain independent.

FFmpeg selection is compared with an independent PyAV decoded-frame reference.
