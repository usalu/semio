# Nested Leases: Current Canonical Detach Boundary

No current public accepted owner exposes a successful standalone detach-retaining-nonempty-image operation. Publication owner exports ordinary/retained publication and retained-custody require/release. Its private `capturePublicationBodyCommand` includes detach/inactive, but only in the fixed stage pipeline: full before witness, detach/inactive, candidate receipt publication, attach/verify, full preserved after witness, settled. On failure after physical effect its accepted cleanup explicitly remounts; it cannot be stopped after detach and called successful. Native platform opening exposes open/close/operation/cleanup custody and destructive retirement, not an arbitrary detach command. Raw command execution is not a substitute for these accepted owner lifetimes.

Canonical image retirement intentionally requires empty application/journal and pending absence before its destructive sequence. The namespace typed payload owner cannot authenticate the observed Leases database/claim/headroom/queue bytes. Existing resource/claim cleanup deliberately retains coordination/catalog/headroom, and no stale PID/queue or old catalog adoption can be inferred. Therefore those APIs do not presently supply a lawful path to make this image empty.

Two distinct future contracts could address the blocker: a Leases-specific typed owner that proves every deletion through current canonical lease algorithms (likely much broader and unable to delete preserved catalogs), or a new explicit Core physical operation that witnesses and detaches while retaining the complete nonempty image/backing/receipt/journal and charge. The latter better matches the immediate goal of permitting enclosing-image publication without deleting Leases state. It must be a closed accepted operation with actual child EX plus ancestor mapping custody, full current mapping/backing/receipt/pending proof, complete before witness, known-reader census, actual detach and kernel-inactive/underlying proof, and a typed inactive result consumed by the enclosing owner. It cannot reuse a publication token with half its stages omitted, claim allocation retirement, or release custody while outer association bookkeeping still expects a mounted child. Cancellation/cleanup must explicitly settle or truthfully retain ambiguity; a durable inactive child may later be opened by its canonical ordinary owner.

This is a Source boundary finding and prospective recommendation only. No implementation, Native command, protected read or deletion authority was exercised. The fixed current helper is correct to refuse rather than treating nonempty Leases payload as namespace WAL data.
