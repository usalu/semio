The current Source contract does not require retiring the remaining ordinary gf-Fk58e1 payload or its committed charged whole Products allocation merely to publish the whole Products image. `repositoryCacheImagePublicationV1` authenticates the setup-all invocation, current partition policy and canonical current request; it supplies physical and logical observation options to the canonical publisher. It does not require an empty allocation journal or no ordinary fixture directories.

`capturePublicationPayload` witnesses before/after/current through the same bounded logical policy and unchanged preservation predicate. The complete walker treats ordinary directories/files as payload and hashes a present single-link journal as content; retained charged rows are preserved rather than converted to zero charge. Its acceptance conditions are complete bounded traversal, actual physical custody, journal/application preservation and strict service association. A nonempty valid payload is compatible with mounted-application-preserved qualification. Publication is therefore materially different from empty-image retirement, whose canonical owner requires zero ledger/empty mapped payload and pending absence.

Remaining live nested images still matter: kernel/foreign-reader/mount proofs must permit actual canonical detach/remount, so completing the currently selected nested Leases role is necessary to resolve that observed remaining association. A planner status complete is only the domain planner's current nested-role result; it is not a successful whole publication, proof of ordinary consumer readiness or permission to remove fixture bytes.

After actual child associations are closed, the next minimal Source-supported action is ordinary whole Products publication retaining the valid ordinary fixture bytes and journal allocation unchanged, with a fresh current request and actual owner reauthentication. It may safely refuse on unknown/unsupported entries, path/work/logical policy, concurrent mutation, pending state, reader custody or service identity. No extra retirement should be invented to avoid those guards. Tool setup needs current ordinary asset admission and its own domain readiness, not zero Products charge.
