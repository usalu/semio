# Incremental Cleanup On 17 October

Superseded Cargo unit hashes were removed again. Incremental directories larger than 1 GiB were left in place because every crate in them still has a `-working` session, and `rustc` was compiling in this repository.

## Removed

- 239 superseded unit directories under the shared Cargo store
- Measured 0.91 GiB
- No open `rustc` or `cargo` file referred to those directories

## Left in place

| Path | Size | Why |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `cargo/build/prototype/incremental` | 2.50 GiB | 15 of 15 crates have a `-working` session |
| `cargo/canonical-architecture/prototype/incremental` | 1.45 GiB | 14 of 14 crates have a `-working` session |
| `cargo/build/debug/incremental` | 0.87 GiB | 4 of 4 crates have a `-working` session |
| `.cache/b` disk images | about 46 GiB on disk | Mounted and written during this pass |
| `⚡️cache/rust/fixed` | 16 GiB image, 12 GiB used inside | Fixed-size image; cargo 5.9 GiB and nx 5.4 GiB sit inside it |

The shared Cargo store outside that image is about 33 GB. The data volume had about 53 GiB free while compilers were running.
