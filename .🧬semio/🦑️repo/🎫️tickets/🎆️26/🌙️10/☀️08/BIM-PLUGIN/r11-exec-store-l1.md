## Root causes and fixes
1. **Replay custody (the ~160 Drop failures).**
   - `store/🔁️replay/🛑️cancel/🦀️.rs`: new `EditReplay::retire_unscheduled` / `EditReplayResult::retire_unscheduled` (drive the owner to its terminal-empty witness granting exactly its quoted per-axis demands; a refusal forgets the owner instead of dropping it) and `cold_authority()`. Inherent retirement impls relaxed from `Send+Sync` to `'static` (needed by free functions). Cold branches added to the snapshot/mutation lanes of `close_original_step` + `retirement_demands`: a replay without installed issuers (store-created Report replays are cold on purpose, equality convergence needs it) retires its aliases through `retire_shared_projection` / `retire_scratch_operations` (the 676 cold vocabulary) instead of erroring "without an installed issuer".
   - `EditReplay::run(edits)` (steps to the result without deadline, retires the raw replay on any refusal) used by `replay_loaded_history`, `replay_suffix_partitioned`, `refuse_blocking_history`, `dry_run`, `reprojection_replay`, the unit-flag replay of store construction.
   - `finish()` took `order` with `mem::take`, which allocates a fresh first page (`HistoryPageStack::default() == new()`), so the residual always kept order capacity 64 and the handoff failed with "output slots must stay empty": now `mem::replace(.., HistoryPageStack::empty())`.
   - Store paths: `adopt_report_replay` split into `take_report_replay` + `retire_replay_result`; new `ArtifactStore::{retire_replay_result, retire_raw_replay, retire_pending_replay}` (displaced queue for replays with installed issuers, unscheduled for cold ones or a saturated queue; cold ones must not queue because their aliases would sit behind the queue's own shared-root retirements). Routed: `adopt_loaded_replay` (+`take_loaded_replay`), `reproject_replayed`, `adopt_reprojection` (preflight/facts refusals), `install_transitions`, `commit_finished_replay` (+`commit_slotted_replay`), `author_supersession` (+`author_slotted_supersession`), `adopt_pending`, `refuse_local_step`, `discard_local_step`, `cancel_reprojection`, `defer_local_step`, `advance_reprojection` (finish refusal), both Merge-replay consumers, `report_replay` and the derived replay start (convergence/factory refusals).
   - `'static` bounds added to `parse_document_spr`, `parse_decoded_document_spr`, `parse_document_pack`, `settle_parsed_envelope`, `replay_loaded_history` and `io::...::typed` (`🚪️io/🦀️.rs`); `prepared_retirement::close` (replication crate) relaxed from `Send+Sync+'static` to unbounded.
   - Tests that held a replay result and let it fall out of scope now retire it: supersede-replay (3 sites), deferred-reprojection (`operation_capped...`, 2 raw replays), unit `derived_child_reads...` (alias taken out of the result first).
2. **Plain catalogs without a wire source.** `test_support::plain_document_store_owners` now installs a `PlainAuthoringFactory<P, M>` (authoring-only semantic authority: names the `OpBinary` image of each mutation as a text wire; prepares no retained gesture). The image is leaked per authored operation because a test mutation owns no wire storage (test support only). Fixes `plain_test_store` users (sync, sqlite space-history, `assert_store_roundtrip`).
3. **Hangs were cost, not deadlocks.** `ArtifactStore::assert_mirrors_are_live` (test-build oracle) rebuilt every mirror and refolded the whole log on every bump for histories up to 256 edits: 45 ms at step 25 to 300 ms at step 200 (measured per dispatch), so a 240-edit `long_history` took minutes in debug. Oracle now runs every bump up to 64 edits and every 32nd length up to 256 (`MIRROR_ORACLE_EVERY_HISTORY`, `MIRROR_ORACLE_STRIDE`). The hot-path/deferred settle loops got a no-progress detector (hot-path) so a real stall fails fast.
