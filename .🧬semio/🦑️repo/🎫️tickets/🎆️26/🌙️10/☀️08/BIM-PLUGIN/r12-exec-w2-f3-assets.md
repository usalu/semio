## Decisions and deviations

- 2x3 has no `IfcFurniture`, `IfcSanitaryTerminal`, `IfcLightFixture`, `IfcDuctSegment`, `IfcPipeSegment`, `IfcCableCarrierSegment` or `IfcDistributionSystem` (probed with ifcopenshell): the 2x3 classes are `IfcFurnishingElement`, `IfcFlowTerminal`, `IfcFlowSegment` and `IfcSystem`, the specific nature sits in the type object (`IfcSanitaryTerminalType`, `IfcLightFixtureType`, `IfcDuctSegmentType`, ...).
- Per-instance bodies instead of `IfcRepresentationMap` per family: a mirrored instance cannot be a mapped item in 2x3 and the instance mesh already is the evaluated family under its overrides.
- Pipe volume: the inference writes the closed form (pi r^2 L) while the solid is the tessellation (about 1 % smaller at 25 mm), so pipes are excluded from the exact kernel-volume rows (`MEASURED` rule) and the python audit brackets them (kernel <= closed form, >= 98 %).
- The footprint quantity is the area of the rectangle of the bounds of the visible solids in the instance frame (the graph's `footprint_area`).
- SVG colours are inline `style` (stroke/fill) because the sheet's `path{stroke:#000}` rule outranks presentation attributes; the colour is resolved from the snapshot (`mep_elements[id].system`, `components[id].system`) with the fixed palette of `inferences::mep::colour`, so no inference is needed in the sheet chain.
- No IFC surface styles (system colours) are written; glTF and SVG carry the colours.
- Foreign IFC import: no `IfcPolyline` axis reading for multi-vertex runs and only a two-point path from the extrusion.
