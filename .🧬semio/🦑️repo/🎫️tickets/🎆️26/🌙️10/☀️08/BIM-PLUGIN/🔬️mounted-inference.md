# Mounted Inference Funding

`MountedAnalysis::read_rendered` lends only an already published `ModelInference` whose revision equals the requested document revision. A first mounted read refuses before any producer is driven. `advance` can admit a session and `begin_sync_funded` can admit its first `SessionRun`, but `ModelInferenceSession::step` explicitly refuses because the funded graph plan and node producers are absent; `finish` also explicitly refuses because funded projection publication and owner retirement are absent.

The framework's funded driver is `infer_field_step<P, F: OriginalInferredField<P>>(snapshot, source, cache, cursor, values, cx)`. It binds the original caller/source, incrementally constructs `OriginalInferencePlan` from borrowed `plan_source_extent`, `plan_source_node`, `plan_source_key`, and `plan_source_link`, then clones parent values and invokes `compute_step` or `compute_cyclic_step` under the retained grant. `ModelGraph<WANT>` currently implements `InferredField`, whose `plan`, `dep_input`, and `compute` are cold whole-value operations, but not `OriginalInferredField`; calling `step_field_cold` from a mounted turn would bypass that funding contract.

The minimum funded path is to retain a stable snapshot source and typed graph keys/links across turns, implement those borrowed graph hooks and bounded parent cloning, computation and cyclic computation for every requested `ModelGraph` node, then retain `InferenceCursor` and `InferenceValues` inside the run and drive `infer_field_step` until done. A funded projection producer must incrementally assemble and publish `ModelInference` for the exact revision, retaining and retiring prior owners on cancellation or replacement. `InferenceSession::begin_update_funded` only admits an empty session and does not itself supply those node or publication producers; its existing `finish_update` is the unfunded cold adoption path. Subsequent revision comparison, diff donation, cache publication and session-entry lookup are also explicitly unsupported by the funded APIs and need their own bounded producers rather than substituting the cold sync path.
