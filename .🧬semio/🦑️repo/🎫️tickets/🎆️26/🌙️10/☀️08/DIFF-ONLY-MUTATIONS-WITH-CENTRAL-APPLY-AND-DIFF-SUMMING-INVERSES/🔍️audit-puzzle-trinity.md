# 🔍️ Audit: Puzzle and Trinity mutation kinds against the diff-only laws

Read-only audit of `✏️s/🔌️plugins/🧩️puzzle` (113 kinds) and `✏️s/🔌️plugins/🔱️trinity` (16 kinds). No source file was edited, no build or test was run, no git write was issued. Repo root: `/Users/ueli/Documents/semio`. In every path below, `✏️s/🔌️plugins/` and `🏅️standards/🔖️1/🪆️subsets/✳️any/` are omitted. Violation codes follow `📋️design.md`. Intermediate data lives in `🗑️generated/` (`final-kinds.json`, `rows-final.json`, `helper-callers.json`, `l3-final.json`, `classified-pt.json`, `bodies-pt.txt`).

## 1. Verdict

- **129 kinds: 28 clean, 101 with at least one violation.** Separately, 13 hand-written `Mutation<P>` instances (window, transient, presence, config; no derive) sit outside the kind set and are listed in section 7.
- **V3-LEAF-APPLY and V1 are the dominant problem.** 69 kinds build their diff by cloning the addressed entity from `base`, writing one field, and returning the whole entity (`replacement: Some(next)`). 14 kinds (2D 4, 3D 3, 5D 4, rewriting 3) return a whole meta record, a kind-compatibility list or a whole sub-document as the diff (V1-GENERIC-DIFF); 8 of them clone `base.meta` or the list before writing (V3). No leaf calls `MutationDiff::apply` and no leaf takes `&mut P`; the scanner found zero such sites in any `🔺️diff` or `↩️inverse` file.
- **V2-DIFF-DERIVED-INVERSE is the reference pattern, found 12 times.** 10 selection kinds (`drag-`, `rotate-`, `scale-selection` in 2d, 3d and 5d) call a shared `puzzleNd_selection_inverse(base, super::diff::diff(payload, base))`. `set-rule-layout` and `drag-rule` (trinity rewriting) do `super::diff::diff(payload, base).diff().rule_layout` in the inverse.
- **V2-RESTORE-INVERSE:** 3 rewriting kinds (`edit-lhs`, `edit-rhs`, `edit-working-graph`) restore a whole sub-document from `base`; all 13 hand-written Snapshot/config instances restore a whole record. The 3d and 5d config `Set*` variants all invert to a whole-config `Snapshot`, not to the one field they changed.
- **V2-EMPTY-INVERSE: none.** The `Vec::new()` returns occur only on target-missing paths where the forward diff is an error.
- **L3 is only partly tested.** 109 kinds are covered by the `assert_mutation_inverse_law` step-wise replay; 14 more by `inverse_restores_before`; **6 have no inverse test** (V4). No test sums inverse diffs with `absorb`. `DiffAlgebra` has zero implementations in scope, so the Σ-form of L3 and `Σ == m.diff(base).diff().inverse(base)` cannot be checked anywhere.
- **Interpretation caveat (entity scope).** The 69 entity-level full-record patches (plus 10 selection kinds that build the same full records through a shared helper) are counted as V1-SNAPSHOT-DIFF because the design lists 'full-replacement diff' under that code. If the team accepts entity-granular `replacement` as sparse, V1 drops by 79 and what remains is the 6 window kinds and the 13 hand instances. V3 (clone then write) still applies to the 69 entity kinds either way.

## 5. L3 test coverage

- **Law helper.** `assert_mutation_inverse_law` (framework `📡️spr/🧪️tests/⚖️protocol-laws/🦀️.rs:572`) replays `mutation.inverse(base)` in reverse, computes each step's diff against the current state, applies it, and checks equality with base. This is the step-wise form of L3 (the `dₖ`/`sₖ` recurrence) without the `absorb` sum.
- **Law call sites.** Four files only: `🔱️trinity/…/♻️rewriting/🧬️schema/⚙️operations/🧪️tests/🔬️unit`, `🧩️puzzle/…/◻️2d|🧊️3d|🖐️5d/🧬️schema/🧬️mutations/🧪️tests/🔬️unit`. They cover 109 kinds.
- **Kind-level tests.** 177 `inverse_restores_before` functions across kind folders. Each applies the inverse steps to a copy of `before` and compares with base. They are the only coverage for 14 kinds: rewriting `set-rule-layout`, `drag-rule`; jack `set-query`; 2d's seven target-region kinds; 5d's four selection kinds.
- **No coverage (V4), six kinds:** rewriting `set-camera`, `set-lod-mode`; jack `set-camera`, `set-lod-mode`, `replace-query-result`, `set-editor-selection`. The 13 hand-written Snapshot instances are also uncovered: none of the editor `🪟️window`, `👥️presence` or `🎚️config` tests mention `inverse`.
- **Absorb.** `assert_mutation_diff_absorb_law` appears in four tests (2d `move_node_inverse_and_absorb_law`, 3d `move_object_diff_absorb_law`, 5d `move_part_2d_diff_absorb_law`, trinity `edit_lhs_diff_absorb_law`). All four and the two other absorb unit tests sum forward sequential diffs (`d1`, `d2`). **No test absorbs inverse diffs, and no test checks `Σ.apply(after) == base`.**

## 6. Non-leaf apply sites (L4/L5 seam, not counted per kind)

- Store absorb folds that call `<Diff as MutationDiff>::apply(&diff, &self.snapshot)` inside the artifact io state's `fn absorb(mut self, diff)`: 2d `🚪️io:251`, 3d `🚪️io:178`, 5d `🚪️io:241`, jack `🚪️io:180`, rewriting `🚪️io:188`. These look like the store fold that the design allows; not verified that they go through `protocol::apply_diff`.
- Editor `fn advance` computes a post root with `diff(...).into_parts().0.apply(base)` or `MutationDiff::apply(...diff(...).diff(), base)`: 2d `✏️editor:2487, :2659`; 3d `:6815, :6993`; 5d `:8653, :8776` (the 5d sites are in `✏️editor/🦀️.rs`). Outside the leaves.
- Second applier helpers listed in section 3: rewriting `⚙️operations:62`, jack `⚙️operations:260`.
- L5 gap: `MutationDiff::apply(&self, base)` is a plain trait method (framework `📡️replication/🎮️mutation/🦀️.rs:104`) with no `ApplyCapability` parameter, so nothing in the type system stops a leaf from calling it. The design's L5 relies on the `verify mutation-outcome-law` policy gate, which was not run.
