# 📋 Stdio Mid-Artifacts Audit: Diff-Only Mutations

Scope: `✏️s/🔌️plugins/🗄️stdio/🗿️artifacts/` → 📐️step, 📜️docx, 🎞️gif, 🏗️ifc, 🎨️svg, 📽️pptx, 📕️xlsx. Read-only audit against `📋️design.md` (L1–L5, V1–V4, Rulings).

## 🔍 Method and limits

- 208 textual `impl MutationKind<…> for X` sites (38 step, 32 docx, 31 gif, 30 ifc, 29 svg, 24 pptx, 24 xlsx). A further 11 `PatchSnapshot` kinds are generated by the `snapshot_patch_leaf!` macro in `📇️registry/🧬️contract/✏️editing/🦀️.rs` and are counted separately, so the scope is 219 kinds.
- Helper bodies were read directly for every aggregator (`agg_diff`/`agg_inverse`), every `apply_*_mutation`, and every diff helper they call. Per-kind codes come from the aggregator arm for that kind, not from a text heuristic alone.
- A line-based script (`🗑️generated/stdio-mid-audit/scan3.py`, `summarize.py`, `gen.py`) produced the arm digests and the kind list. Its raw flags were too noisy to use as codes, so codes were assigned from the verified rules in `gen.py`.
- V4 is reported for all kinds: no leaf runs an L3 absorb-sum check (`Σ = Σ invₖ.diff`). Apply-then-undo round-trip rows exist in 🥒️.feature files for most subsets, but those are single-inverse checks, not L3.
- V5 (absorb soundness, added in the Rulings) was not audited. `absorb` is implemented on every diff type in scope.
- Not verified: the V1-GENERIC-DIFF field-level check of each diff struct (whole-collection fields). Only leaf-level evidence was checked.

## ❓ L3 questions answered

- **Per-kind test that applies inverse steps and compares to base?** Yes for most subsets: a `Scenario Outline: Undoing <id>` apply-then-undo row exists in 🥒️.feature files (see the diff-types section for the subset list). These check one inverse at a time, not the L3 sum.
- **Any test that sums inverse diffs with `absorb`?** No per-kind test does this in scope. `absorb` is exercised only in aggregator or diff-level law tests (xlsx `absorb_law`, docx canonical absorb, gif 87a/89a and ifc `absorb_law_holds_over_curated_ops`-style cases), and those sum *forward* mutation diffs, not `inverse` diffs.
- **Hand-written `impl Mutation<P>` (V3-HAND-MUTATION)?** None. The only textual hit is a doc comment in gif 87a `🧬️mutations/🦀️.rs:15`.
- **Central applier (L4/L5)?** Not enforced in scope. Eight `apply_*_mutation(&mut Snapshot)` aggregator helpers call `MutationDiff::apply` and assign `*snapshot = next`, and `🚪️io` adapter modules call them. Each helper is counted under V3 in the helper table.
