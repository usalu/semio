## Method Notes

1. `rg --no-ignore-vcs -l "impl.*MutationKind<"` over the five artifacts gave 192 kind files; each kind dir was read for `🔺️diff`, `↩️inverse`, `🧪️tests`, and top-level payload.
2. Pattern scan: `base.clone()` bound with `let mut` and then written; `&mut` snapshot parameters; `between(`; `.apply(`; `.diff(` in inverses; `Vec::new()`/`vec![]` returns; base-collection clones in inverses; list-typed diff fields from the artifact diff schema.
3. Representative samples opened: en1993 `update-bolt-inputs` (clone+whole list, sequential inverse test), din4108 `remove-zone` (`apply_in_place(&mut)`), din16798 `change-zone-t-op-summer` (clone+`iter_mut`), en1998 `change-elevation-regular` (clone+`get_mut`, conditional empty inverse), en1990 `change-permanents` (whole-list, no clone), en1990 `change-project-id` (clean scalar), en1990 inverse for insert/remove-permanent (whole-collection restore), en1998 `change-member-detailing` (conditional empty inverse mirrors error branch).
4. Zero hits in scope: `DiffAlgebra` impl, `between(` in leaves, `.apply(` in diff/inverse/leaf top-level, inverse calling `.diff(`, unconditional `Vec::new()` inverse, hand-written `impl Mutation<P>`.
