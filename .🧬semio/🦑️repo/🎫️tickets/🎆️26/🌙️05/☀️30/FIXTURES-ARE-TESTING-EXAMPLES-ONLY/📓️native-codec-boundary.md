Its normal token construction can allocate frames, strings, array/map storage and parser backing; its close is already a full-grant retained step. Reusing this owner requires genuine normal-path demand and exact produced copy/capacity/release/depth controls, then a typed construction phase. A wrapper around its counters would not account for unrelated typed output construction.

No current cumulative normal writer debit is visible in these bounded originals.
