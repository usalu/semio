## Funded trace ownership

The existing ToolRunTraceStore now owns a paged numeric index keyed by trace key, paged live stamp and rejection indexes, and a paged delivery log. Native input borrows the decoded source and advances a fixed-width cursor. Each successful original operation commits its resident mutation and its renderer replay together. The caller gets an acknowledgment for every authored input, including attempts the residency cap declines and retire requests naming an absent key. Oldest rejected eviction keeps its retire and replacing upsert in the same published page. A reset uses numeric generations to avoid dropping or walking a live index inside a turn.

Compaction uses paid page reservations and at most one original row or one log page per advance. The prior coherent resident view and delivery log stay available until the snapshot swaps in. The discarded backing then closes under fresh copy and release grants. Pending snapshot state and old retirement belong to the store, so abandoning a decoded cursor and restarting at the next generation cannot replay or strand them. The native log may publish smaller pages than the cold implementation. Browser cursor order, stale-cursor clearing and replay are preserved. The cold store still groups the authored ops with the same wire cap.
