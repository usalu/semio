Its predicate checks patches, effects, presence, nextWake, both receipts and both ingress lanes, refusing malformed input. Use this conservative rule for pre-capture polling; never consume or skip a carried result. Capture can coexist with a patch or effect: preserve the exact last turn and pass it through the existing React `settlePluginTurn` or wgpu `settleInstanceLifecycle` after binding the owner, before acknowledging capture. If a nonempty result arrives without capture, stop and surface an attributable failure; there is no owner yet to safely admit its UI patch. Likewise stop on idle, fault or malformed response rather than polling blindly. Do not replace the original error during failed-open cleanup.

Check `requireOpening` immediately before and after every poll. The existing worker drive bounded crossing is not an overall guest-liveness proof: a healthy opening may require many crossings, while a guest can stall forever. Use an explicit finite wall or work budget with a named pre-capture stall failure and preserve cancellation and transport failures; do not infer a stalled guest from one empty more-work response or an arbitrary small continuation count. The observed 21k epochs do not prove eventual capture, so polling alone does not repair the downstream guest stall.

## Implementation and verification

React and wgpu now submit an empty lifecycle poll through their existing scheduler when a crossing carries no output, the lease is still opening, and the guest reports more work. The helper passes the final raw crossing to the existing receipt/settlement path. It uses the existing 1,024 host continuation limit from the language-neutral drive contract. The focused Vitest suite passed 32/32, including identity of the captured turn, cancellation, visible and terminal results, transport failure, and the continuation limit.
