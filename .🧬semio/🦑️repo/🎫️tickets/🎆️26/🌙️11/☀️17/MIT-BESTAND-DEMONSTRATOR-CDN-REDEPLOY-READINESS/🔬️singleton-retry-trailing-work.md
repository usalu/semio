# Singleton Retry Trailing Work

Read-only snapshot of Cargo JSON streams, 2026-11-20. On A `wONtSjE`, its first pass emitted E0425 (`kind`) after 1,127 completed artifact messages, then another 74 compiled artifacts before return. In the still-running retry, 63 of those 74 matched exactly by package, target, kinds, features and output filenames: nine were reported fresh and 54 compiled. The remaining 11 have not been established as reused or invalidated; retry is incomplete. On B `w0salWH`, first pass emitted E0432 (`PolygonJsonExport`) after 1,199 completed artifacts, then 228 compiled artifacts before return. Its failed retry announced exact matches for 29 of those trailing artifacts: 22 fresh, seven compiled. Other trailing units may be unannounced, changed identity or invalidated; the counts do not prove which.

The streams lack per-message wall-clock timestamps, so they cannot establish elapsed time from first error to Cargo return, and compiled artifact messages alone do not measure CPU saved by fail-fast. Some completed trailing work was reused; some matching work was rebuilt after source churn. These observations make a controlled singleton fail-fast timing comparison a possible future experiment, not a demonstrated safe or faster policy change. Multi-root partial publication has separate semantics and must be preserved; no policy or builds were changed.
