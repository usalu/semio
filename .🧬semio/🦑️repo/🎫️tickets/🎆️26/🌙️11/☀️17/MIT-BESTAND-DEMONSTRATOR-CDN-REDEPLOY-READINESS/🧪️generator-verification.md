# Generator Wide Contributions Verification

The four whole-pack app routes in the handoff select their declared contributions wire ceiling on `SetContributions` and keep the ordinary ceiling for other commands. The generation3d action producer suite now expects the editor-owned producer. Its regression reads a language-neutral fixture, verifies the padded JSON against `serde_json`, dispatches the 128 KiB body through the mounted editor and requires a single terminal completion.
