
## Library-test fixups (tour-guide, tealight, sectional-garage-door)

1. tour-guide, 1:50 front shape 10 (`neck`): the poly carried `points: [[null, null]]` (a NaN hull serialised as null, present in HEAD), so ajv tried the `oneOf/0` line branch (needs x0, y0). Replaced by the 1:100/1:200 neck quad (`fixup-tour-guide-neck.py`) and, because the validator then raised `lateral-ambiguous` for a flat shape on the head bone, added the same revolved solid as at 1:100 (`fixup-tour-guide-neck-solid.py`). schema-conformance passes (43/43), triage 0 failures, validateAsset 0 issues.
2. tealight 1:200: the 10 x 10 pixel raster had knife-edge cells (coverage 48-59 %), where the owned centre sampling and sharp's half-coverage threshold disagree. Front flame drawing was also unlike the volume flame. Fix (`fixup-tealight-flame-width.py 1.12`): flame width x1.12 in the elevation path, the front path (now the elevation path shifted by 0.0185 m) and the 1:200 flame volume profile radii. A sweep (`fixup-sweep.ts`) showed agreement for s = 1.1 to 1.15 (0.4 to 0.65 also, but too thin). Owned and sharp IoU are now 1.0 in all views; triage 0, validateAsset 0.
3. sectional-garage-door 1:50: the `coil` revolve repeated 122 separate tori (pitch 8.2 mm, wire 8 mm), i.e. 122 connected pieces of 384 triangles. `referenceOf` pushes the whole mesh into `full` once per piece of a section view (5.7 M triangles, 17 M points, 285 MB SVG for sharp, limit 10 MB). Fix (`fixup-garage-coil.py`): pitch 8.0 mm, count 125, so neighbouring tori touch and the mesh is one piece (48 000 triangles); extent -0.4999..0.5001 m as before. Elevation IoU 0.9945 owned and sharp, all views within 0.0001.
Note for the measure owner: in `referenceOf` the `full.push` loop sits inside `for (const s of pieces(whole))`, so `full` grows by pieces x triangles; hoisting it would make any many-piece body cheap. Not changed (the guard is out of scope).
Tools: `fixup-oracle.ts`, `fixup-try.ts`, `fixup-cover.ts`, `fixup-grid.ts`, `fixup-size.ts`, `fixup-bodies.ts`.
