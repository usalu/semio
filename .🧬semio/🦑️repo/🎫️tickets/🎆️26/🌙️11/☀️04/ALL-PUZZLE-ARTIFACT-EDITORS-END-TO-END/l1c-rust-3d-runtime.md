## Fixes
1. Brush reconciler (`🎭️modes/…/🖌️brush/🧪️tests/🔬️unit/🦀️.rs`): test was wrong. It built `ToolRunView` (owns `SharedOriginalStorage`, panics on drop). `run_effects` takes `ToolRunPollView`; the helper now builds that directly.
2. Fill close denial tests (`original_fill_builder_close_denials_and_receipts_match_actual_heap`, `original_fill_run_and_revalidation_close_denials_match_actual_heap`) hit three separate causes:
   a. Authored `closeGrant` too small. Measured `size_of::<FillClosingOwners>()` = 2776; the first close turn quotes copy 6328 / capacity 3552 (the bundle plus its retirement node, 3552 ≈ 1.28 x 2776), over the 4096 copy grant, so no turn could ever fund it. The only frontier over 4096; the rest are under 500 bytes. The grant is a test-authored constant and the job-crate standard policy is 32768, so the grant is raised to 8192 (`⏳️precompute/🪣️fill/🎟️caller/🧪️tests/🧫️fixtures/🔣️.json`: copy and capacity 4096 -> 8192, the smallest power of two above the measured 6328; denial rows still use demand-1, so the law stays tight).
   b. Production heap frees not reported in receipts (the heap-equality law caught them), all in `⏳️precompute/📐️geometry/🧮️pose-math/🦀️.rs` `CollisionMeshRetirement`: (i) the `Arc` block of the last mesh share (120 B) freed by `Arc::try_unwrap` under `RetirementStep::Advanced`; (ii) each BVH `into_step` frees two child boxes (80 B) but reported one node; (iii) the 64-slot BVH stack `Vec` (2560 B) was freed only by the cursor `Drop`. Now quoted and reported through `next_close_byte_demand`/`RetirementStep::Bytes`; `terminal_is_empty` also requires the stack capacity to be 0. New `BvhTree::step_release_bytes` in `🧰️framework/🔨️modules/🧊️3d/🧿️collision/🦀️.rs`.
   c. `JobOutbox::close_step` in `✏️s/🔌️plugins/🧩️puzzle/🎮️commands/📤️jobs/🦀️.rs` dropped its `source` buffer (`self.source = Vec::new()`) without a quote or receipt in three places. It is now one explicit final frontier: `retirement_demands` quotes `release_bytes = source.capacity()`, the step reports it, and `terminal_is_empty` requires `source.capacity() == 0`. (This was found while chasing b; not the failure that tripped the law, but the same defect class.)
