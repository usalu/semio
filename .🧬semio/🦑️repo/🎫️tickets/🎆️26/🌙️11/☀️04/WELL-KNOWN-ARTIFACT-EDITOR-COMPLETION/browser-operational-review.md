## Correct Reopen Ordering

The review identified a real deadlock: LocalFiles persistence flags derive from live actor statuses, while a reloaded catalog can exist before any actor is open. Waiting for persisted=true before clicking therefore prevents the very open that can establish that status.

The corrected sequence is exact catalog UUID visible, real click, same UUID plus file-opened, exact window owner, then persisted=true and edited export. Genuine durable Save acknowledgement before close is unchanged. A closed catalog row alone is never persistence proof.
