# Original Emission Accepted Cancellation Route

Read-only call-order audit found no cancellation-only outer rejection before accepted receipt/page handling. No source changes or native execution.

advance_typed_operation_publication_unit first requires nonzero fuel, selects the publishing owner, removes it temporarily, and chooses publish_original_emission_run when the retained original caller is present without completion/owned identity. Any returned failure reinserts the same mounted owner.

publish_original_emission_run validates the actual source binding, decoder identity and retained caller roots/clock. OriginalCallerSourceLoan::validate_context rejects foreign, returned or closing sources, but does not inspect cancellation. The route propagates original cancellation into the actual lease, then enters the admitted pump without claiming new publication authority.

The pump permits retained page/accepted store custody through should_yield, while still requiring actual fuel. It disables child, interaction and inline shortcuts when acknowledged-page cleanup or accepted custody is pending, and when lease cancellation is observed. The ordinary unit advances pending result custody and recovers AwaitingAck before reject_cancelled_publication and freshness. Thus cancelled accepted work can reach the original-retirement framing path; no normal lease claim is attempted first.

After awaited pump, original cancellation is propagated again and cancelled lease skips follow_derivable_children. Post-run interaction revalidation also skips original or lease cancellation before borrowing authoring authority. Source mismatch and actually closing caller remain legitimate fail-closed conditions; accepted custody does not authorize a foreign source.

The owned completion route has separate authoring-receipt resume/follow logic and is not evidence for this direct original-emission route. This audit establishes source ordering only; genuine native cancelled accepted-result delivery remains required.
