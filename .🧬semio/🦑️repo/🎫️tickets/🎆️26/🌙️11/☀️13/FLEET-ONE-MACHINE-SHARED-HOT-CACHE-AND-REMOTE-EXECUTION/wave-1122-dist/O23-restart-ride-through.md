## 1. What happened at 13:05:47 (12:05:47Z), from the server's own log

So it was a restart for new code (not a crash), and for the scheduler role it was a plain stop: only the cache role had a handover (`sidecarHandsOverV1`: `role==="cache"`), the scheduler kept its operation table in memory only.
- The two requesters (`semio_hub_procedural_component` pid 86470, `semio_hub_layout_component` pid 70270) saw `14 Call closed without status` 17 ms after the restart line and compiled locally at once. ## 2. Fix, both sides
a. Scheduler restart = restore (live tree, active since the restart at 13:38:48): `🗓️scheduler/📓️journal/🟦️.ts` (atomic journal file, age bound), `🗓️scheduler/🟦️.ts` `journal()` (queued and executing operations with identity, action, platform, requester, bounds and the machine that runs them), `restore()` (every row is known again under its own name; an executing one waits for its worker, a queued one for its requester), binding in `register` (a worker that registers again with the task it kept running is bound to the restored operation: counter `resumed`; the action is never run again), dispatch held while a restored action still waits for its worker, at most `server.resumeGraceSeconds` (60, schema-first in `🏃️execution/🧬️schema/🌐️remote-execution.json` + `🔣️policy.json`), then such an action is queued. `🛰️sidecar/🗓️scheduler/🟦️.ts` writes the journal (debounced 250 ms, plus once on stop) next to the sidecar record (`build-scheduler.operations.json`) and restores at start; counters `restored`/`resumed` are in `/status`, the 60 s log summary and the closed `Counter` schema. Not done: handing over the listening sockets (successor with reusePort as the cache does): two live schedulers would each hold half of the workers' connections; the journal makes the gap (about 1 s) harmless instead, because requesters and workers ride through it.
b. Requester (`🚀️offload/🔌️client/🟦️.ts`, patch set for the clone): `executeOffloadV1` reconnects with jittered back-off (0.25 s doubling to 2 s) as `WaitExecution` by operation name; the outage budget counts from the first lost call and is reset by every row heard (60 s; linked units at least `worker.outage.seconds` 600 s); `NOT_FOUND` after the server is back (up to three in a row, counted only for attaches) submits again (join in flight, journal, or action cache); the unit deadline and overall wait keep running; phase timers are paused during the back-off. Both call sites in `🚀️offload/🟦️.ts` use it; `executeOffloadRidingThroughV1` is deleted. Only an outage longer than the budget declines (`infrastructure`, "the server stayed away for N s").
c. Worker (`👷️worker/🟦️.ts`): the completion report is kept and retried with back-off (initial/maximum from `worker.outage`) for `worker.outage.seconds` (600 s; before: 8 attempts, about 12 s); 404 re-registers with the task (adoption), only a refusal other than 404/408/429/5xx ends it. Takes effect when a worker agent restarts (agents drain first).
d. `repo fleet status` (`🚢️fleet-status`, `🚢️status`, `🌡️load`, `🛰️agent`, `🛰️sidecar/👷️agent`, schemas): each agent sends `code` (first 16 hex of its sidecar closure digest) in its load report; the scheduler passes it into the fleet table; status computes the digest of the coordinator's current tree and prints per machine `code <x> (the tree's)`, `CODE <x> differs from the tree's <y>` or `code unknown (older agent)` (EN/DE), in the JSON `machines[].code` and `fleet.treeCode`. Machines show `code unknown` until their agent restarts into this code.
e. Also (coordinator's urgent task, refined): remote non-zero exits are classified in one place (`🚀️offload/🩺️failure`, list in `🧬️schema/🔣️.json`): ordinary compiler diagnostics (the real E0425 of `semio_hub_draw_component` is test sample 1) are the build's verdict; no error diagnostic at all, missing-crate/metadata, unreadable file, linker, crash and full disk are redone locally once with reason `remote-failed-local-redo` and the head of the remote stderr; codes E0460/E0463/E0464/E0583/E0786 stay `incomplete-inputs`. K7's unconditional redo at unit-cache `📜️script.ts:242` is removed (one mechanism).
