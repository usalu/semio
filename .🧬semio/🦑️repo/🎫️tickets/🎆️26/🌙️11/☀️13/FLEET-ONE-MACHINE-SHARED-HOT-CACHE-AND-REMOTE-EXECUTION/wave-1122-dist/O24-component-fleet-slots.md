## 2. The fix: two kinds of slot

`JS🟦️.ts` (runner rewritten, `light` renamed `fleet`), `JS🧬️schema/🔣️.json`:
- **LOCAL slot**: as before (O14): the first pass always; further ones hold a real reservation of the predicted peak and start only while it fits above the floor, beside the running reservations AND the fleet footprints.
- **FLEET slot** (option `fleet`): reserves only the small local footprint of a pass whose units and final run on workers: `fleetPassFootprintMegabytes` 512 (measured: cargo 9 MB + 14 wrappers x 32 MB = 460 MB on the live play pass), charged through the same ledger the local admission reads (`reservedMegabytes` = local reservations + fleet footprints; the owned-memory add-back stays capped at the local reservations). Law `admitFleetComponentJobV1` (schema `FleetJobAdmitHost/Decision`, reasons `fleet-fits|none|busy|cap|pressure|memory`): cap not reached, a worker slot that fits one more final is free (`slots`: `fleetLinkedSlotsV1` = scheduler idle count at the final's peak, capped by the free slots of workers with the requester's linker = O19's rule), pressure not `critical`, and the footprint fits `available - fleetFloorMegabytes (512) - footprints held`. **Raised (warn) local pressure and the local finals' reservations never stop a fleet pass; critical does; unreadable memory admits** (a footprint of a few hundred MB, the warden acts on a real overshoot).
- **Live capacity**: `fleet.slots()` is re-read every `fleetPollMilliseconds` (5 s) and whenever a fleet job settles; a started fleet pass lowers the cached count at once so one burst cannot over-start. Driver option `fleetSlots` (new; `driver-stages` implements it with the median measured final peak and `fleetLinkedSlotsV1`); the cap of concurrent jobs is the authored `componentJobsMaximum` (the whole lane budget, no longer the start-time plan). Total jobs = `componentJobsLimitV1`; local jobs = `planComponentJobSlotsV1` with no fleet share (up to 3).
- **Either kind**: a pass starts as soon as either kind is free (fleet first, it is the cheaper), otherwise it waits, polled every 5 s and woken by every settling job.
- **Pinned remote placement**: a fleet slot pins the pass to workers; no local fallback into unreserved memory. Two guards: (a) the wrapper's local fallback compile of a final already waits at the broker (memory session: units held back until their predicted peak fits, counter `admitted-wait`), and takes one of the 4 local compile slots; (b) the runner contract `FleetPlacementFailedErrorV1`: a fleet pass whose body throws it gives the fleet slot back and is queued for a LOCAL slot only (ahead of the unstarted passes, without holding them back), where it waits for its reservation. Honest limit: nothing throws it in production yet, because the offload wrapper reports a failed final only into `x/offload.jsonl`, not to the driver (O23 owns that client; a hook is to be added there). Until then guard (a) is the effective protection.
- **Texts (EN + DE, schema `Messages.fleet`)**: the plan line names both kinds ("N local slot(s) and up to M fleet slot(s); a fleet job reserves 512 MB here, the free worker slots are read again every 5 s (K fit a final now)"; the start-time line from `componentJobs` is reworded "local slot(s) + fleet slot(s) ... read again while the build runs"); `started` ("starts on a fleet slot ... its final is placed on a worker"); the wait line says what it waits for: `waits for a fleet slot (...)`, `waits for a local slot: it needs X MB, Y MB are free above the Z MB floor (reason); no fleet slot (reason)`, `waits for a fleet slot or a local slot: no worker slot is free (reason) and a local job needs X MB ... (reason)`. Example (the play case): "release-04 waits for a fleet slot or a local slot: no worker slot is free (every worker slot that fits a final is taken) and a local job needs 3774 MB, -16277 MB are free above the 6144 MB floor (memory pressure is raised, so no further local final starts)".
- Not live: if the fleet did not answer at driver start (`fleetRemote == 0`) the run keeps the static plan: the wrapper's light-slot pool (`SEMIO_UNIT_CACHE_LIGHT_SLOTS`) and the fleet jobserver are sized once at start from `fleetJobs`, so fleet passes cannot be added later.

- They are NOT behind a pass slot: a stage starts as soon as its component's `stage` copy is done (dependency edges inside the component), whatever the lane runner holds. They compete for the driver's 4 jobserver tokens (`single` = 1 token, `available/parallel` more, `io` none) with the local rustc units and with the ONE token a LOCAL pass keeps for its cargo's whole life (`runAdmittedPass`: `held === "local" ? jobserver.acquire`). A fleet pass takes no token. So moving a pass to a fleet slot frees a token for stages; with the first pass local they share 3.
