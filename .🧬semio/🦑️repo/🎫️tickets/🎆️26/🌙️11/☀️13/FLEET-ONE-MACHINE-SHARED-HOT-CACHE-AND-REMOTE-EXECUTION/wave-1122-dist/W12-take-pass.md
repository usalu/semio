## 3. What changed (item 3), schema first

a. Verified-once presence (`B👁️observation/📥️reuse/🎫️verified/🟨️.mjs`, new; `B👁️observation/📥️reuse/🟨️.mjs`; policy `verifiedNamesMaximum` 65,536): every name a presence session finds present is remembered with its leaf path, leaf stamp (dev, ino, mode, nlink, size, mtime ns, ctime ns) and the admitted no-follow ancestry. Inside a take pass (`withTakePassPresenceV1`) a look answers a remembered name by rechecking that ancestry once per ask and one `lstat` of the leaf; any change or vanished leaf forgets the name and the ask goes to a session as before. Outside a take pass nothing changes (the race laws of `🪪️presence` need the session to see a change during its own look; a first version answered every ask from memory and broke 39 of them, a second turned a vanished leaf into a refusal and broke the "removed leaf is recomputed" law; the pass scope is the correct boundary: a pass is one snapshot, like a validation round).
b. Batch prefetch (`PN📥️take/🧺️presence-prefetch/🟨️.mjs`, new; policy `prefetchRecordsPerAsk` 64, `prefetchAsksInFlight` 4): at the start of the take pass the stored records of all present owners are read (lean: brotli, header line, `resultSha256` check, no row blocks), their inventory names go to `prefetchInferenceInventoryPresenceV1` in asks of 64 records, which the coalescer runs as consecutive 512-name batches of one session; the looks then hit the memory. Runs beside the lanes (not before them), cancellable with the pass, counted in an `[owners] presence prefetch` line.
c. One slow owner does not hold a lane (`takeBoundedLookV1` in `PN📥️take/🟨️.mjs`, policy `lookMaximumMilliseconds` 120,000, `abandonedLooksMaximum` 12): after the bound the lane marks the owner missing and takes the next; the look goes on (`inflight`), and the inference loop joins it before it would infer, so nothing is inferred twice; set-aside looks are bounded, beyond that a lane waits again. The missing owners are queued for inference by the existing release/want path after the pass.
d. Phase progress for the stall rule (`B🧠️memory/📋️slice-report/🟨️.mjs`, schema text `takeVerified`): the progress line also changes when verified inventory names (`presence.verified.remembered`, distinct names) or fleet-seeded records grow, e.g. `2915 inventory records and fleet records verified / 2915 Inventar- und Flotteneinträge bestätigt; taken 407 of 608.` The graph counter still reads `taken N of M`; looks and polls that found nothing never move it. Seen live on every worker after pick-up.
e. Silent-read stall (`readySliceSilentV1` in `B🌐️remote-execution/🛰️sidecar/🚦️ready/🌲️process-tree/🟦️.ts`, policy `sliceSilentMilliseconds` 900,000; tick in `🚦️ready/🟦️.ts`): a reading stage whose own slice report of this attempt is 15 min old (not the final one, no warden hold) AND whose log showed no new line for 15 min is stopped as stalled, also when the tree spends CPU. The first landing (19:25) lacked the log condition; m01 was stopped as stalled at 19:51 with a report 941 s old while its `taken` still crept 562 → 569 (it may have been this rule or the 10-minute rule: the origin files do not say which), so the log condition was added at about 20:10. m08, m11 and m12 were stopped as stalled between 20:00 and 20:05 at 827 to 940 s of report silence with `taken 0`, frozen as m03 was.
f. Exclusive mapping writer on the take path: none is on it. The take path only takes SHARED grants (pre/post verify of the presence session); the waits are a holder outside the read. (a) and (b) cut the number of sessions of a pass to about one, which is what removes the grant from the take path in practice. The holder itself is still unnamed (section 5).
g. Refused identity classes (GR16/GR19): measured 0 refused, 0 stale, 0 re-asked records in every worker report of this lane; nothing to fix there.
