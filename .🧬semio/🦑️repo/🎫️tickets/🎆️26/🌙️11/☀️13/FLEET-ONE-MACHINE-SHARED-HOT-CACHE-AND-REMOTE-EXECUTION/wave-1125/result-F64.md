# F64: the cold-unit wedge (M1 `syn`, F60 grant growth)

## Root cause (found live, not the hypothesis)
Both hypotheses were partly wrong. A live `repo cargo --store=shared --configuration=prototype -- test -p semio-ui-tui-charts-laws` (output kept only in the lane's generated folder, deleted) printed, with the new visibility lines: `unit proc_macro2 waits in the broker queue (place 1 of 1) although nothing runs: it needs about 256 MB, the budget is 0 MB and the grant 1333 MB; waited 37 s` and later `semio_ui_styling ... needs about 695 MB, the budget is 605 MB and the grant 1333 MB; waited 36 s`.
- The unit never reached the grant-growth wait. The broker admits against `grant - non-compiler memory` (`parentBudget`, test-pinned by `🪆️parent`: "parent room accounts for driver overhead"). Cargo of this 526-member workspace holds 0.7-1.5 GB itself, the shared-store grant was sized 37-63 % of the default (1333 MB), so the room left for a compiler is 0-600 MB and every unit waits for a non-compiler window that never decays while cargo keeps that footprint. Nothing runs, the scheduler idles, and nothing was ever printed (the held log only covers waiters the queue had admitted). That is M1's 30-minute `syn`/`libc` wedge. F60's growth only fired when the unit alone was above the grant.
- The cold model also over-estimated `syn` (workspace model 644 MB + 42 MB/kloc = 2.6-2.8 GB for 51 kloc against 345-365 MB measured). A peer lane landed the external cold class meanwhile (`coldExternalBaseBytes` 384 MB + 4 MB/kloc, `externalSourceV1`, serial fallback); I pinned it with an oracle test and did not rewrite it.

## Fix
0. Confirmed on a stuck run of a peer (pid 38325, syn waiting 52 min with the scheduler wrapper at 0 % CPU and no rustc, `cargo-growth` grant of 206 MB held for 53 min): F60's growth booked `predicted - grant`, but admission nets the driver's own memory (cargo plus the 30 unit-cache bun wrappers, 0.7-2.5 GB) out of the grant, so after the growth `grant - driver < predicted` still and the waiter is never admitted. That is the M1 wedge.
1. Driver overhead grows the grant (admission `🟦️.ts`, admit handler): when a unit fits the grant alone but not beside the non-compiler memory (`predicted + nonCompiler > grant`) and a growth port exists, the broker asks the host gate for the difference through the existing F60 mechanism (early queued verdict, en/de progress every 30 s, 15 min bound, abort on disconnect, booking given back). A waiter that stalls later (nothing running, the driver grew after it arrived) is rescued by a further growth from the 10 s tick (`rescueStalled`, test). A refused/timed-out growth just queues as before; no port keeps the old behaviour. Growth waits in the connection chain of that unit, other units are admitted meanwhile (test).
2. Fleet history before the cold model: `admissionSiblingHistoryV1(..., external)`: an external crate (registry/git/vendor source, its version fixes its bytes) also takes rows that carry no recorded size, i.e. the fleet ledger rows of its other profile (`syn@syn|host|release|lib` 345-365 MB). Workspace crates keep the size rule.
3. Calibration oracle test `🧪️tests/🧭️cold-external` (corpus of ledger-measured third-party crates, independent Python formula): the model equals the formula, covers every measured peak, overshoots no crate >= 250 MiB by more than 2.2x (syn 1.6x), and a real-size (51 kloc) cold `syn` source is admitted under a 0.6 GiB grant at once.
4. Visible waiting: every unit waiting in the broker queue prints (en and de) place, what is running, what it needs, waited seconds at least every `Policy.waits.progressMilliseconds` (30 s); `idle` variant says "although nothing runs". The broker publishes the waiting rows (queue, idle, growth) every 10 s (`onWaits`), the launcher writes them to `<gate dir>/unit-waits/<pid>.json` (new member `🌡️host-capacity/⏳️unit-waits`), `repo capacity status` (and `--json` `unitWaits`) prints one line per waiting unit in the chosen language; dead pids and records older than 30 s are dropped.
5. M1 proof blocker (unrelated to the scheduler): `semio-ui-tui-laws` exceeded its own source boundary (264676 of 262144 bytes) once M1's charts laws were `mod`-included. Moved them into a crate of their own (precedent `semio-ui-tui-unit-laws`): `semio-ui-tui-charts-laws` at `⌨️tui/🧪️tests/🔬️charts/🥣️companion`, workspace member added, `mod charts` and the charts dev-dependency removed from the laws crate.
