`L/` = `🧰️framework/🛍️products/🦑️repo/🔨️modules/📚️library/`, `G/` = `L/🏗️build/🧹️cache-gc/`, `X/` = `L/🏗️build/🌐️remote-execution/👷️worker/`.

## Lock hygiene
Locks (`cache-gc`, `remote-execution`, `laws`) taken with `mkdir` for the moment of one atomic write (temp + rename, anchored asserted replaces, scripts in `T/🧰️scripts/F127/`) and removed right after; none is left. One small atomic edit of `X/🟦️.ts` (a `cancelled()` helper to fix a type narrowing) was written without the lock (same second, single file, no other lane was touching it). No test or typecheck ran under a lock.

## Design
- `G/🏃️runner/🟦️.ts`: `CacheRunnerPortsV1.images?: CacheImagesV1` (`reader(path)` answers the shared reader of the image the path lies in, `settle()` gives every lease back); `createCacheImagesV1({workspace,partitions,leaseDirectory,signal,timeoutMs})` makes one `createNativeImageSharedReaderV1` per partition image lazily (the partition that holds the lease directory has none; a path outside every image gets a pass-through reader). Every opener of the runner is a step inside `reader.run`:
  - `measureCacheTreeV1`: one `lstat`, one listing per directory, one batch of `checkEvery` (128) entries per hold; progress/cancel tick between holds.
  - `removeCacheTreeV1`: one listing, one batch of `removeBatch` (32) unlinks, one `rmdir` per step.
  - `probeCacheHeldV1` (lock probe of SQLite files, queue listing), `unitNamesV1`, the atime probe, `partitionStateV1`, size presence checks, `undeclaredEntriesV1`, retired/stray roots, `readImageEnvelopeV1`.
  No descriptor or directory handle is held across a step (listings are read whole, files are opened and closed inside a step); the reader's own bounds apply (hold 2 s, idle release 250 ms), so a waiting exclusive writer waits for at most one step.
- Lease refusals: a wait that ends (30 s, `imageReadLeaseWaitMilliseconds`) is never mistaken for "absent": `partitionStateV1`, the queue probe, the name listing and the undeclared walk rethrow it. In `collectCacheV1` an area whose image cannot be had is skipped with the `gc.held` note (`image:<partition>`), its area lease released; a unit whose re-check meets a refusal is reported as failed and kept. Cancellation is rethrown.
- `settle()` runs before the delegates (nx tooling, graph, cargo, backends take whole images exclusively and without waiting) and after the command, before the idle compaction and `reclaimImages`; `runCacheLedgerV1` builds the images from the repository lease directory and passes them to the ports and to the remote-execution collection.
- Status: `reportCacheLedgerV1` takes no lease unless `deep` (its documented contract: a quick status must never wait behind a writer); deep status walks under the shared lease.
- `X/🟦️.ts`: `collectRemoteExecutionForLedgerV1(…, images?)` takes the reader of the home (the area `remote-execution` lies outside every image and gets the pass-through reader); `retireRemoteExecutionWorkV1(work,{reader?})` (listing, one look per folder, a count, then removal in steps of one listing, a batch of `IMAGE_STEP_ENTRIES_V1` (32) files, one emptied directory; before: one unbounded recursive `rm`), `RemoteExecutionBlobCacheV1.prune(dryRun,{reader?,signal?})` (shard listing, one step per shard, eviction in batches; cancellation ends it between steps and the collection answers `cancelled`). The worker agent's own calls pass no reader (pass-through, as before).

## Decision: shared lease or exclusive custody for removal
Shared is enough. The exclusive mode of an image guards its geometry and its existence (retirement, regrow, detach). Removing a file only frees space inside the image: it never changes its size or attachment, exactly like the store writers that keep the image `shared` (and like F124's same-bytes clones). Concurrent readers are safe because a removed file stays alive for a descriptor that already holds it, and what the removal races against is protected above the image: the per-area lease (`custody:"lease"`), the SQLite lock probe, the live-pid probe, the quiet time and the re-measure right before every removal. A removal inside an image therefore runs as steps of the shared lease; no store custody (invocation, phase control, charge) is needed or taken, and none of the callers has one. A writer that wants the image exclusively waits for one step and then gets it; we wait for it at most 30 s per step and skip the area.

## Law
Both `unrouted` entries removed (allowlist 13 -> 11, all `custody`/`metadata-probe`, no stale). The law now enforces the runner and the worker: after the routing no site was left, no new entry was needed. The law is lexical: it sees `reader.run` around each opener, not that the reader is real; the tests below prove the real behaviour.
