Status 18:20: analysed and staged during the quiet window; landing after 19:00 under lock `native-build`.

### 1. Where and why
- Thrown only in `🏗️native-build/💾️storage/🟦️.ts` `retireNativeStorageChildChargeV1` (child funding retirement, ~line 162): it `lstat`s `storage-events.jsonl` (bigint), opens it `O_WRONLY|O_APPEND`, `fstat`s the descriptor and requires `nlink==1` and an identical `dev:ino:size:mtimeNs:ctimeNs` stamp; any difference throws that message. It is thrown after the (exclusive) cleanup writer lease is held, so it is not a missing or inherited descriptor: the descriptor was opened and valid, its metadata differed from the name's a few awaits earlier.
- Ruled out: child inheriting no descriptor (no inheritance involved, the fd is opened inside the function), closed or reused fd (opened, stat'ed, closed by `withArtifactDescriptorV1`), collector rotation (the only rename of the journal is `checkpoint()`, which also holds the exclusive lease).
- Remaining causes, all of the same class: something changed the journal between the `lstat` and the descriptor's `fstat` (the descriptor admission queue can delay the open by seconds under load; the log shows 16-19 s of lease waits on `nx:graph-namespace` and `nx:graph-capacity`), a sibling's retirement append (size/mtime/ctime) or a metadata-only touch (ctime/mtime change with identical bytes: xattr, utimes, chmod, indexers). The stamp compared the volatile mtime and ctime, so a metadata-only touch or a sibling append hard-failed the whole partition owner (`scope.failed=true`) though the bytes were fine or merely newer. Which of the two fired on m12 is NOT provable: the log has only the final message.
### 2. Fix (staged)
- New member `💾️storage/📜️journal/🧷️append/{🟦️.ts,🧬️schema/🔣️.json,🧪️tests/🟦️.ts}`: `appendObservedChildFundingJournalV1` (identity = dev, ino, nlink, size, plus the prefix bytes read back through the very descriptor that appends, opened `O_RDWR|O_APPEND|O_NOFOLLOW`; mtime/ctime dropped from the identity) answers `changed` instead of throwing when a sibling got in; `retireChildFundingJournalV1` re-observes the journal, recomputes the suffix and retries, bounded to 4 attempts, then a typed bilingual `ChildFundingJournalRefused` (`kind: changed|notOriginal|noProgress`, `texts{en,de}`, schema-first messages).
- `💾️storage/🟦️.ts`: the stamp block replaced by one `retireChildFundingJournalV1(...)` call, final check uses `retired.observed+retired.suffix`, unused `open` import dropped. Script `🧰️scripts/F32/patch.py` (anchored, asserts one occurrence; run with `--apply`).
### 3. Auto-reopen of a failed partition: decided NO
A failed owner may carry an unresolved cleanup writer (`unresolvedCleanupWriters` retains exclusion by design after a terminal failure) and a funded child whose charge is unresolved; re-opening inside the same command would race the very state that failed, and the E57 orphan retirement already recovers it at the next birth, i.e. when the supervisor reruns the stage (the m12 failure was followed by exactly that rerun). F22's typed `failed` refusal already names the first failure and says to close and reopen. The retry above removes the observed trigger without weakening the poison rule.
