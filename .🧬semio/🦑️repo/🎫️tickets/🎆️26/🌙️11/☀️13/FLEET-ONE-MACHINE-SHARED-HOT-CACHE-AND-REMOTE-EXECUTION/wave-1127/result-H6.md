## Addendum round 5 (03:10): per-unit front-end threads policy LANDED
- Policy (schema-first): `🗃️unit-cache/🧬️schema/🔣️.json` `Policy.threads` = `{constrained: 1}` with the rule in its description. Decision (`🧵️threads/🟦️.ts`, pure): a compile unit (has `--crate-name`, `--out-dir` and an unstable `-Z` flag) gets `-Z threads=1` when its peak (max of the admission carving measurement and the learned peak `readUnitPeakV1`) is above the library budget of the admission policy (1536 MB), reason `peak`; or when the host warden's published state is not `calm` or available memory is below its guard line, reason `host-memory`; otherwise nothing (the rustflags `threads=0` stay). Peak wins over host as the reason; unknown peak or no warden state decide nothing.
