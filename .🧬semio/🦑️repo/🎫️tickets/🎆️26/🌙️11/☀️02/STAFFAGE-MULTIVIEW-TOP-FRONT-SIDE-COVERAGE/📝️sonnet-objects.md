## Audit method

- `T/🛠️scripts/objects/audit.ts` evaluates every pose and every clip sample (0, 0.2 ... 0.999 of the duration) of every authored band and compares the elevation, front and plan extents: elevation y against front y, elevation x against plan x, front x against plan y, with the tolerance `max(2 cm, 3 % of the largest dimension)`.
- It also reports, per bone that moves, whether the shapes of that bone move in the other views, and where each emitter anchor lands in each view (`summary.ts` prints it).
- `T/🛠️scripts/objects/validate.ts` runs the catalog `validateAsset` for the 27 assets (extents against `dimensions` per band and view, symbol views, axis rule, ranges).
- Proof SVG and PNG sheets (side, front and top of the same pose, 1:50 and 1:100, rest pose plus extremes) are in `T/🗑️generated/objects/proof/` and `.../d50/`.
- Handedness check: left is +z, so in the front view the left of the asset is on paper right and in plan up the page. Bookshelf, sofa and park bench carry the back on +z and the seat or open front towards -z in all bands; symbols follow the same side.

Manifest rows for these three 500 bands must flip to authored (haiku-registry).
3. **Symbols, 11 in 9 assets, now have elevation, front and plan** (true-extent in model metres at real size and in the frame of the asset, nominal and true-position signs in paper millimetres with a conventional-symbol note, en and de): bicycle-stand `plan-true`, `plan-mark`; retractable-bollard `plan-true`, `plan-dot` (static, bound to the root bone `sleeve`); bar-stool `stool-plan`; bookshelf `shelf-plan`; dining-table `table-plan`; folding-deck-chair `chair-plan`; picnic-table `picnic-plan`; single-bed `bed-plan`; three-seat-sofa `sofa-plan`. The measured extents of every true-extent symbol (symcheck.ts) equal the dimensions within the rule max(2 %, 15 mm). Symbol ids are kept, although most now carry three views (registry rows stay valid).
4. **Articulated parts that did not follow the pose**: cafe-chair plan legs (now followTip capsules from foot to top at 1:50 ... 1:500, the 1:50 band had the endpoints reversed), cafe-chair back (back panel solid at 1:500, followTip posts at 1:100), cafe-chair rear legs in the 1:500 front, picnic-table 1:500 benches (closed thin profiles so the stowed bench has a plan and front), charcoal-grill vent dial (front extruded solid, so the tilted dial follows), charcoal-grill 1:500 front lid handle (open lid), charcoal-grill lid revolve axis (D21).
5. **Extents against `dimensions`** fixed in the drawing: cafe-chair, waste-bin, playground-swing, retractable-bollard, charcoal-grill, bicycle-stand 1:500 (printing widening removed), office-swivel-chair, operating-table, park-bench 1:200 (rear post height).
6. **valid-range gaps**: 21 band ranges made contiguous (20-75, 75-150, 150-350, 350-1000).
7. **Emitters**: charcoal-grill `lid-vent-flue` `directionDeg` 90 to 0 (bone-relative, the vent bone points up). All other emitters of the batch resolve upwards (emit.ts).
