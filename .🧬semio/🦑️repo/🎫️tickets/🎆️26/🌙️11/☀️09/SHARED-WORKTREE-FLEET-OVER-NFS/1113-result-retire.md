## Rule

- Record `origins/<origin>/retired.json`: `{version:1,originId,by,hlc,wallMs,reason,retired}`, atomic tmp + fsync + rename (`replaceOriginFileV1`). One field beyond the brief: `retired` (boolean). `retire --undo` writes a newer record with `retired:false` instead of deleting the file, because a deletion cannot travel through the relay; of two records the newer `hlc` wins. `by` is the origin of the machine that ran the command. Writing into another origin's directory is the one documented exception to "own origin directory only" (README, contract, docstring of `writeRetirementV1`).
- Verdict `retired` in `fleet`: not compared, not required fresh or settled, and the other origins' cursors are judged against the heads of non-retired origins only (they keep following its journal as before). `ok` needs no non-ok row and at least one `ok` row; a store with only retired origins is not ok.
- `retire <origin> [--reason t] [--force]`, `retire <origin> --undo`. Refused (exit 1): own origin, unknown origin, unreachable store, origin attested within 60 s unless `--force`. Missing origin argument or `--undo` with `--force`/`--reason` is a usage error. Already retired / not retired is a message with exit 0. All texts en and de.
- `status`: retired origins are marked in the lag list (lag not measured); on the retired machine itself `status` (and `status --json` `retired:{by,reason,wallMs}`) says who retired it.
- Relay: `relayOnceV1` copies `retired.json` of every origin of the peer store (homed there or not) when its `hlc` is newer than the own record. Compaction does not touch it (tested).
- Daemon of a retired origin: one `retired` event, publishes and applies nothing, keeps attesting with `sync:false`, re-checks the record each round; after `--undo` it emits `sync`/`on` and does a full scan.
