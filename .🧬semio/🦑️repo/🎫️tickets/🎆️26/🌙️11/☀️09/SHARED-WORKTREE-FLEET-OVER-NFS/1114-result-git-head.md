## Fix (landed 02:58, three-way with live == base, full module green on the live tree)

- `🔁️replica/🟦️.ts`: `unpublishedPacks`; companions (`.idx .rev .mtimes .bitmap .promisor`) of a pack and the `multi-pack-index` are held (retry, `blocked pack-unpublished`) until the `.pack` is published; every delete under `.git/objects` is held while a `.pack` on disk is unpublished or an objects path failed in the round. `packsComplete`: a receiver applies deletes under `.git/objects` only after all creations and only when no object put is queued and each fleet `.idx` has its applied `.pack` (`blocked pack-incomplete`). `healGitV1`: disk against registers for `HEAD`, `packed-refs`, `shallow`, `refs/**`, `objects/**` (existence; size for pack files; content of small ref files when disk equals what was applied; local unpublished changes untouched). `runV1` `watchGit`: every 30 s (first round at start) `gitBrokenV1`, note `git-broken-<reason>`, heal at each finding and every 10 min, restore a missing `.git/index` through `publishIndexV1` (existing `restoreStage`).
- `🌿️git/🟦️.ts`: `gitBrokenV1` (`head-unreadable`, `config-unreadable`, `head-unresolvable`, `tip-missing`, `index-missing`).
- `🩺️attest/🟦️.ts` + schema + texts EN/DE: a fresh origin with a `git-broken-*` failure, or `head: null` with a tree while another fresh origin has a head (settled or not), is `diverged` in `head.commit` and warned `git-broken`.
- Tests: `🔁️replica/🧪️tests/🩹️heal.ts` (5; the two repack tests fail without the fix, verified), a judge test in `🩺️attest/🧪️tests/🟦️.ts`.
- Not done: a machine does not verify with `git cat-file` that an apply keeps HEAD resolvable (the index-pair rule above stands in for it); chunked/resumable large puts and a guard for a large put deferred for long are not implemented (the deletes are held, the dependents `blocked` events are the signal, and `git-broken` raises after the fact).
