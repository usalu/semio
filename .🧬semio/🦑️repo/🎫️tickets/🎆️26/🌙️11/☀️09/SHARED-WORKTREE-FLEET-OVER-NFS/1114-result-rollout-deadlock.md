## Checkpoint/apply fix (2)
`packsComplete`: a pack index whose pack the manifest does not hold as a put and that is older than 10 min is an orphan and holds nothing back. Agrees with W's consolidation (`1114-result-git-bloat.md`: pack is published before its companions and the idx last; deletes after all creations): an idx without its pack for 10 min cannot be an in-flight consolidation. Apply back-off: while a pass is blocked on pack-incomplete and the queue is unchanged the next pass waits 0.5 s, doubling to 30 s; a queue change runs it at once.
