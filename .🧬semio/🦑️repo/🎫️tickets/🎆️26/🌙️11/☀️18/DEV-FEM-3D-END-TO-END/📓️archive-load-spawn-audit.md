# Archive Load and Spawn Audit

Read-only source trace for the fresh 6099 observation. No browser or build was run in this audit.

`Document load: Loading document: 118 of 118` is the history projection of `ingest.live_document_load()` and `ActiveDocumentArchiveLoad::status` (`plugin/🧵️instance/🖼️render/📥️staging/📜️history-view/🦀️.rs:473`). Its counters are archive `completed + fold.0` and `total + fold.1` (`plugin/🗃️document/🗄️document-archive-load/🦀️.rs:311`); equality does not denote a terminal acknowledgment. The active archive can be awaiting replacement, its acknowledgment, or its own source/owner retirement. In `AwaitReplacement` it polls the replacement, acknowledges it only after a terminal poll, and requests an archive terminal target. Terminal cleanup then retires retained inputs before setting archive state Terminal (`plugin/🧵️instance/📄️docs/📥️staging/🪜️maintenance-ladder/🦀️.rs:172,469`).

`pending_effects` refreshes and reads the currently committed store, runs FEM preparation and obtains `store.snapshot_read()` once ready, with no archive-loading guard in that method (`plugin/🧵️instance/🖼️render/📥️staging/🪟️projections/🦀️.rs:137`). FEM transfers this read to its mounted state before emitting `SpawnJob`; the spawn log proves effect creation, not job admission. MountedState retains the read until its close lane 5 (`fem/…/✏️editor/🧵️session/💼️mounted-state/🦀️.rs:214`).

Replacement commits the candidate by swapping it with the current Store; it then retains and closes the displaced Store in `RetiringCommittedMembers` and `RetiringCommittedStore` before reaching complete (`plugin/🪜️maintenance-ladder/🦀️.rs:490,571–602`). An outstanding snapshot read is observable through Store's `outstanding_snapshot_read_count`; the Store disposer has a returned-read phase. Thus a job spawned against the pre-swap Store can plausibly extend that Store's retirement until FEM returns its snapshot. This is a conditional ownership conflict, not proof of the observed stall: a spawn after the swap reads the new Store, and equal counters alone do not identify replacement's state.

To discriminate, capture the spawned job's revision/generation and actual Store issuer relative to the replacement swap; archive phase and terminal target; replacement state, close phase and outstanding/returned reads; FEM current job state and close lane; and exact poll/ACK sequence. Check whether guest turns continue to schedule the replacement and FEM worker/retirement. The WIT trace and owner witnesses are needed before claiming a deadlock.
