## Method

- `authoring-animals-lib.ts` (ticket-local) composes explicit solids per asset script `authoring-animals/<slug>.ts`: `fit` lofts a bone's elevation outline (sagittal half-heights from the 1:50 outline, lateral half-width and offset from the same-id plan or front shape, or set by hand), `tube` (explicit world path), `ell`, `cap`, `stripe`, `shell`, `slice`, `patch` (decals on a trunk loft). Every output is explicit JSON in `volumes/animals/`; none is a dump of `volume-draft`. Scripts are re-runnable (`authoring-animals-run.sh <slug>`: author, check, splice, validate).
- Loft frame follows the kernel as implemented (`rotation [0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5]`, `rx` on v, `ry` on w), see `authoring-requests.md`.
- Validation per asset (`authoring-animals-validate.ts`): Ajv against the Asset schema, `validateAsset` (0 issues, 0 `volume-*`), rest connectivity, every pose and clip at 25, 50 and 90 % evaluated with the staffage `evaluate` (connected components 1 everywhere; lost bbox contacts at moving joints such as jaw, tail, neck and wing are expected). `authoring-animals-sides.ts` checks that near/far (negative/positive z) solids stay on their side in rest and all poses: 0 violations except the fox curl pose noted above. `authoring-animals-render.ts` renders z-buffer previews (rest, gliding, airborne-wings-up) kept in `🗑️generated/authoring-animals/`.
- Flight: wing solids are bound to the wing bones (gull arm/hand, duck wing, pigeon humerus/forearm/hand) and lie in the u-v plane so the abduction about v spreads them sideways; the wing bones' lateral offsets (z = +-0.05 to 0.068) are honoured.
- Hidden upper limbs (sheep, pig, fox; goat humerus) are modelled as tubes or reused outlines on their own bones so every moving bone carries a solid.
- `maxDenominator` is set from the finest scales that still draw the part (eyes, nostrils, pads, teats, patches, mantle, tongue, speculum) and left out for structural parts, because the coarse 2D scales merge limbs into bars rather than dropping them.
