## Gates not met or caveats

- tower-crane: silhouette IoU 0.360 / 0.462 / 0.572 (below 0.80), cause is in the 2D (filled lattice envelopes in elevation and plan, no closed mast shape in the front view); bounding box passes.
- scissor-lift: validateAsset 0 but Ajv reports 994 errors under /volume/solids/*/bone because the rig bone ids `A1..B5` violate the Slug pattern (the rig fails Ajv already); needs a rig rename by the asset owner (note appended to `authoring-requests.md`).
- excavator and forklift fail the bounding-box gate at rest only; both pass in their default pose (judged in the default pose per the coordinator amendment).
- Only the `volume` member of each asset was changed (via `volume-insert.py`). All lofts carry an explicit `rotation`; no `lateralScale` is used; revolve profiles are open polylines (rings are capsule chains with rotational repeat), so no `volume-profile-self-intersecting` finding remains.
- Pose check: only deliberate separations are flagged (evaporative-humidifier drops falling; heat-pump lid opening); poses of every asset keep one connected component and all rod/barrel, hinge and pin joints in contact. Rest-pose linkages of excavator, oil-pumpjack are not closed by the rig itself.
- Other (non-volume) Ajv errors of excavator (276), concrete none, heat-pump 2, expansion-valve 4, solar 1, scroll 1 exist in the assets independent of the volume.
