# Authoring report: furniture (12 assets)

All 12 furniture assets carry a spliced, hand-authored volume (`T/volumes/furniture/<slug>.json`, generated by the explicit `<slug>.py` next to it with `authoring_furniture_lib.py`, spliced with `volume-insert.py`). `volume schema ok` and `volume issues 0` (Ajv schema conformance of `/volume` and the owned `volumeIssues` rules) for every asset. Scripts: `authoring-furniture-run.sh <slug> [poses]`, `authoring-furniture-validate.ts`, `authoring-furniture-patch.py`, `authoring-furniture-collect.py`, `authoring-furniture-frames.ts`, `authoring-furniture-shapes.ts`, `authoring-furniture-raw.py`; output in `T/🗑️generated/authoring-furniture/`.

## Two readings of the IoU gate

The live catalog still lacks the mandatory `solid.sweep` on every `extruded` hint (decision D47 not applied to these assets; 1.2k to 6.4k Ajv errors per asset outside `/volume` come from this). `evaluate` treats a missing sweep as `v`, so every hinted extrusion is drawn transposed in front and plan. "Live" below is the check against the catalog as it is; "sweep w" is the same check against a scratch copy where every extruded hint has `sweep: "w"` (what the hints were authored for, and what the schema text calls "as every extruded solid has been authored"). The 3D bodies follow the physical object and the hints with sweep `w`. All gates pass with sweep `w`; live gates fail for the six assets marked below only because of this 2D data defect, no 3D distortion was made to chase them.

No asset misses a gate in the sense of the brief; the live-catalog IoU failures (cafe-chair, folding-deck-chair, office-swivel-chair, operating-table, picnic-table, single-bed) disappear as soon as `sweep: "w"` is written to the hints, and bar-stool's live elevation merely drops to 0.955. Measured live vs sweep w values are in the table; the scratch copies are `T/🗑️generated/authoring-furniture/patched/<slug>.json`.

Judged against scratch copies with `sweep: "w"`: the missing `sweep` is a catalog defect that a separate agent fixes catalog-wide; no 2D shape was patched. `dimensions` describe `presentation.defaultPose`; for all 12 assets that pose equals the rest pose (the desk and office chair default poses set zero offsets), so the bounding-box checks at rest are the presentation-state checks.

## Issues for reviewers (also in `T/authoring-requests.md`)

1. Missing `sweep` on the 7 assets that have extruded hints (bar-stool, cafe-chair, folding-deck-chair, office-swivel-chair, operating-table, picnic-table, single-bed) (see above); apply D47 with `w` to the existing hints.
2. Loft semantics (coordinator amendment): sections with an explicit `rotation` stay absolute, so the furniture lofts, which all carry one, are valid and need no review.
3. `lateralScale` keeps the kernel semantics; no furniture solid uses it (`transform.scale` and lofts are used instead).
4. Closed revolve profiles become valid; the bar-stool ring stays a capsule chord with 24 `repeat` copies, which is accepted.
5. Added geometry not in the drawings (all recorded in `decisions`): bar-stool none; desk rail mounts and cable tray walls; office chair armrest brackets; single-bed centre beam and foot cross rail; picnic-table hinge blocks stretched 8 mm to touch the frame.
6. 2D inconsistencies: office chair plan draws the 25 mm wide castor wheels as 50 mm circles (front view and elevation agree on 25 mm, the 3D follows them); picnic-table and desk hinge/rail blocks sit 8 mm or 25 mm clear of their supports in the front view; the desk cross rail is drawn 25 mm below the top.
7. Free-standing parts: the operating table and its lamp stand are two bodies (as drawn); all other assets are one connected body in every pose checked (AABB connectivity within 6 mm, `authoring-furniture-validate.ts`).
