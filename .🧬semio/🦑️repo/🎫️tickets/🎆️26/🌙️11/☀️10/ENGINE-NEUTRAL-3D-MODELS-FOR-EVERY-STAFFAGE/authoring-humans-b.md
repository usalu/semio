## Gates

- IoU gate (>= 0.80): met by all 14 assets in all three views (lowest: wheelchair-user elevation 0.906, walking-commuter plan 0.89, tour-guide plan 0.90, standing-phone-user plan 0.90).
- Bounding-box gate at rest: met by 7 assets (running-child, seated-office-worker, seated-reader, sleeping-person, standing-adult, walking-commuter, wheelchair-user). The other 7 miss it at rig rest (pregnant-person, runner-with-blade-prosthesis, seated-guitarist, skateboarder, standing-phone-user, tour-guide, worker-carrying-box) because their `dimensions` describe the default presentation pose, not the rest pose; judged in the default pose (amendment 6) every asset is within 8 % on every axis (largest: standing-adult x +3.7 %, running-child x +3.6 %, runner z +3.0 %, pregnant-person z +2.8 %).
- Validator: 0 `volume-*` issues and Ajv-valid for all 14. `validateAsset` also reports pre-existing non-volume findings on some assets (`lateral-ambiguous` on pregnant-person 6, tour-guide 11, sleeping-person 2), unrelated to the volume.

## Method and notes for a reviewer

- Bodies: every limb, torso, head, hand and shoe is a loft of superelliptic sections whose depth is measured on the elevation outline and whose width and centre come from the front twin (plan twin for near-horizontal bones); rounded joint balls, a shoulder yoke and a pelvis ellipsoid close the joints the front/plan views draw as round parts. Clothing that overlays skin is a 6 to 9 % larger loft. Props are authored from the drawings with the primitive that fits: revolves (wheels, tyres, helmet, cap, cup, stool seat, column), extrudes (guitar body, bench, sling, skirts), boxes (desk, laptop, box, footplate), capsules (tubes, spokes, straps), lofts (deck, blade, visor, chair arms), `repeat` for pairs and the 12 spokes.
- Every loft section carries an explicit `rotation` quaternion equal to the staffage path-following rotation (amendment 2), so the data renders the same under `volume-check`, the staffage conversion and the kernel change. `lateralScale` is used only on revolves about the bone axis (cap, helmet).
- Common 2D inconsistencies resolved the same way: front draws the torso as wide as the sleeves reach (0.36 to 0.43 m) while the plan draws the trunk 0.25 m; torso lofts are narrowed to 76 to 90 % of the front width and the plan shoulder ellipse is modelled separately. Eye/ear/nose are mostly view-only: modelled as small ellipsoids at 1:50 (`maxDenominator`), heads clipped in front of the nose where the side outline includes it.
- Not modelled (drawing texture or unmeasurable): fingers, laces, cuffs/ribs, lapels, bench slats, fold lines, castor spokes, heel loop.
- Tool findings: `volume-lib.meshes` ignores the loft path-following rotation (see `authoring-requests.md`, worked around with explicit rotations). The torso-width and bbox-at-rest reading above should be considered when a catalog-wide gate is built (use `presentation.defaultPose`).
- Review pointers: sleeping-person hidden body (invented), runner blade centre points, seated-guitarist guitar orientation (drawn face-on in the side view), tour-guide/worker shared template (no neck bone), wheelchair castor fork lateral position (front only draws the tyre).
