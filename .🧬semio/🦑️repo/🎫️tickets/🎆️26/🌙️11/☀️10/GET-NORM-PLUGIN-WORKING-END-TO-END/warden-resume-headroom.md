# Warden resume headroom

The 16 GiB host policy enters recovery at 2,457 MiB available and treats swap traffic below 16 MiB/s as quiet when the other measured floors hold. The reported 2,863 MiB available and 3.75 MiB/s therefore permit a quiet recovery action despite an OS warning. Calm recovery also admitted a paused process from host totals alone. The overdue path did the same and could emit several resumes in the same tick.

The 5,600 MiB slice alarm reports its charged footprint. The alarm stores neither the raw RSS nor the stopped tree's nonresident portion. The charged footprint alone cannot establish how much had to be paged in. Reserving all 5,600 MiB again would count resident pages against memory already occupied.

The resume decision now budgets additional nonresident memory above the physical guard floor after the recovery line is reached. Linux reads proportional swapped charge from the same page-table sample as total process charge and includes stopped processes below the normal inspection threshold. macOS estimates charge outside RSS from the positive difference between the kernel footprint and RSS. The loop sums the estimate for stopped members of the actual signal scope. An incomplete observation postpones recovery under warning or active pressure. When calm, the decision charges the entire available footprint of the stopped signal scope. A tick shares its headroom across all resumes and can select a smaller eligible subject.

At the existing overdue limit, a cancellable tree without enough recovery room receives a typed cancellation. The warden only initiates suspensions for work it can also cancel. A noncancellable tree that was already externally stopped remains paused until it can resume within the floor. Cancellation sends SIGKILL directly or queues SIGTERM before continuing a stopped process.

The fixture uses the alarm's reported available memory, swap-traffic rate and total charged footprint. It holds the guard floor at 1,638 MiB and exercises hypothetical additional charges of 1,225 MiB and 1,226 MiB against the 1,225 MiB available above that floor. It also covers already-resident memory, calm recovery, overdue cancellation below the recovery floor, renewed headroom, competing overdue resumes, incomplete readings and noncancellable work. SQLite independently reproduces the fixture results. The old TypeScript decision resumed the first unfunded case.

The macOS estimate depends on available kernel accounting and can miss pages masked by resident mappings. The calm fallback uses the observed stopped scope charge, which can itself be incomplete if the OS refuses a reading. Linux depends on readable `smaps_rollup`. Windows remains observe-only. The recorded swap rate measures traffic in both directions. The historical alarm cannot establish the affected slice's nonresident charge or attribute the observed fleet pressure solely to its resume.
