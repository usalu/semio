## Operating notes

- **Build one component:**
  ```
  ONLY_COMPONENTS=<component> STEP_TIMEOUT=20000 bash <ticket>/play-standards-native.sh component
  ```
  - Success is judged by the mtime of the staged `.cache/b/p/m/browser/prototype/modules/<dir>/*.core.wasm`.
