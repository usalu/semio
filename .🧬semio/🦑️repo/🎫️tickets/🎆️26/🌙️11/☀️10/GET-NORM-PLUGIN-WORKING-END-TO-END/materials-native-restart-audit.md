`coord-materials-bounded-fleet-retry.log` records the first failure at line 18966. The Original Cargo workspace check detected a length change in `🌎️hub/Cargo.toml`. The graph-free `native-exec.ts` parent forwarded 75 directly. The log ended at the restart announcement and the process check found no surviving Materials or `native-exec` child.

The graph-free launcher now owns retries through `runWithStaleReexecV1`. It detects the reserved cooperative status from its owned child, reaps that child and uses the repository's bounded restart policy to launch a fresh Original Script envelope. The launcher holds the same live task reservation across attempts. It propagates ordinary exits and signals. Cancellation terminates and reaps the active child and prevents a further launch. At the retry limit the source-edit handler reports the concrete edited path and exits without announcing or waiting for an additional retry.

Before the fix, tests for one retry and the six-replacement bound both failed against the one-child launcher. After the fix, the launcher law passed all 10 cases, including a typed source edit, repeated cooperative exits, ordinary status 17, cancellation during initial and replacement children, and child-envelope and reservation checks. The repo format-stamp suite passed all 17 cases, including exact terminal text and zero extra delay at the retry bound. These checks used `repo test-file` with real lightweight subprocesses.
