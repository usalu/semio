### The flow is in the model document

New module `S/🔨️modules/🧊️volume/🌊️flow/🟦️.ts` (`flowParts`, `tubeStations`, `tubesOf`, `flowUnitOf`, `excessBinOf`, `excessOfBin`, `FLOW_MODEL`, `FLOW_TAG`; the 14 s are the depiction's `FLOW_DEVELOP_S`), wired into `modelOf` and `modelOfAsset`:

- Field: `developTransport` with `flow: true` in the environment of the thermal colours, 14 s of history before the static instant (whole periods of a looping clip), the request of the drawings (`transportOf`), so the tubes lie on the very streams the drawings draw. Developed once per document, slice by slice; progress and cancellation go through the `Operation` of the model build (cancelling throws the kernel `CancelledError`).
- Per scale scene `s<scale>-thermal`: `flowMarks(flow, flow.timeS, flowDetailOf(scale))`.
  - Stream tubes: kernel `loft` per run of equal temperature-excess bin, round sections on stream points (Ramer–Douglas–Peucker at 0.08 mm of paper), radius half the paper width of the local speed, at most 0.012 of the largest extent; cut like a pipe elbow where the kernel's `loftFault` says the stream bends tighter than the tube is wide (found by the reference walker in cold air: `loft-fold` warnings).
  - Arrow heads: `revolve` cones, tip on the stream point, axis along the flow, paper size 1.1 × 0.75 mm with the same cap.
  - Vapour and fog puffs: translucent `polyhedron` icospheres (80 triangles, 320 from 2 mm paper radius). A kernel ellipsoid at `qualityOf` costs 600 to 4 000 triangles per puff (94 puffs at 1:50 for the standing figure), so the puffs are not ellipsoids.
  - Nodes: `s<scale>-thermal-flow` (root) with children `…-flow-stream-<index of the stream in the field>`, `…-flow-vapour`, `…-flow-fog`; tags `glyph` and `flow` on nodes and solids.
  - Materials: `flow-excess-<bin>` (opaque, unlit), `flow-vapour-<bin>`, `flow-fog-<bin>` with the colours of `flowPaint`; base transparent, bound by the `thermal` variant only. `edgeMaterialOf` gives the feature edges of flow solids the material of their solid, so they show and hide with it.
  - Other poses of a document hide the flow root; a clip carries the flow of its first instant as static geometry (documented in the module, the FBX writer and the README).
- GLB, FBX, model JSON, ZIP and batch need no code of their own: they are made from this document. The batch resume key digests the module tree, so existing sets are rewritten.

### The web stage shows the flow once

- Rule: the stage keeps its live animated overlay; its model is always built with `flowGeometry: false` (new option of `SharedModelOptions`, replaces `emitterMarkers`), which also saves developing the flow. The downloads (`🧊️spatial/📤️exports`) keep the default and carry the flow.
- `S/🎯️targets/⚛️react/🔨️modules/🧊️spatial/🏭️prepare/🟦️.ts`: `modelOptionsOf` sets `flowGeometry: false`; `analyticOptionsOf` no longer reads `flow`.
- `S/🎯️targets/⚛️react/🔨️modules/🧊️spatial/🟦️.ts`: `modelKey` no longer depends on the flow layers (toggling a layer does not rebuild the model).
- `#projection`, the overlay, the viewer and routing files are untouched.

### Package index

## Not done, and limits

- A clip carries the flow of its first instant as static geometry. It is placed on the figure of that instant (the field of a walking clip is developed 14 cycles ahead and carried back by the travel of the rig root), but it does not follow the figure while the clip plays: a field per frame would be a mesh per frame, which glTF and FBX do not animate cleanly. Tracers and parcel paths are motion and are not in the files.
- A non-looping clip is developed like the drawings: the instant moves on by 14 s, so the field is that of the clip's state 14 s later, carried back by the root travel.
- Stream tubes are opaque; the opacity by speed of the paper view is not used in 3D (speed is the tube width).
- The marks are not thinned by an eye: the model has no fixed camera, so streams behind the body are in the file (the `visible` and `puffVisible` of `flowMarks` are for the drawings).
- The temperature-excess bin rule (`bin`, `binColour`) is two private lines of `flowView`; `🧊️volume/🌊️flow` repeats them as `excessBinOf` and `excessOfBin`, because `🎨️depiction/🌊️flow-view` belongs to another package. Exporting them there and importing them here would remove the repetition.
- Observed, not fixed (not this package's file): `transportOf` in `🎨️depiction/🟦️.ts` solves the field at the developed time while the drawing shows the state of the query time. For the walking clip of the sample figure the developed field lies at 12.5 to 15.7 m ahead while the figure of t = 0 spans −0.25 to 0.47 m (`model-flow/clip-probe.ts` before the fix). The model carries the field back by the root travel; whether the drawings and the stage overlay of clips with root motion need the same is for the owner of `transportOf` to check.
- Batch: every pose GLB and every thermal FBX now develops a flow (about 3 to 8 s each on this host under load); existing sets are rewritten once because the producer digest changed.
- Not run: `🧊️model-catalog` and `🚀️export-performance` (whole catalog, hours on this host; the catalog is being re-authored by another session). The geometry audit of `model-catalog` (`auditGeometry`: watertight, no degenerate triangle, triangles falling with the scale) is run on the sample figure inside `🌊️flow-model` instead.
- `S/README.md`, cast row `transport` ("without emitter markers") belongs to the retired transport layers of another package and was left.

- To do first when the dependencies are back: the type checks and the suites listed under "Not verified"; if `model-digests` differs, run `model-flow/regenerate-digests.ts` again (a sibling may have changed the solver since 15:24).
