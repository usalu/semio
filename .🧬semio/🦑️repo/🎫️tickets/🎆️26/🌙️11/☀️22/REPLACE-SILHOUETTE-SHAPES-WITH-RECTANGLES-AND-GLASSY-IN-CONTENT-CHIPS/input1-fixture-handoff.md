## Cluster (c): `retained_pane_actions_address_the_concrete_window_before_guest_admission` - fixed (product)
- Root cause: `ActionsCtx::dispatch_action` builds `live_view_state` (a `ViewModel` whose `active_utility_by_window_id` / `tool_run_trace_cursor_by_window_id` are freshly collected `OrderedMap`s) and dropped it on every exit without retiring them; `OrderedMap::drop` asserts the root was retired. ## Cluster (a): 9 "fixture close never finishes" tests - fixed in layers, last layer unverified
Tests: `the_topmost_retained_world_owns_...`, `captured_world_movement_and_wheel_...`, `cancelled_world_capture_...`, `a_modal_layer_blocks_...`, `a_scene_refresh_preserves_...`, `a_replacement_scene_cannot_inherit_...`, `another_pointers_cancellation_...`, `another_pointers_chrome_release_...`, `replaced_scene_pointer_slots_release_...`.

Root causes, in the order they were uncovered:
1. The paint fixtures finished their caller (and so closed the engine surfaces with `FixtureTurnV1::close`) at the end of every paint call, while the test still held the documents and the shell still owned the World3d states. The surface close then spun forever on `close_retired_scene_surface_one` (the retired world scene needs the shell to retire its owner first and the host to serve the component close request).
2. The caller's Source cannot retire while engine surfaces it birthed are mounted, so the paint fixture cannot simply skip closing either; the closing must be done by the same turn that mounted the surface, after the test retired its documents and worlds.
3. `SurfaceState::advance_world3d_retirement_step` ran on a standalone ownerless `StepContext`; a superseded scene bridge close (`step_world3d_scene_bridge_close`) panics there with `original caller has no parent source supplier`.
4. The world close request must be served by the host lane (`os_host::finish_world_fixture_component_close`), after every close turn and, when a repaint replaces a mounted world, after every paint turn.

Changes:
- `🧪️tests/🎟️original-turn/🦀️.rs` (`FixtureTurnV1`): additive `wide(grant, generation)`, `disown_surface`, `finish_with(host)` (close runs `host` after every close turn); `close` delegates to `close_with`.
- `🎯️targets/🧊️wgpu/🧰️fixtures/🐚️shell/🦀️.rs`: new `PointerFixtureTurns` (one turn per document generation, kept alive across repaints; a surface is owned by the newest turn that painted it; `serving_world_closes(fn)`, `retire_shell_worlds(shell)`, `finish()`, `finish_with(host)`); new `paint_tree_pointer_documents_in` and `paint_component_pointer_documents_in`; the old `paint_*_pointer_documents` are wrappers that finish their own turns (behaviour for non-world callers unchanged). `retained_world_sequence_probe` keeps its signature (identity host) and gains `retained_world_sequence_probe_closing(scenario, close_world)`; the probe retires worlds through the fixture turn, closes its documents, then finishes the turns with the host lane.
- `SurfaceState` (`🧱️elements/🐚️Shell/🎯️targets/🧊️wgpu/📥️staging/🫧️surfaces/🖼️chrome-frame-and-main-window/🦀️.rs`): additive `advance_world3d_retirement_step_in(context)`; the old method builds its ownerless context and delegates. The production caller `AppPresenter::close_world3d_dynamic_step` (`🎞️present-state-and-engine-closers`) now passes its original `cx` (this is a product fix of root cause 3, which would also panic in the real frame loop).
- Tests edited (all in `🧱️elements/🐚️Shell/🧪️tests/🔬️wgpu-shell-input/`): `🌍️retained-world-capture` (the 8 probe callers now use `retained_world_sequence_probe_closing(.., os_host::finish_world_fixture_component_close)`; `replaced_scene_pointer_slots_...` uses `PointerFixtureTurns`), the aggregator `🦀️.rs` import list.
