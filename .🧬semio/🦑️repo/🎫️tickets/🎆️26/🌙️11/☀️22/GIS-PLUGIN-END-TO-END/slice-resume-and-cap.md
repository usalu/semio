# Slice resume, per-plan cap and one service generation

Scope: `repo run @semio-tech/framework-os-dev:dev-s-react-prototype` planning, 01:32 to 03:50 (log `🗑️generated/browser-s-react-3.log`), the four `coalesce-dry-*.log` runs and the service logs `/var/folders/*/T/semio-repo/501/host-*.log`, warden state `~/Library/Caches/semio/host-capacity/warden`.

## 1. Restart from zero: owner checks are never persisted

Findings
- The owner checkpoint (`📥️load/💾️owner-checkpoint`, `flushMilliseconds` 15000) is wired and works: it stores only what `persist` is called for, and `persist` runs only after a fresh discovery. An owner answered from the memo or the checkpoint and then verified (`hold`) is not stored anywhere.
- The 1665 of about 1700 "owner checks" are such verifications (rows of seeded memo or checkpoint owners re-probed, `completedOwnerChecks`), not discoveries. They live in the worker's memory (`trust` set). The journal attestation (`<scope>.verified.json`) that lets a later read skip probes is written only after a finished memo check or publish, and its predicate is built from the rows of an existing memo. A killed worker therefore left nothing: the next one re-probed all 1700 owners from zero. Checkpoint packs of the dev-s-react-prototype scope (`.cache/b/g/m/inference/selections/1557b954.../oc`, 132 packs) hold only 1 to 6 records per pack for the 01:00 to 02:55 window, which matches.
- A second, separate cause of a cold restart: the admission format changed between 02:55 (`a44c6c80`) and 03:15 (`7c380cb1`) because another agent edited a file the format hashes; every pack and memo of the old format became unreadable (packs 0..7 of the same ring are the new format, 8..131 the old one).

Fix (schema-first, same trust model as the memo journal)
- New `📥️load/🪪️owner-vouch` (`🟦️.ts`, `🧬️schema/🔣️.json`): a ledger `<scope>.vouched.json` (key = scope + admission format, journal cursor taken before the first probe, owner digests, link state) written while the read runs (at most every 15 s) and removed after the admission memo is published. A restarted worker asks the journal once for the changes since the ledger cursor and skips the probe of every path row of a vouched owner that no change touched at, above or below its path; non-path rows, a foreign key, an epoch gap, a refusal or a symbolic-link directory vouch nothing.
- Wiring in `📥️load/🟦️.ts`: opened on the cold path next to the checkpoint; `hold` of every owner uses the vouch predicate and records the owner once `hold` succeeded; cleared after `writeAdmissionMemoV1`.
- Test `🪪️owner-vouch/🧪️tests/🟦️.ts` (3 pass): scenario rows in `🔣️.json` checked against an independent CPython model of the touched-path law; a kill after a flush loses at most the unflushed owners; foreign key, refusal and clear.
- Not fixed here: a format edit mid-run still makes the old packs unreadable by design (the format is a content hash).

## 2. Per-plan cap and its message

## 3. Two resident generations ran two queues

Cause: a superseded generation only drains: it keeps its whole slice pool and every queued load running until idle or `drainStallMilliseconds` (1,800,000 ms) without progress, while the newer generation accepts new plans with its own pool, its own gate queue and its own workers (`c7208dac666a` and `241c2e22607a`, each with its own `[capacity]` queue); coalescing does not cross processes, so both read overlapping closures (6 GB bookings each) on a 16 GB host.

Fix
- Service (`🏛️service/🌐️host/🟨️.mjs`): on supersession and on every drain `handOverQueued` aborts the governor-queued jobs and calls the pool's `handover`; their clients get the typed refusal `handover` (retry later) and ask the newer generation (new catch branch for `error.code==="handover"`). The descriptor now carries `busy` (workers running or queued, updated by the sweep); exported `olderBusyServiceV1`.
- Pool (`👷️slice-pool/🟨️.mjs`): `handover(error)` aborts every load whose worker has not started and refuses to start another worker (a restart included) in this generation; a running worker finishes. New option `predecessor`: before a worker is booked the pool waits (progress line `host-slice-owners-predecessor`, en/de) while an older generation's descriptor says `busy` and its process lives, so one generation at a time reads the graph. Wired in `⌨️command/🌐️host/🟦️.ts` and `runHostForegroundV1` (`workerHandover`).
- Test `🏡️resident/🧪️tests/🥇️generation-handover` (3 pass): predecessor rows against an SQL oracle, handover of a queued load plus a restart, wait for the predecessor then spawn. Needs a resident restart to take effect (not done: other lanes run on the services).
- Resident tests green: supersession-drain 5, handover 2, restart 8, slice-priority, plan-guard 13, reload 12.

## Standing grant for the read, and activation (12:4x to 13:0x)

What the waits were: "waiting for a host capacity decision on the N MB request" (321, 749, 1018, 2211 MB ...) is the slice pool's own reservation message. In the `excl-*` logs these are the in-place growth asks of the running worker (`grow`: 125 % of the tree minus the grant, at least 256 MB), during which the pool holds the worker stopped (SIGSTOP) until the gate answers. Discovery-farm children are not separate gate requests: they run inside the worker's process tree and its grant (`grantedPoolV1`), and the warden's `slice-worker` subject is the whole tree (`scope: tree`), so the children are already covered by the worker's claim (megabytes of the tree against `grantMegabytes`); with the claim refresh (every 60 s, window 600 s) that cover no longer expires.

Implemented (schema-first, with test)
- `🏡️resident/🧬️schema/🔣️.json` `sliceGrantGrowth.standingBasisPoints` = 11500: the grant of a read stands at its measured peak. The first growth of a worker asks the gate once for `learned peak x 1.15 - granted` (the measured peak of the tree, children included, of earlier reads of that root, capped by the host ceiling) instead of a series of 256 to 1000 MB steps, each of which stopped the worker; a read with no history grows by the headroom step as before. The minimum of an ask stays 256 MB, so a host that has less than that free still answers "wait".
- `👷️slice-pool/🟨️.mjs` `grow` uses `max(minimum, headroom step, standing - granted)`; test `📈️grant-growth/🟦️.ts` last test (known peak asks once for the peak, no history keeps the headroom step); grant-growth 9, booking 7, claim-refresh 1 pass.
