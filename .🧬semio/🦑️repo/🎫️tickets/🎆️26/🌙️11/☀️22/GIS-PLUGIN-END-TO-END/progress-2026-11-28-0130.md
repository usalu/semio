## Problems Fixed Since 18:00

All are repo-wide and tested; reports are in this folder.

1. **Graph read restarts.**
   - Cause: every pass handed over to a cold worker (about 19 min each) because the heap was slightly above 2 GB.
   - Fix: hand over only when at least 25% of the owners must be read again, or the heap is above 150% of the threshold.
   - Report: graph-owner-read-throughput.md.
2. **Silent service treated as lost.**
   - The client now probes the service and keeps waiting on a live but stalled one (up to 30 min).
   - It follows a successor generation without losing progress.
   - The launchd bootstrap race is fixed.
   - Report: graph-service-silence.md.
3. **Service generation churn.**
   - At most 2 live generations.
   - Only plan code and schema change the implementation key.
   - Report: service-generation-churn.md.
4. **Capability token refused.**
   - One key per generation, 10 min lifetime, a typed refusal in en and de, and a client retry with a fresh token.
   - Report: capability-token-expiry.md.
5. **Silent client exit.** A plan request that ends without an answer now always prints a bilingual verdict and exits nonzero (`🏁️verdict`).

Proof of effect: planning one target now takes minutes. The combined four-target plan took more than 5 h and never finished.
