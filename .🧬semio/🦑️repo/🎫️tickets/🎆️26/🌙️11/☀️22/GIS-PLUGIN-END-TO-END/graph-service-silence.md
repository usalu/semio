# Graph service silence, generation changes and the launchd refusal

Scope: `🗑️generated/excl-ts-test-1.log` (run-many pid 89217, ended after 5.4 h with no tests), service logs, `exit.log`, launchd reproduction `launchd-bootstrap-repro.ts`. pid 20374 was not touched.

## What the log and the service say
- The service side already keeps a held plan audible: the pool beats every `heartbeatMilliseconds` from the service process (not from the worker), `keepaliveDueV1` adds an `alive` frame every 10 s while a job holds the client. A SIGSTOPped worker therefore does not make the service silent. The client aborts after `planSilenceMilliseconds` 120 s without a frame of its plan; after `attempts` (3) and `reattachMaximum` (5) it gives up.
- `exit.log` shows the generation churn behind "2 implementations seen": between 14:17 and 19:03 about 14 services started and were `superseded` (every edit of a file in the service closure, by any of the 50 machines or by me, changes the implementation hash). A client that loses its generation reconnects, the successor starts its own read, and each loss used up an attempt or a reattach. Service pid 52292 of the run was `RSS 76 MB, state UN` (paged out) while it held the job, so a stall of more than 120 s of the process itself is the one cause of true silence the log supports; I could not prove which of the two (paged-out service or lost job after a generation change) ended the run.

## (1) Silence counts only for the service, and the service names a quiet worker
- `🚪️client/🛰️plan/🟨️.mjs`: when a plan connection is quiet for the silence bound, `probeServiceV1` asks the service on a fresh connection: `answered` (responsive service, quiet plan: the plan is lost, the client asks again as before), `starved` (connection accepted and process alive but no `welcome` within `quietProbeMilliseconds` 5 s: a swapping host stalls it; the client keeps waiting and tells so in both languages) or `dead` (nothing listens or the pid is gone: silent). A starved service is waited for at most `quietHoldMilliseconds` 30 min.
- `🏛️service/🌐️host/🟨️.mjs`: when the pool reports no worker message for `workerQuietNoticeMilliseconds` 30 s (worker held by the host, waiting at the capacity gate, handing over) the slice progress line carries a typed notice `host-slice-owners-worker-quiet` (en/de) with the quiet seconds; the progress frames themselves keep flowing from the service.

## (2) A plan is never lost to a generation change
- A hand-over refusal (`code: handover`) now ends the exchange as the typed reason `handover` (progressed), and a lost or silent connection after job progress, a hand-over, or a service whose current implementation (`identify`) differs from the one connected, are followed: the client asks the successor again without using up `attempts` or `reattachMaximum`, up to `generationFollowMaximum` 64 follows, 2 s apart, with the bilingual line `host-service-handover-follow` (follow N of 64). The read keeps what it finished (checkpoint and vouch ledger on disk); only the connection is new.
- Policy numbers are in `🏡️resident/🧬️schema/🔣️.json` (`generationFollowMaximum`, `generationFollowMilliseconds`, `quietProbeMilliseconds`, `quietHoldMilliseconds`, `workerQuietNoticeMilliseconds`).

## (3) Why launchd refuses the bootstrap
Reproduced with the real plist writer and `launchctl` (`launchd-bootstrap-repro.ts`): a sequential boot out, bootstrap, kickstart, boot out loop always succeeds, but a `bootstrap` of a label that is already loaded answers `Bootstrap failed: 5: Input/output error`. `startViaLaunchdV1` booted out and bootstrapped the same label on every start, so two clients of one implementation (the "2 implementations seen" waits, several run-many and other agents starting while a generation is still coming up) race: the loser's bootstrap is refused, and its `undo` booted out the winner's job. The start is now idempotent: a loaded label is neither booted out nor bootstrapped again; a refusal that finds the label loaded counts as loaded; `kickstart` starts it by demand; only a job this call created is removed on failure; the reason carries what launchctl said (`bootstrap refused: Bootstrap failed: 5: ...`), and the production `run` returns stderr. The direct start remains the fallback (needed for checkouts below Documents when launchd cannot run the launcher), now reached only on a real refusal instead of after every race.
