# Sealed image ENOSPC (2026-11-28 about 00:00)

## Why a nearly empty sealed image answered ENOSPC
Measured on the live host (not inferred), `statvfs` of the mounted shard images against the host volume:
- Reported free of `sealed` (declared 26.0 GiB, 0.8 GiB held) and `w` (27.0 GiB, 0.9 GiB held) is `hostFree - K` with K constant per image (sealed 25.63 GiB, w 26.27 GiB), checked at host free 31.0 and 35.3 GiB; it moves 1:1 with the host. Both report 5 to 10 GiB free for 25 GiB free inside. `cargo` (17 GiB) and the lane `p91c459` (11 GiB) are clamped the same way but the clamp is above their inside free, so it is invisible until the host free drops under their declared size (about 16 and 11 GiB).
- Consequence: an image is only usable while the host has more free space than about its whole declared capacity. Below that it reports 0 free and every write (the `.leases` file, `cargo/prototype/tmp/jobserver-*`, `captures` mkdtemp, `cargo/home/.semio-ready`) is ENOSPC, which is exactly the set of failures in the log (sealed and the cargo shard, all at once, all four generators).
- Host free was at or near zero for long stretches (`u/fill.jsonl`: home reads 0.0 free 17:10-17:56 and 20:25-21:05 on the 27th, 00:11-00:17 on the 28th; no sample at all 21:05-00:11 because the sampler itself could not write). My own scratch test (`hdiutil create` of a 20 GiB image) pulled host free from 31 to 15.7 GiB within a minute: such spikes are routine on this host.
- Not the cause: the sparse backing limit by itself (sealed backing is 27.9 GB sparse, 1.1 GB allocated) and a full parent: `r/m` reports 35 GiB free (it is host clamped but has no phantom). `r` is not nested in anything that fills.
- Why `removed build unit ... (image below its free-space floor)` churned: reclaim inside a mounted image does not return blocks to the host, so free (clamped to the host) never rises and collection keeps evicting warm units for nothing.

## Why `r` is 96 GiB and over 32 GiB
Chain found in the ledger and the commitment numbers:
1. The commitment ceiling is `allocated + 3 * (hostFree - reserve)`; at 31 GiB free it is about 122 GiB against about 120 GiB committed. A lane birth declares 8 to 16 GiB and was compared in declared units while every standing image is counted as `min(declared, allocated + 4 GiB)`. The unit mismatch refused births for a fleet that promises almost nothing.
2. A refused birth leaves the lane in the home image: `p19965c` and `p4e9a2b` (10.5 and 18.3 GiB, both live: pids 82246 and 56319) sit in `r` with its other content; home content is 38.5 GB, above 32 GiB, and a 96 GiB declared home cannot be shrunk while those lanes are live.

## Fixes (all tested, see below)
1. Typed refusal and retry, never a raw ENOSPC (`🍎️image/🪫️relief`, schema-first, Python oracle): `retryOnImageFullV1` releases the reserve file, reclaims, backs off 0/2/8 s and writes again 3 times, then fails with `NativeImageFullErrorV1` (code ENOSPC, en+de, names image, usable, inside free, host free, reclaimed); the CLI boundary renders it as the typed refusal `disk.image-full` (`refuseDiskFullV1`, catalogue en/de). Wired into `retryAfterOutOfSpaceV1` (now multi-attempt with ballast release, used by the plugin driver stages) and around the whole body of `launchRepositoryCargoV1` after the store exists, which covers the setup writes that failed (jobserver dir, captures, `.semio-ready`, sealed lease).
2. Commitment unit fix: `nativeImageCommitmentRequestCostBytesV1` (a birth promises its growth reserve) and `nativeImageCommitmentDeclarableBytesV1` (a room under the reserve bounds the declaration, otherwise unbounded). `admitNewLaneV1` and `laneImageHostRoomBytesV1` use them, so lanes get their own image again instead of falling into the home. Oracle rows added.
3. Clamp awareness: `nativeImageClampV1` (phantom = inside free minus reported free, starved = room inside but under the 2 GiB floor), `nativeImageRightSizeV1`, `nativeImagePressureV1` (hard under 20 GiB host free or a starved image, soft under 40 GiB). `retireClampedLaneImagesV1` re-creates a clamped, oversized lane image at its sized capacity when no build holds it (never a claimed/live lane, a busy mount stays); it runs on every pooled-lane acquisition and every product-lane acquisition, i.e. on use, not in quiet windows.

## Not done live, and why
- `r` (96 GiB), `sealed` (26), `w` (27), `cargo` (17) were NOT touched: a cargo test and a TS test are running, all four are leased or busy, and detaching or re-creating them would lose held units. They are brought in line without loss as follows: the two home lanes retire through the normal pool trim when idle; the next acquisition then births them as shards (fix 2); once the home holds under 32 GiB the existing `repo cache gc` oversize path (`repositoryPartitionOversizeV1`, dismantle plus regrow) re-creates `r` within the cap in the first quiet moment. `sealed`/`w`/`cargo` are not lane images and are not covered by `retireClampedLaneImagesV1`; they re-create via the same gc dismantle of the partition. A drain gate (new leases wait while the regrow runs) is the remaining piece for a never-quiet host; it needs the lease admission code and is not implemented.
- Host-watermark gc: `nativeImagePressureV1` is the pure decision; it is not yet read by `watchUnitCachePressureV1` (the watcher still keys on per-image and aggregate free). The retry path now collects on the first ENOSPC, and clamp-driven lane re-creation runs on acquisition.
- No `repo cache gc` was started (one is already running).

## Follow-up: what drove host free to 0, and the watermark wiring

### What consumed the host (measured now, `du` and `stat`; the ledgers have no host-wide breakdown)
Host data volume 228 GiB, 175 GiB used, 27 GiB free (87 %). The base is already large, so a 20 to 30 GiB burst empties it:
- Sparse images, allocated on the host: `r/fixed.dmg` 55.1 GiB (content 38.5 GiB, so 16.6 GiB of unreturned slack), `d` 10.3, `g` 4.9, `p` 3.5, `t` 3.2, `n` 1.1, shards sealed 1.1, w 3.5, cargo 2.6, lane 0.7: 86 GiB. These are the only consumers that grow within minutes.
- Not images: `Documents/semio-recovery.VM9RiFR0` 21.4 GB (a recovery copy from 2026-11-20, static, not deleted: it is a user data copy, remove it by hand when no longer needed), `~/.cache/semio/cas` 13.6 GB (own cap and gc), `.git` 11 GB, `/private/tmp` 4.6 GB (agent scratch), `/private/var/folders` 4.4 GB, swap 3.1 GB (`/System/Volumes/VM`, 2.5 GB in use), logs 0.1 GB, no Xcode or DerivedData, no snapshots. None of them changed by tens of GiB in the incident windows.
- The windows match image growth: `u/fill.jsonl` profiles show the lanes in the home grow from 0 to 5.4 + 6.3 GiB between 18:44 and 21:05 on the 27th (`p4e9a2b`, `p7d7867`), and 7.6 + 17.2 GiB at 00:11 on the 28th (`p19965c`, `p4e9a2b`); the home allocation stays after the lanes go (blocks are not returned while mounted), so free falls in steps and only recovers when a gc retires an image or the kick runs (17:56 and 21:05 on the 27th, 00:17 on the 28th). The ledger confirms: free is 0.0 while those lanes grow, never because of a single external writer.
- Contributing: a 21 GiB recovery copy and 16.6 GiB of home slack take away the margin that a lane in the home (17 GiB) needs; one 20 GiB scratch image of mine moved free from 31 to 15.7 GiB within a minute. Swap (3 GB) is not the cause.

### Fix of the cause (budget plus automatic relief)
- The lanes land in the home because births were refused by a unit mismatch in the commitment budget: fixed above, so lanes get their own image (retired whole, blocks returned) instead of growing the home.
- Host watermarks now act on their own (`nativeImagePressureV1`, `criticalHostFreeBytes` 8 GiB added: levels none, soft below 40 GiB, hard below 20 GiB or with a starved image, critical below 8 GiB):
  - `watchUnitCachePressureV1` reads the host free of the partition behind the cache root (`hostFreeBytesOfRootV1`, unbounded for a root that is not sharded). Soft and above: once per poll interval it shrinks idle clamped lane images (`shrink` port, wired in the `--watch` command to `retireClampedLaneImagesV1`; busy or claimed lanes stay). Hard and critical: the collection need is raised by the host shortfall (to the hard watermark plus the 2 GiB floor, never to the soft 40 GiB, which would evict warm units for nothing), through the same single-flight, lock-aware, cancellable collection, bounded by the watcher's lifetime and stalled rounds. Critical also gives the ballast back first.
  - At the critical watermark `launchRepositoryCargoV1` (compile) collects once and then refuses with `NativeImageHostCriticalErrorV1`, rendered as the typed refusal `disk.host-critical` (en, de, exit class refused), instead of writing until ENOSPC; lane births and growth happen only inside such a launch.
- Tests (pass): relief 10 (critical level in the oracle corpus, host-critical refusal), gc 17 (new: host watermarks, shrink once per poll, ballast released before the critical collection, calm host does nothing), disk refusals 11 (new: host-critical en/de). `repo typecheck` of the launcher, gc, script and disk module: clean. No second `repo cache gc` started (the earlier one finished, exit 0).
