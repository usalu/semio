## Method

- Every asset is derived from the closest existing human, as the brief asks (rig, outlines of all 12 drawings, per-scale body, thermal and
  structure volumes), then made a different person by a **uniform rescale** (`hw.scale_asset`: rig, anchors, every 2D point and solid
  placement, every 3D solid, offsets `dx/dy`, `advanceM`, areas by f², masses by the new body mass) and by its own activity, props, poses,
  clips, thermal scenario and texts. Scales below 1 were tried for the presenter (0.971) and rejected: the 1:500 solids of the base sit at
  exactly the 75 mm feature and fall below it (`volumes-feature`), so all factors are ≥ 1.
- Props (desk, laptops, flipchart, marker) are drawn by `hw.Part`: one bone-local box per part that yields the elevation outline in the bone
  (u, v) plane, the front and plan outlines from the rest-pose world frames (`rest.ts`), and the matching `box` body solid, `{of}` thermal
  and structure references. Parts on bones that leave their rest plane (marker, carried laptop) are drawn as `solid: extruded, sweep w`
  prisms in front and plan, so they read right in every pose (`lateral-ambiguous`, rest-frame squashing).
- Paint order is authored per view (`zv`): e.g. the desk lies under the arms in plan, in front of the person in the front elevation, its
  near (right) side in front of the person in the side elevation.
- Arms that must touch something were placed with an inverse-kinematics tool on the rig chain (`ik.ts`, Nelder–Mead with restarts on
  `solveChain`), then checked in all three views.
- Scripts (inputs, kept): `T/🧰️scripts/HW-01/` — `hw.py` (kit), `fingers.py`, `build_desk.py`, `build_presenter.py`, `build_carrier.py`,
  `texts_*.py`, `scrub.py`, `rows.py`, `validate.ts`, `probe.ts`, `rest.ts`, `ik.ts`, `dims.ts`, `extents.ts`, `png.ts`, `zoom.ts`, `env.sh`.
  Run `python3 build_<x>.py` (writes the catalog file), `python3 rows.py`, `bun validate.ts`.
