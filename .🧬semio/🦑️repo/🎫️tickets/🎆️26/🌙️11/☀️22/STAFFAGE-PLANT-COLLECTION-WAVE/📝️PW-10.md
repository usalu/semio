# 📝️ PW-10 — windmill-palm, banana-plant, tree-fern

Worker PW-10 of the Staffage Plant Collection Wave. Three new `semio.staffage/v2` vegetation assets, generated end to end by scripts in `🧰️scripts/PW-10/` that reuse the round-3 date-palm generator of the 3D-models ticket (`☀️14/PERFECT-HANDCRAFTED-…/r3-date-palm-kit.ts` for frames, lofts, outlines, unions; `r3-date-palm-parts.ts` `FrondGeom` for the arched two-bone leaf; `r3-project2d.ts` `forward` for the product's placement of 2D shapes; `volumes-lib.ts`), imported from their place, never copied.

## Finisher pass (2026-11-22, after the interruption)

State found: no catalog file and no row for any of the three plants; the generators built candidates. Measured on the candidates: `windmill-palm` validator clean, `banana-plant` 20 and `tree-fern` 7 `volume-size` issues (lamina polyhedra that cut themselves), and all three far outside the catalog-silhouette gate (banana elevation IoU 0.82, Hausdorff up to 469 mm against 15 mm).

What was wrong and what changed (all in `🧰️scripts/PW-10/`):

- `pw10-plant.ts` `lamina`: a tear opened by up to 45 % of the whole strip, but the rows of a side also hold the tears of the other side, so an opened corner could pass the next row and the thickened sheet cut itself. The opening is now limited by the distance to the neighbouring row.
- The leaves were curved, folded and torn polyhedra, drawn by a few thick slabs per bone that had to cover fold, curvature and midrib at once. Body and drawing could not agree. Now every leaf bone is cut into bands; in each band the blade is one plate and the rib (petiole, midrib, stipe, rachis) another, with straight upper and lower faces; the plate is written once as the body solid (a prism triangulated by the kernel's `triangulatePolygon`, checked with `polyhedronFault`) and once as the drawn `extruded` shape. Plates are cut at the band edges, at the bone origin and at the bone tip (the span of a drawn plate is straight only between origin and tip), and a plate narrower than the smallest feature is left out.
- Consequences stated plainly: blades are flat across (no fold along the midrib, no cupping, no pleats) and whole. Banana tears, fern pinnae and the splits between the segment tips of the palm fan are texture strokes in the drawings and are absent from the 3D body; the palm fan has a smooth rim. A slit of a few millimetres cannot be an outline shared by body and drawing: the comparison raster closes it in one and not in the other.
- The droop of the palm fan is the same at every scale (it was 12, 8, 9 and 6 cm, which made the 1:100 body 22 mm taller than `dimensions`).
- Thick tubes (stem, crown cup) are outlined with 72 points per section instead of 20 (3 mm inside the solid on a 0.5 m trunk).
- `dimensions` are measured on the tessellated 1:50 body in the default pose (height is the full extent; the banana suckers start 17 mm below the ground plane).
- The first volume decision of 1:50, 1:100 and 1:200 of each species was rewritten to describe the plates.
- `pw10-solids.ts` imports the kernel with one `../` too few and does not run; `🧰️scripts/FIN-B/fault.ts` replaces it.

Remaining doubts: the representation decisions of the three species still speak of "torn" leaves and "pinnae" in places where these are now strokes; the summary texts describe the real plant, not the simplification. The 3D body is plainer than the date palm of the catalog, whose leaflets are texture solids.

After the first full proof: the banana `pseudostem` region states the measured 3.16 m² (2.6 before; `volumes-thermal-coverage`); bands in which no face centre lies are no longer skipped and a short bone gets fewer bands (the 1:200 and 1:500 banana bodies were in pieces, `volumes-connected`); at 1:500 no plate is thinner or narrower than 120 mm, a rib is widened to that, and a palm fan is one plate (`suitability-feature` 0.16 to 0.199 mm against 0.2 mm on fern, palm and banana plates).

## Previews opened and judged (finisher)

One contact sheet per plant from `🧰️scripts/FIN-B/sheets.ts` (default pose, third-angle sheet of plan, side and front at every authored scale and the axonometric view of the 1:50 volume, light solid, rasterised with sharp); the PNGs were generated output and are deleted.

- `windmill-palm`: reads as a fan palm at every scale: a slender fibre-coated stem with stub texture, a round crown of fans with fold strokes, a skirt of dead leaves; the 3D body shows the fans as banded plates with a smooth rim (no split segment tips).
- `banana-plant`: reads as a banana clump at every scale: pseudostem, paddle leaves with tear and vein strokes, cigar leaf, hanging dead leaves and two suckers; the 3D leaves are plain banded blades.
- `tree-fern`: reads as a tree fern: flared fibrous trunk, a shuttlecock of arching fronds with pinna strokes, a skirt of dead fronds; with whole blades the crown is close to a palm in 3D.
