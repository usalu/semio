### Preview sheets opened and judged

Sheets: the product's own 2D drawings (side, front, plan; line and solid) at 1:500, 1:200, 1:100 and 1:50 on one contact sheet and the six shaded 3D views at 1:100 (`🧰️scripts/finish-a/look.sh`; the PNGs were tool output and are deleted).

- `norway-maple`: dense, broad, rounded dome on a short stem, scalloped outline; shade core visible in the gaps at 1:200 to 1:50, leaf and samara marks at 1:50; reads as a Norway maple at every scale.
- `field-maple`: small rounded dense crown on a short stem, 10 m; clearly the small brother of the other two maples.
- `sycamore-maple`: large broad dome, 25 m, heavier and wider than the Norway maple; 1:500 one calm outline.

### What stays imperfect

- The cross-scale rule (`volumes-cross-scale`: the boundary of each coarser body within about seven smallest features of the next finer one for 95 % of its length) is not met: the scales are separate designs whose crown lobes or tiers sit at different places. This needs a redesign of the coarse crowns (the same silhouette lobes at every scale, as `PW-03` and `PW-04` did) and was not done by the finisher. Affects all three maples (9 or 7 findings each).
- `norway-maple` and `field-maple`: one group of body solids at 1:100 is 33.5 mm away from the rest (`volumes-connected`, "r4-2" from "r4-3").
