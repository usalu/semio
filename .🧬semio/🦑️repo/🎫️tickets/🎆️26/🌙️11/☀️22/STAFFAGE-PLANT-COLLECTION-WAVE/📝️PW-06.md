## Method

Generator (inputs, kept): `🧰️scripts/PW-06/`

- `plant-kit.ts`: converts and projects only. It imports the round-3 kit of the per-scale 3D ticket (`☀️14/PERFECT-HANDCRAFTED-3D-MODELS-PER-SCALE-AND-MODE-FOR-EVERY-STAFFAGE/r3-apple-tree-kit.ts`: swept tube lofts, oriented ellipsoid lobes, exact orthographic outlines, polygon union through the product's `unionPolygons`; `volumes-lib.ts`: rest frames through the product's `restFrames`) from its place. It derives the planar botanical rig (every bone in the sagittal plane, lateral position as `z` offset, as the apple tree), masses (green wood volume of the 1:50 tubes × density; fresh leaves and twigs by lobe volume) and solid circular sections, wind loads `F = ½ ρ cd A v(z)²` (front area of each foliage mass at 1:50, power-law profile v10·(z/10)^0.22), snow/fruit loads on plan areas, the pose offsets, sway/gust/diurnal keys, the per-scale volumes (body; thermal references, hidden shade lobes and outlet lenses tagged with their emitter; structure members under the bark, joints, the root-plate support and the masses), and every 2D shape of every scale as the true orthographic view of that scale's body (ruling E1 of the 3D ticket): one shape per bone and region, the outline the union of the projected solids, cleaned with a quarter of the smallest feature. Leaf glyphs, bark marks and lobe arcs are placed only where they stay 0.35–0.4 mm of paper inside every outline. Thin wood is raised to 1.15 × max(0.15 mm, 0.2 mm) of paper at each scale.
- `rowan.ts`, `hawthorn.ts`, `black-alder.ts`: every number by hand: stem stations, limbs (height, azimuth, elevation at the stem and the tip, length, radii, bend), side branches, the crown envelope, the lobe rings per scale (heights, semi-axes, droop, count and tangential width), berry corymbs (rowan), outlets, materials, regions, emitters, poses, clips, all texts in English and German.
- `ship.sh`: generate, validate the candidate (`r3-validate.ts --file … --volumes`), or with `catalog` write the catalog file and the manifest row and run the staffage check CLI.
- `cross-probe.ts`: diagnostic of the boundary distances between two scales (used once to find the cause of the first `volumes-cross-scale` failures).

Design rule that made the scales agree (`volumes-cross-scale`, `volumes-bounds`): every scale uses the same main ring heights with the same radial and vertical semi-axes and lobes at the four cardinal azimuths, so the silhouette lobes are the same at every scale; the coarser scales have fewer, tangentially wider lobes, no in-between rings, fewer stations, no side branches (1:200) and at 1:500 a crown bound to one stem bone with the limbs as thermal-only solids inside. The shade foliage is a `hidden` region (its lobes are thermal-only solids inside the crown), because a drawn inner core is painted over the sun foliage in the depth order of the views.
