### What stays imperfect

- The cross-scale rule (`volumes-cross-scale`: the boundary of each coarser body within about seven smallest features of the next finer one for 95 % of its length) is not met: the scales are separate designs whose crown lobes or tiers sit at different places. This needs a redesign of the coarse crowns (the same silhouette lobes at every scale, as `PW-03` and `PW-04` did) and was not done by the finisher. Affects all three (7 to 9 findings each); the ring heights, lobe counts and the per-scale jitter of `kit.ts` differ between the scales by design.
- `horse-chestnut` and `common-ash`: the final whole-asset validation is two runs (with print checks without volume rules, and with volume rules without print checks) because one combined run took more than 15 minutes on the shared host; `european-beech` has the combined run.
- The beech text of the 1:500 body still says "seven rings" (there are eight with the bridging ring).
