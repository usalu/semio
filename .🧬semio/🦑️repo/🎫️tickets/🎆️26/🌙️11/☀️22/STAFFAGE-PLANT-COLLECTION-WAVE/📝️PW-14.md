# 📝️ PW-14: Herbs and Roses

Assets: `lavender-bush`, `rosemary-bush`, `shrub-rose` in `🎓️teaching/🏛️architecture/🧍️staffage/🗂️catalog/🌳️vegetation/<id>/🔣️.json`. Manifest rows: `📥️manifest-rows/<id>.json` (derived from the evaluated assets, power 0 W).

## Method (common to the three assets)

- Generators (inputs, kept): `🧰️scripts/PW-14/`
  - `plant_lib.py` kit: a sagittal botanical rig authored in world rest metres; primitives `Ell` (ellipsoid), `Cap` (capsule, or a pen stroke for a slender stalk/cane), `Rev` (solid of revolution about the vertical) and `Mark` (2D texture line). Every primitive gives one bone-local volume solid **and its exact projection** in side elevation (bone-local), front and plan (world rest coordinates). So drawing and volume are one body at every scale by construction. Paint order `z` per view = nearness of the nearest point (right side in the elevation, ahead in the front, height in the plan); marks sit just above their host lobe.
  - Imports the archive's NW-V2 helpers (`T`, `r4`, `leaf_region`, `passive_region`, `transpiration`) from `ARCHITECTURAL-STAFFAGE-ARCHIVE/🧰️scripts/NW-V2` (not copied).
  - `shrub.py`: wind profile `v(z) = v10 (z/10)^0.22`, drag `F = ½ ρ cd A v²` (ρ 1.25, EN 1991-1-4), pose and region bindings, gust clip (load tracks 0.05 → 2.75 → 0.05, Iv 0.25), periodic sway clip, 24 s diurnal clip, `assemble()` and `outline_symbol()`.
  - Species scripts `lavender_bush.py`, `rosemary_bush.py`, `shrub_rose.py`; bark area overrides `<id>.areas.json` (outer bark area measured on the 1:50 thermal solids by `checkVolumes`).
  - `check.ts`: ajv schema, `validateAsset`, the catalog check (`checkAssetDetailed`, 9 samples per clip, all poses, 7 views × 3 viewpoints × every authored scale), `checkVolumes` (all 14 volume rules incl. silhouette, cross-scale, connected, flood thermal area) and the catalog-silhouette gate (`measureAsset`: bbox within one feature, IoU ≥ 0.92, Hausdorff ≤ 2 features per view and scale). `--areas` prints the outer bark areas and cross-scale measures.
  - `preview.ts` + `previews.sh`: the staffage preview CLI with sharp as rasteriser (rsvg-convert is not installed on this machine).
- Coarse scales are redrawn, not filtered: other lobe counts, sizes and placements per scale; for rosemary and rose the across/along lobe factors of 1:100/1:200/1:500 are found by bisection so the redrawn masses keep the true 1:50 extents.
- Rig: planar (no rest spread or twist); lateral positions by bone `z` offsets; member solids follow the true 3D stem lines. Wind along +x. Known simplification: shoots/canes leaning sideways bend only in the side plane (stated in the asset notes and in the snow/rain pose texts).
- Physics: thermal regions foliage/flowers as `leaf` with `areaBasis: exchange`, bark `passive` (area = 1:50 outer solid area); two transpiration emitters per asset with 5 to 10 anchors; all poses bind every region and emitter; `structure.dynamics: quasi-static`, fixed `base-support`; every bone has mass (green wood volume × density, foliage authored estimate) and every woody bone a round section. Power: 0 W (no powered element).
