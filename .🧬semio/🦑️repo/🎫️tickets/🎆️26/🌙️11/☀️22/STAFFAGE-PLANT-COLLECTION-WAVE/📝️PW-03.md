## Pipeline (generators)

The shipped JSON is generator output only; never hand-edit it, rerun the generators.

1. `pw03_build.py <species module> <out>` (Python, through `repo tools exec python`): the species file `sp_<id>.py` states every number of the tree. `pw03_tree.py` authors limbs as 3D polylines (start, azimuth, elevation path, length, radii), gives the planar NW-V1 rig their elevation projection plus the lateral offset `z` of the bone origin, computes wood masses from the true 3D frustum volumes and writes the 3D skeleton beside the base document. Poses, clips (`wind-sway`, `gust`, `diurnal-transpiration`), wind/snow/fruit loads, thermal regions, emitters, materials, structure come from the NW-V1 library (`ARCHITECTURAL-STAFFAGE-ARCHIVE/🧰️scripts/NW-V1`, imported from its place, `common.py`, `lib.py`, `sp_plane.py`).
2. `pw03-run.ts <id> [--volumes] [--check]` (bun): the species design `pw03-<id>.ts` (per-scale wood sections, side branches, crown clumps on an envelope, flowers or fruit, outlets, bark marks, leaf glyph, decision texts) and the engine `pw03-engine.ts` build per scale the bone-local volume (body, thermal by reference plus hidden parts and outlet lenses, structure: members, joints, support, masses) with the kit of the apple-tree round (`PERFECT-HANDCRAFTED-…/r3-apple-tree-kit.ts`, `volumes-base.ts`, imported from their place). The three 2D views of every scale are drawn as the true orthographic views of that scale's body (unions of tube and lobe shadows, visible flowers or fruit, arcs, leaf marks, bark strokes). Coarser crowns are calibrated so that their body extent equals the 1:50 body in the default pose (`dimensions` = 1:50 body bounding box). Symbols are drawn from the evaluated 1:100 default pose. Writes the catalog file and the manifest row, then runs `validateAsset`, `checkVolumes` and the catalog check.
3. `pw03-look.ts <id>`: previews through the staffage preview CLI with an owned sharp rasteriser behind its `Rasterizer` port (`rsvg-convert` is not installed on this machine). `pw03-cross-probe.ts`: diagnosis of cross-scale silhouette differences.

Run: `python pw03_build.py sp_japanese_cherry ../../🗑️generated/PW-03/japanese-cherry` then `repo tools exec bun -- pw03-run.ts japanese-cherry --volumes --check` (same for `sp_saucer_magnolia`/`saucer-magnolia`, `sp_crab_apple`/`crab-apple`).

- **Season decision**: shapes cannot be toggled per pose, so the asset is the flowering state; blossom and half-expanded young leaves are drawn in every pose.

- **Volumes**: 1:50 235 body solids (root flare, stem, ten scaffold parts, three uprights, fifteen side branches, twenty clumps with bosses, cap with five bosses, six shade lobes, hanging flower clusters of 20 cm); 1:100 187; 1:200 102; 1:500 30 (crown bound to the central upright, outer limbs and ten clusters as hidden thermal solids).

- **Reference**: Stephan, Wagner and Kleinschmit (2003), EUFORGEN Technical Guidelines, *Malus sylvestris*: small tree up to about 10 m, broad dense crown, often thorny short shoots, fruit 2 to 4 cm, yellow-green, hard and sour. - **Volumes**: 1:50 285 body solids with fruit of 3 cm hanging under the clumps; 1:100 fruit of 3 cm (0.3 mm dots); 1:200 one typified fruit cluster of 9 cm per clump; 1:500 four hidden clusters.
- **Representations**: plated bark fissures and cracks, spur shoots with thorns as strokes at 1:50, small ovate 6 cm leaf marks.

## Common representation decisions

Pens, hatches and valid ranges of the NW-V1 library (1:500 0.15 mm, 350 to 1000; 1:200 0.18/0.13, 150 to 350; 1:100 0.2/0.13/0.1, 75 to 150; 1:50 0.25/0.18/0.13, 20 to 75). Every scale draws elevation, front and plan as the true views of its own body, so the silhouettes agree with the volume by construction; foliage masses are one outline per foliage bone, painted in depth order of the viewpoint; flowers and fruit are drawn where they are seen from the viewpoint. 1:500 binds the crown to one bone (a single crown shape listing every merged region), as the apple tree does. Decisions (silhouette, retained, merged, omitted, thin elements, hatching, styles, overlays) are written per asset and scale in English and German with the counts of the model. Symbols: a true-extent symbol (outlines of the evaluated 1:100 default pose in the three views, stem section, limb directions) and the nominal 3.2 mm paper sign.

## Remaining doubts

- Foliage is ellipsoid lobes (the C8 abstraction of the existing trees); close up the clumps read as bubbles, as in the apple tree.
- Seasonal states are fixed by the drawing: the cherry and the magnolia are flowering assets, their summer foliage is not a pose. The crab apple shows fruit in every pose (sample of the load), like the apple tree.
- The planar rig turns limbs about the world lateral axis only; limbs far out of the side-elevation plane sway in that plane (as for all existing trees). Droop of lateral limbs is therefore approximate.
- Flower and fruit counts, petal and tepal areas, leaf areas and masses are authored estimates (stated in the references `cherry-blossom-load`, `magnolia-flower-load`, `crab-yield`).
- The coarser crowns are calibrated in place (lobe centres moved radially by up to a few centimetres) so that every scale's body has the 1:50 extent within one smallest feature, as the catalog silhouette guard demands.
- `catalog-silhouette` only runs under vitest (its `vi.setConfig` fails under the plain bun runner of `repo test-file`); its measure was reproduced with the product module (`measureAsset`, `bodyDimensions`) in `pw03-run.ts`.
