## Tooling

- `T/🧰️scripts/PW-13/lib.ts`: my shared helpers. It **imports** (does not copy) the hornbeam-hedge kit `r3-hornbeam-hedge-kit.ts` (panel lofts, tubes, flares, blobs, outlines, `toDrawing`) and `volumes-lib.ts` (rest frames) from `…/☀️14/PERFECT-HANDCRAFTED-3D-MODELS-PER-SCALE-AND-MODE-FOR-EVERY-STAFFAGE/`, and adds:
  - `leafySolid` / `leafyPolygon`: a closed, outward, star-shaped leaf skin (polyhedron) with a deterministic leaf-cluster relief from nine 3D waves; its outline in a view is exact per ray (ray-cast against the projected triangle edges), so every 2D body shape is the true projection of that scale's body ("ruling E1" of the hornbeam).
  - `fit`: per-axis envelope fit, so the coarser scales keep exactly the 1:50 envelope (catalog-silhouette gate: bbox within one feature at every scale).
  - leaf marks (`boxMark`, `privetMark`, `laurelMark`: open strokes only, never slivers), `marks` scatter, `clipTube`, `clipStrip`, `ovoid`, wind physics (`windAt` power law 0.22, `drag` ½ρ cd A v², ρ 1.25), `swayKeys`, `GUST`, `diurnalClip`, `REFERENCES`, shared thermal assumptions, `save`, `manifestRow`.
- `check.ts`: `validateAsset` + product `checkVolumes` (findings with data) + `measureVolumes` infos (thermal outer areas, silhouette IoU/p95/Hausdorff, bounds).
- `preview.sh`: preview CLI with `--no-png` and rasterisation via `qlmanage` (**`rsvg-convert` is not installed on this machine and not a registered repo tool**; the preview CLI without `--no-png` fails with `ToolMissingError`).
- `dbg-shapes.ts`, `dbg-solids.ts`: `[DEBUG]` extents of evaluated 2D shapes and tessellated solids (used to find the plant-seam crack, see privet).

## Common decisions

- World frame as the hornbeam: x ahead (elevation paper x), y up, z left; wind bearing 0 along +x; sway is sagittal only (no `abductionDeg`), so no `lateral-ambiguous` flat shapes.
- Poses calm (1.5 m/s at 10 m), windy (8 m/s, Beaufort 5), storm (22 m/s, Beaufort 9) and a snow pose; drag numbers in every description are computed by the generator (`windAt`, `drag`) and match the loads. Clips `wind-sway` (looping, first key = last key), `gust` (8 s, EN 1991-1-4 peak factor 2.75 on `load:*` tracks), `diurnal-transpiration` (24 s loop, emitter and `region:*:sunlitFraction` tracks).
- Thermal: bark `passive`, foliage `leaf` with `areaBasis: "exchange"`, an outer sun region and a hidden inner shade region, two transpiration emitters with ≥ 5 anchors on the crown surface and one outlet lens per anchor tagged with its emitter. The passive bark `areaM2` is the outer area of that scale's thermal bark solids measured by `measureVolumes` at 1:50 (`check.ts --info`) and stays within the 15 % / 30 % gates at every scale.
- Representations 500 / 200 / 100 / 50 each drawn from its own volume (different relief, station counts, stems merged per scale), pens and valid ranges as the existing vegetation, shade hatch at 1:50 and 1:100, decisions (8 fields, en and de) per scale; volume decisions (3 per scale).
- Symbols: one `true-extent` plan symbol per asset with elevation, front and plan on the root bone.
- References: Wood Handbook 2021, EN 1991-1-4, EN 1991-1-3, Vogel 1989, Jones 2014, Monteith and Unsworth 2013, Stace 2019 (New Flora of the British Isles), Roloff and Bärtels 2018 (Flora der Gehölze). Every mass, material, load, region and emitter carries `source` and `reference`; species-level dimensions are typical horticultural specimens and declared as authored choices in `provenance.notes`.
