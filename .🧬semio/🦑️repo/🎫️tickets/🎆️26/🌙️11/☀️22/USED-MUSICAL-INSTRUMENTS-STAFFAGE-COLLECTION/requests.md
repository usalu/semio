
## used-trumpet — product `shapeDepth` (paint order of `sweep: "v"` / `"u"` solid shapes in a rolled pose)

`🔨️modules/🧮️evaluation/🟦️.ts` `shapeDepthIn` ranks a shape in the elevation by its 2D profile points placed in the bone's (u, v) plane at w = 0 (`local(p, 0)`), and in front/plan by the extruded span mid only. For an extruded solid with `sweep: "v"` the profile is a (u, w) outline and for `sweep: "u"` a (v, w) outline, so their points are read in the wrong plane. Measured on `used-trumpet` (candidate) in the pose `carried` (case bone twisted −91°): the 6 mm case bottom drawn as a `sweep: "v"` box ranked ~0.2 m too far, so `depthOrder` flipped the revolved trumpet parts (bell, caps, buttons, mouthpiece) over it and the trumpet showed through the closed case in the side elevation. Workaround used: boxes and capsules of the (unrotated-at-rest) case bone drawn with `sweep: "w"`, whose profile lies in (u, v). Proposal: in `shapeDepthIn` map the profile point to the bone frame by the solid's sweep (u: (z, p.x, p.y); v: (p.x, z, p.y); w: (p.x, p.y, z)) with z sampled at z0 and z1, as `solid3` already does for the placement.
