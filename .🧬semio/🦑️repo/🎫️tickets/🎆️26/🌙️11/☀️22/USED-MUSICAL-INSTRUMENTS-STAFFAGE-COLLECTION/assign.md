# Assignments — Used Instruments

One author per row. `slug` is the asset id, the folder `assets/<slug>/` and the catalog folder name in the category of the row. Acoustic instruments are `objects`, instruments and equipment that draw electrical power are `appliances`. The subject line is the minimum; the author researches the real instrument type of its period and fixes every number in the report's reference table. The used upright piano belongs to the sibling wave `USED-HOUSEHOLD-OBJECTS-STAFFAGE-COLLECTION` (`used-upright-piano`) and is not repeated here.
