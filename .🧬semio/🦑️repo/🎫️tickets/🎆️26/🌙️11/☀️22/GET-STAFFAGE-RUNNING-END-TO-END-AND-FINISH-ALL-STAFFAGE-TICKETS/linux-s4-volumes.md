## 2. The Scripts

`🧰️linux/s4/<asset id>.ts`, one per asset, each `repo tools exec bun -- <script> <asset document> [--force]`:

- writes `asset.volumes["500"]` (the microwave oven also `["200"]`) by a splice of that one JSON value into the text of the document (`lib.ts`, `applyTo`, `spanOf`): every other byte stays, proven per asset (`untouched=true`: the text without the replaced values is identical before and after);
- prints `written` when the scale was `unavailable`, `unchanged` when it already holds exactly this volume, and refuses by name when the document is another asset, lacks the scale, lacks a bone, material, region, layer or anchor the volume names, or holds another authored volume (`--force` replaces it);
- `apply-all.sh <staffage root> [ids]` applies them in a row. This lane never ran it on the catalog.

Tools beside them: `dump.ts` and `dump-all.sh` (authoring aid: a finer volume per solid with its box in the bone frame and in the world), `view.py`, `view2.sh`, `prove.ts`, `prove-all.sh`, `summary.py`.

How each volume was made: the 1:200 volume (1:100 for the microwave oven at 1:200) was listed with `dump.ts` and simplified by hand for the 75 mm feature: parts below the feature merge into their neighbour or fall away, hollow parts that show their contents stay hollow, declared sheets and true-section slender parts keep their declaration, and the outer extents stay those of 1:200. The decision of each scale says what merged and what fell away, in English and German.
