## 3. What stays open, plainly

One cause: a part that the silhouette round traced from the 3D body in the default pose and laid as a plate 3 to 20 mm thick in whichever bone plane faced the view (`extruded`, `z1 − z0` of 0.003 to 0.02). The plate is right in the default pose and nowhere else, and each view has its own plate. Tried and measured for every one of them, and not kept because the silhouette guard of 1:50 (15 mm) or the IoU of 1:500 (0.92) then fails:

A repair of these needs the part redrawn as a body from its volume solids (as the coarser bands have it) together with a silhouette round on that band; the ops of this batch cannot do it inside the 15 mm of 1:50.
