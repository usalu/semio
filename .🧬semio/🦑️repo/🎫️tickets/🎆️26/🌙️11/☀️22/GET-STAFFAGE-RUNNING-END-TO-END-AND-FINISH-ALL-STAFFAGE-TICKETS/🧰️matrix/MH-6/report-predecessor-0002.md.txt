## 5. The tools of this batch (`U/🧰️matrix/MH-6/`)

| Script | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `fit.ts` | the `fit.ts` of MR with further ops (header of the file): `plan-sweep` (plan outline of a lying part kept, cut to the elevation twin, swept along v), `plan-prism` (plan outline of a standing part as the section of a prism along the bone; also rebuilds a sheet across the bone), `core` (the middle slice of a trunk under its free plan outline), `thicken` (a tracer plate gets its real depth; the outline is narrowed by what that depth adds on the sheet of the default pose, and the op refuses a plate that lies oblique), `rebind` (a flat shape bound to the wrong bone), `keep-as`, `carry` and `carry-solid` (a part of a finer band drawn and modelled in a coarser one), `anchor-split` (D45: one anchor per wheel or foot; contacts and events follow), `"twin": [ids]` for `front-sweep`, `"across": "range"` for `extrude` |
| `ops/<id>.json` | the repair of each asset, one op per line |
| `land.sh` | applies the ops to the catalog file until a run changes nothing |
| `try.sh`, `try-all.sh` | the same on a copy, then gate and silhouette, validate, sweep against the baseline |
| `var.sh` | compares variants of a repair by the silhouette guard (and the gate of chosen scales) |
| `proof.sh` | the whole proof of one asset with its matrix |
| `gate-all.sh`, `hard.py`, `pick.py`, `cmp.py` | gate of the batch; hard findings of a gate log in short; the proposals of `MR/suggest.ts` that a gate log asks for; two measure logs side by side |
| `ext.ts`, `facts.py` | extents of every shape of chosen bones in one subject of the matrix; construction of every shape of a document per bone and view |
| `small.sh` | product sheet of an asset, with a copy 1200 px wide |
| `q.sh`, `wait.sh`, `cap.sh` | the one-at-a-time worker of the batch; `cap.sh` starts a run through the machine-wide memory queue (`repo capacity run`) |
| `report-data.py`, `report-build.py`, `report-head.md.txt`, `report-tail.md.txt` | this report from the logs |

What the next fixer should know: `suggest.ts` proposals answered every flat limb; the picks failed where a band holds tracer plates (section 3). `thicken` with its hold is the op to try first on a plate; it works when the plate faces the view (waiter sleeve, traveller coat) and says by name when it does not. A plan trunk whose outline is free keeps it with `core` underneath. `fit.ts` needed a second run on six of eleven assets (`land.sh` loops).
