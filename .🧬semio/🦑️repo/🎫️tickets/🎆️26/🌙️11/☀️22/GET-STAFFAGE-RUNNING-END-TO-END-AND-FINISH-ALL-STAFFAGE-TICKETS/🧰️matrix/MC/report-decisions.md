- **Wheels: front and plan tyres bound to the axle bone, not made solids** (`forklift`, `city-bus`, `city-car`, `vintage-car-1929`, rear tyre of `motor-scooter`; drums and hubs of the vintage car; hinge knuckles of the generator flap). A tyre is a body of revolution about the lateral axis: its front and plan picture does not change when the wheel spins. The engine has no solid for that (an extruded circle is a plain cylinder and loses the rounded shoulders, the grooves and the twin tyres the fronts draw; `revolved` about `w` gives a flat disc), so the handcrafted outline stays and is carried by the bone that carries the wheel and does not spin. Side elevation shapes stay on the wheel bones and spin. Consequence, measured: the reported tier (`group-drift`, not a failure) now compares the small elevation parts of an axle bone with its front tyres: reported findings of the authors' gate rose for `forklift` (75 to 109), `city-bus` (29 to 42) and `motor-scooter` (0 to 22) and fell or stayed for the 17 others. The alternative, tyres as cylinders on the wheel bones (as the forklift has at 1:500), removes that drift and flattens the drawings; not done.
