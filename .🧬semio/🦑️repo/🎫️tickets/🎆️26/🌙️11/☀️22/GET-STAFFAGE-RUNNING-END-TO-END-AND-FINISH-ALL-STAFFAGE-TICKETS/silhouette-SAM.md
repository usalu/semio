## How it was measured

- The real test per asset: `bun x vitest run --config ../../🧪️tests/🎚️config/🟦️.ts ../../🧪️tests/🧊️catalog-silhouette/🟦️.ts -t "(<id>)( |$)"` in `S/📦️packages/🟦️typescript` with the pinned bun (`vt.sh`, `prove.sh`). It stops at the first failing expectation and starts with the coarsest scale.
- Every check: `measure.ts` (the test's own `measureAsset` with the smallest feature of the scale; limits 7.5, 15, 30, 75 mm per axis, IoU 0.92, 15, 30, 60, 150 mm largest distance).
- Where: `where.ts` reproduces the tiles of `measureAsset` (a large object is measured in tiles of 512 cells, which changes the largest distance, see `U/requests.md`) and names the nearest drawn shapes and solids of each mismatch, with a PNG of the two rasters.
- One measurement differs between my tool and the test and is not explained: `tower-crane` 1:500 elevation reads IoU 0.794 in `measure.ts` (before and after) and in `SAM.tsv`, the test run of this session reports 0.830.

## How it was repaired

The volume is the reference. Every repair is a list of idempotent ops `U/🧰️silhouette/SAM/ops/<id>.json` (shape, solid, declared dimensions) that `apply.ts <id>` applies to the catalog file; it can be applied to the file before or after the repair with the same result. The ops were authored by the scripts in `U/🧰️silhouette/SAM/author/` (one per asset, the reason in its docstring). These assets have no generator that still reproduces them (each is the result of a chain of patch scripts of the earlier tickets), so the repair is this last step. The authoring scripts read the asset as it was before the repair: run them with `SAM_SOURCE=<folder with <id>.json of the state before 2026-11-23 16:30>`; the backup used here was in `🗑️generated` and is deleted, the state is in the history of the archivist.

Kinds of repair:

1. **Declared dimensions** where the body of 1:50 is larger or smaller than declared by a plain amount (handles, hinges, fasteners, fittings): the declared value becomes the extent of the body.
2. **A missing part is drawn**: by hand where it is a rectangle or a stroke; by `trace.ts` as the outline of named solids exactly as the guard projects them, carried into the drawing frame of the bone; by `patch.ts` for the machines with many undrawn parts (every piece of the body that no shape covers, thicker than half a smallest feature, becomes a shape on the bone of its solid, under the existing shapes). `printable.ts` takes added parts out again that would make a scale unprintable.
3. **A wrong drawn shape is redrawn** from its solid (`trace.ts`), or corrected by hand (a seam closed, a hole that the body does not have turned into a dark shape).
4. **The volume was wrong** in three assets, at 1:50 only: hose coils and pad rims of 16 to 24 mm declared `slender` although they are well above the smallest feature (the guard then compares a stroke instead of the body), and string solids of role `texture` without the `texture` flag.

A mistake of mine that was caught and repaired within the session: the first patches of `wheel-loader`, `oil-pumpjack` and `concrete-mixer-truck` made single scales undrawable for about 15 minutes (the print profile refused gaps of 0.07 to 0.15 mm between new and old shapes; `validateAsset` and the check CLI do not see this). `printable.ts` and `drawable.ts` were written for it; all 23 are drawable at every scale now.
