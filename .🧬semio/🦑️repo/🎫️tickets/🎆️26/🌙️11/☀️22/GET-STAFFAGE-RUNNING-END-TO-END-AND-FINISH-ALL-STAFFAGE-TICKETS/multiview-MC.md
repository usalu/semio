## 6. Decisions worth a second look

- **Wheels: front and plan tyres bound to the axle bone, not made solids** (`forklift`, `city-bus`, `city-car`, `vintage-car-1929`, rear tyre of `motor-scooter`; drums and hubs of the vintage car; hinge knuckles of the generator flap). A tyre is a body of revolution about the lateral axis: its front and plan picture does not change when the wheel spins. The engine has no solid for that (an extruded circle is a plain cylinder and loses the rounded shoulders, the grooves and the twin tyres the fronts draw; `revolved` about `w` gives a flat disc), so the handcrafted outline stays and is carried by the bone that carries the wheel and does not spin. Side elevation shapes stay on the wheel bones and spin. Consequence, measured: the reported tier (`group-drift`, not a failure) now compares the small elevation parts of an axle bone with its front tyres: reported findings of the authors' gate rose for `forklift` (75 to 109), `city-bus` (29 to 42) and `motor-scooter` (0 to 22) and fell or stayed for the 17 others. The alternative, tyres as cylinders on the wheel bones (as the forklift has at 1:500), removes that drift and flattens the drawings; not done.
- **D45 splits beyond contacts**: the attachments of `sliding-door` and `sectional-garage-door` were split like contacts, and the garage door's two pose loads with them (half the force each, 11 poses, clip tracks doubled). The reaction on the drum stays bound to the spinning shaft bone, as it was: it orbits the shaft axis with the drum angle and stays on the drum in every view, but a cable leaves a drum at a fixed place in space; not changed here because the shaft moment of the structural view rests on that binding.
- **Stand feet**: three two-wheelers now stand on tyre plus two stand feet (one contact per foot) instead of tyre plus one lumped contact. `catalog-physics` passes for all three (40 of 40).
- **`used-air-water-heat-pump-monobloc` 1:500**: the body solid `unit-block` of the 1:500 volume was widened with the drawing (0.56 to 0.60 m), the only volume edit of the batch besides the support pads of the humidifier and the compressor.
- **`vintage-car-1929` plan 1:50**: four tyres added that the fenders hide completely (paint order below the fenders).
- **`glulam-beam` 1:500** (takeover): the rule now holds through a true but sub-printable step (0.04 mm). If the coordinator prefers the merged bar, the alternative is an allowlist line for the plan; `make/glulam-beam.py` states both shapes.
- **`reclaimed-precast-stair`, front of the lifted and reinstalled flight**: a plain face under the nib without riser lines; seen from ahead the flight shows its soffit side, so this is what the body gives, but it reads bare.
