## How it was measured and repaired

- Measured with the test's own `measureAsset` (`measure.ts`), located with `where.ts` (the tiles of the guard, a PNG of the two rasters) and with the new `diff.ts`, which lists every piece the drawing has and the body lacks, and the reverse, per view, with the shapes and solids that cover it (a whole work list instead of the worst place).
- New small tools: `boxes.ts` (world box of every solid, used to find mirrored copies that are not mirrored and the ends of a body), `extrusions.py` (drawn extrusions whose lateral range differs from their solids).
- Repairs are idempotent ops `🧰️silhouette/R2-M/ops/<id>.json`, authored by `author/<id>.py` (hand repairs, reason in the docstring) and `author/<id>.sh` (the script to run: it calls the `.py`, then traces solids with `trace.ts`), applied with `apply.ts <id>`. The two components have generators that reproduced their catalog file byte for byte; the repair went into the generator and the catalog file is its output.
- What turned out to be behind most of the remaining red of the first round, and how it was answered:
  1. **The volume was wrong.** Copies meant as mirrored were shifted instead (`forklift`); a frame was open to the ground through an authoring gap (`wheel-loader`); two scales ended 29 mm short of the 1:50 body (`excavator`); the top sheet of one scale lacked the corner trim of the others (`sheet stack`).
  2. **A drawn extrusion had another lateral range than its solid**, or one drawn cylinder stood for two parts and a shaft (`oil-pumpjack` crank hubs, counterweights).
  3. **A drawn shape was the smooth envelope of a body that has relief** (tyres with lugs, a track with grousers, a skid of beams and cross members): redrawn as the solids project.
  4. **Seams of one raster cell** between shapes that the first round added and their neighbours: the added shape now overlaps its neighbour by 3 mm.
  5. **A thin band drawn as a pen stroke** encloses the void beside it, which the guard then reads as closed while the body leaves it open: the band is drawn as its true filled section (`sheet stack` strap).
