# CAS in-process collection custody

The previous guard stored one completion timestamp per root even when the collector threw, found another process's lock, or returned a partial scan. Every local guard then treated it as relief for its own policy and current pressure. A synchronous throw could also settle before the in-flight promise was assigned and leave an already-settled flight permanently installed. The isolated starting version failed the retry case with one total scan where two were required.

The guard now tracks a single owned flight per root and remembers cooldown only after an eligible completed scan. Native soft-cap work reuses the existing verified on-disk scope. Native critical refusal can reuse only a matching scoped receipt from critical relief completed by this process. The scope records whether the measured partition was critical, and the current quote is re-evaluated after asynchronous verification. Custom collectors keep completion only for the same guard and exact policy, partition and pressure reason. Locked, failed, partial, dry and lockless outcomes leave no future completion authority. An incomplete attempt retains prior usage accounting.

Each hard admission can make one cap attempt and one critical attempt, and can await one unrelated flight before it seeks the necessary relief. Continued critical pressure after that bounded attempt produces the typed disk refusal. Cap-only pressure retains the store's soft-over-cap behavior even when collection cannot finish. A later call can attempt relief again. Two critical calls which encounter the same high-pressure flight wait for it and coalesce one separate critical scan. The fixture measures that at most one collector was active.

Explicit enforcement and actual disk-full relief coalesce an already-running scan only in the same effective scope. A direct request that meets incompatible live work can queue one follow-up and remeasure after the other collector settles. The direct calls use no stored cooldown. If an incompatible new flight has already won, the guard finishes the bounded attempt without treating that other scope as relief. The previous direct path failed a separate red fixture by joining a high-pressure scan after the disk had become critical. Both explicit and ENOSPC cases now execute the subsequent critical attempt.
