## Where the timing lines come from
`createOwnerClockV1` (`🧠️memory/🦶️footprint/🟨️.mjs`) writes `owner <name> wall=.. queue=.. publish=.. authority=.. farm=.. observe=.. refs=.. observations=.. release=.. other=..` for owners of 3 s and more. The spans are charged in `inferProject` of `🕸️graph/📥️inference/📦️project-nodes/🟨️.mjs`:
- `farm` = the wait in `farm.prepare` (`timedFarmV1`: dispatch to a child, its reply, adoption of the leaves).
- `observe` = the whole wall time of `observeProjectInferenceV1` (`👁️observation/🟨️.mjs`) MINUS `farm` MINUS `inline` (the inline body). It is therefore not CPU: it is every await of the lookup (`read`, `settle`, `hold`, `reusable`, `offer`, `gate`, the second lookup after the farm) including each wait for the one event loop, which a saturated parent hands out one sync stretch at a time (25 owners queue behind each other).
- `refs` = `resolveClosureRefsV1`; `observations` = `onOwnerObservations`.

## What the parent does in `observe` for one owner
`observeInferenceComputationV1`: `lookup()` (read the stored record, `settleClosureRowsV1` = load and verify, or derive when stale, the closure entries the record names, `projectRowsHoldV1` = probe its file/analysis rows and verify its closure rows), the fleet `gate`, `prepare` (farm), `lookup()` again, then (only when computed inline) the record write.

- In a take the stale entries are NOT derived (`runClosureTakeV1`); in the read of the server they are (`produceClosureEntryV1` derives on the thread, offloaded only after 4 recipes and with two walk children granted).

## Cause found (the biggest one that can be removed without a redesign)
In `lookup()` of `observeInferenceComputationV1` a stored record whose digest is NOT the asked one (written under an older key: any landing, edit of a command input or registry moves the head of the key of every owner) was settled anyway: `else{await settleClosureRowsV1(header.observations,record.value)}`. That record is never reused; the settle loads its closure entries and, in a read (no take), DERIVES every stale one in the parent, a walk of the owner's script closures on the one thread (the stacks above), for every owner with an old record, before the farm child computes the real one. The only thing the else branch is needed for are the `hints` of its file rows (a loop that follows and needs no settle). Test red before, green after: `👁️observation/🧪️tests/🧯️old-record-settle` ("an older record settles no closure entry, the record of the asked key settles its entries").
