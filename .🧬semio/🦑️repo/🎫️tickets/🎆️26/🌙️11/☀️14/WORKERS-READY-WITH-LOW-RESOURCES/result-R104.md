## 1. What a wave is, who publishes it, when
- A wave is a manifest (head with `revision`, the serialized session, the owners in the order the publisher infers them) plus an entry in the `pointer` (the open waves, at most 8, at most 3 h old). Code: `owner-pool/🟦️.ts` (`publishOwnerPoolV1`, `readOwnerPoolPointerV1`), publisher `owner-pool/📣️publisher/🟦️.ts`.
- Only a reader publishes (`createOwnerPoolPublisherV1`, used by `📦️project-nodes/🟨️.mjs`), and only through `miss(count)`: (a) once the take pass (`resolveOwners`: every owner one look at a time, 4774 owners) ended with missing owners, (b) `publishRunning` when an owner starts to infer and `missed.size >= 8`, (c) the heavy wait. All three come after the take pass crawl. So while the take pass runs no wave exists; and a worker read restarts every few minutes, which resets the crawl. It is not a strict deadlock (some read ends its take pass), but the time to the first wave after any revision move equals the crawl of one reader.
- The pools want a wave whose `head.revision` equals the revision of their own record code (`projectRecordKeyTextV1(null)`); a child that opens another one exits 76 and the wave is `declined` for that pool as long as the pointer lists it (`ownerPoolKeepWaveV1`).
- Store at 23:42 to 23:53: 8 waves in the pointer, all published 21:34 to 23:17 under revision `56d8513a` (live `7738520a`, `5484ac8b` between), so none acceptable. First waves of `7738520a`: 23:44:05 and 23:44:13 (workers m05-wlan and m01-wlan, i.e. 3 min after the landing that moved the revision), later 00:28 and 00:31 (also workers). Nothing from the server: its own read (pid 58106, started about 22:07, old code) was still in its take pass.
- Why `reason: None` on an idle pool: `report()` of the runner wrote `reason` anew every loop (`no-wave` only without waves, else the capacity gate's reason, null when it has none), so the `revision` reason a decline wrote was erased within 2 s.
