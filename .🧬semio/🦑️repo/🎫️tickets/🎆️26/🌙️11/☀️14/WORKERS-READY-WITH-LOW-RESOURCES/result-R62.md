## 2. Fixes

2.1 **The closure walk farm ignored the memory plan** (`B/🕸️graph/🧮️derived-nodes/🧩️closure-kinds/🏭️walk-farm/🟨️.mjs` `walkFarmCapV1`, call sites in `closure-kinds/🟨️.mjs` `farmOf` and in `offloadClosureRecipeV1`). The farm of R53 was sized from the granted memory and processors only (up to 8 children of 768 MiB). The project farm already obeys `planFarmWorkersV1` (tree within half the physical memory, minus what the parent holds, capped by the cut of the load `SEMIO_GRAPH_CUT_CONCURRENCY`); the walk farm did not, so on a 16 GB worker the verification of a cold slice (farm starts after 48 derivations) added up to 8 children to a 3-4 GB parent, the tree stood at about 10 GB whatever the cut level, and the load was refused although nothing could be cut any further. Now `size = min(walkFarmSizeV1(grant), planFarmWorkersV1(...))`: on a 16 GB worker with 3 GB held that is 5 children at level 0 and the cut ladder (8/4/2/1) shrinks it, one child is no farm at all (the settle walks in process), a serving machine (24 GB and more) is unchanged. Evidence for the cause is circumstantial (the constant ~10 GB across machines and cut levels, the farm start after 48 derivations, the child budget of 768 MiB x 8 = 6 GB); I could not measure a worker tree.
  - Laws (walk-farm tests, 9 green): `the farm obeys the memory plan of the process like the project farm` (16 GB host: floor((8192-3000)/768) = 6, 8 GB host: 1, cut 2: 2, 32 GB host: Infinity, plan limit off: Infinity); `a settle whose load was cut to one project at a time starts no walk child` (cut 1: 0 answers by children, 120 derivations in process). Control run of the same settle without the cut: 116 of 120 answered by children (red against the old code on this host).
2.2 **`human.json` expires on evidence** (`B/🌐️remote-execution/🛰️sidecar/🚦️ready/🟦️.ts` `readyHumanVerdictV1`, `readyHumanRecordV1`, `readyHumanActiveV1`; schema `Human` and `humanLegacyCommand` in `🧬️schema/🔣️.json`). The wrapper of a person (`native-bootstrap.sh`, switched by atomic rename to a new inode, old sessions keep their file) now writes `{"version":1,"pid":<shell pid>,"startedMs":<ms>}` and refreshes it every 20 s. A marker counts only while it was touched within 90 s AND its pid lives AND its `startedMs` is less than 2 h old (taken from the record, not from the file system's creation time). An empty marker of an older wrapper counts only while a process whose command line contains `native-bootstrap.sh` is in the process list (`ps -A -o args=`; never on Windows), so a marker left by a dead or killed process, or kept alive by nothing, is ignored at once. Cross-platform: pid liveness by `process.kill(pid,0)` (works on Windows), no platform-specific parsing in the verdict.
  - Laws: 8 authored cases in `🧪️tests/🧫️fixtures/🔣️.json` (`human`) evaluated by the module and by the independent Python oracle (`🐍️oracle.py`) give the same booleans; real-process test with a real exited pid (dead owner: ignored), the live test pid (counts), a live owner started 2 h + 1 s ago (does not count), empty marker with and without the wrapper command in the process list, stale touch, a failing `ps`, schema validation of the record.
2.3 **Exit 75 ("run the command again") keeps the first back-off** (`readyBackoffV1(result.exit===75?1:attempts,...)`, policy key `retryExit`): a stage that ends in the retry exit of `repo` (graph too heavy after a cut, wait-for-fleet) is retried after 30 s each time instead of doubling to 20 minutes; real failures still double. Law: `a stage that ends with the retry exit ... waits the first back-off every time`.
2.4 **The `workspace` stage cap is 4 h** (was 1 h): the cut-and-continue cycles of one graph read take 15-20 minutes each at up to 6 steps, a cap of 1 h kills a read that is progressing (stage digests do not contain the timeout, so nothing re-runs). **Windows relative open** errors now read `Windows relative open failed (status 0x...)` (`🧰️framework/🔨️modules/📁️filesystem/🧾️observation/📁️descriptor/🟦️.ts`, message only; transpiles, cannot run on macOS), so the next m05 failure names the NT status.
