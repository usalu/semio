No Job Object: the existing helper needs `rustc` (absent before `tools`) and kill-on-close would kill the resident service of a good stage.
- Orphan reaping (stage start and every 15 s while the log shows a holder wait): lease registry entries (read raw, no freshness rule) alive with registered (pid, start), belonging to this checkout, started inside the window of a stopped try (stalled/stage-timeout/interrupted from `ready-timings.json`, 60 s grace, 2 s edge), outside the `human.json` working window, not in or below the current stage tree. Each reap: bilingual log line (also in the stage log), tail line, `containment`. Verdict per entry (orphan/protected/stage/not-ours/not-alive/identity/window/person/sheltered) mirrored to the store as `ready-log-<stage>-containment.txt`.
- Landing c: the `session` address of a registry entry is NOT evidence of a person (128 of 148 entries on g1-m05 carry one, the worker's own stages included); dropped from the rules.
