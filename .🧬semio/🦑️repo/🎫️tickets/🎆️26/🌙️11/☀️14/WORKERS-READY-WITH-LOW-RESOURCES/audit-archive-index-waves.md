# Archived Fact Index Reads During Recovery

The native graph query asks for rebuild capacity before acquiring structural graph custody. The host quotes the larger of the invocation's current reservation and the learned rebuild estimate capped at 50% of capacity above the operating-system floor. On a 32,768 MiB host with a 4,096 MiB floor the rebuild estimate reaches a 14,336 MiB cap. On a 16,384 MiB host with a 2,048 MiB floor it reaches a 7,168 MiB cap. The existing reservation and fairness rules still govern admission.

The 299 MiB read-only closure census measured discovery of the 442 absent leaves and their 488 affected owners. A repair also verifies the retained frontier and facts, performs owner inference, builds a complete replacement index, and qualifies the candidate. That census leaves the native peak of those stages undetermined. The resident slice worker can get elastic capacity and narrow its owner wave. Its process-tree guard uses a separate plan limit, and direct whole-graph queries run in the command process. A lower rebuild booking would need explicit coupling of the worker guard to the issued grant and native measurement across planning, inference, patching and qualification.

The repair engine now verifies the archived fact index from storage before planning, including when an earlier build left a decoded view in cache. Consecutive waves under the same tombstone reuse the verified plan and check that the archived index still exists. The final patch reopens and authenticates the archived bytes. An index that disappears or changes before patching causes a typed repair refusal before candidate qualification.

An in-memory 488-owner fixture ran 11 waves and reproduced 11 archive reads before the change. Afterward all three cases made two reads. The intact archive published once and kept unaffected fragments. Missing and altered archives left the tombstone in place. At the archive's measured 14,011,996 compressed bytes and 19,574,436 bytes of index-view side tables, nine avoided reads correspond to about 120 MiB of cumulative compressed reads and 168 MiB of cumulative side-table allocation. Those totals do not establish a change in peak resident memory. No live graph, process, or reservation was changed.

The full adaptive-wave suite passed all 24 tests. Seven focused closure repair tests and two host-capacity phase tests also passed.
