## Cause
Not a lost wake-up, not a lease, not the admission governor and not the 10 minute watchdog: the governor admitted the read correctly and `warn` does not stop a running grant. The read's resident service **grew to a 31 GB physical footprint against a 14.3 GB grant** on a 32 GB host (the grant is a reservation, nothing enforces it), so the machine paged: the main thread spent its time in page faults (13 % CPU, resident 130 MB = almost everything compressed or swapped), its farm children sat idle in `__ulock_wait` waiting for a parent that could not run, and the row log stalled at 16 MB. The "fact verification" phase printed one progress unit per 12,288 facts, which at paging speed was one line per 15 minutes, so it looked frozen. The idle farm children are healthy (parent alive, wait is for work), not orphaned; nothing needs to be replaced there. Why the heap reaches 31 GB in the facts/`source.served` phase (426k fact rows plus the row log and edit index held in memory) is NOT established: that is the real fix for the fleet and belongs to the read (R28 resumable manifest, R30 `wanted/📖️read`), which I was told to stay out of. What this package does is make it visible within minutes and bound it.

## What was built (all prepared)
- New `🛰️sidecar/🕸️graph/🔬️stall/🌳️tree/🟦️.ts`: `parseProcessTableV1`, `readTreeRowsV1` (root and descendants), `summarizeReadTreeV1` (running / idle >= 60 s without CPU / paging = state `U`, resident, physical footprint, largest process), `parseFootprintsV1` + `readFootprintsV1` (macOS `footprint`, kB/MB/GB), `measureReadTreeV1`, `memoryOverrunV1` (footprint >= `memoryOverrunFactor` x grant AND host pressure not `normal` AND silent >= `memoryOverrunMilliseconds`). Windows returns an empty table (no change there).
- `🔬️stall/🟦️.ts` `describeGraphReadStallV1(tree?)`: new lines `stallTree` (processes, running, idle, paging, resident, footprint, largest pid) and, when the footprint exceeds the grant, `stallOverrun` ("holds N MB, X times its grant: it pages against the swap and waits for no lease and no queue"). The dump now never needs a manual `sample` for this case.
- `🕸️graph/🟦️.ts` `inferGraphReadV1(measure?)`: once the read has been silent for `memoryOverrunMilliseconds` it measures the tree every `memorySampleMilliseconds`; on overrun it stops the read (SIGINT, SIGKILL 10 s later) with the typed detail `memoryOverrun` (English and German). `runGraph` wires `measure` (tree footprint, grant of the read's pid, host pressure) and the tree into the dump.
- Schema `🙋️wanted/🧬️schema/🔣️.json` `Server.const`: `memoryOverrunFactor` 2, `memoryOverrunMilliseconds` 300000 and `memorySampleMilliseconds` 30000; texts `stallTree`, `stallOverrun`, `memoryOverrun` in both languages.
- Effect on the observed case: the read at 08:07Z would have been stopped after 5 minutes of silence with a footprint of at least 28,672 MB (2 x 14,336 MB) under warn, instead of at 25 minutes, and the next read starts from the published waves (and R28's manifest).
