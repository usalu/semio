A plain lstat walk of the same trees on this host is 9-13k entries/s under load 35. Cause of the 20x gap: `scanJavascriptDependencyInstalledPathsV1` ran the whole authority check (`requireIssuedResourceControlV1`, `checkResourceLeaseControl`, store custody with its refinement walks, each of which walks the control lineage with Map/Set allocation) up to four times per entry.
