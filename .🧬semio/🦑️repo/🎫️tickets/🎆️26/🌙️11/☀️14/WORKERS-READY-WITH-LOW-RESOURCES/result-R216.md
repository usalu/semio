## 4. B: the read infers what the fleet already stored

Code reading (the numbers of R215 section 4 fit it): the owner loop (20 parallel workers, `loop` in `📦️project-nodes/🟨️.mjs`) starts inferring an owner as soon as the pass is released, while the adopt loop looks at the owners the fleet lists only 2 at a time (`adoptConcurrency` 2) and only owners not yet in flight; the look of a native owner is minutes under load, so the loop reaches a listed owner long before the adopt loop and infers it (259 of 439). The adopt looks also wait at the footprint gate that the inferences fill, and the poll after the pass has no counters at all.

- Counters (`createAdoptCountersV1`, texts EN/DE in the take schema, one line every `adoptReportMilliseconds` = 3 minutes through `reportWhilePollingV1`): owners listed, looks by lane (adopt loop, before a local inference, pass), outcomes (fetched, present, stale by the kind of the first failing row, incomplete, missed, faulted), listed owners set aside because they were inferring, waits of looks at the memory gate (count and seconds), owners inferred here although listed. `incomplete` was counted as a miss before; it is its own number now.
- `createHotNamesV1`: the list of the fleet is read from the store at most once per `hotNamesMilliseconds` (10 s) and shared by the pass, the adopt loop, the poll and the owner loop (it was read at every poll and every lane build).
- Adoption has priority: the adopt looks and the poll looks use their own lane (`takeAdmittedV1` with no footprint gate and their own in-flight counter) so they never wait behind the memory the inferences hold; the pass keeps the gate and counts its waits. `takeFetchFirstV1` gets `adoptAttempt`.
- `takeBeforeInferV1`: before the owner loop infers an owner the fleet lists, it looks at it first (the look in flight is shared, one look per owner); a record that is taken or fetched ends the owner (counted adopted), anything else (stale, missed, incomplete, faulted, look thrown) is inferred as before. An owner is not started locally while its look is pending.
- Law `🥇️before-infer` (5): farm model (6 children, look 20 ms, inference 120 ms, 20 workers, 60 owners, every owner stored in the fleet): zero inferences, 60 looks, ends in look time (under 600 ms against 1200 ms of inferences), the control (old loop) infers 60; stale/miss/incomplete/fault/unlisted owners are inferred, a taken owner is not looked at; one look for two askers; the adopt lane takes 12 listed owners while the pass lane is blocked at the gate for good (control: the same look behind the blocked gate never starts); wiring text laws. `📊️adopt-counters` (3): 200 seeded event sequences equal a Python model, EN/DE line with the same fields, hot names read once per interval.
- Red: `takeBeforeInferV1` made to always infer: 3 of 5 fail.
- NOT built (said plainly): cancelling a LOCAL inference that is already running when the look adopts (the inference of a listed owner now starts only after its look failed, so the remaining case is an owner that becomes listed while it runs; it is counted as `inferredListed` and cannot be avoided without plumbing a signal through `inferProject`); a separate look charge "derived from measured look footprints": the look job already carries `weight 0.01` per owner in the farm and the parent holds no look memory, so the lane is separated by gate and in-flight counter, not by a measured MB figure (no look footprint is measured by the code today); the listing defect of R215 section 4 (`pool-published` lists only owners a claim found stored, not owners the pool published itself) is still open.
- Cost: an owner whose fleet record is stale is looked at twice (once before, once by the inference's own look), about 130 of 4785 on the numbers of R215.

## 5. C: what the whole read does at the end of the owner pass

Code (`🕸️graph/📥️load/🟦️.ts` `loadBuildGraphAttemptV1`, R156 landed 2026-11-17 22:22, so the running read carries it): a whole read (selection scope) and a selected read run the same function. After the owner pass (`discoverOnce`, hours) it re-reads the inventory and checks every observed row against the live tree (`buildGraphSourceFactsHoldV1`, collecting stale rows); if every row holds and the membership and admission did not move it seals; otherwise it prints `[graph-repair] {round, stale, staleCount, membershipMoved, admissionMoved}`, follows the new inventory and runs another owner pass; after `repairRounds` (4) it seals with the records it has: `[graph-seal] {mode, rounds, sealedWithLaterChanges, laterChangedOwners, membershipMoved, admissionMoved, owners}` (the verdict and per-owner digests are also in the authority receipt `seal`). So it does NOT chase the tree forever: at most 1 + 4 owner passes, then it seals. Implementation files are not facts of the tree for a running process (R181 `registerLoadedImplementationRowsV1`), so the landings of this window (and every landing) cannot cause the `unrefreshed-facts` exit (`[graph-retry]`) that R154 had; that exit remains only for a non-implementation fact that a re-inference leaves unchanged.
What it costs: each repair round is a whole discovery over 4785 owners (the take pass of today's read took 73 minutes to look at 4658 owners with 8 children, most of them present), plus the re-inference of the owners whose rows moved since they were observed (edits every ~20 minutes touching tens of owners are hundreds by the end of a 6 to 12 hour pass). Expect after the end of the owner pass (R215 projection: not before ~01:30 UTC, probably later) up to four more passes of roughly one hour or more each, then the seal with `sealedWithLaterChanges` and the stale owners named in `laterChangedOwners`. Nothing breaks and nothing needs to be fixed before the read gets there; one thing NOT verified: no real read of this size reached the seal yet (the model law `🌊️seal-under-churn` of R156 drives the real function with 200 seeded worlds).
