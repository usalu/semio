## 2. The design (built in the scratch copy, tested, packaged)

Target: every core of every machine busy, minimal coordination. `owners/min ~= cores x 60 / CPU seconds per owner` per machine.

1. **One child process per core, one owner at a time** (`OP/🧒️child`): a long-lived process that opens the wave once, then claims, infers and publishes; replaced (exit 75) after an owner when its resident size passed `clamp(6 % of memory, 1.2 to 3 GB)` or after 30 min (the allocator never returns memory, a fresh process does), killed above `max(3 GB, 30 %)`. It reports `ready`, `beat`, `done` (wall, CPU, resident, peak), `fail`, `end` as JSON lines; it stops after its owner when its input closes (the supervisor died). The inference driver (`OP/🔬️inference`) is the old per-wave code moved out (same claim, failure note, publish).
2. **The supervisor** (`OP/🏃️runner`, sidecar role `pool`): stays small (about 100 MB, 0.9 s of CPU for 80 owners), follows the pointer, writes each wave once to a private directory, keeps `ownerPoolTargetV1(...)` children running (at most 2 start and 1 drains per 2 s look), measures the children's own resident sizes and uses the newest 5 plus 20 % as the size of the next child (no learned parent growth, no worst-case guess). A pointer fault keeps the waves; a wave a child could not open backs off 30 s; a child that dies in under 10 s backs the wave off; a finished wave reopens after 60 s.
3. **Admission that separates the machine's own work from idle headroom** (`ownerPoolTargetV1`, pure, with a Python oracle): cores less the cores its grants book, less a quarter of the cores while its own requests wait, less the load that does not come from the children (ceiling 1.5 per core); memory: available less the system floor (12.5 %, 1.5 to 4 GB) less the untouched part of its grants' reservations, divided by the child size, added to the children that already hold theirs; warn pressure never grows the pool; **one child always runs** (a pool starved by the machine's own queue helps nobody) except under critical pressure or swap thrash. The sidecar (`🏊️pool`) feeds it from the host capacity gate view (`ownerPoolHostOfV1`: the gate's grants are the machine's own work, the pool's children are no grants).
4. **Claims that scale** (`pullOwnerPoolV1`): each child starts at a point of the list given by a digest of its name (mulberry32, equal in Python), walks it downwards claiming a window of 4 neighbours in one round trip (neighbours share imports, so the program stays warm), keeps one granted ahead, jumps to a random place after 4 stored or held owners in a row and ends after 3 jumps that found only stored owners (so no puller looks at every owner of a wave: the old loop made every puller query all 4 774). Held retry: a quarter of the owner's expected time, 2 to 15 s.
5. **A stable manifest followed incrementally**: pointer version 2 is a list of open waves (newest last, at most 8, none older than 3 h); publishing adds a wave by a swap against the pointer read just before, so a newer wave never replaces one a puller works on; each child serves one wave, the supervisor spreads children over the 3 newest open waves.
6. **Status** (`pool.json`): children, target, claimAttempts, recycled, ownersPerMinute (5 min), childMegabytes, waves, reason with the English and German text.

Group server (items from the coordinator, all in the package unless marked): farm replies carry `peakMegabytes` (the child's peak over its resident size at the start of the job) and the governor takes it as the cost and the learned peak of a farmed owner (`measured`), not the parent's growth; **new cost file `owner-costs.3.json` and a new fleet table state (`costs: "3"`), no migration** (old inflated samples are not read); the **60 s rule fires beside parked owners** when the smallest waiting owner fits the memory that is really free and the one-owner grant (`🧠️memory/🟨️.mjs`, text in both languages); **parked owners say why**: an owner with 0 rows after 120 s is named every 60 s with its stage (inferring in a farm child or queued, resolving closure references in the parent thread, recording rows).
**Not built**: a "recent window plus margin" for the reservation (it changes the independent model of the governor and 6 existing tests; with exact child peaks and the new file the reservation is the largest of the last 8 real samples, and the 10 % safety margin of the headroom stays), the farm vs governor distinction inside the parked stage (an existing structural test pins `away(()=>farm.prepare(`), making the server's own inference the last resort (its wave still walks from the head in parallel; it takes records of pullers through `port.gate` as before), and the shared closure derivation off the parent thread (R57 section 3).
