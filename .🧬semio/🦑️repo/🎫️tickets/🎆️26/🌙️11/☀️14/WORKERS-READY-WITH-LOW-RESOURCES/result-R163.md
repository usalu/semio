# R163: the 4 to 7 GB "single owner" refusals on 16 GB workers (living report)

Scratch: `scratchpad/R163/` (probe copies of `☀️16/SHOOTING-PLUGIN-END-TO-END/farm-child-*.mjs`). No landing yet (windows now hh:00-05 and hh:30-35 UTC; every test must be run in scratch first).

## 1. What the numbers are (code reading, verified in the sources)

- The text "(about N MB)" of `graph.owner-too-heavy` is `named()` of the take loop (`project-nodes/🟨️.mjs`): `enforcement.known(name)` = `estimateOwnerCostV1` over ALL recorded samples of the owner (max of the newest samples, each halved per age half-life, lowered to the host ceiling) times `footprintFactor` (2). Unit: footprint MB, but built from MANAGED samples (JS heap + external) of any kind: lax-GC in-process peaks measured on 32 GB hosts, wave estimates and farm stops. It is NOT the size of a stopped child.
- The decision that put the owner into `never` is separate: `neverHere` with a farm looks only at samples with `stopped:true`, ages them, multiplies by 2 and compares with `farmAloneCapV1(host)` (4200 MB on 16 GB). So the refusal text mixes two different numbers: the displayed one (all samples) and the deciding one (stopped samples only). Neither is shown with its provenance.
- A stopped sample is written by `setAsideOverrunV1` (`graph.farm-overrun`) as `takenBytes = error.megabytes*1MiB/2`, `stopped:true`; `error.megabytes` is the footprint (macOS `footprint -p`, Linux smaps Pss) of the farm child that ran the job, read every `footprintMilliseconds` by `measure` in `project-farm/🏊️pool/🟨️.mjs` and compared with the cap. Real stops seen earlier (workers/*/round-31.md): 4231 to 4837 MB with cap 4200, always barely above the cap: a child that grows until killed.
- Attribution defect (code): `job.footprint` is the footprint of the CHILD PROCESS, which is reused: an `alone` job is dispatched to the next idle slot, not to a fresh child, and children are recycled only by `recycleReasonV1` (footprint above max(declared, 1.5 x baseline)) or 96 weighted jobs; warm/look jobs weigh 0.01 and read no footprint unless `suspect`. So the footprint recorded as "needed by owner X even when it ran alone" includes whatever earlier jobs (look batches of many owners, warm jobs, other owners) left in the same process; `measure` also stops at the first reading above the cap, so the first reading of a dirty child can be the whole "peak". The overrun also fires for `observe`/`warm` jobs, which carry the owner name.

## 3. Fixes (task 3) and laws

1. `project-farm/🏊️pool/🟨️.mjs` `pump`: a job that runs alone is only dispatched to a child that served no job (`slot.served === 0`); idle children that served jobs are retired first (they come back fresh on demand). By construction the footprint recorded for an alone overrun is the owner's own. Law `project-farm/🧪️tests/🧊️alone-fresh` (new): three children serve one small job each, a heavy job passes the cap, its alone run must be in a pid none of them has: RED on the live code (`the alone run is in a fresh child`, received true), green staged. Staged battery with the patch: `🧯️cap` 4, `🦶️footprint-recycle` 3, `♻️retirement` 3, `🥇️first-child` 5, `🧮️admission` 2, `🛑️hold` 3, `🔒️farm-close` 2, `🪦️orphans` 2, `🧽️retained` 2, all 0 fail.
2. `project-nodes/🟨️.mjs` `graphOwnerEnforcementV1`: `stopped(name)` = footprint of the recorded STOP samples only (the number `neverHere` decides on), and the refusal text (`named`) names that number instead of the maximum over all samples (lax-heap managed peaks of 32 GB hosts x 2). Law `🧪️tests/🐘️never-here` 4th test (cost-table unit law: a `mixed` owner with a 3358 MB non-stopped sample of a 32 GB host and a 1500 MB stop of a 16 GB host names 3000, a never-stopped owner names none and is not refused): RED live (`enforcement.stopped is not a function`), 4 pass staged.
