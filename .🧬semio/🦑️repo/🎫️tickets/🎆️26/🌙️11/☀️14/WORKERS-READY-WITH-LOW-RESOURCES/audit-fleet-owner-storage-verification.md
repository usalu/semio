# Fleet Owner Storage Verification Cost

At about 03:08 UTC m05 and m12 were in live workspace pass two. m05 had completed 472 of 596 owners, including 294 fetched from the fleet. Its 217 native pre-verifications totalled 11,419,784 ms. The directory backend recorded 5,876,747 ms waiting for mapping admission and 5,540,354 ms in allocated-usage measurement. The inventory bodies totalled 33,934 ms. m12 had completed 277 of 596 owners, including 146 fetched. Its 369 pre-verifications totalled 4,890,287 ms. Its mounted-image reservation reported 4,819,402 ms in journal work, which includes nested physical verification. These counters measure inclusive elapsed time and can overlap. They identify the storage boundary, with no estimate of host CPU cost.

The presence coalescer previously separated otherwise compatible requests by cancellation-signal identity and checked names again when a caller had queued before an earlier batch established the same name in the validation round. The native tests reproduced each behavior. The new group retains the same issued storage control, verification limits, pre- and post-verification and per-caller error attribution. A mixed caller cannot cancel the shared check while another observer still needs it. Queued work rechecks the existing successful within-round memo before opening another verified batch.

Separately, the owner publisher treats a bulk keep-alive as renewal of its accepted manifest. The loopback case removes a single unit from a 500-owner wave and verifies that renewal keeps the accepted roster and both CAS payloads. An explicit successful shrink still establishes a new wave. A refused candidate or a forced targeted ask leaves the accepted bulk wave intact. [Owner pool keepalive audit](audit-owner-pool-keepalive-roster.md)


An additional capacity edge opened two verified operations for overlapping callers even though their union fit the batch limit. The fixture's SQLite query returned three distinct names under a limit of three. The old implementation opened two operations for those names. The corrected admission counts only distinct additions when the upper bound would exceed the limit. All 12 focused coalescing and inventory-counter cases passed, with 169 assertions.
