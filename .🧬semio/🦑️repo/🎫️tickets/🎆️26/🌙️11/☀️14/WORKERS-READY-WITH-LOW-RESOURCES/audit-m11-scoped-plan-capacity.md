# m11 Scoped Plan Capacity

The replicated g1-m11 workspace is running and not Ready. Its retained native tail shows a selected graph reservation of 4,188 MiB with a 2,048 MiB minimum and a separate 2,048 MiB host floor. At 1,095 seconds queued it reported 985 MiB usable and nine running grants with four queued requests. At 20:05 UTC a later line still reported 1,282 MiB usable and a swap stop. The pool was already idle at 64 MiB, zero slots and zero children, with its own waiting reason. The worker mirror does not list the nine live grant holders or identify memory that could safely be returned.

The resident service’s `slice-pool` computes the requested amount from the host-scoped `slice:graph` history and the declared unlearned, cap and minimum controls, then calls `reserveGraphPlanCapacityV1` before constructing a worker. On a 16 GiB host the policy’s 40% cap is about 6,553 MiB. The 4,188 MiB request is consistent with learned sizing, but m11’s estimate store is not replicated. The existing gate permits a smaller full-scope run when the actual headroom meets 2,048 MiB and pressure permits it. The wave shrinks with the grant after admission. At the reported 985 MiB the minimum is short by 1,063 MiB. Changing the learned wanted amount would leave that shortfall and the swap gate.

The file `slice-memory.json` was mirrored at 20:04, but its actual sample was 19:45:29.480 UTC. PID 45003 reported scoped discovery, 210,495 distinct source observations, 356 completed owner checks and 796 MiB process-tree footprint. Its Ready stage identity began at 19:18:47.920 UTC. Its exact settled-read byte counter was 29,750,643 bytes and its row store held 40,945,026 bytes. The report’s 359,346,050-byte estimated settled-read collection and 64,943,728-byte estimated scope map are sampled estimates, with possible overlap. They represent the earlier worker and do not establish the footprint of this queued attempt or of another live grant. The current worker-side cache disks and spill files are not exposed by this mirror.

The 32 GiB coordinator is at a different custody boundary. At 20:09:41 UTC, PID 24613 observed 474 MiB and was still queued for the exact 14,336 MiB whole-graph rebuild with 8,310 MiB host headroom, a 4,096 MiB floor and 19,510 MiB swap in use. Its current whole generation remains quarantined. Its cold fragment, index, post-seal stamp and retirement optimizations do not change m11’s pre-worker selected reservation.

Two selected-read changes can reduce future admitted work. The current `inventory` reads and validates an aggregate memo but eagerly expands every per-owner row-ID list before asking whether the aggregate holds. Trying aggregate verification before seeding, then materializing only the owner rows used for stale recovery, avoids a redundant resident copy while preserving the existing per-owner verification, checkpoint and drift checks. A second candidate is streaming the selected publication’s authority records into its private temporary file under the existing byte bound, retaining the same ordering, nested observation references, exact digest and atomic graph/authority installation. The 19:45 sample demonstrates inventory, not selected serialization, so it cannot assign either cost to the current wait. Each candidate needs source-drift and cancellation fixtures comparing exact owner closure, facts and sealed bytes before its performance claim can be applied to m11.
