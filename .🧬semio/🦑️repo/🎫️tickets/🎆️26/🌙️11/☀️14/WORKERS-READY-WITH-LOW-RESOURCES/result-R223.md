## 2. Does the stall detector ignore heartbeat lines, and is a typed progress line killed too?

Yes and yes, proven by code and by a law that is red on the base:

- `readyStallTrackerV1` takes a line as progress only when its normalised form is new; `normalisePatterns` replace every number with a time unit (`1800 s`, `30 s`, `Sekunden`) and timestamps by `#`. `[repository:command] still running after N s`, `[repo] still working after N s; last phase started: X` and the R222 hook line `… has run N s of at most 1800 s` are therefore one line forever: no progress. CPU liveness (`readyLivenessV1`) is read only through `ps` (`!windows`), so on Windows a busy but silent child has no sign of life at all.
- The R222 hook bound is 1800 s, the stall window 600 s: a legitimate tectonic verification (observed > 1359 s) would have been stopped as `stalled` after 600 s, before its own typed timeout could speak. R222's declared wait and the detector disagreed.
- What the detector does accept as a declared wait is a step line (`stepLinePatterns`, the slice worker form `… <step en> / <step de> <done> of <bound>.`): the count without unit is fresh each beat and the step is overdue only after its bound plus the silent window (`readyStepOverdueSecondsV1`), which is later than any bound it declares.
