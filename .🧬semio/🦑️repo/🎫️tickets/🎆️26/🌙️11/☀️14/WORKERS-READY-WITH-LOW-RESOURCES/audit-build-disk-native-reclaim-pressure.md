Normal build disk intake used the automatic collector whenever the configured cache volume was short of the command’s need and reserve, but omitted the measured pressure context. Its reclaim child therefore used the routine predicate and could refuse an image that the legitimate automatic predicate would consider. This reproduces without acquiring a real image lease. The regression sees the original volume context as absent and independently derives the required target, 19 GiB for the modeled 7 GiB command plus 12 GiB reserve.

The ingress now binds the same existing recovery target to the measured cache volume and a live reader for that volume. A changed or missing volume reading supplies no new reclaim opportunity. The real writer verifies the device from the measured mount and considers images on that backing device, including for any ordinary fallback. Only the in-place child receives the existing pressure receipt. This route does not inspect detached images or authorize the special pressure-driven whole-image retirement path. The mounted same-device fallback still enters the ordinary regrow owner with its existing checks. Explicit Ready stage pressure keeps its existing behavior. The in-place child retains its existing issued control, exact device, live pressure and slack rechecks and nonwaiting exclusive lease. Admission reserve, lab floor, retry policy and physical image capacity stay fixed.

The test fixture covers a different-volume backing, a detached same-volume backing, a held same-volume Rust image, and an eligible same-volume dependency image. The native wrapper confirms the exact target and device arrive at reclaim only. SQLite independently models the order of eligible actions and the admission target. No manual pressure marker, live image operation, worker signal or deployment was issued.
