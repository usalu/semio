## 1. What `look` computed in the parent (the stacks, mapped)

The stacks of `7756.log` end in `takePassV1 < takeAdmittedV1 < look` (`project-nodes/🟨️.mjs`, `const look`): `look` runs `inferProject(job, takeAway)` inside `runClosureTakeV1` and `takeScope.run` for every owner, on the parent thread. Before the farm is asked anything, `inferProject` (frames `<anonymous>@538` = its `yieldInferenceV1`, then the directory visits) did, per owner:
- `existsSync(<nativeRoot>/Cargo.toml)` (an observed, recorded fact) and the scan of the target commands for `native`; the `pipeline` flag.
- `projectAssemblyAuthorityV1` for every native or release owner: `nativeDependencyRoots` walks the Cargo path-dependency closure of the crate, each directory visit a `memoizedObservedComputationV1` (`compute@276 ... visit@295 ... existsSync@257/237`, each `existsSync` goes through `record@1182`, which also pulses `footprintPulseV1`, hence the "starved sample" frames), then hashes the result into the `head` of the record key.
- Then `observeProjectInferenceV1`: reading the record and the verdict (child, R122) and the references (child, R122).
So of what a look is, the Cargo closure walk with its observed visits and the recorded `existsSync` were still in the parent; the label "inferring in this process" is `STAGE_INFER_V1`, the stage every owner carries while `inferProject` runs outside the verdict and reference waits, which is why the walk shows under it.

Live numbers (log `7756.log` at 12:43 UTC, 39 minutes into the read, 148 of 4775 done): 116 finished owners with a phase line spent 1672 s in `authority` (mean 14 s, one owner 42 s: the wall time of a walk on a thread whose event-loop lag reached 193 s), 15 212 s in `observe` (waiting for children), 35 424 s in `queue` (governor slots), 1928 s in `refs`; 181 `slow owner-inference` lines. 5 of the 6 starved samples inside the take pass are in the authority walk (`existsSync` under `compute`/`visit` under `memoizedObservedComputationV1`), the sixth in `yieldInferenceV1` (the pulse that fires at the start of `inferProject`).

## 2. What runs in children now

New member `📦️project-nodes/🔭️look-queue` (schema first: `🧬️schema/🔣️.json` policy and EN/DE texts; `🟨️.mjs`; `🟨️.d.mts`; tests):
- `createLookQueueV1`: the take pass hands it its lanes (`start`), it cuts them into batches of at most 6 owners (`lookBatchSizeV1`: enough for every child to be fed 6 times, one slice of each lane in turn, `lookBatchesV1`), dispatches a batch as one farm job (`farm.observe` with `look: true`), at most as many at once as children are ready (re-read at every settlement and when the farm grows: `farm.onReady` kicks), keeps the answers nobody asked for yet below a lead of 3 jobs per child, moves the batch of an owner that asks first to the front, answers an owner once, asks again after 60 s, answers undefined when the farm is gone and rejects with `farmJobRefusedV1` (the owner is set aside with its reason, EN and DE) when the farm could not look.
- `lookOwnersV1` (child, `project-nodes/🟨️.mjs`, served by `👷️service` `job.look`): for each owner of a batch `ownerJobV1` (native, pipeline, assembly authority, head, in the child with its own `nativeCache` and `nativeClosures`), then the existing verdict and the closure references (`observeProjectRecordV1`, the share of the per-read verdict directory of R119 is unchanged), answered per owner `{name, native, pipeline, head, verdict, headerDigest, refs}` or `{name, failed}` (one owner never fails its batch), weight 0.01 per owner.
- Parent `inferProject`: asks the queue first (stage `STAGE_LOOK_V1`, EN/DE, "the parent computes nothing"), takes `native`, `pipeline` and `head` from the answer (no `existsSync`, no authority walk), uses the verdict and the offered references of the look when it judged the record the parent holds (same header digest), else asks a child again as before.
- Guard (observation `🚧️read-parent`): kind `observe`; `projectAssemblyAuthorityV1` refuses in the parent of a read with a farm; `withTakeLookScopeV1` wraps every look of the take pass and `noteObservedInReadParentV1` counts each `memoizedObservedComputationV1` and each observed `record` that still runs there (`readParentObservedCountsV1`); the read says the count in both languages when it is not zero.
