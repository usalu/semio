2. **What the slice read does per owner when every record is available, and what a hit costs.**
   - Counters (code): `owners N of M` is the phase `owners` of `loadGenerationGraphV1` (`LIB/🕸️graph/🕰️generations/🔌️load/🟨️.mjs`, `compute`): `ownerDone` once per finished owner, summed over all waves of the read (`before + inferred` of `spec.done`/`spec.total`); that is why m06 shows `69 of 659` while one wave has 48. `build:projects · Inferring projects: n of 48` is the same loop inside one wave (`emojiProjectJsonNodes`, `yieldInferenceV1(context,"build:projects",…)`). `build:project-assembly` is called once per owner that is NOT a hit (`inferProjectBodyV1`). `build:closure-entry-rows` is the cooperative verification of the stored rows of a closure entry (`closureEntryHoldsAsyncV1`). `graph:slice-worker` is the issued resource control of the slice worker process.
   - `running (name, kind, seconds, rows)`: the owners that hold a slot of the governor; `kind` is `language:cargo` when the root has a `Cargo.toml` (`ownerKindV1`); `seconds` are since the slot was taken; `rows` is `observedInferenceRowsV1(cachePath)`, a counter that exists ONLY while the body of a MISS records its observations. So `language:cargo, 133 s, 0 rows` is not a miss that records nothing: it is an owner that is a hit (or waits for a farm worker, or for a closure) and costs seconds outside any counted computation.
   - A hit is not adopted for free. Per owner: the record is read and decoded (brotli), `settleClosureRowsV1`, then EVERY observation row of the record is checked against the tree (`current(header.observations)`), `inferenceInventoryReferencesHoldV1`, and the rows are merged into the capture of the wave (shared row objects). Then, because the records are written in `closure-references/4` form (`late`), `resolveClosureRefsV1` ANSWERS the closure recipes of the record: `produceClosureEntryV1` takes the entry from the in-process cache, else from the durable closure store of THIS machine (`…/inference/selections/<id>/ce/{e,l,v}`), checks its rows against the tree, and only then, when it is absent or stale, DERIVES it: a walk of the TypeScript or Cargo source closure (`produceClosureRecipeV1`).
   - PROVEN by code and sizes: the fleet moves only the document `record` (`🪪️graph-state/📦️project-records/🟦️.ts`, `documentsOf`); the closure entries are local. On the server `ce` holds 40 675 entries, 32 078 layers and 3 384 values in 76 131 files, **2.4 GB**, against 579 MB for the 7 457 project records and 27.7 MB of fragments: the answers are four times the size of the records and no worker receives them. About 8.5 entries per project: a slice of 659 owners needs about 5 600 entries and 4 400 layers (about 0.3 GB) that a fresh worker derives. Also: the entry id contains the workspace root (`workspaceRoot + "\0" + recipeKey`) and the rows hold absolute paths, and the server's root (`/Users/Shared/semio`) differs from the workers' (`/Users/ueli/Documents/semio`), so the entries would need the same root neutralisation the project records have.
