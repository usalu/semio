## 6. Task 3: fetch instead of infer. PREMISE DOES NOT HOLD AS STATED

R192's 91 of 100 sampled fleet records "fresh" are records of the fleet published by whole-read pools for this very wave and of the workers' admission reads. The 590 owners the workers published are SELECTED-read (admission) records: `ownerAdmissionSelectionV1` reduces the project to the selected targets (`activeJson = {...json, targets: only the selected ones}`), `inferProjectBodyV1(..., admission)` passes `selectedTargets ?? []` and `selectedInputs` to `projectWithDefaults`, and `projectRecordKeyV1` is `[...head, root, json, selectedTargets ?? null, selectedInputs]`. So an admission record (a) is filed under another key text and (b) describes only part of the owner's targets. A whole read needs every target; seeding it with the subset would be a wrong graph, not a faster one. The law "a record published by a selected read is accepted by a whole read for the same owner inputs" is only true for owners whose selection covers all targets, and there the key would additionally have to be normalized and the content proven equal (`native`/`pipeline` are decided by the SELECTED targets). I did not build it: no evidence that it applies to more than a handful of the 590 owners, and the unproven equality is a correctness risk.
