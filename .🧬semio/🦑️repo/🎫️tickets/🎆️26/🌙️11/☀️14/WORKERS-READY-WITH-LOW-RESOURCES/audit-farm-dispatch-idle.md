# Farm selection under an observed hold

Read 67785 reported 69 of 4,808 owners decided. Its oldest zero-row waits were two farm inference requests and one farm look. The farm snapshots showed nine busy slots and roughly one queued job per subpool. An outer process-tree observation at 02:53 counted 18 processes, with 7 running, 1 idle, and 3 paging, and a footprint of 7,985 MiB. Those operating-system states do not establish dispatcher availability. The shared slice-memory file belonged to an independent scoped discovery. The owner pool had published its initial 4,808-owner wave, while later publications included a 20,183 ms CAS deadline and opened-breaker refusals.

The farm's pool selector preferred the first pool with a physically idle worker. With one active job and a hold, the same pool's dispatcher declines further jobs even if another worker is idle. A closed native fixture with two pools reproduced the mismatch. It selected the first pool while its active worker was gated and while the second pool could run the next job under the same hold. The unheld control dispatched to the first pool.

Selection now applies the dispatch predicate to current slot and queue state and the hold observation already taken by the normal pump. It also excludes idle workers that would need to be replaced for a fresh solo job. Selection does not resample host pressure or release caches. When no immediately dispatchable worker is advertised, the existing usable-pool fallback still queues the work and the pump takes a fresh hold decision. The native regression lifts the hold after selection's retained sample and proves that the remaining sole pool dispatches the new job before the original gated job ends.

The neutral fixture and SQLite independently select the same eligible pool in both native cases. All 14 farm hold, queue ordering, fresh solo worker and footprint cap checks passed. This establishes the selection defect under the reproduced conditions. The live samples do not establish which subpool accepted each of the observed jobs. No live process, lease, or store was modified.
