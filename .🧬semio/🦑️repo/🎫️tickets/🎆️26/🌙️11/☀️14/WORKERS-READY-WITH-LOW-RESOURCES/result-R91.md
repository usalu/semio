## 3. Fix (landed 19:30 UTC, `landing.lock` 45 s, 11 files)

3.1 **A take never derives** (`script-inputs` `runClosureTakeV1`, `takenClosureEntryV1`): the look of a stored record runs inside an `AsyncLocalStorage` scope; in it `produceClosureEntryV1` loads and checks the stored entry (as the first step of a derivation does) and otherwise ends the look with `BUILD_TAKE_MISS` (`miss`), joining only a flight that a governed caller already started. The owner is then inferred by the governed owner loop (owner limit, `owner-too-heavy` hand-off, fleet wanted/claim, plan guard), where one heavy owner is accounted and bounded, instead of the ungoverned take. No walk and no walk farm start from a take.

3.2 **Heavy owners one at a time, after the light ones** (`take` `takeSplitV1`, `project-nodes`): owners the cost table calls `alone` (recorded or seeded cost above the grant) or `slow` are looked at after every light lane, concurrency 1.

3.3 **Walk children are recycled at what the plan reserved** (walk-farm schema `recycleBytes` 1 610 612 736 -> 805 306 368 = `childBytes`): the tree of a farm stays within its reservation (up to the overshoot of one answer).

3.4 **Instrument** (worker report, `sources.owners.looks`): `active` (name and seconds of the looks in flight) and `slowest` (five longest looks), so the next stop names the owner.

## 5. Not verified / consequences

- 10 GB itself was never reproduced here (section 2). The landing removes one unbounded part of the take (derivation in the parent); the m07/m08 burst that remains is a different look (section 4) and is unfixed.
- Consequence to watch: owners whose entries are stale are no longer "taken" but `miss` and go to the owner loop (and the fleet). The take count (`taken N of 4774`) will therefore stay low on a churning tree while the work moves to the loop: the right place, not a faster one. R89's warm pass (children that bring entries up to date and persist them) is what makes them present for the take; the two compose, and without the warm pass nothing derives in the parent's take any more.
- The warm pass itself (R89) puts up to 10 children on m07 (5.0 GB rss at 240 s, parent 1.3 GB, tree about 6.3 GB against 6.55 GB): close to the limit; its sizing should use the 1.5 GiB-class children measured here, not 768 MiB (3.3 lowers children that are walk-farm children, not the warm children of R89).
