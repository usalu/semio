## 5. `/proc/self/statm` (for the replica agent, nothing edited)
- No replica source reads `/proc/self/statm` or `process.memoryUsage`. - Most probable: during adoption of 279 313 entries a loop grows the large arrays by exact counts, each growth copies the array and schedules a forced full GC; a full GC on a heap of hundreds of MB takes milliseconds, so a few hundred per second keep the event loop blocked with one core busy. The rate of 1300 reads per second is closer to a cheap call than to a full GC, so the alternative is a runtime-internal poll triggered by the same allocation pattern. Not reproduced (Linux only). Suggested check on the Linux worker: `strace -f -e trace=openat,read -p <daemon>` for the call stack timing, or count `Bun.gc` calls with a temporary `[DEBUG]` counter in `settleHeap`.
- Fix direction for that agent: grow geometrically everywhere (`reserve` with `Math.max(count, length*1.5)`), and rate-limit `settleHeap` by time (at most once per second) instead of by call site.
