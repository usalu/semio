## Measured cost of one binding on Windows
No per-binding native numbers exist yet: the stage ended before the profile line reached the log (the log ring keeps 16 KB, 91 lines at the end). What the live logs do give:
- Unchanged binding (already published by an earlier try): bindings 1 to 200 passed in about 35 s of the try (after the replay ended at 2005 s, `publish-binding:200` at about 2040 s): 0.17 s per binding including the pair-open, the endpoint comparison and the interim fences.
- Changed binding (link swap): `publish-binding:200` at about 2040 s and `:208` at about 2090 s: 6 s per binding, in a window without a full fence (fences are every 64 bindings, so 200 to 208 has none). R77 saw the same 6 s on try 8 (112 at 1420 s, 192 at 1900 s).
- Full fence: ownership walk 1.4 M steps at 455 per second on average and 1780 lately (3086 s into the try), i.e. about 0.56 ms per lstat-like step; `preservation-ownership` 291 k to 816 k in 5 minutes. A full ownership recheck is therefore 10 to 13 minutes on this host; the budget (`🧮️fence`, factor 8) keeps fences at one ninth of the stage.
- So the 35-fold difference unchanged to changed (0.17 s to 6 s) sits in the part that only the changed path runs.

## Causes, from code (`LIB/⚡️caching/🚀️bootstrap/📦️dependencies/💾️storage/🟦️.ts` line 789 loop, `publishJavascriptDependencyPreservingSourceDescriptorV1` line 635, and `🔨️modules/📁️filesystem/🧾️observation/📁️descriptor/🟦️.ts`)
Per binding the producer opens the common ancestor by handle, re-walks every path component from it twice (`capture(parts)` in `withJavascriptDependencySourceDirectoryPairV1`, O(depth squared) opens), then the Windows `publishBinding` does: roster enumeration of the parent three times (`creationNames`), `readEntry` three times, a stage directory create, a reparse-point set, a rename (plus open/rename/delete-disposition of the old link), and THREE `FlushFileBuffers` (staged child, final child, parent). Every guard (`bindingGuard`, about 25 per binding) runs `internal()`, which re-reads the full `facts()` of the held parent: GetFileType, four `GetFileInformationByHandleEx`, `GetFileInformationByHandle`, `GetKernelObjectSecurity`, a stream query and for links a reparse read, about 9 native calls each. POSIX measured on this Mac with the new counters: one link replace is about 107 `fstat` plus 10 `openat` plus 8 `readlinkat` (a first publish 53 `fstat`), so on Windows the order is 1000 native calls per binding.
Which of these costs the 6 s is NOT measured. Ranked by my reading: (1) the three physical flushes (a Windows flush forces the disk; macOS `fsync` does not, which fits "100 to 800 times slower than macOS"; the unchanged path flushes nothing and costs 0.17 s); (2) the guard re-reads (9 calls each, about 25 per binding); (3) path re-walk and roster enumerations; (4) Defender on the created and deleted directory entries.
