# Package P2: one graph builder per machine, grants that hold, a refresh that is watched by progress, a parent that adopts (2026-11-14, landed 17:34 after LAND-OK)

Landing: `T/landings.md`, "Package P2 landing (17:34 …)"; T = `…/☀️13/FLEET-ONE-MACHINE-SHARED-HOT-CACHE-AND-REMOTE-EXECUTION/`. Scripts (anchored patch list, overlay runner): `T/🧰️scripts/P2/`. No graph, build or `repo` command was run on this Mac; every proof is a unit or integration test.

## Verdicts

- **(a) single flight: FIXED in code and proven by tests, not observed live.** The custody never let two builders run (disjoint owners aside); what ran twice was (1) a reader that found the custody held PREPARED the same projects beside the builder (the builder's waves take the first 48 stale owners in code point order, exactly the first claim chunk of the prefetcher, and the builder ignores claims), and (2) a reader that passed the idle check at the same moment took a full rebuild grant while it waited for the custody. A second state root (the old checkout in `~/Documents/semio` beside `/Users/Shared/semio`) has its own custody and cannot be seen by it; (b) covers that case. Now a reader whose stale owners a live claim holds ATTACHES: it neither prepares nor builds, tells the waves in EN and DE, retries a shared read at most every 2 minutes with the generation published so far (served early when a wave covers it) and builds only after the builder let go, to find the work done.
- **(b) grants: FIXED in the gate, proven by decision and gate tests, not observed live.** Cause of the starved `exec` requests and of the second 14 889 MB grant: the `rebuild` phase grant counted in full only for its learned time to peak (at most 30 minutes), then only by what the process held (4 to 10 GB). From then on the unused part was neither protected from a second `show` grant (admitted beside it) nor lent to the build's own workers (V's lending only applies while a grant ramps). The grant now counts in full and lends for the whole rebuild (`wait.staleMilliseconds`, 6 h), and returns to the ordinary ramp when the run leaves the phase. A second rebuild of that size then waits in the capacity queue (reason `memory`) unless it fits physically; the workers fit inside the grant.
- **(c) server budget: FIXED, proven by tests with real child processes, not observed live.** `inferTimeoutMilliseconds` (2 700 s) is gone. The read is stopped (SIGINT, SIGKILL after 10 s) only after 25 minutes without a new generation or pause line, or after 12 hours; it runs as long as it publishes waves. Published waves are durable: a test cuts a refresh after two waves and the next read infers only the owners no published wave holds. The refresh gives way between waves (below).
- **(d) parent: DECIDED and built, proven with the scripted farm, not measured live.** From `result-U.md`/`result-V.md`: a wave inferred by the parent alone peaks at 7.1 GB (run g), a wave with 3 to 4 workers at 3.3 GB, and the parent reached 12 to 21 GB when workers were starved. With a ready pool the parent never inferred (`choosePoolV1` always picks a ready pool); the footprint came from the window before the pool is ready (up to 8 parent loops infer at once) and from starved workers. Now the first 2 projects are inferred by the parent while workers start, the others wait at most 20 s once for the pool, then go to it. A farm without workers (off, refused, never ready) infers in the parent as before, after one wait.

## What was built (all landed)

1. `🔐️custody/🟨️.mjs` (+ `.d.mts`, schema): `attachToBuilderV1({look,recheck,say,signal,policy})`, policy `attachPollMilliseconds` 5 000 and `attachRecheckMilliseconds` 120 000, messages `attached`, `attachedWave`, `attachedServed` (EN, DE). `🔌️load/🟨️.mjs` calls `host.attach(request,recheck)` right after the fleet-first step and before the idle check and the prefetch; a served answer ends the load like a first shared read. `🕰️host/🟦️.ts` binds it, `🤝️prefetch/🟦️.ts` `builderLookV1` reads custody and pointer in one shared store scope. The contract "capacity upgrade before the custody" stays as it was.
2. `🎫️gate/🟦️.ts` (process member `host-capacity`): entering `rebuild` sets the grant's ramp to at least `wait.staleMilliseconds`, leaving it without a resize restores the policy ramp. No change to the decision function, so its SQLite and Python oracles stay valid.
3. `🪪️graph-state/🙋️wanted/🛠️server/🟦️.ts`: `createGraphReadWatchV1` (progress = a generation number higher than any seen, English or German, any chunking, or a "paused after" line), `graphReadEndTextV1`, `yieldToInteractiveV1` and `yieldBackgroundGraphV1`. Schema `Server`: `inferStallMilliseconds` 1 500 000, `inferMaximumMilliseconds` 43 200 000, `watchMilliseconds`, `yieldPollMilliseconds`, `yieldNoticeMilliseconds`, `yieldMaximumMilliseconds` 1 200 000; texts `stalled`, `tooLong`, `paused`. `🛰️sidecar/🕸️graph/🟦️.ts`: `inferGraphReadV1` (stderr read as it arrives, child started with `SEMIO_GRAPH_BACKGROUND=1`). `🕰️host/🟦️.ts` `published`: with `SEMIO_GRAPH_BACKGROUND=1` it pauses after every published wave while another command of the machine waits for memory (not small forecasts, workers or itself) or the pressure is raised, at most 20 minutes, then goes on with one wave.
4. `🏊️project-farm/🟨️.mjs` (+ pool schema, `.d.mts`): `parentWhileStarting` 2, `startWaitMilliseconds` 20 000, message `holding` (EN, DE).

## NOT verified

- Everything live: a real second reader attaching to a real builder, a real rebuild grant lending to real farm workers, the server stopping or not stopping a real 37-minute refresh, the parent footprint with the hold (the 7.1 / 3.3 GB figures are from U, not re-measured). No graph command was run, as ordered.
- Two checkouts on one machine (different state roots): only the capacity gate sees both; the second rebuild now waits for memory but nothing attaches across roots.
- The race where two readers pass the idle check together: the loser may still take its rebuild grant before the custody (the contract keeps the upgrade before the custody); with the new gate its reservation counts but it is no longer lent twice and a third rebuild waits. It is not attached until it holds the custody.
- Type check (`tsc`) of the touched TypeScript: only transpile checks and tests; `attach` is declared generic in the host so it should fit `GenerationHostV1.attach`.
- Windows and Linux paths; the `SEMIO_GRAPH_BACKGROUND` pause on a machine whose gate directory is not readable (it is then skipped by design).
- The granularity of fleet-first reads from N8 (all or nothing) is unchanged.

## For others

- A held rebuild reservation lasts the whole `rebuild` phase: a machine with a 14.9 GB learned rebuild size admits no other 14.9 GB `show` grant beside it. V's outlier rule keeps the learned size near 3.2 GB; watch `phase:rebuild` in `estimates.json`.
- The stall limit counts a pause line as life; a machine that stays busy for good advances one wave per 20 minutes.
- Scratch copies were an overlay (`T/🧰️scripts/P2/overlay.ts`, bun preload plugin that resolves live paths to a mirror of changed files); nothing was left in the worktree.
