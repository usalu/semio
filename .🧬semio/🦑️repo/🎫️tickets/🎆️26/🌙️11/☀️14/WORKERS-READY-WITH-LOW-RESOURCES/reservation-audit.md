# Reservation Audit

On 2026-11-21 `repo capacity status` reported 8,646 MB available of 32,768 MB, a 4,096 MB floor, 4,550 MB spare, warning pressure, and 12,933 MB swap used. The `show` grant for PID 24613 held 2,747 MB, observed 543 MB, and had waited 6,180 seconds to grow to 14,336 MB. Even before accounting for other unused reservations, the 11,589 MB increment exceeds physical spare memory. The graph-plan grant PID 27947 held 2,048 MB while its observed process tree used 4,588 MB. Two cargo grants held 3,840 and 5,474 MB, observing 2,269 and 2,512 MB respectively. This is a point-in-time physical headroom shortage; it does not prove an accounting defect or a capacity dependency cycle.

`repo leases status` reported no waiting leases and no lease deadlock cycles. This does not rule out a dependency cycle outside the lease graph. The graph-plan child held graph facts and graph state leases with progress reported. The upgrade code explicitly replaces the held grant rather than charging it twice and permits partial admission when all other grants are quiet upgrade waiters. No evidence establishes that relaxing the floor or bypassing the full graph request is safe. Further diagnosis requires tracing the graph-plan process's actual progress and dependency on PID 24613, and verifying whether a smaller full-graph memory envelope is independently safe.
