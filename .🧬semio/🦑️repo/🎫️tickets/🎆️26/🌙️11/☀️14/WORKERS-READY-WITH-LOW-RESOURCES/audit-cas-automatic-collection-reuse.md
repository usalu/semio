# Reusing completed automatic CAS collections

Two read-only dry-run previews found the CAS collector lock held. The same store recorded completed usage at 11:27:20 and 11:29:48 UTC on November 21, inside its 600,000 ms default minimum object age. The recorded usage was approximately 37.0–37.5 GB against the 34.36 GB host cap. The physical observations show repeated collectors and an above-cap store. They do not identify the initiators or the binding reason of each recorded run.

The prior guard remembered a run only in process memory. A fresh process could read an accurate above-cap usage estimate and immediately scan the same store again. The private baseline reproduced one redundant collection on the first identical cold admission. The completed collector now attaches a scope digest to its existing atomic usage hint. The guard supplies the scope only for automatic runs. The collector verifies the same scope before and after the scan, under the normal collector lock. Dry runs, lock contention, partial scans and lockless runs cannot issue a matching scoped completion. Explicit collection and full-disk relief remain direct paths.

The digest identifies the CAS root by path, device and inode, the effective policy and partition, and the ordered area paths, device and inode identities and eviction rules. Missing directories have a distinct representation. Unreadable or ambiguous directories yield no proof. Verification resolves the current scope only under collection pressure with a potentially usable receipt, and coalesces it within the existing usage-hint refresh cadence. A synchronous writer adds no synchronous file operations for this check. A cold synchronous writer checks the existing small hint in the background before measuring the full store.

A different policy, changed area, different partition scope or stale/future receipt cannot establish a matching completion. An earlier scope that finished at or below the cap cannot defer an above-cap write after new accounting. Before using a matching soft-cap receipt, the guard rechecks the live partition. Critical space and actual full-disk failure retain the previous local relief and refusal rules. Historical usage files still supply bytes as data. They do not by themselves establish the new completion scope.

The neutral timeline and Python oracle cover matching over-cap reuse, cap and minimum-age changes, a replaced area, expiry at the exact age, a future stamp, manual usage, new growth, criticality and criticality arising during verification. The matching case also ran in a separate Bun process using the default collector path and preserved the original receipt timestamp. Synchronous admission, explicit collection and ENOSPC were checked independently in the fixture. All 37 CAS tests and 820 assertions passed. The red and final receipts are `🗑️generated/cas-automatic-scope-red.log` and `🗑️generated/cas-automatic-scope-final.log`. No production collection, deletion, restart or other live mutation was performed.
