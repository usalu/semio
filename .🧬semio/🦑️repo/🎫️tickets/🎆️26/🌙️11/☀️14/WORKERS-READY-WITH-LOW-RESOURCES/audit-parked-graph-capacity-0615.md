# Parked graph door capacity

Read-only local snapshot on 2026-11-20; no process was controlled. The graph-server record names runner 57002 and read door 57004, started 05:57:48 UTC. Its log says the front door yielded behind live generation holder PID 33405; the process table at the sample showed runner and door without a loader child. Capacity status names the door's `show` grant: 2,869 MB reserved, 573 MB observed, phase `plan`, no `lentSinceMs`. The parent foreground holder has its own separate grant and exclusive generation custody. The gap is a reservation versus sampled process-tree footprint, not proof that exactly 2,296 MB of physical RAM is occupied or currently unavailable under every admission calculation.

Current graph queue yielding ends the loader with a ticket and lets the outer door poll for a newer generation or a bounded wake before reexecution. Current capacity code can lend an active grant when an owned wait episode is marked and the process is childless or the tree is proven waiting; it removes the lent marker on resumption. It can also resize at recognized phase boundaries. However, on restartable failure the grant holder's `finally` stops that lender and parks the grant for handover; `parkHostGrantV1` explicitly removes any `lentSinceMs` and keeps the full reservation counted. The restart wrapper waits for the graph ticket before offering that custody to its replacement. Thus, under current source, a genuine front-door-only ticket wait after grant parking is outside the active wait lender and can retain the parked reservation until reexecution or park expiry. The visible process/grant/log state is consistent with that gap, but the receipt does not prove the exact parked control path or the code revision loaded at 05:57.

A narrow improvement is warranted for this identified source path: specifically recognize a valid graph-yield ticket while parked, release or lend unused capacity during the wait, and require normal capacity re-admission or a safely resized handover before a replacement can load or compute. Preserve cancellation, expiry, one-time custody and actual observed resident accounting. Blindly shrinking the parked reservation while allowing the replacement to execute without reacquisition can oversubscribe memory; general childlessness alone is insufficient evidence of a graph wait. A test should cover the real ticket/restart path, wait, cancellation and wake/re-admission with a competing grant. No fix or live intervention was applied.

## Reproduced and fixed

The owner saw the grant already absent at 06:16 while the door still waited. The observed booking is consistent with an interval ended by the existing two-minute park expiry. The source and the private reproduction establish an early-release opportunity during that interval.

The door now settles its parked custody after a valid ticket is consumed, before the first generation poll, including the immediate-wake case. Settlement withdraws its registered handover offer, releases its ledger row and runs the gate's existing grant-sidecar cleanup. The ownership check preserves a row already taken by a legitimate handover. A graph replacement uses the ordinary fair gate before its loader. The first request time and yield round follow the ticket. A missing or invalid ticket proceeds through the ordinary implementation-stale handover. An inherited reservation remains with its owner. Interrupt and terminate signals use the existing typed wait cancellation control, with localized graph-generation wording.

A private fixture ran the actual door, actual file-backed gate and separate loader and competitor processes on a deterministic host sample. Before the fix, delayed wake and cancellation left the old 12,000 MiB fixture reservation booked, and immediate wake adopted it. After the fix, delayed wake freed the full ledger reservation before publication, then a competing 12,000 MiB grant admitted. The replacement waited in the fair queue and entered with its new grant after the competitor released. SQLite independently confirmed the single-grant capacity and each expected route. The suite checks immediate wake, typed cancellation, malformed tickets, inherited custody, original process attribution and phase-file cleanup.

The final macOS run through `repo tools exec --light bun -- test` passed 35 tests and 490 assertions across graph-yield capacity, ordinary grant handover, door ticket eligibility, graph yield and holder notices, and format-stamp restart.
