- **C29 as measured (thermal maintainer, verified on ten assets against trimesh: laptop, stepladder, cantilever-balcony, etics-brick-wall-12, park-bench, sorption-wheel, standing-adult, labrador-dog, city-car, apple-tree; every region within 1 % at 1:50).** (a) Only closed meshes bury, with outward normals; (b) two solids of the same region that meet within a fifth of a smallest feature (`SEAM_FEATURES`, 1.5 mm at 1:50) count as touching along a seam and leave no surface, so a disc in a ring is the same area whatever the tessellation of the two (before: +14 % to +23 % for 48 or 32 against 64 segments); a real gap above that keeps both faces; (c) a hidden region is its full mesh area, nothing buried; (d) `sectionFaces` is honoured on a `{ of }` thermal reference (the body solid's own is not inherited); (e) emitter and envelope solids never count at any scale.
- **C25 (c) — the interior face of a conditioned layered plate.** The area of a region of thermal model `conditioned` is the outward faces (outer face and exposed edges): the face of its layered solid that looks at the conditioned interior is a face against a surface the asset is mounted on or built into in the sense of C25 b, and is marked `sectionFaces` on the thermal solid or its `{ of }` reference. The validator has no special case for conditioned regions: without that mark both faces of the slab count and the area reads twice the value (etics-brick-wall-12 `wall-field` 2.08 against 1.0, cantilever-balcony `wall-face` 2.28 against 1.1, `thermal-break` 0.688 against 0.44). Area reviewers and authors mark the interior face; the area reviewers' rule R-C and the validator then agree.
