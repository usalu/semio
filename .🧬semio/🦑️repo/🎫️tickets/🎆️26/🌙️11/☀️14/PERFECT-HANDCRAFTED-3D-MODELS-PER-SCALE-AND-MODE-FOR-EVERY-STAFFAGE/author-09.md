## The kit (reusable for any asset, all in T)

- `author_09_lib.py` — data helpers. `asset(slug)`, `seed(slug)`, `save(slug, volumes)` (writes `T/volumes/humans/<slug>.json`), `run([...])` (bun with retry while the product module is mid-edit), `measurements(slug, scale, rules)` (parsed `--json` check). Solid builders `loft/ellipsoid/capsule/box/revolve/sec`. Operators: `dp_sections` / `coarsen_loft` (greedy section reduction with tolerance, minimum radius from the scale's feature), `shrink` (inset solid for a thermal layer shell), `scaled`, `thermal_from(asset, body, spec, scale, factors)` (region -> body solid ids; `=id` forces an `of` reference; at 1:50 multi-layer regions become nested shells, innermost first, each inset by the layers outside it), `structure_from(asset, body, scale, joints, masses, skip, extra)` (member loft along every `eligibleBones` bone from the body loft section times a core factor, joint balls, mass ellipsoids for bones with at least 10 % of the mass), `emitter`/`plume`, `struct_ref`.
- `author_09_build.py` — drivers. `fit(slug, scale)` calls the fitter twice (per-solid, then per-section). `coarsen_all(body, scale, drop)` derives the next scale's body from the previous one (cascade 50 -> 100 -> 200 -> 500). `tune_thermal(...)` rescales thermal copies from the check's finding text ("exposed surface of region R ... is A m² against areaM2 T"). `finish(slug, vols, cfg)` builds thermal (+ emitters) and structure for all scales, tunes areas and writes `decisions` from `cfg["decisions"]`. `D(en, de)` makes bilingual text.
- `author-09-fit.ts` — silhouette fitter: `bun author-09-fit.ts humans/<slug> <scale> [--ids a,b] [--sections] [--bounds 0.7,1.8] [--steps ..] [--rounds N] [--write]`. Coordinate descent on cross-section multipliers (per solid, or per loft section with a smoothness penalty) maximising the mean IoU of the three views in `presentation.defaultPose`; incremental masks, about 1-2 s per scale.
- `author-09-diff.ts` — `bun author-09-diff.ts humans/<slug> <scale>`: per view IoU, the solids that stick out, the drawn shapes that are missing and the point of the largest Hausdorff distance in each direction with the shapes/solids there.
- `author-09-area.ts` — `bun author-09-area.ts humans/<slug> <scale> [body|thermal|structure]`: per-solid triangle area and world bounds, per-region sums (plain mesh sum, not the exposed-surface measure of the check).
- `author-09-skateboarder.py` — worked example: repair the 1:50 seed (exponents, mitten hands, ears, deck kicktails), `DROP` lists per scale, per-scale replacements (1:500 wheel blocks), thermal `SPEC` (region -> ids), `struct_extra` for props, bilingual `decisions`. `python3 author-09-skateboarder.py` rebuilds everything, `--finish` only thermal/structure/decisions, `--bodies` only bodies. Start a new asset by copying it.

How to use for a new asset: `volumes-seed.ts`, copy the seed to `author-09-seed/`, copy the example script, change `slug`, the repair block, `DROP`, `SPEC`, `struct_extra`, `decisions`, run it in the background (a full run is 5-15 minutes under load), then `volumes-check.ts`, `author-09-diff.ts` for any silhouette failure, `volumes-render.ts` and read every sheet.

Lessons: limbs drawn as rectangles need superellipse exponent about 4 (1.0 of the IoU is lost at 2.3); coarse-scale minimum feature enlarges thin parts (wheels, deck at 1:500) at the price of IoU; thermal copies that are smaller than the body are buried under the C1 exposed-surface rule and measure zero, so factors are floored at 1.0.
