# Coarse vegetation geometry

All four installed subjects below matched the revisions in the large-form review. Each four-scale body, surface-temperature and structure sheet was inspected in full. The measurements use the mesh vertices in each source.

| Subject and source | Fine and middle scales | Coarse geometry and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| [Agave](🗑️generated/living-current-views/agave-summer-turgid-all-temperature-09d04e6dcd.png), `09d04e6dcd43226c` | The 56 leaf-member sections have median maximum ring diameter 62.1 mm and largest 70.5 mm at 1:50, 1:100 and 1:200. | The 1:500 structural maximum diameter is 76.0 mm on every leaf segment. The blue members obscure the blade's taper and width variation. The previous [agave report](r3-vegetation-agave.md) also records that the coarse physical blades were thickened to 75 mm where the real blade is about 50 mm. |
| [Apple tree](🗑️generated/living-current-views/apple-tree-summer-fruit-set-all-temperature-22da0dbd31.png), `22da0dbd3167b69b` | Body fruit counts are 42, 20 and 10 at the first three scales. There are 56, 24 and 12 distinguishable woody solids, respectively. | The 1:500 tree has 24 faceted foliage bodies, three bark bodies and no physical apples in its fruit-set pose. The structural skeleton still shows the scaffold branches. The loss of branch windows and fruit is visible beyond the difference in polygon count. |
| [Miscanthus](🗑️generated/living-current-views/miscanthus-grass-calm-all-temperature-a21ab0f430.png), `a21ab0f43079c442` | The 278 fine blade meshes have maximum cross-section widths of 10.8–16.7 mm. The 136 medium meshes span 13.7–16.3 mm at 1:200. | The 1:500 body retains 30 true-width blades but has another 42 smooth lobes 112.0–135.6 mm wide. The green strips in the physical and thermal panels are individual solids. Two structural members reference such full lobes directly. |
| [Multi-stem birch](🗑️generated/living-current-views/multi-stem-birch-calm-all-temperature-c14b7f870e.png), `c14b7f870ee92f0d` | The 22 mass solids at 1:50, 1:100 and 1:200 represent localized loads. The largest bounding-box diagonal among their polyhedra is 0.531 m. | At 1:500 there are 40 mass solids, including 27 direct references to physical geometry. The largest reference spans 1.565 m. These carry real distributed foliage load, as the [original birch report](r3-vegetation-multi-stem-birch.md) explains, and obscure the distal structure as drawn. The mass magnitude and attachment can be preserved independently of the display envelope. |
