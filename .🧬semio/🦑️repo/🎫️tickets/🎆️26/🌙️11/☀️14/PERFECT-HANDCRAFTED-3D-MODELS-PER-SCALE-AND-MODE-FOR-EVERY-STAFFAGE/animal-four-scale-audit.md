The pigeon’s missing 1:200 extended wings and the hedgehog’s missing 1:200 and 1:500 spines were the most consequential discrepancies in this set. The thermal panels show solved temperature for the supplied scenario. Small temperature differences can produce similar colors across neighboring surfaces. An apparently uniform panel alone does not establish a missing thermal region.
