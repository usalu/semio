## 2026-11-21 r5 r3-lib `meshesIn`: solids with `weights` were placed rigidly — fixed (appliances/vacuum-cleaner, and every asset with weighted solids)

`r3-sheet.ts` (through `r3-lib.ts` `meshesIn`) carried every solid by its own `bone` frame only and ignored `weights`, while the product's mesh (`🎨️depiction/🧊️axonometric/🧱️mesh`) blends a weighted solid over its bones. A weighted bend piece of the vacuum hose therefore stuck straight out of its joint in the sheets although the product bends it; the weighted solids of sleeping-person, walking-commuter, dairy-cow and external-venetian-blind were shown wrong the same way. Fixed at the root in `r3-lib.ts`: `weightsOf` places a weighted solid in the rest frame of its bone (`restFrames` of the rig module) and sums, over its at most four normalised weights, each bone's posed frame applied to the point in that bone's rest frame (linear blend skinning, the product's rule). Rigid solids are unchanged. Still open for the validator maintainer: `🧊️volumes/🌓️silhouette` `copyMesh` also drops `weights` (poses a weighted solid by its `bone` only), so silhouette and cross-scale numbers see the unblended piece.
