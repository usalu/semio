## structure.supports on the three two-wheelers (answer to the check requested)
Set to `["contact-f"]` on all three (city-bicycle already by 43, cargo-bike and motor-scooter by me, text-level in the asset). Reason: `support-switched` in `🩺️check/🟦️.ts` (line 81) defines a `structure.supports` entry as permanent: it must be in the `contacts` of every pose that has contacts and cover the whole duration of every clip. A parked two-wheeler bears on the front tyre and the stand foot (rear tyre lifted: cargo-bike `loaded-on-stand` 4 cm off the ground; clips `brake-and-park` and `stop-and-shut-down` switch the rear contact off at 2.6 s and 4.2 s and the stand foot on), a ridden one on both tyres. `contact-f` is the only anchor in every pose and every clip of all three assets; `contact-r`, `stand-pad` and `stand-foot` are pose-dependent and would trip the rule. The solver still unions the pose contacts with the supports, so the rear patch and the stand foot carry load in the poses where they apply; in the 3D structure that path is the member of the wheel bone (tyre / rim) and the stand legs with the foot bar. The single support solid per scale is a pad at `contact-f` with `anchor` (C6).
