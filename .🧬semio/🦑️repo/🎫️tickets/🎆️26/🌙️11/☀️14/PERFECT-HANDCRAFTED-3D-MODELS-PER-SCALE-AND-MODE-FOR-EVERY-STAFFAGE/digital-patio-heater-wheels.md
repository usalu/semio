# Patio heater transport wheels

The original 1:50 shaft passed through unbored hubs and bracket plates. The 1:100 model omitted the brackets. The 1:200 and 1:500 models omitted the entire assembly. The installed heater now carries the wheel pair on the pan at all four scales. Its coarse side elevations include the wheel in the base outline.

| Measured feature | Installed geometry |
| --- | ---: |
| Tyre diameter and tread width | 90 × 30 mm |
| Wheel centre spacing | 320 mm |
| Shaft diameter | 12 mm |
| Hub and bush bore | 12.5 mm |
| Bracket plate thickness | 8 mm |
| Parked wheel clearance above ground | 5 mm |

The shaft passes through two flanged bushes in the pressed-steel brackets. Its shouldered ends retain the hubs. Each bracket root intersects the base pan. The nine pieces contribute surfaces to the base thermal region and members or joints to the structural model. The pan supports the parked heater.

A Three.js ray test checked radial and axial intersections against the actual tessellated tyres, hubs, shaft, bushes, brackets and pan at each scale. The candidate passed document validation, the 13 volume rules at each scale and 12 GLB scene checks. An independent rebuild produced the same document for publication. The installed catalog, validated candidate and ticket volume source agree. The full-height heated view and enlarged assembly and cutaway were rendered and inspected at every scale.
