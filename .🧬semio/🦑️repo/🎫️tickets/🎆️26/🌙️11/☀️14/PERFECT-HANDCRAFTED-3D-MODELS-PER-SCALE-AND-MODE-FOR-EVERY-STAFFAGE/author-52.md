## What the exposed-area measurement taught (used throughout)
From the product code (`🧊️volumes`): (1) at 1:50 the region area is measured on the solids of the LAST layer of the region (the schema says layers are innermost first, so the last is the surface); at 1:100 and coarser on all solids of the region (1:200/1:500 only the sum). (2) A face is buried only by solids of OTHER regions or by body/structure solids; thermal solids of the same region never bury each other, so nested layer shells of one region count both big faces unless a body solid sits against them. (3) A structure solid that is thicker than a pane buries its faces. (4) `sectionFaces` is accepted on `{ of }` references and replaces the faces of the referenced body solid by the declared ones. I used `sectionFaces` only where a face is a contact with a neighbouring layer or a cut through the continuing construction (wall edges, reveals, the room side of a glass unit counted once), never to shrink a region.
