## Method
- Body: `auto()` lofts every closed body shape of the scale; open strokes (legs at coarse scales) become tubes of the pen width (at least 0.58 feature); bones drawn only in front/plan get a tube along the bone; ellipse-only shapes (far ear) an ellipsoid; every loft is bulked to at least 1.08 feature in both cross directions. Sections per loft 18/13/9/7 for 1:50/100/200/500. The drawings of each scale are different (merged masses), so each list differs from the finer one.
- Thermal: `{ of }` references to body solids; at 1:50 multi-layer regions are nested shells (outer = reference, inner = loft shrunk by the outer layers' thickness); regions that the scale draws merged get own capsule/ellipsoid solids derived from the 1:50 drawing; emitters: nozzle capsules ending at the anchor (breath) and a flat ellipsoid on the back (plume), tagged with the emitter id.
- Structure: member lofts along the body centre lines (radius 0.5 of the thinner body radius, stroke tubes copied), spine as a slender beam along the back of the trunk, joint spheres at 1:50 (all) and 1:100 (limbs/neck) only where they lie inside the body silhouette, compact mass spheres at the centres of mass of segments with at least 10 % of the mass, members of bones with no drawn body at the scale clipped to the part of the bone inside the silhouette. No `structure.supports` exist for animals (all empty), so no support solids.
- Decisions: bilingual per scale (body notes by me, thermal/structure sentences derived).
