## Chisel

- `T/rework-13-kit.py` — helpers only, no numbers of any asset: `tube` (limb, stem, culm through hand-stated stations with radii, parallel-transported rings, rounded ends), `pad` (leaf cushion: dome over a flatter underside, lobed outline, rim serrated at leaf size), `plate` (a flat leaf from a stated outline), `ribbon` (blade, leaflet, strap with fold and keel), `Plant.solid` (world mesh into the rest frame of its bone as a closed outward polyhedron, with its thermal and structural reference), `Plant.drawings` (the elevation, front and plan outline of every drawn solid traced from the very same mesh: raster of a quarter feature, boundary walk, Douglas-Peucker at a fifth of the feature; layered by depth towards the viewer), `Plant.publish_root` (member splice into the catalog file with the formatter of `volumes-insert.py`, every other byte kept).
- `T/rework-13-frames.ts` — rest frame of every bone from the product's `restFrames`, as JSON for the kit.
- `T/rework-13-info.py` — what a plant script has to bind to (bones, anchors, regions, pens, old ids).
- One script per asset, `T/rework-13-<slug>.py`: the hand-stated numbers (stations, rows of cushions, leaf outlines) and the decisions text; `--publish` writes the drawings and root fixes.

## 1. vegetation/japanese-maple

### Reference (Acer palmatum, garden specimen of about 25 years, multi-stem)

| Feature | Real subject | Model before | Model now |
|---|---|---|---|
| Height / spread | 4 to 6 m high, usually as wide or wider than high | 4.50 x 5.65 x 5.39 m | the same (declared dimensions kept) |
| Stems | 2 to 5 from a collar at 0.1 to 0.4 m, 10 to 18 cm at the base, sinuous, splayed 15 to 30 degrees, smooth grey bark | three straight poles 0.17 / 0.15 / 0.14 m | three stems of the same diameters with a stated sweep (3 to 7 cm off the bone axis), taper to 6 to 10 cm, leader 9 to 3 cm |
| Collar and roots | flare to about twice the stem group, 4 to 6 surface roots | truncated pyramid at 1:500, lumps at 1:50 | turned flare 0.60 to 0.22 m, five roots of 0.38 to 0.46 m at 1:50 and 1:100 |
| Branching | sympodial, opposite; long thin limbs of 4 to 6 cm leave the stems nearly horizontally and fork into opposite pairs; layered ("tiered") habit | six limbs in the sagittal plane, twelve secondaries, twig sticks | 22 limbs fanning to every quarter under the three tiers (8, 8, 6), each forking once into a pair; a twig into every cushion |
| Foliage | horizontal layers 0.3 to 0.5 m deep with gaps between them; leaves opposite, 5 to 7 lobes, 6 to 10 cm | 101 crystalline shards with flat facets ("boulders") | 73 cushions (dome over a flatter underside, lobed outline, rim toothed at leaf size, 26 teeth of about 10 cm) in three warped tiers and a top; two rings of seven-lobed leaves of 10 cm on every cushion at 1:50 |
| Coarse scales | the layered habit stays readable | 1:200 and 1:500 were the same list | 46 / 23 / 18 cushions, modelled per scale (deeper below at coarser scales so that no cushion is a paddle) |

### Defects found

- Foliage: every "pad" was a faceted polyhedron with planar faces up to 0.6 m; the crown read as stacked rocks from every direction.
- Stems: straight tubes without sweep; limbs only in the plane of the rig although the crown is round.
- 1:500 was a copy of 1:200 with a pyramid for the collar.
- Structure: member solids were copies of the body; masses of the limb bones sat in the leaves.

### Rebuilt (script `T/rework-13-japanese-maple.py`)

- **Rig**: kept (root plate, three stems, leader, six tier limbs, nine foliage bones); it is the right topology for a three-stem tree and all poses and clips bind to it. The stems now pass through every joint of the rig (the lateral `z` of the tier joints is the lean of the west and east stem). Anchors: the twelve transpiration anchors moved onto the crest of a cushion of their own bone.
- **Body**: collar as a turned flare, five roots, three sinuous stems and the leader as tubes through stated stations; 22 limbs with 44 forks and 73 twigs; 73 / 57 / 40 / 18 cushions per scale. 1:100 and 1:200 keep the outer ring of every tier (it is the outline of the tree) and merge the inner rings; 1:500 generalises each tier to four deep cushions. One factor per half space fits the stated radii to the declared extent at every scale (printed as `fit`).
- **Thermal**: stem bark (collar, roots, stems, leader), limb bark (limbs, forks), sun leaves (tier and top cushions), shade leaves (inner cushions at the stems) by reference to the body; twelve lens patches on the crests are the emitter solids. `thermal.regions[limb-bark].areaM2` set to the measured 6.506 m² of the 1:50 model (was 8.286 m² for the old limbs).
- **Structure**: the wood is the load path by reference (collar, stems, leader, limbs, forks, the twig of every cushion); a joint ball inside the wood at every joint, the base disc, one mass ball per bone inside its member (stems at mid height on the swept axis, limbs on the limb, foliage bones in a cushion).
- **2D**: all three views of all four scales redrawn from the solids themselves: one filled outline per cushion, stem and limb, layered by depth (painter's order), forks and twigs as lines where the pen exists, one leaf drawn per cushion at 1:50. Cushions are `detail` at 1:50 and `body` from 1:100 on (a covered cushion leaves slivers thinner than the 0.13 mm detail pen allows at the band end).
- **Decisions** rewritten per scale (en, de) in `volumes[scale].decisions` and `representations[scale].decisions.silhouette`.
