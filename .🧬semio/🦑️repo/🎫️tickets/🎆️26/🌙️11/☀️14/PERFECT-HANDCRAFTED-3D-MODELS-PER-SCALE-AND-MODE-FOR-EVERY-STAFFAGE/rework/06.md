### Defects found

- Shoulders (all four scales): the trunk was widest below the shoulder joint and only 0.15 m wide at the height of the sleeve cap, so the upper arms sat beside the trunk like pauldrons with a notch to the neck; at 1:500 the sleeve began as a peg point.

### Rebuilt

- Body 1:50 / 1:100 / 1:200 / 1:500: yoke of shirt (and the coarse vest/collar solids) re-sectioned to the acromion line; sleeves re-sectioned by hand per scale (14 / 9 / 7 / 5 sections) with deltoid cap, deltoid maximum 40 mm below the joint, insertion, biceps and elbow.

- 2D: front elevation `torso` (1:50, 1:100, 1:200, 1:500) and `vest` (1:200) redrawn to the new shoulder line.
- Rig: unchanged. The rig is planar (bones turn in the sagittal plane, lateral offset `z`, spread and twist per pose); a clavicle bone has no sagittal length, so the shoulder girdle stays a `z` offset of the upper arm (0.18 m).

## Fundamental issues

1. **`volumes-feature` was tightened while this rework ran** (`S/🔨️modules/🗂️catalog/🧊️volumes/🧱️rules/🟦️.ts`, modified 2026-11-22 01:10 by another author, together with `🧮️measures` and a new `🔗️connected` rule; uncommitted, still changing). Worker, courier and cyclist were `VALID` with `--catalog-only --volumes` at publication. Re-run afterwards, catalog only: worker-carrying-box 47 findings (all `volumes.200.structure` and `volumes.500.structure`: ulna, radius, fibula, patella, condyles, joint spheres of 24 to 28 mm against 30 mm, none on a solid of this rework), city-bicycle 14 findings although I never wrote that asset (axle and nuts of 8 to 10 mm at 1:100 and coarser), cargo-bike 72 findings of the same kind plus `volumes-connected` at 1:200 (stand legs 13 mm from the beam). Courier and cyclist were not re-run after the rule change. These are thin skeleton bones and hardware that the earlier rule accepted; they need either thickening to the feature per scale or the owner of the rule deciding what a structure joint marker may be. Not fixed by me: the rule was moving and the findings are in lists I had not re-authored.
2. **The rig is planar.** Bones turn in the sagittal plane and carry a lateral `z`; a clavicle, a scapula or a pelvis-width bone has no length in that plane. The brief asks for clavicle and shoulder-girdle bones; in this schema they can only be `z` offsets with spread and twist per pose. I left the three human rigs as they are and say so instead of adding zero-length bones.
3. **Coarse lists thinned from the 1:50 list** exist in courier (limb lofts), cyclist and cargo-bike (the lost seat post was one consequence). I repaired the visible consequence, not the cause.
