### Defects found (model before, 1:50; the coarser scales share them)

1. Skeleton: no lumbar segment, so every lean pivots the whole trunk about L5/S1 as a rigid board; fingers have no joint; the base arms are projections, not radiating bones.
2. Trunk: box section from waist to shoulder with sharp vertical corner lines on the back, flat front, no thoracic kyphosis or lumbar lordosis, no scapular plane, no pectoral or abdominal form; shoulder caps are squares that stand above the trapezius line; collar floats as a ring.
3. Arms: straight cones; no deltoid insertion, biceps/triceps, olecranon or epicondyles.
4. Head: correct size, flat face without brow ridge, orbits, cheekbones, philtrum and lips; ears as flat discs.
5. Sitting: buttocks and thighs are round ellipsoids sunk in the cushion instead of flattened on it; the back is 80 mm in front of the backrest in the default pose; black z-fighting patches where the thigh roots, trouser seat and pelvis coincide.
6. Chair: seat slab too deep and too thin, backrest a flat slab on a strap without lumbar bulge, armrest posts flat bars, no visible mechanism housing with levers.
7. Desk: 0.60 x 0.78 m with the short side to the sitter.
8. Structure: the skeleton is anatomical (skull, spine, ribs, girdle, pelvis, long bones, hand and foot rays) but follows the rigid trunk; chair backrest member is the whole pad instead of the carrier; desk members are the visible parts (correct for a frame).
9. Thermal: nested and complete; inherits every form defect of the body.
