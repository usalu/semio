### 1.2 Defects found in the catalog model (measured on the rig and on the sheets)

- Rig: one torso bone from the lumbosacral joint to C7, straight, tilted 16 degrees, running through the middle of the chest instead of along the vertebrae; the kyphosis existed only as a jacket shape. No thoracic segment, no shoulder girdle, no finger bones (the hands were lofts glued to the hand bone), the head pivot at chin height (head bone 0.167 m). Knees and hips locked straight in the standing poses.
- Cane: a crook cane whose crook stood in the palm with the hand bone sloping 50 degrees; the fingers were bands laid on the outside of the crook, not closed round it.
- Body 1:50: buttocks 14 cm behind the hip axis (10 to 12 cm real), trouser legs as untapered tubes (hem as wide as the knee), sleeve heads as blocks above the shoulder line, the knee as a ragged intersection, neck missing between collar and chin in front.
- Structure: ribs as nine revolved hoops without connection to spine or sternum, no single vertebrae, scapulae as ellipsoids, no costal arch.
- Coarser scales: mitten hands at 1:100, structure at 1:500 as 75 mm sticks.

### 1.5 Still imperfect

- The clavicles carry no arm: the rig solver composes rest spread as a rotation, so an arm hung on a laterally pointing clavicle would swing in the wrong plane. The clavicle bones exist, carry clavicle and scapula and have limits; the arms stay children of `chest`. A shrug does not move the arm.
- Wrist: the angle between forearm and hand in the sagittal plane is ulnar deviation. It is 24 degrees standing and reaches 46 degrees in the swing of the gait (anatomical limit about 35); the elbow reaches 50 to 65 degrees in the gait because the shoulder drops with the stride. The standing elbow is 27 degrees (rule 15 to 30).
- Garments are rigid per bone: the knee shows a seam, the jacket has a seam at the waist, and in the gait the trailing thigh and the seat show through the back of the jacket skirt.
- Face: cheeks, brow and chin are added forms on a sliced head, not one sculpted surface; no eyelids, no nostrils.
- Jacket collar is a tube round the nape, not a cut collar with a notch.
- Coarse structure: by the feature rule a 1:500 clavicle or forearm is a 75 mm rod.
- The thumb of the right hand lies along the inner top of the handle but was checked only in the six-view close-up, not from below.

## Fundamental issues

1. **Test against rule.** `🧪️tests/🧊️model-skeleton` ("adultLongBones" of `🧫️fixtures/🧊️model-skeleton`, subjects include `elderly-with-cane`, `person-on-crutches`, `person-with-rollator`, `person-with-white-cane`) demands that `femur`, `tibia`, `humerus`, `radius`, `ulna` (`-l`, `-r`) exist in the structure list of every scale with the narrowest section of 1:50 within 0.1 mm. The tightened `volumes-feature` (01:10, "a solid of a coarser scale gains nothing from being identical to a 1:50 solid") forbids exactly that below the feature size. The published `elderly-with-cane` follows the validator: bones are floored at 15 / 30 / 75 mm and `radius` is merged into one forearm bone from 1:200, so this test fails for it until rule and test are reconciled (either the test accepts floored shafts, or the rule accepts true-size long bones). I did not run the vitest suite (no `repo` binary allowed) and did not change the test.
2. **Rig: no rest twist and rotation-composed spread.** A bone cannot hang on a laterally pointing parent and still swing sagittally (clavicle to arm), and a pronated hand needs a twist at rest. `Bone` would need a rest `twistDeg`, or pose `angleDeg` of a child would have to be applied in the sagittal frame.
3. **Rules moved under the work.** `volumes-connected` and the coarse `volumes-feature` changed three times between 01:03 and 01:22; a candidate that was `VALID` at 01:05 had 107 findings at 01:14. The first version of `volumes-connected` demanded `support` solids that `volume-anchor` only allows for permanent `structure.supports`, which a walking figure cannot have; the 01:22 version accepts groups that rest on the ground.
4. **`r3-sheet.ts` has no `--file`** (also noted by author 08): candidates with a changed rig cannot be seen without writing the catalog. `rework-04-sheet.ts` is a copy with `--file`, `--box` and `--tag`; folding these into the shared tool would serve every author.
