## Method of this pass

Every asset was rendered with `rework-14-look.sh` (four scales x body, thermal, structure, the thermal cut, every pose, two instants of every clip) and the sheets were read at full size. Each model was then measured with `rework-14-measure.ts` (world extents of every solid) against the tables above, and `rework-14-float.ts` grouped the solids of every list of every scale by contact, so that parts that hang in the air and load paths that do not close show up as separate groups. The baseline of all twelve assets was `VALID` with `--volumes`. The earlier rounds had already rebuilt these twelve pieces by hand at every scale (real members, joinery, hardware, nested thermal shells, frames as structure); the measured dimensions agree with the reference tables. What this pass rebuilt is therefore listed per asset as measured defects, not as a wholesale replacement of models that hold up.

## Fundamental issues

- `validateAsset` and `checkVolumes` pass a structure list whose members do not touch (open load paths of 3 to 67 mm in five of these twelve assets, a top in five loose pieces, benches in the air at 1:500) and a body list with floating parts. A rule "every member, joint and support of a structure list is connected to a support through touching solids; every body solid touches another one" would have caught all of them. `rework-14-float.ts` is a bounding-box approximation of it (it over-connects, and its strict form misreports crossing bars and tangent contacts), not a validator rule; a real rule needs mesh distances and belongs in `S/🔨️modules/🗂️catalog/🧊️volume` with a fixture and a test. Not implemented here.
- `r3-sheet.ts` draws a bone from joint to tip only; a bone whose solids lie on its negative side (bed head section) reads as a wrong skeleton although the mechanism is right. The rule that would settle it ("the solids of a bone lie on its positive side") does not exist.
