# Rework 17 — building-services appliances (author 17, 2026-11-22)

Scripts: `rework-17-kit.py` (constructors, list editing by id, exact catalog text patches), one `rework-17-<slug>-author.py` per asset (all hand-chosen numbers), `rework-17-check.sh`, `rework-17-areas.sh`, `rework-17-look.sh`, `rework-17-publish.sh`. Untouched sources are kept under `rework/17-base/` so every author script can be run again.

Frame reminder for wall appliances: the side elevation of the product is the face of the appliance (bone u = width, v = height, w = depth, front at negative w).

## Fundamental issues

- **mvhr-unit needs a redesign, not a rework**: its front porthole with a spinning supply impeller in a sealed cup is not how any MVHR is built, and the air paths between the four spigots, the two fans and the four faces of the core cannot be made right inside the present rig, 2D drawings, thermal regions (`window`, `impeller`) and clips. It needs a decision on the product layout first (two fan bones, filter caps instead of the porthole, four chambers).
- **Thermal area rule and shells**: `volumes-thermal-coverage` counts the inner faces of a thermal shell into an operating region as soon as a solid of another region stands within about half a feature of them or the flood finds a slot of about 2 mm (seen on the pump flanges and the TRV sensor cap). This forces solid lumps where the product is hollow, or a re-declared `areaM2`. I worked around it and did not touch the rule; a `sectionFaces`-like way to exclude declared inner faces would let the hollow parts be modelled as they are.
- **`polyhedron` solids** exist in the schema but not in the README table of solid kinds, and were used for dumps (cylinder 13 per scale, pump 1 per scale; both replaced). Other assets may carry more.
- `r3-sheet --cut` cuts at the middle of the bounding box, not on the symmetry plane of the asset; on the TRV the front section therefore runs through the chamber wall.
