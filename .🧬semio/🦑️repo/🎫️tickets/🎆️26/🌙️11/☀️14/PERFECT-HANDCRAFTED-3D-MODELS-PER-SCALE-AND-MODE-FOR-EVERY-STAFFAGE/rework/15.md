# Rework 15 — street and household objects

Author 15 of 19, 2026-11-22. Assets in order: shopping-trolley, playground-swing, market-parasol, charcoal-grill, patio-heater, stepladder, street-lamp, drinking-fountain, waste-bin, bicycle-stand, retractable-bollard, storage-crate-stack, chalkboard, cooking-pot, tealight.

Shared scripts (all in T): `rework_15_kit.py` (load catalog + source, candidate, apply root members and source), `rework-15-sheets.py` (several `r3-sheet` renders tiled into one PNG), `rework-15-look.sh` (one reduced render). Per asset: `rework-15-<slug>-*.py`.

Method note, stated plainly: every asset of this list had a complete hand-authored round-3 model with its own reference table (`T/r3-objects-<slug>.md`). I did not throw those away. For each asset I rendered it at full size, measured it against the real product, and rebuilt what was wrong or missing (mechanism bones, floating parts, missing construction); the parts that were right stay as they were authored.

### Defects found (sheets at 1:50/100/200/500, body, thermal, structure, poses `loaded`, `gate-raised`)

- Rig: four bones only (chassis, basket, handle, gate). The castors, the one mechanism a trolley is known for, could neither swivel nor roll.
- 1:500: basket carried only at the rear; front struts absent (floating front).
- 1:500 body carried 1:50 castor parts (8 mm fork legs, o10 axle, crown) far below the 75 mm feature; 1:100 fork legs 8 mm under the 15 mm feature (hidden before only because they sat on the long chassis bone).
- Hub read as a blank disc; a turning wheel would not show.
- Not changed, stated plainly: no child seat in the gate (the model is the variant without one); the mesh planes stay thin glazing sheets (opaque in `r3-sheet`, translucent in the product).

### Rebuilt (`rework-15-shopping-trolley-castors.py`)

- **Rig:** eight new bones. `castor-rear-l|r`, `castor-front-l|r`: on the kingpin axes (chassis x 0.035 / 0.595, z +-0.25 / +-0.198, top at the underside of the rail), pointing down 92.5 mm to axle height, joint revolute with `continuousTwist` (a castor swivels without limit). `wheel-rear-l|r`, `wheel-front-l|r`: children of their castor at the axle, 35 mm behind the kingpin (trail), revolute. The four wheel contact anchors moved from the chassis onto the castor bones (155 mm below the swivel, 35 mm behind), so the contact point follows the swivel.
- **Body:** 1:50 crown, both fork legs and axle bound to the castor bone, tyre and hub to the wheel bone (coordinates carried into the new frames), five 7.5 mm ribs on every nylon hub (`hub-ribs-*`); top plate and ball-race head stay on the chassis. 1:100 the same with fork legs thickened to the 15 mm feature. 1:200 housing and wheel on their bones. 1:500 re-modelled for its feature: one 75 x 75 x 85 mm fork housing on the castor bone, an o125 x 75 wheel on the wheel bone, the 70 x 21 x 60 mm swivel block on the chassis; the 8 mm fork legs, crown and o10 axle are gone. 1:500 gets the two front struts (`strut-l|r`, slender o19 rods) so the basket is carried at the front.
- **Thermal:** follows by reference (`frame-tube` for castor steel, `wheels` for tyre, hub and ribs); new ribs and struts referenced.
- **Structure:** castor and wheel parts are members of their own bones (the load path rail -> plate -> kingpin -> fork -> axle -> wheel -> ground is now a chain of bones); 1:500 wheel pads are 30 mm blocks on the ground under each wheel.
- **2D:** wheel, hub and tread circles of the elevation bound to the near wheel bones, fork and axle to the near castor bones (re-expressed in the bone frames); front and plan wheels and forks bound to their own side's bones. Drawings unchanged in the default pose (checked with `r3-2d` at 1:50).
- **Decisions:** added per scale (en, de).

Validation: `VALID objects/shopping-trolley (catalog asset + volumes source): schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0`; published; `--catalog-only` VALID.

Still imperfect: no pose or clip swivels the castors or rolls the wheels yet (the bones are there for casts that push the trolley); a swivelled castor would need `solid` front and plan shapes for the fork. No child seat. At 1:500 the wheel is 75 mm wide (feature rule), not 32 mm.

| quantity | real | model | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|

### Defects found

- Rig of two fixed bones: the three joints that make a stepladder a stepladder (head hinge, spreader knuckles, platform axle) did not exist; `open-in-use` and `open-empty` were the only states.
- At 1:100, 1:200, 1:500 each spreader was one 585 mm bar bound to the front frame: with any hinge motion it would have torn off the rear rail.
- Body proportions, sections, shoes, treads, braces at four scales: right, kept.

### Rebuilt (`rework-15-stepladder-fold.py`)

- **Rig:** `rear-frame` revolute 0..31.5 degrees (closed: rails 7 degrees off the stiles, behind the tread backs). New bones `spreader-front` (on the stile pivot, u 0.5737), `spreader-rear` (on the rail pivot, 0.8322 from the hinge), each 280.4 mm to the knuckle, and `platform` (on the axle, u 1.1289, 244 mm deep, 0..-62 degrees).
- **Body:** spreader links re-authored in their own bone frames at every scale (flat 20 x 8 on edge, 12 mm ends past the holes; 1:100-1:500 two slender links per side instead of one bar); knuckle bolts ride on the front links; platform deck and cheeks carried into the platform bone at every scale. 1:200 platform 56 mm deep (print rule of the band).
- **Thermal:** by reference (`spreaders`, `platform`). **Structure:** links and platform are members of their own bones; the tie load path stile pivot -> link -> knuckle -> link -> rail pivot is now two members and a joint.
- **2D:** elevation platform, spreader links and knuckle re-expressed in the new bone frames (1:100-1:500: one stroke per link instead of one bar); plan strokes split at the knuckle; front and plan platform bound to the platform bone.
- **Poses and clip:** pose `folded` and seven in-between key poses `folding-1..7`; every one is solved exactly from the two triangles (hinge-feet triangle for the lean so all four shoes stay on the ground, pivot-knuckle-pivot triangle for the link angles), so the knuckle never opens. Clip `fold-up` (5 s) runs through them.

Validation: `VALID objects/stepladder (catalog asset + volumes source): schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0`; published; `--catalog-only` VALID. Rendered: open, `folded`, `fold-up` at 1.4 s (1:50) and `folding-5` (1:200): links meet in the knuckle in every state.

Still imperfect: the platform is lifted by a linear law (the real strap or lifting link between platform and rear frame is not modelled); in `folded` the rear shoes are rotated with the rail, so only their toe edge touches; rivets, latch and extrusion grooves remain as in round 3; no manufacturer drawing was read for the closed angle (7 degrees is derived from the tread depth).
