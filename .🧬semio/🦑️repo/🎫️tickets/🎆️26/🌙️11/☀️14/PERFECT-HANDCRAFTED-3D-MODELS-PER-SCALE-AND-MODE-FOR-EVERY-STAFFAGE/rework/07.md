# Rework 07 — horse, dairy cow, goat, sheep, pig, roe deer, red fox (2026-11-22)

Status in one line: **horse, dairy cow and goat were partly re-cut and published; sheep, pig, roe deer and red fox were only looked at (1:50 body sheet) and are not started.** None of the seven is a full rebuild as the brief demands; what is missing is listed per asset.

Scripts (all in the ticket folder): `rework-07-lib.py` (section-table helpers), `rework-07-dump.py` (prints section tables), `rework-07-horse-body.py`, `rework-07-dairy-cow-body.py`, `rework-07-goat-body.py` (the hand-chosen numbers), `rework-07-2d.py` (carries re-cut bones into the 2D shapes with `r3-project2d.ts` as ruler, splices only `representations`), `rework-07-2d-restore.py` (puts one scale back from a saved catalog copy; the copies under `🗑️generated/rework-07/` were deleted at the end, so it needs a fresh copy to be useful).

A finding that cost one wrong cut and matters for every author: the README says a loft section has `rx` along w and `ry` along v. That holds for the trunk rotation (about v), but for the limb rotation `[0.5, 0.5, 0.5, 0.5]` used on almost every leg solid `rx` is the sagittal half extent (along v) and `ry` the lateral one. Measured on the sheets (horse forearm 0.156 m deep in the side view = 2 x rx, 0.12 m wide in the front view = 2 x ry).

## 2. animals/dairy-cow — chest re-cut at 1:50 and 1:100, published

Reference (Holstein-Friesian cow, 1.45 m, 650 kg): chest between the shoulders 0.44 m wide and about 0.62 m deep below the back solid, ending in a blunt dome at the manubrium; hip joint about 1.10–1.15 m, stifle about 0.75–0.80 m, hock about 0.48–0.52 m, elbow about 0.78 m, carpus about 0.39 m; tibia 45–55 degrees from the horizontal. Measured on the rig: hip joint 1.13 m, stifle 0.73 m, hock 0.46 m, elbow 0.78 m, carpus 0.39 m, tibia 45 degrees — the rig is plausible, I changed nothing in it.

Defect found and fixed: the barrel tapered from 0.43 m to 0.24 m width within 0.14 m in front of the girth, so there was no chest between the shoulders, the shoulder and arm slabs stood off a hollow and the black chest patch hung free under the neck like a strap. The front of `barrel` was re-cut (1:50: seven stations to a dome at lumbar u = 1.395; 1:100: five). The patch now lies on the chest, a thin edge of it still shows. 2D: `hindbody` (lumbar) replaced in all three views at 1:50 and 1:100.

Validation: `--volumes` → `VALID … checkVolumes 0`; published; `--catalog-only` → `VALID`.

Not done: 1:200 and 1:500 unchanged (their `trunk` does not have the hollow; only the 1:200 sheet was looked at); legs are still plain tapered rods with cylindrical knees, no forearm or gaskin muscle form; shoulder slabs still read as plates; the neck is a thin wedge; rig has no separate pastern bone (cannon → hoof); the default pose puts the near hind leg back with a flexed stifle, which reads crouched; thermal, structure, other poses and all four clips not rendered; `decisions` not updated.

Model before: `costal-barrel` 0.30 m wide from the last rib to the shoulder (a tube), 0.34 m deep. The shoulders now stand on a narrowing chest in plan and front view. 2D: `chest` replaced in front and plan at 1:50, 1:100, 1:200.

## Fundamental issues

- README "Volume" table: `rx`/`ry` of a loft are described for one rotation only; for the limb rotation they are swapped (see above). The README should state the section frame through `rotation`. Not changed by me.
- `r3-project2d.ts` outlines at 1:500 for thin leg bones can fail `volumes-silhouette` although the previous hand-thickened drawing passes; pasting ruler outlines at 1:500 is therefore not safe.
- `rework-07-2d.py` rewrites the whole `representations` member in the compact layout of `volumes-insert.py`; the data of untouched shapes is unchanged (checked by the round-trip comparison in the script), the text layout of that member is not.
