# Rework 12 — trees (oak, lime, pollarded plane, Lombardy poplar, Scots pine, birch, weeping willow, apple)

Author 12 of 19, 2026-11-22. Assets in the order of `rework/12.txt`.

## Tools of this author (all in the ticket folder)

- `rework-12-kit.py` — the chisel: hand-stated woody axes (stations x, y, z, radius; or a branch by attachment fraction, azimuth, elevation, length, base radius, upturn, turn) become tapered tube lofts with collar, rounded tip and bark relief; hand-stated foliage masses (the branch they follow, start fraction, half width, half height, lift, relief counts) become lobed lofts along that branch. Bones are cut out of the axes, so every joint lies on the wood it moves. It chooses no number of a tree.
- `rework-12-assemble.py` — carries one tree into a candidate asset document: 3D rig (rest `abductionDeg`, no floating `z` offsets), masses and sections from the modelled wood, anchors, dimensions, pose and clip offsets re-solved for the 3D bones (lee = the old sagittal lean, droop = towards the ground), loads redistributed by foliage area, bark areas from the 1:50 model, the four volumes (body, thermal, structure, decisions) and the drawing plan.
- `rework-12-draw2d.ts` — the ruler: draws every 2D shape of every scale and view as the outline of the rest-pose projection of the solids it stands for, in the product's own shape frames (probed through `evaluate`), painted in the depth order of the view; redraws the true-extent plan symbol.
- `rework-12-look.sh` — renders an `r3-sheet` of a candidate document (`r3-sheet.ts --file`) and keeps it reduced.
- `rework-12-<slug>-model.py` — the hand numbers of one tree.
