## Second pass after the validator changed (02:30 CET): `volumes-connected` and the per-scale `volumes-feature`

While the van was in validation the product gained `volumes-connected` (exact triangle distances, contact tolerance a fifth of the smallest feature: 1.5 / 3 / 6 / 15 mm; the structure must reach a support) and `volumes-feature` began to judge every coarse scale for its own smallest feature. Both are the product's form of what `rework-09-float.ts` measured with 12 mm voxels, only ten times finer, so the three assets above were measured again with `rework-09-connected.ts` (only these two rules, 7 s) and reworked a second time. Shared decisions for all three:

- `structure.supports` at the root was empty; it now names the four tyre-patch anchors (`contact-f-l`, `contact-f-r`, `contact-r-l`, `contact-r-r`), and every scale has a support pad of one smallest feature on each contact plane inside the tyre (`rework_09_lib.pads`). Not test-run beyond `validateAsset`: `🧪️tests/🌡️catalog-physics` and the force views should be looked at in the final sweep, because the analysis now sees declared supports.
- Wheels at 1:500: spokes (24 / 18 mm), wheel nuts (22 mm), vented rotors (22–28 mm) and hub caps (39 mm) are below 75 mm, so each wheel is tyre + rim + a 75 mm wheel disc + hub + one brake solid of 75 mm; the decisions say so. At 1:200 spokes are 32 mm sections, the front rotors plain 30 mm discs, the van's wheel nuts left out.
- Structure at 1:500: cage members under 75 mm (pillars, roof rails, headers, bows, the vintage car's sills, cant rails, floor bearers) are 76 mm.
