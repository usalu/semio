## Fundamental issues

1. `r3-sheet.ts` reads the catalog asset with the volumes source merged over it and has no `--file`: a rig change (a new bone) can therefore only be looked at after the catalog file is written. I validated the candidate with `r3-validate --file … --volumes` first and wrote the catalog only when it was VALID, but one wrong step slipped through this way (heat pump: thicker coarse fan blades were published and failed `volumes-feature` for two minutes until the next run restored them). Request: `--file` for `r3-sheet.ts`.
2. `machinery/expansion-valve` was INVALID in the catalog before this rework (`volumes-silhouette` front 1:50, iou 0.869 < 0.88). On an object of 60 mm the comparison raster (a quarter of the 7.5 mm feature) is coarse against the part sizes, and a 2D stroke is compared at its pen width (0.13 mm on paper = 6.5 mm in the model), so a stroke that ends 5 mm early costs more than one percent of IoU. Fixed here by completing the stroke; other very small assets may fail the same way.
3. The feature rule cannot express the true wall of thin-walled vessels and sheet casings at 1:50 as revolves or boxes (a 3.5 mm compressor shell is drawn 7.5 mm; a 75 mm wall at 1:500 leaves no interior). For the scroll compressor at 1:500 I used the rule's allowance that a coarser solid may keep the exact dimensions of its 1:50 counterpart; a declared material gauge for revolved shells (as flat sheets already have through the thermal layer) would be the clean answer.
4. `r3-sheet.ts --list thermal --cut` cuts every copy of a repeated thin solid separately and did not finish within two minutes for the finned coil (132 fins) on the loaded host.
5. A second `r3-sheet.ts machinery/finned-heat-exchanger --list structure` process that I had not started was running on the host while I worked on that asset, so another agent may be reading or rendering assets of list 11.
