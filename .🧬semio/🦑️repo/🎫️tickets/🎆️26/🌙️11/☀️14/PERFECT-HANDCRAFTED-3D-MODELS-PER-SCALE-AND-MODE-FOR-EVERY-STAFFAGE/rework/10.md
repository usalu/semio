### Kinematic chain (proved)

### Defects found

- 1:100, 1:200 and 1:500 carried the 1:50 undercarriage and bucket unchanged: two 14 mm shoe bands on two 45 mm link rails, 18 x 26 mm grouser ribs, 23 sprocket teeth per side, flanged rollers on 70 mm shafts, an adjuster with 5 mm steps, 16 mm bucket shell and cheeks, 12 mm wear strips. ### Rebuilt (`rework-10-excavator-scales.py`)

- 1:50: stack wall 8 mm; two step plates per track frame; door and front grab rails (30 mm tube, bent, ends in the cab posts); two roof work lights. Thermal references added (tracks, house).
- 1:100: shoe band 20 mm; wear strips left to 1:50; steps, rails, lights as at 1:50.
- 1:200: track modelled anew: one 40 mm shoe band that includes the grousers, on one 62 mm link chain 180 mm wide; plain sprocket disc, plain 230 mm rollers, plain pin, barrel and rod of the adjuster; bucket of 30 mm shell and cheeks (cheeks with the side cutter edge).
- 1:500: one 100 mm band (shoe and chain together, 0 to 100 mm under the rollers), plain sprocket and idler discs, plain rollers, closed box track frame with two 75 mm idler arms, bucket of 75 mm shell, cheeks and ears with five teeth and the cross beam.
- Thermal and structure lists follow through their `{ of }` references; removed parts lost their references, new parts got one in `tracks` or `attachment`.
- 2D: unchanged; the outline moved by at most the 26 mm grouser depth at 1:200 and 1:500 (now included in the band, as it is at 1:50 through the grouser ribs).
- 2D (`rework-10-excavator-2d.py`): the 1:200 elevation drew the track frame down to the chain and hid the rollers; the model shows the frame from the roller axis up with the lower half of the eight treads. Redrawn: frame from y 0.215 m, eight roller discs. Found through `volumes-silhouette` (p95 90 mm) once the grouser ribs no longer padded the boundary length.

### Validation

`r3-validate.ts machinery/excavator --volumes`: `VALID machinery/excavator (catalog asset + volumes source): schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0`; published with `volumes-insert.py`; `--catalog-only`: `VALID ... schema 0, validateAsset 0`.

### Remains imperfect

- The house is still a set of rounded boxes (engine hood, tank, tool box); doors, hinges, latches and the hydraulic hoses from the boom foot to the valve block are not modelled.
- The slew ring bone has no slewing pose or clip (limits 0/0 in the plane, twist free); the dig cycles are swing-free by their own description.
- 1:100 keeps 57 of 95 body solids identical to 1:50 (all at or above 15 mm); only the shoe band and the wear strips were touched there.

## New rule met on the way: `volumes-connected` (landed about 01:03 on 2026-11-22)

`checkVolumes` now reports parts that float, wheels that do not touch their axle and structures with an open load path. `volumes-check.ts` prints only counts, so `rework-10-connected.ts <asset> [--scale N]` prints every loose group with its solids and gaps (about 20 s). The excavator was validated and published before the rule existed; it is re-checked at the end of this report.

- Structure: tyres are members, so the load path runs support, tyre, rim (hub), axle, cradle, rails, hitch plates and pins, front frame, tower, arm, bucket; the articulation member is the lower hitch pin, its joint the upper one.
