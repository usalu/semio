### Defects found

- Legs read as sticks: thighs 8 cm too slim in girth, no vastus form, calves without the gastrocnemius set back, shanks nearly cylindrical in the front view.
- Trunk a slab with a flat front and back, no waist; shoulder caps stood out as separate spheres; the collar sat at chin height so the figure had no visible neck from the front.
- Hands: palm with one fused finger block and one thumb block.
- Seat a hemisphere attached to the back of the shorts.
- 2D drawings carried the old thin limbs.
- Rig: lengths, joint positions and limits agree with the reference (see table), so the topology and all poses and clips were kept; nothing had to be rebound.

### Rebuilt

- **Body, all four scales** (`rework-02-jogger-body.py`): every part re-authored from stations. 1:50: 70 solids (shirt, collar, neck, head, nose, ears, eyes, hair, sweatband, shorts with legs, thighs, knee caps, shanks, socks, midsoles and uppers with toe parts, deltoid caps, sleeves, upper arms, forearms, palms, 2 x 12 finger phalanges, 2 x 2 thumb segments, armband with phone pouch). 1:100: fingers as one curled blade plus thumb, no ears, eyes, collar, pouch. 1:200: one sleeve per arm, one shoe per foot, mitten hands, no nose and knee caps. 1:500: the same masses through two to four stations each, hand 52 mm thick so it prints at the end of the band.
- **Thermal**: rebuilt over the new body in the stored scheme: fabric plus air shell per clothing region at 1:50, shoe upper / air / sock layers, bare skin cut at the hems (thigh at the shorts hem, upper arm at the sleeve hem, calf at the sock), face below the band, neck above the collar, hair cut by the band, emitters at their anchors.
- **Structure**: skeleton kept (it was anatomically laid out: skull with mandible, spine with processes, 12 rib pairs on the sternum, clavicles, scapulae, pelvis of ilium, pubis, ischium and sacrum, long bones, hands and feet); the finger loft of 1:100 follows the new curled hand.
- **2D**: every filled shape of the three views at the four scales redrawn over the new body (`rework-02-draw.py`); `dimensions.depthM` 0.475 to 0.462 m (the measured lateral extent of the default pose).
