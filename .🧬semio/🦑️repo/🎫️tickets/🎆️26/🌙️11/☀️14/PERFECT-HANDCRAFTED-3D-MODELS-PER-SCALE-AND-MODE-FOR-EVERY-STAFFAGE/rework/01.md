# Rework 01 — humans (standing-adult, walking-commuter, standing-phone-user, tour-guide, leaning-person)

## Final rig topology of `humans/standing-adult` (the standard for authors 02–06)

Sagittal rig, x ahead, y up, z left; every bone revolute; 51 bones, bilaterally symmetric.

```
pelvis (root, hip-joint centre, y 0.903, 0.0951 up to L5/S1)
├─ lumbar      L5/S1 → T12/L1, 0.170 m, −25…+15°          (new: lumbar spine and abdomen, de Leva middle trunk 8.638 kg)
│  └─ torso    T12/L1 → C7/T1, 0.297 m, −15…+10°          (thorax, de Leva upper trunk 9.109 kg; id kept so shapes and clips stay bound)
│     ├─ neck  C7/T1 → atlanto-occipital, 0.102 m
│     │  └─ head  atlanto-occipital → vertex, 0.149 m
│     ├─ clavicle-l / clavicle-r   sternoclavicular joint (z ±0.020) → sagittal projection of the glenohumeral centre, 0.055 m   (new: shoulder girdle)
│     │     twistDeg = elevation / depression (left −30…+10, right −10…+30: the bone lies along the antero-posterior axis, the arm hangs 0.133 m lateral of it),
│     │     abductionDeg = protraction / retraction, angleDeg −5…+5 = roll
│     │  └─ arm-l/-r (z ±0.133 from the clavicle = ±0.153 world) → forearm → hand (wrist → knuckles, 0.098 m, both sides)
│     │        └─ thumb-metacarpal → thumb-proximal → thumb-distal
│     │        └─ index|middle|ring|little -proximal → -middle → -distal        (left hand new: mirror of the right, z and twist limits mirrored)
├─ thigh-l/-r (z ±0.085) → shank → foot (ankle → ball) → toe
```

- Rest frames of every pre-existing bone are unchanged to 0.1 mm (the clavicle angle and the arm angle are solved so that the arm keeps its rest frame); only `torso` moved its origin 0.170 m up its own axis (all torso-bound elevation shapes and `solid` profiles shifted by −0.170 in x).
- Mirror rule for poses: left = right with `abductionDeg` and `twistDeg` negated, `angleDeg` unchanged. Spine angles of the old `torso` are split 40 % lumbar / 60 % thorax.
- Shoulder shrug in a pose: `clavicle-l.twistDeg = −d`, `clavicle-r.twistDeg = +d`, and the arm's `abductionDeg` reduced by `d` on the left, raised by `d` on the right so the arm stays where it was.
- Mass: 59.21 kg as before (hand 0.19 kg + 15 finger bones 0.14 kg per side; clavicles carry no mass, their solids belong to the thorax mass).
- Standing leg: thigh −2.5°, shank −2.5°, foot +5° (hip 53 mm ahead of the ankle, knee straight) instead of −1/−5/+6, which read as a bent, backward-bowed leg.

Tools written for this rework (ticket folder): `rework-01-lib.py` (rest frames, world→bone conversion, loft/ellipsoid/capsule writers, catalog layout), `rework-01-sheet.ts` (`r3-sheet.ts` with `--file` for a candidate document), per asset `rework-01-<slug>-{rig,body,skeleton,lists,build}.py`.

### Defects found (old model, read at full size)

- Rig: no spine segments (one rigid torso), no shoulder girdle, the right hand with 15 finger bones and the left hand one 0.18 m bone with painted fingers; hand lengths 0.098 against 0.1805; left hand a slab at 1:500 while the right kept 15 finger solids.
- Body: side view a flat slab (no chest, no round of the upper back, no lumbar hollow, almost no buttock), neck too thin, shanks tubes without calf, thighs without front/back fullness, legs bowed backward by the −1/−5 stance, shoes 74 mm wide, kneecaps and hood as stuck-on blobs, the hood detached from the back, 1:500 hands asymmetric.
- Structure: spine a straight rod with nine loose spinous pegs, ribs as hoops of one size, clavicle and scapula rigid on the thorax, pelvis as two plates, only four mass markers, coarse scales with a different (thicker) skeleton than 1:50.
