### Defects found

- **No landing.** The stair ended on a fifteenth wedge tread of 22.5 degrees at floor level; a person arriving had 0.19 m of going at the walking line to step off, and nothing connected the stair to the upper floor except a "roller pin" drawn floating above the column cap. A real kit stair ends in a landing platform that is the upper support.
- **Guard ended in the air.** The helical rail stopped at an end post on the last wedge, 3.89 m high at that point (1.09 m above the tread and still rising); there was no guard for the arrival.
- **Column stopped at the top tread** with a cap one riser below nothing; real columns run on as the landing post.
- **Top support in structure mode** was a cylinder hovering over the column head on no real part.
- Rig, treads 1 to 14, hubs, base plate, anchors, helix rail and baluster spacing were measured against the table and are right (round 3 work): rise 0.18667, pan 70 mm, lap 30 mm, 89 mm clear between balusters, 0.909 m rail over nosing.

### Rebuilt

- **Rig:** `column` bone 2.8 -> 3.728 m (the core runs on as the landing post; mass 91.4 -> 114.5 kg), `tread-15` is now the landing (length 1.131 m = its diagonal, mass and centre from plate, flange and hub), `balustrade` mass 57.0 -> 71.7 kg with the landing guards; anchor `top-tie` moved from the column head to the landing's exit flange on `tread-15` (the real fixing), anchor `rail-point` to the end post of the landing. Poses and clips unchanged (the walker still climbs units 1 to 15).
- **Body, all four scales:** unit 15 is a 0.8 x 0.8 m pressed pan between the rays at 315 and 45 degrees (1:50: plate, folded flange, hub; coarser: solid 70 / 75 mm slab), two packer plates on the exit flange (1:50 with their M12 bolts, head inside the pan, shank into the slab), the column core up to rail level with its cap, turn post where the helix meets the landing, the handrail continued level (0.907 m above the landing) along the outer edge to an end post, a second level rail from the column post to an inner end post, six and five balusters under them (99 and 102 mm clear). 52 balusters in total.
- **Thermal:** by reference as before; the new parts belong to `treads`, `column-steel` and `balustrade-steel`; `areaM2` of the three regions recalculated (see validation).
- **Structure:** the landing is the member of `tread-15` with its hub as joint and the packer plates as the support `top-tie`; landing rails, posts and balusters are members of the balustrade with their flanges as joints (1:50); the core member runs to the post top.
- **2D (all four scales and the symbol, three views):** the landing bar in elevation and front, the square in plan, the stepped column (177.8 mm sleeves, 168.3 mm post), turn post, end posts, landing balusters and both level rails, depth-ordered by their true azimuth.
