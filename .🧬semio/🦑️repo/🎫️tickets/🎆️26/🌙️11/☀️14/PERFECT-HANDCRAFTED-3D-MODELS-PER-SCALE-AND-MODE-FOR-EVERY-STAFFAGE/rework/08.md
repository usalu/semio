# Rework 08 — labrador, cat, red squirrel, hedgehog, herring gull, mallard, pigeon (2026-11-22)

State in one line: two assets were rebuilt in part and published (labrador, pigeon); four were looked at and left unchanged (cat, squirrel, hedgehog, herring gull); the mallard has a known wing defect that was not started. Every asset validates. Not every sheet the brief asks for was rendered and read: per asset I read the 1:50 body and structure sheets and one to three poses; coarse scales, thermal sheets and most walk/clip instants were read only where named below.

Scripts (all in the ticket folder): `rework_08_fk.py` (planar forward kinematics for solving poses), `rework_08_root.py` (splices changed root members into the catalog file, other members byte-identical), `rework-08-labrador-dog-sit.py`, `rework-08-labrador-dog-sternum.py`, `rework-08-labrador-dog-bars500.py`, `rework-08-pigeon-wing.py` (input `rework-08-pigeon-coverts-original.json` = the covert and shoulder lofts before the rework), `rework-08-pigeon-decisions.py`.

Defects found and what was done:

- **Sitting pose was a crouch.** Measured by forward kinematics: hind pads 19 mm in the air, sitting bones 87 mm above the ground, hocks 111 mm up, forearms raked backwards under the chest. Solved anew by hand: sitting bones on the ground (3 mm), hip 0.132 m high, femur 7 degrees above level with the stifle forward, tibia back down to a hock 45 mm high, metatarsus and paw flat, back at 60 degrees, scapula at its limit (-35), forelegs straight with the pads 0.14 m ahead of the hind pads (they overlapped before), neck at 70 degrees with a level head, tail on the ground. All five contacts within 3 mm of the ground. Joint limits widened where the anatomy allows it: hind paw flexion -60 -> -80 (plantigrade rest in the sit), tail root -50 -> -75. `sit-and-pant` uses the pose and follows.
- **1:200 and 1:500 structure: rib hoops hung loose under the spine.** A sternum bar now closes the ribcage at both scales.
- **1:500 structure: limb bones were wedge lofts thickest at the wrong end** (femur thin at the hip, wide at the stifle). Femur, tibia, metatarsals, humerus, radius/ulna and metacarpals are now straight shafts joint to joint, as thick as the rule demands for 1:500 (the drawn limb or 75 mm).
- Not changed: body, thermal, 2D shapes (the 2D follows the rig; the new sit made `near-hind-thigh` too narrow at the end of the 1:200 band at a 10 degree femur, which is why the femur stands at 7).

Validation: `VALID animals/labrador-dog (catalog asset + volumes source): schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0`; after publishing `--catalog-only`: VALID.

Remains imperfect: trunk about 10 % long; 1:500 structure mixes feature-thick limb bars with real-width spine, ribs and tail, and its sternum is a 76 mm sausage; phalanges at 1:500 are thin strokes; walk phases, sniffing pose, thermal sheets and 1:100 were not re-read after the changes (they are untouched).

## 7. animals/pigeon — wing rebuilt, published

Reference (feral pigeon, my knowledge): length 0.31-0.34 m, span 0.63-0.70 m, wing chord (wrist to tip) 0.21-0.23 m, humerus about 45 mm, ulna 55 mm, carpometacarpus with digits 55-60 mm, ten primaries, about ten secondaries, tarsus 30 mm. Model before: humerus 45, forearm 54, hand 200 mm (the hand bone ran to the primary tips), no feather bones.

Defect: in every opened pose (`wings-half-open`, the four take-off poses, the three airborne poses, clip `take-off`) the wing was a blade 25 mm wide — the folded primaries swung out as one stick, no wing surface, span of a stick. The covert shield on the forearm and the scapular lobe on the humerus reached 80 and 65 mm past the elbow and stood out as prongs when the wing lifted.

Rebuilt:

- **Rig**: hand bone 0.200 -> 0.058 m (carpometacarpus and digits). New bones per side: `p1`-`p10` on the hand (bases every 6 mm, lengths 105-150 mm, longest P9, tip 0.20 m from the wrist as before) and `s1`-`s10` on the forearm (lengths 100-75 mm). Rest = folded: primaries along the hand, turned 10 degrees inward so the tips lie over the tail; secondaries converging under the covert shield. Feather bones carry no mass (the hand and forearm masses include them).
- **Poses**: eight opened poses fan the tracts (primaries 2 to 68 degrees from the hand axis, secondaries 84 to 100 degrees from the forearm), scaled by how far the pose opens the wing (0.3 crouch, 0.5 half open, 0.9-1 flight); the inward fold is taken out when the wing opens.
- **Body, all four scales**: the primaries blade is replaced by 20 feather vanes per side (lens section 22-26 mm wide, 7.6 mm thick = the 1:50 feature, identical at every scale), a hand mass with its coverts, the forearm coverts cut 14 mm behind the elbow, the scapular lobe split: its front stays on the humerus, its rear lies on the trunk as `near-/far-scapulars`. Folded, the bird reads as before; opened, it has a feathered wing of about 0.58 m span in the downstroke.
- **Thermal**: every new solid referenced in `plumage-wings`. **Structure**: one rachis bar per remex.
- `decisions` of every scale state the tract model in English and German.

Validation: `VALID animals/pigeon (catalog asset + volumes source): schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0`; after publishing `--catalog-only --volumes`: VALID.

Remains imperfect, plainly:

- **2D drawings were not redrawn.** The hand still carries the old `near-/far-primaries` blade shape in all views and scales. Folded it matches the feather stack (the silhouette rule passes), but in the opened poses the 2D drawings still show a stick wing, and the humerus shape still swings the scapular lobe out. Per-feather 2D shapes are needed (20 per side); a 22 mm feather is below the print minimum at the ends of the 1:100, 1:200 and 1:500 bands, so the coarse scales need grouped fan shapes.
- Feathers are 7.6 mm thick at every scale and overlap one another instead of lying shingled; rachis bars are 7.6 mm thick.
- Opened wings have no patagium and no covert rows over the feather bases; a notch shows at the wrist between P1 and S1.
- The upstroke poses keep their old bone angles (wings swept forward over the head).
- Read after the rebuild: 1:50 standing, half open, wings up, wings down; 1:200 wings down. Not read: thermal and structure sheets in flight, 1:100, 1:500, walk and peck clips (untouched by the change).

## Fundamental issues

- `volumes-feature` lets a thin solid pass at coarse scales only when it is identical to a 1:50 solid, and at 1:50 only at 7.5 mm: a feather, a membrane or a sheet that is not an `extrude` cannot be modelled at its real 1-2 mm thickness. The herring gull's 2 mm vanes pass only through the "thinnest drawing of the bone" branch. A sheet gauge for lofts would be the root fix; not done.
- `r3-sheet.ts` has no `--file`, so a pose or rig candidate can only be looked at after it is written to the live catalog file.
- The 1:500 structure rule forces limb bars as thick as the drawn limb while ribs, spine and tail may keep real widths; the result is a mixed diagram (labrador).
