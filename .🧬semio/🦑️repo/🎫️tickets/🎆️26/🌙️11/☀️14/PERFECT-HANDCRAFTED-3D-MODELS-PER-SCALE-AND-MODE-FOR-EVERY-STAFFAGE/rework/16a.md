# Rework 16a — toaster, electric kettle, laptop, desktop monitor (2026-11-22)

Author 16a, taking over four assets of `rework/16.txt` after report `rework/16.md` (which only added heat sources). Scripts with the hand-chosen numbers: `rework_16a_lib.py` (constructors, member-wise catalog write), `rework-16a-<slug>-model.py` (one per asset: rig, body, thermal, structure, 2D of every scale), `rework-16a-look.sh` (renders the sheets of the loop). Each script writes the volumes source and a candidate document; `--publish` re-reads the catalog file, replaces only the changed members and splices the volumes.

Order of work per asset: all sheets of the old model rendered and read, reference table, deviations, rebuild, `r3-validate.ts --file <candidate> --volumes`, publish, re-render, re-read, `--catalog-only`. Because `r3-sheet.ts` has no `--file` form, a new rig can only be rendered after it is in the catalog, so the candidate was validated first and the sheets were read after publishing.

### Defects found in the old model
- Lever, lever slot, dial and cancel button on the long face; the lever bone and the dial bone placed and oriented for that; the 2D drawings of all three scales drew them there.
- Slots centred, so no room for the mechanism behind the control end; wrap 300 mm long and only 152 mm deep (too long and too slim for the class).
- No cord, crumb tray as a strip on the long side, structure with the guide rod beside the long wall and a lever arm through the long face.
- 1:500 body without any lever; 1:200 lever as a cube on the long face.

### Rebuilt
- **Rig**: `slices` moved to x = -0.010; `lever` now at the end face (x 0.134, y 0.140, prismatic 0…-40 mm, length 16 mm); `dial` at (0.134, 0.056) pointing along +x, revolute with `twistMinDeg -10`, `twistMaxDeg 270`; the three poses set the dial by `twistDeg 120` instead of a sagittal angle; anchor `slot-outlet` follows the slots; foot footprints 24 mm.
- **Body** 1:50 (24 solids): feet, moulded plinth, chrome wrap as a ten-section skin (superellipse plan, exponent 6), cap rim of seven sections, deck with two real slots, chamber with wells, crumb pan, the two lifter bars of the carriage on which the slices stand, lever slot, lever (stem and paddle as one loft), turned dial with pointer, two turned buttons, crumb-tray pull, sleeve, cord drop and cord run, each slice a crust ring with its crumb. 1:100 (14), 1:200 (5), 1:500 (3) modelled anew for 15 / 30 / 75 mm; the lever stays a part on the narrow end at every scale.
- **Thermal**: closed envelope of plinth and wrap, cap rim and deck, bread, controls (lever, dial, both buttons), feet, slot-air patches, four element cards (7.5 mm) inside; coarser scales with centre element or element bank. `thermal.regions.control.areaM2` 0.0057 → 0.0068 (measured surface of the new controls).
- **Structure**: plinth frame on the foot pairs, chamber tube (1 mm), slotted end plate, strut, guide rod, two lifter bars, yoke, yoke arm, sleeve joint, lever arm with joint, dial spindle with joint, masses.
- **2D** 1:50, 1:100, 1:200 redrawn in all three views from the same section numbers as the solids: lever and dial at the right end of the side elevation, the control end with lever slot, lever, dial, pointer and buttons as the front elevation, sleeve and cord at the other end; the dial is a `revolved` shape about its own bone so it stays right while it twists. 1:500 stays `unavailable` in 2D (0.6 x 0.4 mm on paper), which I did not change. Representation decisions, summary, provenance notes and `dimensions.widthM` (0.30 → 0.29) rewritten to the new object.

### Validation
`r3-validate.ts --file <candidate> --volumes`: `VALID … schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0` (run again after the rule `volumes-connected` appeared in the shared modules during my session: it rightly found the slices floating in the chamber and the 1:100 plinth 4 mm off the wrap; fixed with the lifter bars, a 1:100 carriage bar and a hem section, then `VALID` again); after publishing `r3-validate.ts appliances/toaster --catalog-only`: `VALID`. Read after the rebuild: 1:50 body, structure, thermal cut, poses `popped`; 1:100, 1:200, 1:500 body; 2D 1:50. Rendered but not re-read after the rebuild: 1:100/1:200/1:500 thermal and structure, pose `idle`, the four clip instants (the clips are step clips between the three poses), 2D 1:100 and 1:200.

### Still imperfect
- The cord is cut after 130 mm at the outline; no plug; at 1:100 only its sleeve is modelled (the 7.5 mm cord is below the feature) and at 1:200 and 1:500 neither. The slot guard wires and the bread rack are not modelled (2 mm wire).
- In pose `idle` the slices are still hidden by sinking the carriage 40 mm (inherited device of the asset), so lever (up) and carriage (down) disagree in that pose.
- The end strut of the structure is a full-width plate, a simplification of two side rails.
- 1:500 lever is a 75 mm lug (the feature size), far larger than the real paddle.

## 2. appliances/electric-kettle — done

| Feature | Real product | Old model | New model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Height on base | 220–250 mm | 240 mm (with a 46 mm lid knob) | 223 mm (lid crown 219, release button 223) |
| Jug | 150 mm at the foot tapering to 140 mm, 175 mm tall, 0.7 mm stainless, rolled rim | the same | kept (turned hollow shell with floor, rim and seat collar) |
| Pouring | short pressed beak at the rim, 20–30 mm proud, with a scale filter | **teapot spout** from two thirds of the height, 46 mm proud, tip at 190 mm | beak of five sections, 26 mm proud, top at the rim |
| Lid | hinged at the handle, opens 75–90 degrees, release button on the handle | **loose lid with a knob**, prismatic "rattle" bone | lid bone on the hinge axis at the handle (revolute 0…-85 degrees), knuckle, release button; rattle as 0.5–2.3 degrees about the hinge |
| Handle | C handle from the hinge housing over the rim into the wall above the skirt, grip 20 x 28 mm, clearance 35–40 mm | C handle, starts below the rim, no relation to the lid | swept through twelve sections from the hinge housing, 20 x 28 mm, 37 mm clearance |
| Switch | rocker paddle low on the grip, on a pivot | fixed 10 mm box | paddle 22 x 16 x 7.5 mm on its own bone (revolute 0…-20 degrees), down in `heating` and `boiling` |
| Water window | both sides | one side | both sides |
| Base | 180 mm, 30 mm high, cord 0.75 m from its edge | no cord | strain-relief sleeve and 7.5 mm cord under the handle, cut at the handle's reach |
| Overall length | 215–240 mm | 245 mm | 230 mm |

### Defects found in the old model
- The jug carried a teapot spout and a loose knobbed lid: the object read as a stove-top kettle on a power base, not as a jug kettle.
- Rig without any real pivot: lid on a vertical slide, switch not a part, no pose with the lid open.
- Handle with no hinge housing; one window; no cord; at 1:200 lid and base inflated to 40 mm.

### Rebuilt
- **Rig**: `lid` re-rigged as a revolute bone at the hinge (body-local 0.066, 0.179, pointing over the lid to the beak, limits -85…0); new bone `switch` (child of `handle`, revolute -20…0, 5 g taken from the handle mass); poses `heating`/`boiling` press the switch, `boiling` lifts the lid 1.4 degrees, clip `rattle` now keys hinge angles; new pose `filling` (lid open 80 degrees); anchor `spout-tip` moved to the beak mouth; `dimensions` 0.24 x 0.245 → 0.223 x 0.23 m.
- **Body** 1:50 (15 solids): base, sleeve, cord drop, cord run, skirt, shell, beak, two windows, water, handle, release button, switch paddle, lid (turned about its own centre in the lid bone's frame), hinge knuckle. 1:100 (9), 1:200 (5), 1:500 (5) modelled anew; the handle is a sweep along its centre line at every scale (helper `sweep` in the lib computes the section planes from the hand-chosen centre line).
- **Thermal**: base with sleeve and cord, element disc, closed jug envelope, beak, water and windows, lid skin with underside marked, knuckle, handle, button, switch, steam patch and plume sleeve at the beak mouth.
- **Structure**: base housing with ribs on its support ring, connector joint, wall, skirt, beak, hinge pin as the lid joint, lid and knuckle, handle core with two boss joints, switch with its pivot, water footprint, masses.
- **2D** 1:50, 1:100, 1:200 redrawn in all views; the lid is a `revolved` shape about its own axis on the lid bone, so side, front and plan stay right when it opens (checked in pose `filling`); handle outline from the same sweep stations as the solid. 1:500 2D stays `unavailable`.

### Validation
`r3-validate.ts --file <candidate> --volumes`: `VALID … schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0`; `--catalog-only` after publishing: `VALID`. Read after the rebuild: 1:50 body, pose `filling` (3D and 2D), structure, thermal cut; 1:200 body; 2D 1:100. Rendered but not re-read: 1:100 and 1:500 body, the coarse thermal and structure sheets, poses `off`/`heating`, the four clip instants.

### Still imperfect
- At 1:200 the lid dome is raised from 14 to 25 mm, because a 14 mm dome is below the print minimum at the end of the band (1:350); the 3D lid of 1:200 follows the drawing.
- The beak is a closed cone that passes through the wall (no filter, no open channel); the opened lid's skirt would graze the seat collar near the hinge (no clearance modelled).
- The cord is cut under the handle, no plug; at 1:100 only the sleeve, coarser nothing.
- The jug still does not lift off the base as a pose (the rig keeps `body` fixed to `base`).
- By mistake one intermediate publish of this asset went out while two 1:100 cord solids failed `volumes-feature` (the shared rule changed during the session and my command chain did not stop on `INVALID`); it was corrected within minutes and `rework-16a-publish.sh` now publishes only after `VALID`.

### What I looked at
Old model, all read: 1:50 body (open), `closed`, structure, thermal cut; 1:100 body; 1:200 body; 1:500 body; 2D 1:50. Rendered but not read: 1:100/1:200/1:500 thermal and structure, `open-idle`, the four clip instants, 2D 1:100 and 1:200.

### Judgement
The body is a real clamshell at every scale (tub with bilge, deck plate with keyboard well and caps, glass trackpad, speaker fields, ports, intake slots, screws, hinge barrel on its ledge, lid shell, cover glass, panel, camera), the rig has its one real pivot with real limits, the structure is the tray, walls, ribs, deck plate, hinge brackets, lid back plate and rim with the hinge joints, and the drawings agree with it. I found nothing there that a rebuild would make more right, so I did not replace good work for the sake of having touched it. `r3-validate.ts appliances/laptop --volumes` was `VALID` before I started, including the new `volumes-connected` rule.

### Rebuilt
- **Thermal 1:50**: `th-logic-board` and `th-battery` (two slabs) replaced by logic board 190 x 70 mm, shielded processor, flat heat pipe with its bridge to the processor, two fin stacks at the exhaust, two 48 mm blower fans over the intake slots, three battery cells of 86 x 78 x 5 mm; board, processor and pipe belong to `deck`, fans, fins and cells to `underside`. Checked in a closed-pose section. **1:100**: board, two fans, one battery pack. 1:200 and 1:500 unchanged (no internal part there). Script `rework-16a-laptop-internals.py`.

### Validation
`r3-validate.ts appliances/laptop --volumes`: `VALID … schema 0, validateAsset 0, checkVolumes 0`; `--catalog-only` after publishing: `VALID`.

### Still imperfect
- No charger or cable; the fans are plain discs (no scroll housing, no blades); the internal parts are solids inside a tub that the body models as a closed loft, not parts in a modelled cavity.
- 1:500 is three square-edged slabs with feet (legitimate at 75 mm, nothing more).
- The 2D plan at 1:50 draws the six key rows as bars, not as caps.
