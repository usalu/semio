# Willow, ivy and bamboo detail review

| Subject | Inspected source | Defect in the canonical model |
|---|---|---|
| Weeping willow | `8e2bc7f15a` | The lower crown consists of uninterrupted, opaque, metre-scale strips. Several pass in front of the fork for nearly its full height. The fine and coarse physical views do not show individual lanceolate blades. |
| Ivy on trellis | `1566b5e367` | The fine trellis has eight vertical and seven horizontal timber laths, each 20 mm in face width and 10 mm deep. The 1:200 physical and structural models omit both courses while the thermal model still carries them. At 1:500 the thermal model also omits the grid. |
| Bamboo clump | `bfee357024` | The low-detail apex joins separate terminal leaves into a single blade. Some grouped side branches at 1:200 occupy over 0.5 m in length and 0.2 m in width while the 1:100 individual leaves span about 0.1 m. |

The willow's blade profile and dimensions follow the narrow, long-pointed leaves described in the [Flora of China](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=2&taxon_id=200005760). The woody centreline, foliage clusters and terminal blades remain independently identifiable in posed geometry.
