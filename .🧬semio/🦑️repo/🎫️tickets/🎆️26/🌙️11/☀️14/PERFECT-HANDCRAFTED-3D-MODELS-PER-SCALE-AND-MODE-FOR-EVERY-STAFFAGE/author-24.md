## How to use author 09's person kit (and mine) — corrected

Run from `T` with the repo bun. Author 09's kit (`author_09_lib.py`, `author_09_build.py`, `author-09-fit.ts`, `author-09-diff.ts`, `author-09-area.ts`) works but has these traps: its thermal tuner no longer exists (thermal copies are never scaled; `thermal_from` factors stay 1.0), its fitter uses one multiplier for rx and ry per section and cannot move section centres, and its `finish()` generates members over the bone length (not the body length), joints larger than the limb and emitters at full length. My additions sit on top of it:

1. `author_24_lib.py`: `load/save`, `symmetrise(body, skip)` (mean of mirrored lofts/ellipsoids, w sign kept), `clip_members(slug, vols)` (re-sample member cores inside the u-range of the bone's primary body loft), `fix_joints` (ball <= 1.5 x member core), `shorten_emitters` (0.6 feature), `mess(slug, scale, rules)` (failing measurements as text).
2. `python3 author-24-person.py <slug> [--only N] [--finish]` with `author_24_cfg_<slug with underscores>.py`: starts from the seed (`author-09-seed/<slug>.json`, a copy of `volumes-seed.ts` output), replaces fists by mittens, sets exponent 4 on sleeves and legs, ends the forearm at the cuff, fits 1:50 (author 09's descent), mirrors (`sym_skip` for asymmetric poses), runs `post50`, derives 1:100 -> 1:200 -> 1:500 by `coarsen_all` + `DROP` + `special` + fit (+ `KITFIT` fine fit) + floor (75 mm), `--finish` builds thermal (spec from the seed's 1:50 references, `thermal_skip`, `thermal_extra`), structure, joints, emitters, decisions (`DECISIONS`, `D(en, de)`), `post_finish`. The config keys are documented at the top of the script; `author_24_cfg_pregnant_person.py` and `author_24_cfg_runner_with_blade_prosthesis.py` are worked examples, `author-24-child.py` the hand-written variant.
3. Tools I added (all read `volumes/<category>/<slug>.json`, write nothing unless named): `author-24-kitfit.ts` = `kit-fit.ts` with raster cell `feature/8` (the kit's `feature/2` is 37.5 mm at 1:500 and overestimates IoU by 0.03-0.05 against the product; `--shift --sections --rounds 3 --bounds 0.6,2.2 --write` is what made 1:500 pass for both new assets), `author-24-limits.ts <asset> <scale> [list]` (per solid smallest extent against the C13 limit: the product's aggregate `minDimensionM` ignores C13), `author-24-outside.ts` (which structure/thermal solid leaves the body silhouette, per view), `author-24-ext.ts` (drawn extents per view and the shapes at each end: the C11 check), `author-24-th.py <slug> [scales]` (area deviations of the product engine), `author-24-sil.py` / `author-24-exp.py` (try a Python edit of the body list, print silhouette/bounds or the kit-thermal table, restore the source).
4. Order that worked: seed -> `--finish` is cheap (3 s), bodies take 1-2 minutes per asset; check -> `author-24-ext.ts` for views-disagree -> `author-24-th.py` -> `kit-thermal.ts` for the per-region table (the `model finding` lines say which regions bury a surface) -> try fixes with `author-24-exp.py` -> render and read every sheet.
5. Lessons: at 1:500 a toe bone with a drawn capsule needs a solid of its limit size (merge the toe into the heel capsule in 2D only when the toe has nothing else drawn); interpenetration, not size, is what lowers exposed clothing area (pack on the shirt, hands in the thighs); `kit-thermal.ts`' region binding and the product's differ, always judge by `volumes-check.ts`.
