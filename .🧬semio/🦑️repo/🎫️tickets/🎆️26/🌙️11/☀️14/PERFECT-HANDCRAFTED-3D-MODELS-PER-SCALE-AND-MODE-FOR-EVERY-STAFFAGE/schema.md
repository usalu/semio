## Measurements and threshold proposals (design values kept in code)

Measured on the mechanically derived sample figure (the old 1:50 solids at every scale; real authored data will differ):

- `volumes-silhouette`: old 1:50 body vs 2D: IoU 0.918 elevation / 0.875 front / 0.944 plan, Hausdorff 56 / 43 / 18 mm (limit 15 mm). The Hausdorff limit of 2 features is very tight at 1:50 (15 mm) because a single stray 2D stroke or an appendage (hair, shoe) dominates the maximum; **proposal**: keep, but report the 99th percentile alongside the maximum if authors find outliers unfixable. Not weakened.
- `volumes-thermal-coverage` area: sample regions deviate 21-129 % (torso 45 %, feet 129 %) against `areaM2`; `areaM2` of body regions is probably the exposed skin/clothing area, not the geometric outer area of the solids; **proposal**: allow a per-region `areaBasis` or compare against `areaM2` only for non-body kinds. Kept 15 % / 30 %.
- `volumes-structure-coverage` containment: members of a mechanically sized capsule (0.04 m) stick out up to 82 mm; the rule works as designed.
- `volumes-monotonic`: triangle counts grow with radius-dependent tessellation (structure 1:500 radius 75 mm gives more triangles than 1:200 with the same solid count); **proposal**: compare triangles at one fixed chord (not per-radius adaptive) or use only the solid count and require a strict drop of the count or the vertex budget declared in decisions.
- `volumes-bounds`: one smallest feature (7.5 mm at 1:50) is satisfied only by solids with exact extents; the sample's depth is off by 12.7 mm at 1:50.
- `eligibleBones` uses `dimensions.heightM` as the drawn height.
- `volumes-bones`: a coarse scale that drops a limb part (feet at 1:500 in the sample) fails because the 2D shapes still draw it; that is intended.

## Proposals with measurements (2026-11-14 21:20)

1. **Lattice thermal area at the coarsest scale (author 23, tower-crane).** Measured with the product `measureVolumes`: exposed area of the lattice region 374 / 323 / 296 m2 at 1:50 / 1:100 / 1:200 (every bar modelled) against 768 m2 at 1:500, where an `envelope: true` prism replaces the lattice: the hull surface of a lattice is about twice the surface of its porous bars (ratio 2.05), and every web inside a prism is buried. No closed solid at 1:500 can reproduce the area. Proposal (two options, the second is the smaller change): (a) an optional solid field `porosity` in (0, 1] on `envelope: true` thermal solids, the exposed area of such a solid counts times `porosity` (here 0.49, derived by the author from the finer scale and stated in `decisions`); (b) at scales of 1:200 and coarser compare slender and envelope regions with the exposed area of the finest authored scale instead of `areaM2` (the totals rule C15 then becomes a consistency check between scales, which is what an envelope can honestly promise). I would take (a): it is explicit in the data, testable (the validator can check `porosity` against the finer scale's measured area within 15 %), and it needs no new rule.
2. **Section views declare the retained side (author 20, retractable-bollard).** The section silhouette assumes the retained half is the one away from the camera (`CUT` in `silhouette`: elevation keeps z >= plane, front keeps x <= plane, plan keeps y <= plane). A front section drawn at x = 0 that still draws parts at x = +0.15..0.36 can never agree, and the author had to delete drawn parts. Proposal: optional `ViewSection.retained: "far" | "near"` (default `far`, today's behaviour); the validator reports `volumes-views-disagree`-style information when a drawn part lies on the removed side. Needs a schema field, so it is the coordinator's decision.
3. **Hollow parts in section views (author 20).** The silhouette is the clipped projection of everything beyond the plane, so the far inner wall of a hollow sleeve fills its bore; the author repaired the 2D at the root (closed outline, C17). This is correct behaviour and needs no rule change; the 2D guide should say that a hollow part in a section view is drawn as a filled closed shape with the bore as a hole.
4. **Product speed after swap 8-10:** tower-crane draft: silhouette rule 1:50 25 s (it was about 10 min), english-oak 1:50 silhouette 31 s (104 s); the full catalog (106 spliced assets) ran in 233 s wall with 4 workers.
