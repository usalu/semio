## 7. Still imperfect, stated plainly

- The part sizes beyond the data sheet (rail sections, carriage bars, cowl, seat, guard tubes, counterweight shape, motor and housing sizes, tilt-cylinder mounting points) are my knowledge of the class, not published numbers; the published dimensions (S1) are met exactly.
- Lift chains are modelled as front runs fixed to the inner stage (rigid solids cannot change length); in lifted poses the chain runs on below the carriage anchor behind the carriage. Back runs, hoses, cables and the hydraulic power unit behind the battery are not modelled.
- Clip timing kept: the lift of 2.4 m in 4 s is 0.6 m/s against 0.48 m/s laden (S1). The clips interpolate between key poses, so between `lowered-forks` and `lifted-load` the pallet rises with the forks from the first frame instead of after the 40 mm the forks travel inside the openings, and the ZT free-lift kink is not shown.
- At 1:500 the forks run inside the pallet block (fork openings are not drawn at 75 mm), and the 1:500 roof is a slab.
- `chassis-paint` 12 and `brakes` 0.25 are area corrections for the reviewer (section 3); the chassis value contains the battery-facing faces until C31 is implemented in the product `outerArea`.
- The tyre tread is drawn (texture strokes), not modelled; tessellation of the round parts is the tool's.
