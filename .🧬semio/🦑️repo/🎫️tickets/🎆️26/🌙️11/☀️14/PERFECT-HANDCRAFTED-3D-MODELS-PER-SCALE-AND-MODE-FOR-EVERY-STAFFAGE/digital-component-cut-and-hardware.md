## Verification method

The 1:500 glass-balustrade and tilt-window print bands remain intentionally unavailable in the catalog. The four digital body, temperature and structural scenes for each are still verified. The source inspection compared each physical cut or mechanism against its thermal and structural counterparts. The maintained geometry tests reopen the native solids, sample their meshes and compare measured widths, clear gaps or individual fastener rows. For the insulating glass, the normal of the largest pane triangle gives an orientation-independent measurement of both glass thicknesses and their clear cavity.

All 78 maintained equipment, plant and component geometry tests passed against the published sources. The independent four-scale section tests measured the glass cavities normal to the panes, counted wheel and bolt rows, and reopened each masonry course.
