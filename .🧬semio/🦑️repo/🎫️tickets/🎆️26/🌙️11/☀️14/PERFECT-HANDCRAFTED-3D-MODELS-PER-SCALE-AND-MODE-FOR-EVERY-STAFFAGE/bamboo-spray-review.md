The 1:500 physical model also omits the visible leaf-litter planting bed and its root barrier, while the structural representation continues to show their footprint.

The leaf revision puts a sequence of narrow, folded blades on each of the original articulated side branches. The blade root is an open face of the supporting twig that continues directly into the closed blade shell. Each terminal fan reuses its three separate, individually shaped 1:50 leaves on the moving culm tip. A reverse detail view also exposed blunt blocks along the 1:100 lateral branches. Each such station now retains two opposed blades from the corresponding fine fan, including its actual supporting twig wherever present.
