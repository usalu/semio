## Loop per asset

1. **Look.** Render `r3-sheet` for every scale (50, 100, 200, 500) and every list (body, thermal, structure) in the default pose, plus at 1:50 every other pose and two instants of every clip. Read every PNG with the Read tool; use `--only <view>` for close-ups of hands, faces, feet, wheels, joints, hardware.
2. **Judge against reality, written down first.** Before touching anything, write into your report a reference table for the subject with real numbers from your own knowledge (anthropometry such as DIN 33402 / Drillis-Contini segment lengths and breadths, head 1/7.5 of stature, biacromial and hip breadth, limb girths at several stations; veterinary and zoological proportions and skeleton topology; manufacturer-typical dimensions of vehicles, machines, appliances, furniture, building components to DIN/EN; botanical habit, branching order, leaf arrangement). Then measure the model against it (bone lengths from the rig, section radii from the solids, extents from the sheet titles) and list every deviation. Typical defects found so far: stick-thin legs and arms with no muscle form, narrow shoulders, boxy torsos, missing necks, tiny or narrow heads, mitten or missing hands and feet, one hand with finger bones and the other without, kinked animal forelegs and wrong joint order (scapula–humerus–radius–carpus–cannon–pastern–hoof; hind: femur–tibia–hock–cannon), a second skull at the pelvis, ribs as loose hoops, mirrors and structural members floating in space at 1:500, wheels without tyres/rims/arches, slab-like car bodies, furniture without real joinery, appliances as plain boxes, trees as ball clusters.
3. **Rebuild by hand.** Re-author every solid that is not right, at every scale. No generator, no fitted-from-drawing dump, no copy of another asset's list, no 1:50 list with entries deleted: each scale is modelled for its smallest feature (7.5 / 15 / 30 / 75 mm) with the same proportions, bones and ids where a part survives. Organic forms are lofts through real anatomical cross-sections (a thigh, a calf, a forearm, a ribcage, a neck each have their characteristic section sequence), turned parts are revolves, sheet and profile parts are extrudes; boxes only where the subject is a box. Parts meet: no gaps at joints in any pose (overlap the joint with a rounded end so bending never opens a crack), nothing floats, nothing pokes through. Small python or TypeScript edit scripts named `rework-<NN>-<slug>-*.py|ts` in `T` that state the hand-chosen numbers are fine; they are your chisel, not a generator.
   - **Skeleton / rig** (`asset.rig`): right topology for the subject, bilaterally symmetric where the subject is, joints at the anatomical or mechanical pivots with real limits, bone lengths to the reference table. Add the bones that are missing (e.g. clavicles/shoulder girdle, spine segments, both hands alike, ankle/toe, scapula, tail chain, steering/suspension/door/boom pivots) and bind poses, clips, 2D shapes, anchors, thermal regions and structural members to the corrected rig so everything stays valid. Every pose and clip must still read naturally afterwards — render them.
   - **Body** (dark/light line and solid): right from all six directions and in every pose.
   - **Thermal**: every region a spatial solid at every scale, layers nested, hidden regions inside, emitters as solids, areas consistent with `areaM2`; the thermal sheet must read as the same object.
   - **Structure** (forces, moments): the real load path. For living bodies an anatomically correct skeleton (skull with mandible, cervical/thoracic/lumbar spine with its curvature, ribcage attached to sternum and spine, scapulae and clavicles, pelvis with sacrum, long bones with their epiphyses, hands and feet), simplified per scale but never wrong; for objects the actual frame, chassis, joinery or load-bearing section. Members connected at joints, supports where the asset stands, masses where they are.
   - **2D drawings** (`representations[scale].geometry.elevation|front|plan`): when the 3D form changes, redraw the affected shapes by hand so drawings and model agree (use `r3-project2d` as a ruler, then draw economical, well-layered shapes; check with `r3-2d`).
   - `decisions` (en first, de second, real sentences) per scale say what was modelled and why.
4. **Prove.** `r3-validate.ts <asset> --volumes` must end `VALID` with no finding. Never weaken a rule, threshold, test or tool to pass; if a rule is measurably wrong, fix the rule at its root in `S/🔨️modules` with a fixture and a test and note it. Re-render everything from step 1 and read it again; iterate until you would sign the model as a professional modeller. Then publish with `volumes-insert.py`, run `r3-validate.ts <asset> --catalog-only`, and go to the next asset.
5. **Report** after each asset in `T/rework/<NN>.md`: reference table, defects found, what you rebuilt (rig, body, thermal, structure, 2D), validation line, what remains imperfect stated plainly. Assets you did not reach: list them as "not started".
