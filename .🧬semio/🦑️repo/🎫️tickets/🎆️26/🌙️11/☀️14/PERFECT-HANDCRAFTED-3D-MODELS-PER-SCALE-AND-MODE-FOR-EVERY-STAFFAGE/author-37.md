- Root fix in the asset (2D): the section elevations (1:50, 1:100) and the 1:200 elevation drew the hollow interior above the water empty, so the visible far inner wall that every correct 3D shows there gave p95 0.039 m (limit 0.015). Added the filled detail shape `wall-far` (bone pot, region wall, tone 0.55) at all three scales (the far inner wall seen through the cut); IoU 0.986-0.995 afterwards.
- Check (product, final run): PASS at 1:50/1:100/1:200 (all rules). When I first ran it, `areaDeviation:base` (declared 0.0232 m2 is the hob contact face, exposed 0.0052) and `areaDeviation:handle` (0.003 vs 0.00496, ratio 1.65) failed and I listed both in `area-review.md` unapplied; the coordinator then set `base` to `areaBasis: exchange` and `handle` to 0.00496, which makes the pot pass; the aggregate `volumes-feature` line (C13 limit, tool defect noted in `tool-requests.md`) also passes now.
- Tool quirk found and fixed: the kernel closes a revolve profile that does not touch the axis through the axis, so a hollow wall or bead profile becomes a filled disc unless its first point is repeated at the end (`ring()` in `author_37_lib.py`); the first version of the pot (wall, rim) and the fountain basin carried such lids, caught by the renders and `author-37-mesh.ts` (mesh area/volume per solid).
- Renders (all three sheets read after the last change): overlays green with thin red slivers at the chamfer, rim bead and handle tips; the axonometric body reads as a stock pot with rim bead and ear handles; thermal cutaway shows base, wall skin, water slab and steam disc nested; structure inside the body.
- Imperfect: water volume 1.07 L vs the stated 1 L comes from the 0.0853 m inner radius; the 2D wall (6 mm) is thicker than the stated 0.8 mm material wall (3D follows the material at 1:50 and the drawing at 1:100/1:200).
