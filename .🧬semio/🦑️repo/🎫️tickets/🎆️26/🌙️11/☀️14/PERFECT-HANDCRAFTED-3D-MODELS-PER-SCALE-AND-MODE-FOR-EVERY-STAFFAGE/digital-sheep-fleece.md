# Sheep fleece

The published ewe has staggered wool locks across the rump, chest and neck. The collar carries shallower crimp than the trunk and follows the neck into the grazing pose. At 1:500 the larger locks preserve the silhouette and the rump rounds through a 34 mm dome. The sheep’s light fleece contrasts with its darker muzzle and lower legs.

Relief is the greatest difference between a radial peak and the average of its two angular neighbours over four sampled cross-sections. The independent Three.js ray test also checks positive signed volumes, two oppositely oriented faces at each edge and rump curvature. The plan, elevation and front contours of each shell were fitted against its posed triangles and retained in the authoring inputs. The 1:500 elevation associates the dock with the fleece of the rump.
