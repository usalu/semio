## Method

**Things (C29, C31).** `areaM2` is the total outer area of the real object, by hand: geometry and reference dimensions from the data sheet, standard or drawing named in the row. Decision: SET the hand total. When the honestly modelled 1:50 value agrees with the hand total within 15 % the check turns green; when it does not, the value still is the hand total and the model is the defect (finding), never the other way round. `heatW` of operating regions is the physical datum and stays; the changed flux density `heatW / areaM2` is stated.

**Living subjects (C18, C27).** The body-model `areaM2` are physiological data. Decided per subject:

1. Never changed: `areaM2` of exchange regions (`areaBasis: "exchange"`: hair, fur, fleece, feathers; C19) and every `metabolicShare` (heat production is physiology, not geometry).
2. Target per non-exchange body region: the honest outer area of the real segment or garment, by hand (girths and lengths from ISO 7250 / DIN 33402 / ANSUR II for people, breed and veterinary measures for animals, garment and shoe sizes), cross-checked with the product's 1:50 outer area. A region whose model is not sound (solid running under another region's garment or hair, buried or overlapping solids, garment drawn larger than the real one) gets the hand value as target and a model finding.
3. Humans: the sum of the non-exchange body regions is kept (C27; with the fixed hair the subject total `Σ areaM2 = skinAreaM2 × fcl` stays, i.e. the ISO 9920 whole-body clothing area factor of the outfit stays). Bare-skin regions (kind `skin`) are anatomy with clothing factor `f = 1`: they keep their value unless their own hand value (the bare part of the real body between garment edges) differs beyond the band, then they take it. The garments (clothing, shoes, headwear) carry the outfit's looseness and are rescaled together: new `= target × k`, `k = (Σ declared non-exchange − Σ new skin) / Σ garment targets`, where the target is the declared value for a garment inside its band and the hand value for one outside; the last digit absorbs rounding so the sum is exact. Then every new value must lie within 30 % of the 1:50 model (C18) where the model is sound.
3a. Physical floor: a garment cannot have less outer area than the skin it covers (`f ≥ 1`). Where the fixed sum would push a garment below `f = 1` (or squeeze sound garments out of their band), the declared outfit total itself is below what the outfit physically has: the declared `Σ areaM2 / skinAreaM2` is the whole-body clothing area factor `fcl`, an outfit datum (ISO 9920: `fcl ≈ 1 + 0.28 Icl`), not a physiological one. Then the garments take their honest hand values (`r5-area-c27.py --honest`), while the physiological datum, the DuBois skin area `skinAreaM2`, `Σ skinFraction = 1`, every bare-skin area and every `metabolicShare` stay; the new `fcl` is listed against ISO 9920. This goes beyond the letter of C27 (which equates the sum with DuBois) and is listed as rule conflict for the coordinator.
4. `skinFraction` (the skin share under a region, `Σ = 1`): bare-skin regions keep `f = areaM2 / (skinFraction × skinAreaM2) = 1`, so a changed skin value moves its `skinFraction`; the difference is booked against the garment that shares the moved boundary (shorts hem: `leg-skin` against `leg-clothing`; sleeve hem: `arm-skin` against `arm-clothing`; hat or helmet rim: face skin against the headwear). Garments keep their `skinFraction` (a larger shoe does not cover more foot) unless the garment covers more anatomy (a boot shaft takes the ankle and lower-shin skin from the trousers). Every resulting garment factor `f` is listed; plausible is 1.0 to about 1.7 (ISO 9920 local factors; shoes and boots up to about 2, a hat brim more).
5. Furred animals: every donor segment is an exchange coat, so C27's "sum of `areaM2` unchanged" cannot be met without changing an exchange area, which C19 forbids. Ruling of this pass (listed as rule conflict for the coordinator): the physiological total kept fixed is the Meeh skin area `body.skinAreaM2` with `Σ skinFraction = 1`; the non-exchange parts (hooves, horns, bare lower legs, udder, muzzle, eyes) take their honest area with `f = 1`, and their `skinFraction` is taken from the coat region that covers the adjacent anatomy; coat `areaM2` stays; the change of `Σ areaM2` is stated per subject. Animals without exchange regions (pig) are rescaled as humans (sum fixed).
