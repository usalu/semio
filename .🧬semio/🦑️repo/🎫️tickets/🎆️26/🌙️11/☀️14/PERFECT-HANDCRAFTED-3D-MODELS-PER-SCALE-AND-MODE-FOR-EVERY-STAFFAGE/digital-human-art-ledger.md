# Human staffage artistic review, 21 November 2026

Twenty-eight canonical human assets were rendered at 1:50, 1:100, 1:200 and 1:500 in body, surface-temperature and structure modes. Each asset was inspected in its presentation pose and a second authored pose from the opposite side, for 672 panels. Additional exposed-side views resolve wall, instrument and bench occlusion. Shaded surfaces were used to judge actual shape. Coloured supports and mass markers are identified as diagnostic symbols when interpreting the structure.

The reproducible review inputs are `digital-human-gestures.json`, `digital-human-review.py` and `digital-human-contact.ts`. Full SHA-256 values identify the reviewed canonical documents. Rows revisited after publication describe the installed geometry. The linked studies retain the original findings and source hashes. The breadth of the review establishes the displayed poses and scales only.

## Selected repair

On 21 November 2026 the person with umbrella was republished at SHA-256 `0f8819d6425b73fd6aeb92cfb971b27a2576f9bcab9a967ce767413d87ee2b99`. Eight sewn nylon gores now have two accordion pleats each. Each 0.16 mm thick face follows the adjoining ribs. The reinforced hem is present in structure and the wet cloth follows the same faces in thermal. The new canopy measures 1.02323 m open and 0.10949 m furled at all four scales. The measured single-sided area is 0.82721 m², with 1.65442 m² exposed across both faces.

The independent Three.js fixture measured vertices around the posed shaft, the stitched boundaries, the cut area, the structural hem endpoints and the rib-tip attachments. It checked the five published poses, 17 closing-clip samples, and 1,920 orthographic facet-centre projections (32 faces × 4 scales × 5 poses × 3 views). Native validation passed the document and 52 scale rules. All twelve GLB exports passed readback. Fresh shaded product sheets for both open and closed poses show the narrow folded profile in body and thermal and the rib/hem structure. The 27 mm midspan bow in the fine steel ribs can still stand proud of the ruled cloth between its crown and tip attachments. The test establishes continuity along the sewn gores and at the rim. It does not model continuous elastic contact along the rib.

The asset-specific inputs are `digital-umbrella-gore.ts`, `digital-umbrella-gore-case.json` and `digital-umbrella-gore-check.ts`. Regenerating from the installed document reproduces the same asset. At the time of the initial survey, the sleeping person had the clearest pose-dependent body and thermal defect.
