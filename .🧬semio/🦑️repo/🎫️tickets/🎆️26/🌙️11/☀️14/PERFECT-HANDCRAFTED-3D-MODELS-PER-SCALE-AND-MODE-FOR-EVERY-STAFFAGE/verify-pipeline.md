## Amendment D1: glyph sizes in model space (coordinator decision, implemented)

Rule: in the 3D model (`modelOf`, all exports, the viewer) every glyph solid is the smaller of its paper-millimetre size times the denominator and a share of the asset's largest extent (`max(heightM, widthM, depthM)`): 0.35 for arrows and plumes, 0.06 for the radius of the centre-of-mass sphere; moment arcs get half of 0.35 as radius (my reading, the decision names arcs only as glyphs). The thickness (shaft, head) of an arrow follows the capped longest arrow (`unit = min(mm, cap / longestMm)`), the length of each arrow is `min(paper length, cap)`. 2D sheets and the axonometric DRAWING pass `paperSizing(mm)` and are byte-identical to before (axonometric tests unchanged and green). The viewer frames body plus glyphs as before.
- Code: `S/🔨️modules/🧊️volume/🪧️glyphs` (`GlyphSizing`, `paperSizing`, `modelSizing`, `ARROW_SHARE`, `MASS_SHARE`; `arrowPatch` takes metres), `🧷️frames` (`FrameSetup.sizing?`, `glyphOf` takes a sizing), `🟦️.ts` (`fragmentOf` builds `modelSizing`), axonometric mesh passes `paperSizing`.
- Tests first: new `S/🧪️tests/🧊️model-glyph-size` (red before: the arrows were 1.08 m against a 0.61 m cap; now 7 green): the shares, `paperSizing` and `modelSizing` values, and for the four-scale sample figure plus `cantilever-balcony`, `tied-canopy`, `electric-fan-heater` at every authored scale: no arrow longer than 0.35 of the extent, the longest arrow equals `min(paper, cap)`, the sphere radius at most 0.06 of the extent, and 1:500 is not longer than 1:50. `model-looks` (arrow and arc sizes by the 2D rule) now states the capped rule; `model-digests` regenerated by `pipeline-digests.ts` (GLB and FBX of the structure and thermal scenes changed by design; 175 of 175 green over digests, glyph-size, glyph-edges, axonometric-depiction, model-scales, model-exports, model-looks).
- Shots regenerated for the five assets (`🗑️generated/verify/shots`), 1:200 and 1:500 read: the figure now fills the frame in thermal and structure (jogger, patio heater, stair flight, car, pot), arrows are a fraction of the body, the mass sphere is a small ball instead of an opaque blob. Remaining visible: the thermal plume of the jogger (head cone) is about a third of the figure by the 0.35 rule, as decided.
- README and the glyph docstring state the rule.
