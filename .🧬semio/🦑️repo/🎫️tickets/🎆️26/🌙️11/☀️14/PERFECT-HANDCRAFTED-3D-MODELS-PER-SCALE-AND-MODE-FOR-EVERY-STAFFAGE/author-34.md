## spiral-stair

- **1:50**: CHS 168.3 x 6.3 column as a hollow extrusion, 20 mm base plate with four bolt holes and four anchor bolts, fifteen folded treads on their own bones (7.5 mm plate plus a 52.5 mm hollow flange ring, the drawn 60 mm bar; the declared tread mass of 8 kg = 4.9 kg plate + 3.9 kg flange confirms a pressed pan), balusters 12 mm and handrail 42 mm (slender, swept along the true helix as one loft through 71 stations; the previous capsules were 28 mm and 70 separate pieces). Thermal by reference. Structure: tube, tread pans (plate and flange), weld hubs 30 x 45 x 20 mm in each pan, balusters and rail, fixed disc and roller supports at their anchors, two mass points.
- **1:100**: tube with 15 mm wall, 15 mm plate and flange ring, rod balusters, rail through 36 stations. **1:200**: solid 168 mm column on a 30 mm disc (the 20 mm drawn plate is below the feature), 60 mm solid wedges per tread, rail through 19 stations, the balusters only in thermal (slender, not drawn at this scale). **1:500**: solid column, 75 mm disc, 75 mm wedges, 65 mm rail through 15 stations.
- 2D repair at 1:500 (`author-34-fix2d-spiral.py`): the elevation `stair-mass` closed the notches between the treads while the front kept them (the text claims they close because the gaps are thinner than 0.2 mm, but they are 0.27 mm); no body could match both (elevation IoU 0.58 with treads, front 0.51 with full-rise blocks). Both outlines are now regenerated as column plus fifteen tread bars of 0.075 m and a 0.075 m foot plate, and the `merged` and `silhouette` texts say so.
- `areaM2`: `column-steel` 2.28044 -> 2.95 (ratio 1.29, the CHS bore is exposed, 1.37 m2). `treads` NOT applied: measured 6.67 m2 vs 3.729 (ratio 1.79 > 1.5): the declared value is twice the plan area (bare 5 mm plate), the pan flange adds 2.85 m2; listed in `area-review.md`. `balustrade-steel` unchanged (1.176 vs 1.1917 with the true 12 mm balusters and 42 mm rail).
- Imperfect: `treads` fails at 1:50 and 1:100 until the coordinator decides; the sleeve joints between the treads stay texture of the column; 1:500 plan shows the blue sliver of the column bore edge; handrail starts at baluster 1 as drawn (not down to the floor).

## stair-flight

Thermal: stringer parts by reference; `tread-top` as a 7.5 mm skin patch inside the top of each tread whose back, edges and ends are `sectionFaces`, so only the walking face counts (the declared 4.75 m2 is the sum of the tread tops). Structure: stringer parts, treads, gussets as joints, pin wedge and roller at their anchors, one mass point.
- **1:100**: 15.2 mm flanges and web, triangular brackets, plain boards, no screws. **1:200**: stringer as one extrusion of the drawn outline (with the bearing seat), 60 mm boards, no gussets. **1:500**: stringer block without the 75 mm bearing nose (to drop triangles), 75 mm boards.

Texts adapted.
- `areaM2` unchanged (stringer 10 %, tread-top 8 % at 1:50). Imperfect: the stringer region is 34 % short at 1:200 and 1:500 (the merged block has no flange inner faces); the total stays within 21 %.
