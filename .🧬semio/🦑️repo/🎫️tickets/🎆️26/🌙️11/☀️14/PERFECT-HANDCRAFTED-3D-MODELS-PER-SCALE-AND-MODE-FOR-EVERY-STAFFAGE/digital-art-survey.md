# Visual survey of the 152 published forms

The five scale-1:50 body sheets cover every asset. Their companion source index links all four scales to body, numeric temperature and structural renders. This survey identifies visible issues that whole-catalog geometry checks cannot decide.

- The hornbeam hedge has conspicuously smooth panels with repetitive seams. An individually attached leaf-and-shoot study is underway. The foliage needs relief across clipped faces and lateral response under wind.
- In the alert goat and Labrador, broad transitions where the shoulder and croup meet the trunk still read as separate solids. The sheep needs a readable fleece boundary in its coarser views.
- In the gliding gull the earlier coarse head and legs protruded and there was a gap at the wrist. The revised wing has independent cubital and manual fans and retains the wrist connection at all four scales. See the [pose measurements](r3-animals-herring-gull.md).
- The single camera sees the rear of some fixtures, including the bookshelf and meter enclosures. Their closed rear faces alone cannot establish a missing shelf or control. These require front and rear inspection before grading.

The [source index](digital-art-index.md) records the rendered catalog. Individual asset reports distinguish checked features from unfinished work.
