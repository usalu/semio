# Cervical articulation of the primary human figures

All 28 primary human figures now have separate neck and skull frames. The tour guide and the nine figures below received new articulations. Each of the nine has its own atlas location, segment mass and cervical angles in every named pose. The paired occipital joints, collar, lower cervical spine, eyes and respiratory outlet surfaces follow their respective segments at all four scales.

The independent pose comparison measured 85 named poses against their original skull orientations. It also checked the location of the skull pivot, the neutral combined centre of mass, and the physical and structural attachment at each scale. All nine candidate documents passed the paper checks, the 13 volume rules at every scale and the independent GLB loader. The installed documents matched the accepted models.

For the leaning figure and the parent carrying a toddler, the thermal surface stops at the garment and mandible. Its loft follows the authored neck contour and has open cut planes. The measured combined face and uncovered neck areas are 0.092 m² and 0.098 m². The remaining three garments needing explicit coarse coverage use separate 17, 35 and 34 mm exposed strips.

The worker’s carton uses a separate plan outline at the coarsest paper scale. Its front and elevation retain space for the thighs during the low carry. Both the nominal 1:500 and the 1:1000 print endpoint passed.
