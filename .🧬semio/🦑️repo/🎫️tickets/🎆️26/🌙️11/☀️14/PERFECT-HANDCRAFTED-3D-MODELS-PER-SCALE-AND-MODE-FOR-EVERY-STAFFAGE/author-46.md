## Method (all four)
One loft per drawn bone, sagittal profile from that scale's elevation in bone-local coordinates, lateral half width and centre per station from the plan (horizontal bones) or front (vertical bones), section exponent by anatomy (trunk 2.2, thigh/shoulder 2.7, limbs 2.4, head/neck 2.2, tail/ear 2.2), end sections shrunk, floor 0.51 of the thinnest drawn extent. Barrels: trunk sections are superellipses of the drawn back, belly and flank lines (round pig and sheep barrels, narrow fox barrel), never tubes of constant section; ears, snout, hooves, tail, brush, eyes, nose are own solids. Thermal: `kit-thermal` with an explicit region map (references; nested shells at 1:50 for the layered squirrel), plus own solids where a region has no body solid at that scale (ears, hooves, snout, tail, squirrel limbs at 1:100). Emitters: nose and mouth jets clamped inside the head loft; the plume is a thin horizontal disc lying on the back (thin axis up, half width limited to the body half width, no region so that it does not add exposed trunk area) (`author-46-lib.ts`). Structure: capsule members and joint ellipsoids from `buildStructure`, then centred in the body loft section and clamped to the loft (`centreOnLofts`), members of undrawn bones moved back along their bone (`shiftUndrawn`).
