# Author 08 report: seven humans with a carried or pushed object

Assets: parent-carrying-toddler, parent-with-stroller, person-cooking, person-on-crutches, person-with-rollator, person-with-umbrella, person-with-white-cane. All seven are spliced (`volumes-insert.py`), every scale authored with body, thermal, structure and bilingual decisions. **None of the seven passes `volumes-check.ts` completely**; the failures are listed below as measured, nothing was weakened.

## Method (what was modelled by what)

- Pipeline: `author_08_lib.py` (profiles, lofts, shells), `author_08_person.py`, `author_08_tail.py` (thermal, structure), `author_08_build.py`, `author-08-run.py` / `author-08-white-cane.py` (per-asset specs), `author-08-profiles.ts` (per-scale 2D shapes in bone frames), `author-08-measure.ts`, `author-08-diag*.ts`, `author-08-areafix.py`, `author-08-sum.py`. Tool output under `🗑️generated/author-08/`.
- Person bones (every scale): own loft per bone whose sections are sampled from that scale's own elevation, front and plan outlines (scanline per station, centre and half-widths), reduced with a tolerance of 0.5 x the scale's feature and at most 12/7/4/3 sections (1:50/100/200/500), explicit A2 rotations, bones with only a front/plan outline get a round section from it, bones with no usable outline an ellipsoid. Multi-region bones (head and hair, torso and scarf) are split by the region named in the 2D shapes.
- 1:50 details (ears, eyes, thumbs, fingers, buttons, glasses, hoods, shoes' soles...) and the props are the previous author's hand-placed solids from the seed. At 1:100/200/500 the props are NOT the seed list with entries removed: slender tubes (crutches, rollator frame, umbrella shaft, cane) are the seed capsules re-sized per scale and marked `slender` where length >= 22 x diameter (C2); everything else (stroller seat, hood, basket, wheels, child, counter, pot, canopy) is the per-scale draft of `volumes-draft.ts` (extrudes/revolves from that scale's own 2D shapes), repaired (self-intersecting profiles reduced to their hull, minimum thickness). This is the weakest part: these prop solids are plank-like extrudes, not handcrafted lofts.
- Thermal: 1:50 nested shells per layer (shrunk by the thicknesses outside it, layer material), outer layer `of` the body solid; thin detail parts only in the outer layer; coarser scales one surface solid per part. Regions without a body solid at a scale get a real solid copied from the main solid of the bones named by the 2D shapes. Emitters are small solids tagged with their id at their anchors (plume as a flat disc at the crown, clamped to the scale's feature).
- Structure: member solids (about 0.45 of the limb section, 0.5 torso/head, following the body sections) on every B1 bone, `of` references to the tube/body solids for prop bones, joint ellipsoids at 1:50/1:100, mass ellipsoids at heavy point masses and prop masses, support solids with `anchor` (C6) at `structure.supports` (cane tip, rollator wheel contacts).
