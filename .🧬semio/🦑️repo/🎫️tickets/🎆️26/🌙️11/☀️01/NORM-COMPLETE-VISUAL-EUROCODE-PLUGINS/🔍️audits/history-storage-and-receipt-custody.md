# History Storage and Receipt Custody

RetainedHistoryDecode now quotes/refills paid original failure storage ahead of a selected decoder, active retirement ticket (including its terminal frame), or recipient owner. Spare nodes remain inert while decoding and drain only after the decoder has returned or during final cancellation. Refill receipts and actual work remain observable through the same forwarded control.

Active retirement forwards the original controlled ticket port and its actual work. Typed fault/partial frame birth preflights actual work before original owner transfer, then commits even when allocation or admission refuses.

A receiving grant mismatch now remains an independent receipt refusal alongside the original ValueError; it no longer replaces and drops the primary owner.

These are source changes. Native tests have not executed against this snapshot; the shared native allocation event/caller migration remains in progress.
