## Changes and clauses
1. DIN 4108-2 6.2 f_Rsi: `check_f_rsi(element)` (signature lost the temperatures) uses `R_SI_MOULD` 0.25, `MOULD_T_INT_C` 20, `MOULD_T_EXT_C` -5, f_Rsi = 1 - 0.25/R_T, reports theta_si, verdict >= 0.70 (`F_RSI_MINIMUM`). Files: `S/🧬️schema/💡️inferences/⚖️checks/🧮️resistance-helpers/🦀️.rs`, `.../🧱️part-2-minimum-insulation/🦀️.rs`, `.../💡️inferences/🦀️.rs` (caller, unused `t_ext` removed).
2. EN ISO 6946 6.7.2.4 combined method: `total_resistance_upper` now sums f_a f_b.../R_T over every section combination of all inhomogeneous layers (previously the first inhomogeneous layer's fractions for all), lower limit unchanged, `R_T = (R'_T + R''_T)/2`. New `combined_method_ratio`, `COMBINED_METHOD_MAX_RATIO` 1.5; `check_u_value` appends a localized EN+DE "Validity warning / Gültigkeitswarnung" to the explanation and sets status Warning when the ratio exceeds 1.5 and the limit is met (a failing U stays Fail). File `.../🌡️part-6-u-value-checks/🦀️.rs`.
3. DIN 4108-3:2018 Glaser, rewrite of `.../💧️part-3-moisture-protection/🦀️.rs`:
   - Saturation: water branch at and above 0 C, ice branch below (610.5 exp(17.269 t/(237.3+t)) and 610.5 exp(21.875 t/(265.5+t))); `saturation_vapor_pressure_pa(-5)` = 401.18 Pa (was 421.99, Magnus over water).
   - Climate: `GLASER_WINTER` 20 C/50 % to -5 C/80 %, 2160 h; `GLASER_SUMMER` 12 C/70 % on both sides, 2160 h, as `GlaserPeriod`. Removed: orientation shift (`facade_temp_adjustment_k`, `glaser_boundary`, climate-region summer minus 16/8 K, the wrong 20 C/50 % summer interior, `sd_req = 0.5 + az/360`). Glaser no longer reads the document interior climate or region (Annex A is fixed): `check_glaser(element)`, `check_drying_reserve(element)`.
   - Flux: `vapour_diffusion_resistance_z` Z = s_d/delta0, `vapour_flux_kg_m2_s` g = (p_i - p_c)/Z; condensation planes by the iterative ISO 13788 procedure (plane at the largest excess, held at saturation), mass per plane = (g_in - g_out) x period seconds, summed, not negative; evaporation: planes at the evaporation-period saturation drying to both neighbours, negative fluxes zero.
   - Defect found while rewriting: the old code and the old oracle multiplied the flux in kg/(m2 s) by hours instead of seconds, so every Glaser mass was 3600 times too small (vapour-tight example 1.2e-3 instead of 4.2 kg/m2). Fixed in Rust and in the oracle (`PERIOD_SECONDS`).
   - The sd floor 0.1 m became `GLASER_SD_FLOOR_M` 1e-3 (numerical guard for zero resistances only, not a normative number).
   - Diagrams `S/🎬️scene/📈️diagrams/🦀️.rs`: `winter_profile(element)` shows the plane-limited vapour pressure, condensation markers at planes.
