Catalogue cracked state `serviceability::cracked_state(b, h, d, …)` is now a facade that builds the rectangle `Sec` and calls `Sec::elastic` (same signature, so SL-01/03/06/08 and the committed concreteproperties-checked SL-03 cases keep one implementation).

Remaining rectangle inputs, honestly: catalogue entries whose input form is a rectangle (S-03, S-07, SL-01/03/08 state I closed forms, A-05, A-09…A-13, R-05) keep their closed forms as catalogue cases; the member checks do not use them. Slender columns call A-10 with the equivalent rectangle (A_c, h). Bonded tendons are not in the crack-width elastic section (previous behaviour kept).

New catalogue entries: SG-01 (general section properties incl. torsion section) and SG-02 (linear-elastic state II/I with A_c,eff), both on any polygon with holes and bars. Behaviour changes that can move existing numbers: SLS stresses and crack width now include N and compression steel; M_cr includes N; torsion t_ef has the 2c + φ_l floor; l/d uses the flange factor.

Handles and diagrams: existing outline-vertex, width, height, effective-depth, axis-distance and tendon handles drive every changed check (no new fields, no new leaf kinds). New diagram `<member>/section-geometry` (outline, torsion wall centre line, A_c,eff band, b_w, d, z, t_ef, A_k, h_c,eff as table twin). Remedies unchanged in form (height, width, bar count, stirrup spacing, effective depth).

## 3. Fire
- One thermal solver: `⚖️checks/🌡️fire-field/🦀️.rs` (moved out of the scene, scene `🌡️thermal` deleted). Polygon aware (rectangle is a polygon), progress and cancellation per step (`solve_outline_with`), cached, plus `bottom_depth`, `side_depth`, `peak`, `profile`. R-02 and R-03 now run on it (the old explicit finite-difference solver with its own c_p model is gone); R-03 takes M_Rd,fi from the reduced `Sec` of the 500 degC isotherm instead of the rectangle formula.
- Zone method Annex B.2 (spec R-04): `zone_section` finds, per exposed edge (face by dominant normal), w = half the local thickness, n = 5 zones, θ_i and θ_M from the field, `a_z = w (1 - k_c,m / k_c(θ_M))`, and `offset_polygon` moves the outline in by a_z per edge (mitre corners, empty if the offset exceeds the section). Drawn: scene entity `<member>:zone-section` (translucent surface) and the `zone` series of the `fire-section` diagram next to the isotherm; both reduced sections and per-bar θ_s and k_s are in the diagram.
- Used: new checks `en1992.1-2.nm.<id>` (isotherm method, Annex B.1) and `en1992.1-2.zone.<id>` (Annex B.2): M_Ed,fi ≤ M_Rd,fi(N_Ed,fi) by strain compatibility on the reduced polygon (`fire_field::reduced_section`: concrete f_ck, bars k_s(θ_s) A_s at f_yk, tendons dropped), for the frequent combination at the rated duration; remedy: height estimate by the moment ratio. NDP cited: `ndp.en1992.fire-ks-kc` (unverified, so a pass becomes UnverifiedBasis).
- Not done / limits: bars outside the reduced polygon in compression still subtract concrete stress in `Sec::state_plane`; hollow sections and tendons are not part of the fire section; no concrete strength reduction in the 100-500 degC ring (isotherm method assumes none); θ-dependent shear, torsion, anchorage in fire (R-08) remain formula-free flags.
