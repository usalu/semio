# Round 2 (W3-EN1992b): torsion superposition, strut and node stresses

Nothing compiled (no cargo). Artifact `✏️s/🔌️plugins/📕️norm/🗿️artifacts/🏛️en1992`.

## 1. Safety fix: torsion longitudinal steel in addition to the bending steel (6.3.2(3))
- Old behaviour: `util_long` = ΣA_sl,req over ALL bar area (passed bars that bending had already used up).
- New T-02 (`✂️shear-torsion`): `util_long` = (`asl_bending` + `tension_share`·ΣA_sl,req) / `asl_provided` for the tension chord, plus `util_long_comp` = (1 − `tension_share`)·ΣA_sl,req / `asl_compression_provided` for the compression chord. New inputs `asl_provided`, `asl_bending`, `asl_compression_provided`, `tension_share` (units script `catalogue-bridge-en1992-units.py`, tsv and registry inputs json/rs regenerated through `catalogue-bridge-gen-inputs.py`; they were not in the vocabulary before).
- Member check (`resistance-and-serviceability-checks`): new `torsion_chords(sec)` (exported): bar area below / above the mid-height of the outer outline and the share of the outline perimeter below the mid-height (exactly 0.5 for a rectangle). `asl_bending` = tension-chord bar area x M_Ed/M_Rd (flexure utilisation, no clamp: a failing flexure check also fails the sum). New check family `en1992.6.3.torsion-longitudinal-compression.<id>`; `en1992.6.3.torsion-longitudinal.<id>` now means the tension chord incl. bending steel. Remedies: tension layer count/diameter, compression (top) layer count/diameter (`minimal-remedies` plan entries before the generic `en1992.6.3.` stirrup plan).
- Test where the old check passed wrongly: catalogue test `e23` (b 400, h 600, T_Ed 35 kNm, cot 1.5: ΣA_sl,req = 682.8 mm²; provided 1000 mm², bending 700 mm², share 0.5): torsion-only 0.683 (pass), combined (700 + 341.4)/1000 = 1.0414 (fail), compression chord 341.4/300 = 1.138. Mirrored in the python oracle (`t02`: independent arithmetic, same inputs), 2 new T-02 vector cases in `catalogue.json` and a Gherkin row (1.0414063).
- Not done / conservative: no reduction of the compression-chord steel by the available compression force (allowed by 6.3.2(3), not applied); the side-wall share is assigned to the chords through the perimeter split (rectangle: half each); tension chord is the lower half (sagging convention of the flexure model); governing T and M may come from different combinations (conservative superposition).
