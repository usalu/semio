- RW-12 embedded wall in soil and water: effective stresses behind and in front of the wall with buoyant weight below the water tables, net water pressure `gamma_w (z - z_w)+ - gamma_w (z - z_wf)+`, free earth support, cantilever (x1.2) and Blum fixed earth support (zero-pressure point y, equivalent-beam reaction C, Blum length x from the moment equilibrium of the lower part, t = y + 1.2 x). The existing handles (height, embedment, prop level, anchor lengths and inclination) and wall pressure diagrams already cover the 3D side; the 3D flow net is new (section 4).
- Oracle: Python `rw_11`/`rw_12` with `scipy.integrate.quad` (kink points) and `scipy.optimize.brentq`; a line-by-line replica of the Rust midpoint algorithm gives the same y 2.957635, x 5.712579, C 109 822, A 111 832, t 9.8127 m as the oracle.

## 4. Section 10, hydraulic failure (`K/📇️catalogue/🌊️seepage`)
- UPL-01 (uplift), HYD-01 (heave, filter criteria), HYD-02 (Lane/Bligh creep, conformal-map exit gradient) were already complete; new HYD-03.
- `FlowNet`: cell-centred finite-volume Laplace solver (SOR, tolerance 1e-12 DeltaH) for a sheet pile in a layer of finite thickness (impermeable wall, bottom and ends). Outputs: surface exit gradient next to the wall, discharge per metre (retained and excavation side, continuity error), the Terzaghi prism (mean excess head `h_m` on the base of the strip of width d/2 at the wall toe) with `gamma_H gamma_w h_m d/2 <= gamma_H,stb gamma' d^2/2`, piping `gamma_H i gamma_w <= gamma_H,stb gamma'`, Darcy velocity. Stream function on the cell corners (largest value equals the discharge, bottom row zero) and marching-square contours give 5 streamlines and 5 equipotentials.
- 3D: scene `seepage` now draws the finite-difference flow net (cylinder chains, streamlines 0.6 / equipotentials 0.35 opacity, bound to `en1997.hyd-03` utilisation, exit gradient in the accessible detail); the four conformal-map streamlines are gone. The layer thickness is the investigation depth below the dredge level (at least 2 d), 8 cells across the embedment for speed.
- Validation: Python `_flow_net` builds the same stencil as a `scipy.sparse` matrix and solves it with `spsolve`; deep-layer exit gradient is within 2.8 % of `DeltaH/(pi d)` and the prism ratio h_m/DeltaH is 0.337 (Terzaghi ~ 0.35). Values for the Rust test: 168 x 36 cells, i_exit 0.2007577, h_m 0.6747857, q 1.2583577 (k = 1).
- Not implemented (no verified normative value): internal erosion/suffusion criteria (Istomina, Kenney-Lau thresholds); the filter criteria of HYD-01 stay as they are.
