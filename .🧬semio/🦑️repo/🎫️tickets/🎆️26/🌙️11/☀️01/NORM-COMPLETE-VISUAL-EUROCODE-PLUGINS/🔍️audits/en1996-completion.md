## Fixed defects (inventory section 8)

- Hard-coded utilisations removed. Material conformance now reports the largest of its ratios (mortar deviation over 15 %, unit dimension, group-2 aspect, bed joint, unit strength range). Bed joint reports `max(t/max, min/t)` (0.8 for the compliant wall). Durability reports `required/provided` mortar strength, and an inadmissible combination fails by status. Reinforced masonry reports `A_s,min/A_s` against `A_s/A_s,max`, is `NotApplicable` for unreinforced walls and fails by status when reinforcement is given without `reinforced`. Flexure with `f_yd <= 0` fails by status. Fire reports `t_min/t` plus a second check `μ_fi <= 1`.
- Literal remedy targets and fudge factors removed. Every wall remedy is solved on the real check (`solve_threshold`, bisection on a cloned wall, rounded to the passing side); no `x1.01`, `x1.2`, `0.113`, `435e6`, `0.85`, `0.7`, `1.25`, `0.9` targets remain. The only constants are named: `F_YD_B500_PA = 500 MPa / γ_S` and limits taken from the checked rule.
- Formulas: Annex G Φ_m with `λ = (h_ef/t)/sqrt(K_E)` and `u = (λ-0.063)/(0.73-1.17 e_mk/t)`; ρ_2 from the slab bearing (0.75 when it bears at least 2t/3, else 1.0) and the `h > 3.5 L` / `h > 1.15 L` branches of ρ_3, ρ_4; ℓ_ef subtracts opening widths (no unsourced 0.35/0.65/0.05/0.85); f_vk without the redundant `min` and with the EN cap `0.065 f_b` or the NA-DE `f_vlt`; sliding resistance without the `max(γ_M, 1)` guard; concentrated load β of 6.1.3 with `F_Ed = γ_G F_k` (the force is in the permanent combination), A_ef from 60 degree spreading, plus a separate `A_b <= 0.45 A_ef` check; lateral moment now includes the earth pressure and the apparent flexural strength `f_xd1 + σ_d`; the accompanying-action factor `0.25` is gone (accompanying snow only); `γ_G` 1.20 for transient is gone (1.35; 1.0 with ψ_1/ψ_2 for accidental and seismic); the min-N combination label no longer names a seismic action; transient and accidental use `γ_M = 1.3` in NA-DE; snow and wind factors are the EN 1990 values (they are not zone dependent); the imposed categories F and G are accepted by integrity and by ψ.
- Legacy traces removed: `fire_min_thickness_m` "Compatibility" wrapper (the name now belongs to the single full-signature function), `MortarClass::compressive_strength_mpa`, the mutation groups `🧰️loads-2` and `🪜️walls-2` (merged into `⚖️loads` and `🧱️walls`, workspace `Cargo.toml` and four `Cargo.lock` files updated). `Lightweight` mortar now has K and f_vk0 rows. The `0.5 + 0.4` style hack factors in the oracle are gone.
- Oracle: rewritten, see below. Oracle tests no longer write to the system temp directory; everything goes through stdin and stdout.
- New wall checks that make every editable leaf matter: `en1996.8.1.5.unit-fit` (unit width within the wall, running-bond overlap), `en1996.5.4.opening` (sill plus height within the wall), `en1996.6.1.3.area` (A_b against 0.45 A_ef), `en1996.1-2.fire.load`.

## Oracle

`SUB/🔮️oracles/⚖️compliance/🐍️.py` no longer compares a function with itself. It reads its own independently keyed `📚️tables.json`, enumerates the EN 1990 combinations by brute force, and uses third-party libraries: scipy `RegularGridInterpolator` for δ, shapely plus scipy `minimize_scalar` for the yield-line load (explicit facets, per-line orthotropic rule), scipy `brentq` for arching statics and equilibrium, numpy fibre integration for M-N. Closed-form entries are labelled independent formulation. No third-party library implements EN 1996; `SUB/🔮️oracles/🔣️.json` rationale updated.

## Pipeline integration and Annex E (second pass)

- Document cases: `MAS/📇️catalogue/🧮️document-cases` builds catalogue cases from the wall model (M-01, M-02, M-04, M-06, M-10, M-31, M-32, M-33, M-40, M-52, M-70) and `evaluate_wall` appends their results, so they run from the document, appear in the results window and the viewer, are re-evaluated on every mutation, and carry subjects and remedies that resolve in the document (`Case::bound`, `Case::with_path`). The two alternative lateral resistance models (two-way yield lines, arching) are advisory: they warn, never fail, because the one-way flexure check remains the verdict.
- Annex E fixed-edge cases: the yield-line solver takes four edge letters (top, bottom, left, right; `F` free, `S` simple, `X` fixed with hogging capacity equal to the sagging capacity), minimises over ridge height and corner offsets, and tries the transposed pattern. Verified against closed forms: SSSS square 24, XXXX 48, SSFF 8, XXFF 16, FXFF 2 (times m/l^2). The Python oracle reproduces them with an independent facet model (shapely, differential evolution, per-line orthotropic rule and hogging on fixed supports). These are yield-line values, not the verbatim Annex E table entries (no table text exists in the repository); registered as unverified (`m31.yield-line`).
