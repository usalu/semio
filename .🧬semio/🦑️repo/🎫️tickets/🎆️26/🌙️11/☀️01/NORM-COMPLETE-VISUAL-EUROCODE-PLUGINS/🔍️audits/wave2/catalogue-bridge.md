## Conflict with the owner's generic `cases` implementation
The owner added `CatalogueCase/CaseNumber/CaseText`, 12 mutation kinds (`mut-cases`, `mut-case-inputs`), per-case units and `🎬️scene/🗂️cases`. Decision: the owner's entity layer is the persisted shape and stays; my bridge does not add a second entity, mutation set or unit row. ## Shared mutation/delta mechanism (added in the second pass)
`P/⚖️compliance/🧰️design-case/📇️rows/🦀️.rs` (mounted and re-exported by `design_case`) now owns, once:
- the persisted records `CaseNumber`, `CaseText`, `CaseRow` (same field shape as EN 1995's `CatalogueCase`, derives like the other compliance records) with `From` conversions to and from the neutral `DesignCase`;
- the delta types `CaseNumberPatch/Delta`, `CaseTextPatch/Delta`, `CaseRowPatch`, `CaseRowDelta` (via `protocol::row_patch!` / `list_delta!`), the lane every artifact diff embeds as `cases: CaseRowDelta`;
- the pure diff logic of the four shared kinds as functions: `add_case::<Catalogue>` (duplicate id, unknown entry, clamped index), `remove_case`, `set_case_number` / `set_case_text` (modify, insert, or remove for `None`; absent+`None` is the empty delta), the inverse helpers `previous_number/previous_text`, and `CaseRefusal { code, message, targets }` for `MutationOutcome::fatal`;
- the payload schemas `P/⚖️compliance/🧰️design-case/🧬️schema/🧬️mutations/🔣️.json` (`AddCase`, `SetCaseNumber`, `SetCaseText`, `RemoveCase`); unit tests in `📇️rows/🧪️tests/🔬️unit/🦀️.rs`.
Why four thin leaves per artifact remain: the `MutationLeaf` derive binds each leaf to its artifact mutation root (source authority, descriptor `🔣️.json`, workspace token) and the aggregate `Mutations` enum is per snapshot, so a leaf cannot live in the shared crate. A leaf is a payload struct, label, `target()`, descriptor and `diff()`/`inverse()` one-liners calling the functions above (inverse of add = `remove-case{index}`, of remove = `add-case{index, case}`, of set-* = set-* with `previous_*`).

## Rollout recipe for the other owners

2. Vocabulary: author one TSV (`key|scope|kind|unit|example|flags|en|de`), copy and adapt `🧰️scripts/catalogue-bridge-gen-inputs.py`, generate `📚️inputs/{🔣️.json,🦀️.rs}` next to the registry. 3. Catalogue impl: `impl CaseCatalogue for <Family>Cases` mapping the registry row to `CaseEntry` (geometry when width/depth keys exist) and `evaluate` to the existing evaluator; mount with two `#[path]` lines.
4. Snapshot: owner-style `cases: Vec<Case{id,labelEn,labelDe,entryId,numbers[],texts[]}>` (JSON/GraphQL/proto/TS twins, diff lane, SQLite, text and binary io, field order identical). Until the shared lift lands copy EN 1995's shape exactly; the lift will replace it.
