# EN 1998 wave 2 v14: live seismic views

Nothing compiled or run (no cargo, no python). All checked by reading. Every view is a pure projection of document plus report; the live path (`live_scene_of`) and `project` share `fragment()`.

## Changes
1. Spectrum (`🎬️scene/📈️diagrams`): markers `t1-se` (Se at T1), `t1-gross` (Sd at the uncracked T1), `t1-gross-se`, limit line `t1-gross-line`, shown only when gross differs from cracked. Needs `Response::t1_gross` (`🧮️response`) and `cracked_stiffness::gross_storeys_xy` (schema). Dragging masses, stiffness or site inputs re-derives them.
2. Storey response: new module `🎬️scene/📊️storey-response` (mounted in `🔬️analysis`, re-exported as `storey_response`): `storey-shear:<b>` (step profile per direction, resistance steps, markers bound to storeyForces checks) and `storey-drift:<b>` (d_r in mm vs limit theta*h/nu, markers draggable onto `driftXM/YM`). 3D drift heat prisms already existed (`drift-map`).
3. Bridges (`🌉️ground-and-bridges`): piers bound to the capacity-design shear check (V_CD/V_Rd) with label `<pier>:capacity-ratio`; bearings get `<k>:demand` bar bound to the displacement check, `:demand-dim` and `:seat-required` dimensions; deck ghosts now also longitudinal (`<b>:mode-x1..3`, layers modeX*).
4. Tanks (`🏭️vessels-and-towers`): `<t>:pressure-impulsive` and `-convective` line loads beside the total, `:slosh-dim` and `:slosh-height` label.
5. Tests: unit (spectrum markers, stiffness drag halves T1, storey shear/drift vs forces and limits, shear accumulation, bridge ratio/demand/ghost, tank split/slosh) and live (`live_drags_...` incremental == full across mass, stiffness, site, bearing, tank edits plus T1 moves).
