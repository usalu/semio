- `🧬️schema/📇️masonry-catalogue/🥞️diaphragm/🦀️.rs`: the hand-written tridiagonal solve is gone. `deflections` builds a `mechanics::Model` (axial bar of rigidity G t D, `A = rigidity`, `E = 1`, uniform global load `F/L`, every node fixed in the other five degrees of freedom, wall springs as support springs on the spring-refined station set) and solves it with `mechanics::Analysis::new(..).solve_case(..)`. ## 2. Diaphragm UX
- Stiffness handle (`🎬️scene/🥞️diaphragm`, wired in `🕹️handles`): grip `<wall>:diaphragm-<axis>:stiffness`, `ScalarAlong Z` above the plate; one drag writes `walls[id=..].diaphragmStiffnessNPerM` of every wall that declares one in one `setFields` commit, all scaled by the same factor (0.5 decade per metre, four steps per decade, limits keep every value within 1e3..1e12 N/m).
- Log law: the affine handle schema has no log law, so a shared fix was needed and is announced: `Quantise::Decades { per_decade }` in the structural-scene handle schema (rs, ts, proto, graphql, unit test). The affine map yields a decade exponent, the quantiser turns it into `10^(round(x n)/n)`; `FieldBinding::value_at` stays the only evaluator, so the drag tool, the live overlay and the one-commit rule work unchanged.
- Diagram `diaphragm-<axis>` (wall force distribution): per bracing wall at its position across the diaphragm two bars side by side, rigid assumption (by stiffness) and flexible assumption (tributary width); the classified rule is solid, the other hatched; diamond markers with the governing share, a note with the ratio and the 1.10 limit, table twin (position, k, rigid kN, flexible kN, drift mm).
- Diagram `diaphragm-ratio-<axis>` (classification): displacement ratio over G t (log axis, 25 solves of the mechanics model), limit line at 1.10, dotted line at the declared G t, draggable marker bound to the weakest wall's `diaphragmStiffnessNPerM` with a 1-2-5 ladder, utilisation ratio/1.10 and bound check `en1996.9.3.diaphragm.<axis>`.
- Live updates: both diagrams and the plate are pure functions of the document, so they follow every commit and every drag preview through the existing scene and `incremental_evaluation` path (the diaphragm check is a unit of the `detailing` part; no new read paths).
- Tests (scene `🥞️diaphragm`): the grip writes all declared stiffnesses on a log law (+2 m gives 10x, two writes, anchor inside the limits); the two diagrams exist with the 1.10 limit line, the bound marker and four bars; a missing focus yields none.
