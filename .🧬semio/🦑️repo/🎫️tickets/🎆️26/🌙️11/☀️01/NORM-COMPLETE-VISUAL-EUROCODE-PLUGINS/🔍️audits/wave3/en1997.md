## 3. SLOPE-06 Spencer full-equilibrium method (catalogue + diagram)
- `slopes::{spencer_pair, spencer}`: constant interslice inclination lambda = tan(theta) in [-1, 1], moment factor (about the centre) and horizontal-force factor, each fixed-point on its own F, root nearest zero bracketed on a 0.05 grid and bisected. At lambda = 0 the moment factor is the simplified Bishop factor (asserted to 1e-7). Entry `SLOPE-06`: `spencer` (1/F), `f`, `theta_deg`, `f_bishop`.
- Diagram `spencer-equilibrium:<slope>` (F_m and F_f against theta, root marker, table twin).
- Oracle `slope_06`: simultaneous solve of (F, lambda) from the two residuals (scipy `fsolve` when installed, own damped Newton otherwise), 400 slices vs 60 in Rust. Checked here with a Python port of the Rust nested algorithm: standard slope Rust-port 1.48396 vs oracle 1.48418 (theta 17.0 deg), dry phi 35 slope 1.84499 vs 1.84510, ru 0.25 slope 1.09182 vs 1.09221; Bishop 1.4842 / 1.8472 / 1.0877. Test `spencer_moment_factor_at_zero_inclination_is_bishop_and_the_root_closes_force_equilibrium` and scene test `spencer_diagram_marks_the_catalogue_root_where_moment_and_force_curves_meet`.

- PILE-12: Boussinesq centre stress by dblquad of the point-load kernel: s_group 0.0257276 vs 0.0257275, 0.0275148 vs 0.0275147 (2e-6).

Case 1 F 1.48425 vs oracle 1.48418 (theta 17.029 vs 17.030), Bishop 1.48428 vs 1.48421; case 2 F 1.84510 vs 1.84510 (theta 17.9744 vs 17.9745), Bishop 1.84718; case 3 (ru 0.25): F 1.09068 vs 1.09221 (0.14 %), Bishop 1.08767 vs 1.08766. Spencer(theta=0) equals Bishop to 1e-7 when no slice is clamped (cases 1 and 2).
- Finding (case 3): 57 of 2000 slices hit the no-tension clamp, theta is then ill-conditioned (16.5 vs 18.3 deg) and Spencer(theta=0) differs from Bishop by 0.5 % because Bishop clamps (W - u) while Spencer clamps N'. Not a bug of either implementation, but the Rust test `spencer_moment_factor_at_zero_inclination_is_bishop...` must stay on unclamped cases (it uses the standard slope). The script compares only F for clamped cases.
- Own bugs in the cross-check, fixed: gamma_q factor on the surcharge, Bishop bracket.
