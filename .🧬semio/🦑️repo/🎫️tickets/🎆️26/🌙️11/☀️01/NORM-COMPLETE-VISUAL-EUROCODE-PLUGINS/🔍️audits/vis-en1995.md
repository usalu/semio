- `🧰️kit`: layers (members, connections, fire, zones, loads, supports, annotations), entity registry (member, connection), report bindings by subject and check-id prefix, `Spec`/`Out`. `📍️layout`: members along +X (columns upright, continuous beams drawn over two spans), connections row at y = -4 m, persisted `Placement` wins.

- `🪵️members`: member as extruded rectangle (glulam: lamella lines from 40 mm composite parts), residual section plus `:char` and `:zsl` hull entities when `fireDurationS > 0`, supports (pinned, roller, fixed, interior), bearing pads bound to the c90 check, lateral restraints, end-notch void box bound to 6.5.2 or shear, line and point load glyphs per action, vibration mode shape (floor and footbridge, with f1) or deformed shape from w_fin when the SLS ratio is at least 0.5, dimensions bound to span, width and depth, clause badge, failure glyphs per verification group.
- `🔩️connections`: stacked timber boxes and steel plates, fastener grid (nail, screw, bolt with 1 mm hole clearance, dowel) as a `Pattern::Grid`, translucent a1/a2, a3 and a4 zones each coloured by their own spacing check, a1/a3/a4 dimensions bound to the fields, governing Johansen mode label, clause badge, failure glyph, group.
- `📈️diagrams` (all with table twin and `bound_check`): k_mod over duration class and service class, k_c and k_crit curves with y/z markers (k_c markers drag the buckling lengths), deflection components against L/limits, char depth over fire duration (marker drags `fireDurationS`), floor f1 and velocity response over span (markers drag `spanM`), footbridge f1 over span, embedment strength over diameter (marker drags `diameterM`), Johansen failure-mode bars, required against provided spacings (markers drag the three distances).
- `🕹️handles`: span (halved for two spans), width, depth (40 mm lamella snap for glulam), bearing length, lateral restraint spacing, notch depth and distance, fire duration; connection a1, a3, a4, number, rows, diameter, t1, t2 or plate thickness, shear planes.
