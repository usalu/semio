Disagreement candidates (Rust read statically after the oracle was written):
1. Not changed (the effect on F_t,Rd of the group is a conservative or unconservative shift depending on p, m; needs a rebuild to quantify and a decision by the owner; also the Rust uses the first and the second row for the end row pitches the same way as the oracle).

## 2. Second-order frame analysis (EN 1993-1-1 5.2.2, 5.3.2)
`Frame` (8 Euler-Bernoulli elements per bar, cubic Hermite stiffness, consistent geometric stiffness with the element axial force iterated to 1e-9, pinned ends with own rotations, kernel local-axis convention for the bow load) written from the clause: imperfections by 5.3.2(3) (phi = phi0 alpha_h alpha_m with m the columns of at least half the storey mean compression, smallest m over the storeys, equivalent forces phi V at nodes without fixed/pinned support, dropped where H >= 0.15 V below), 5.3.2(6)/(11) (bows e0 = L/350..150 as q = 8 N e0/L^2 on compressed bars with a moment-resisting end and lambda > 0.5 sqrt(A f_y/N)), the four sign combinations with the largest sum of peak moments, class 1-3 cross-section interaction (6.2.9, `mn_eta` of the main oracle) and the member buckling check 6.3.3 with Annex B on the sway-only forces (reusing the main oracle's chi, M_cr, Table B.1/B.2 functions: independent of the Rust but not of the previous oracle).
Third-party and closed-form checks (`--self-check` of the module, all pass): cantilever column with tip load and axial load against the exact second-order solution M = H tan(kL)/k (85913.7 vs 85892.1 N m, 2.5e-4, the 8-element mesh) and against PyNite `analyze_PDelta` on 8 member segments (85892.06 vs the exact 85892.07).
Rows exist when the governing alpha_cr is below 10 and the frame has node loads only (no `frameActions`, no semi-rigid releases): `frame-second-order` (portal), `-fail`, `-pinned-beam`. Tolerance 2e-2 (4 vs 8 elements). 14 frame rows.
Risks (cannot be decided without running the Rust): (a) the bow direction per bar follows the kernel's local z; if the Rust applies the same sign on bars with opposite orientation, the "unfavourable" variant may differ (visible in the portal as a few percent); (b) the beam of the pinned-beam fixture has a numerically zero axial force; its rows were left out of the feature (existence depends on the sign of noise); (c) the Rust takes the peak moment at 4 stations, the oracle at 8, which can differ where the peak lies between stations (bow of a long bar: the middle is a station in both).

## 4. EN 1998-5 liquefaction and piles
Independent closed forms: CRR_7.5 of Youd et al. 2001 from (N1)60cs (fines correction alpha, beta), MSF = 10^2.24/M^2.56, Annex B stress ratio c (a_g S/g) sigma_v/sigma'_v without r_d, the Youd comparison with the Liao-Whitman r_d, nine depths per layer band, stresses integrated over the layers; screening a_g S/g; piles: Winkler beta, long pile test beta L >= pi, the inertial moment (free head: maximum of V/beta e^-x sin x over a fine grid, hand value 0.3224 V/beta; fixed head: V/(2 beta) at the head), Nikolaou et al. 2001 kinematic moment with tau_c = a_g S/g sigma_v and E_1 = 3 rho v_s^2, and the sum against M_Rd. Self-check: Hetenyi maximum 0.322397 against the quadrature of the shear course, CRR_7.5((N1)60cs = 10) = 1/24 + 10/135 + 50/145^2 - 1/200.
Fixtures `geotechnics-pass/fail` (families-pass with a pile through a contrast of 12 so the kinematic moment counts, a free-head pile, loose layers, high water table, short pile). 49 rows.
Findings: the Rust formulas agree with the textbook ones term by term (read after writing). The registry cites remain unverified (`youd-2001`, Nikolaou coefficients 0.042 / 0.3 / 0.65 / 0.5 equal the published values, screening 0.1 / 15 m).
Risks: (1) the pile moment adds the inertial and the kinematic maximum (conservative, stated by the Rust); the oracle does the same; (2) `pile.length` is a `minimum` check: utilisation = pi/(beta L); (3) read leakage: the registry reads of `stiffness-contrast` and Nikolaou happen before the `moment` check is built; if the builder attaches pending reads to it, the passing `pile.moment` rows become `unverifiedBasis` at the first run (then the 6 pass rows of `pile.moment` change status).
