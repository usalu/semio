## 2. Fire (EN 1994-1-2)
- `ANY/🧬️schema/💡️inferences/⚖️checks/🧮️catalogue/🔥️beam-fire` (new): `SlabField` (explicit finite-volume 2D conduction of the slab half section, ISO 834 on the soffit with the half-cell surface resistance, steel temperature under the top flange, 9 W/m²K on the top), lumped protected steel through the new `protected_steel_history_c` (the old `protected_steel_temperature_c` is its last entry), `Beam::reduced` (M_fi,Rd with k_y,θ on the steel = model of the `mfi` check; additionally with the mean k_c,θ of the compression zone from the slab field), `Beam::evolve(minutes)` -> temperatures of gas, steel, soffit, reinforcement layer (4 cm below the top), slab top and the resistance per time.
- `part_1_2::beam_fire(beam, f_y, board)` is the single source of inputs; the `mfi` check body (`🧮️compliance/🔥️fire-and-bridge`) now calls it (the same numbers as before, local duplicates removed).
- Scene `🎬️scene/🔥️fire-section` (new): at mid-span three plates with one scalar field per fire minute, driven by the existing `fire` track and legend: `<beam>:fire-plate` (steel, bound to the critical-temperature check), `<beam>:fire-slab` (13 x 25 grid of the slab field), `<beam>:fire-rebar` (reinforcement layer), the last two bound to the `mfi` check. The old `beam_fire_plate` in `🌈️fields` is removed (helpers made `pub(crate)`).
- Diagrams (same module): `fire-temperature:<beam>` (gas, steel, soffit, reinforcement, slab top over 0..rating in 5 min steps, marker bound to the theta check) and `fire-resistance:<beam>` (M_fi,Rd(t) with k_y,θ, with k_y,θ and k_c,θ, limit M_fi,Ed, marker bound to the mfi check, table twin with θ_a, k_y,θ, k_c,θ).
- Oracle `🧰️scripts/en1994-beam-fire-oracle.py`: scipy BDF on a converged 75-cell column, numpy replica of the 2D scheme, RK4 steel, strip-integration moment of the stdlib oracle. Findings: the first version (flux at the cell centre) overheated the soffit cell by 90 K against the converged solution; the half-cell resistance cut that to 5 K at the soffit (12 K worst row at 30 min); the far column of the 2D field equals the 1D column to 1e-4 K (775.9448 °C soffit and 125.9835 °C mid cell at 60 min, steel 368.86 °C, M_fi,Rd(120 min) 245.75 kNm). All hard-coded in `🔥️beam-fire/🧪️tests` (6 tests).

Only for bridges with the stud check present.
