## 1. Free outlines (task 2)
- **I_t accuracy** (`section-solver`, Python prototype `prandtl.py` against `sectionproperties` at mesh 2e-6): the old raster had -4..-15 % on slanted walls and up to +-9 % on axis-parallel walls whose thickness is not a whole number of cells. Now: 12 cells across the mean wall; **axis-parallel polygons** get a raster width fitted so that all coordinate gaps are whole cells (`fitted_width`); **slanted polygons** are solved at `h` and `2h` and I_t is extrapolated `2*I(h) - I(2h)` (the error is linear in h). Measured in the prototype (Rust mirrors it): hex tube -0.16 %, rotated box -0.12 %, rotated I -0.95 %, rotated solid 0.0 %; axis-parallel box -0.1 %, solid -0.3 %, tee -0.9 %, I -0.65 %, ragged I +1.0 %. So "below 1 %" holds for all tested shapes with at least 12 cells across the wall; wide sections hit the 40 000-cell cap and fall back to fewer cells (Warning below 4 cells). `Solved.it_spread` reports the extrapolation size. Rust tests: hex tube vs `sectionproperties` (1.5 %), rotated solid vs series (1.5 %), I section vs 4.12e-8 (1.5 %).
- **General M_cr** (`section_solver::stability`, new module, pure functions): principal axes (|theta| <= 45 deg), exact polygon cubic moment `k = ∫z(y²+z²)dA` (4-point triangle rule, degree 3), Wagner half coefficient `z_j = z0 - k/(2 I_y)` and `M_cr = C1 π²EI_z/L² (sqrt(I_w/I_z + L²GI_t/(π²EI_z) + z_j²) + z_j)`. Derived by the energy method (second variation of the initial stress `σ0 = M z/I_y`: the in-plane displacement decouples even with a shear-centre offset along y, so the formula holds for **asymmetric** sections in their principal frame). Sense: M_y,Ed >= 0 puts the +z fibres in compression (documented in the check text, the other sense is printed too). Reference: `sectionproperties` `get_beta_p()` matches `-2 z_j` to 6 digits for a tee and an angle (rotated principal axes) in `wagner_ref.py`; tee M_cr(flange in compression) 13.37 kNm > stem in compression 8.69 kNm at L = 3 m. C2/C3 (load height) are not applied (load at the shear centre; unverified in the check text), C1 comes from the member.
- **Torsional and torsional-flexural buckling (6.3.1.4)**: `n_cr_tf` = smallest root of the coupled cubic `i0²(N-Nu)(N-Nv)(N-NT) - N²z0²(N-Nv) - N²y0²(N-Nu) = 0` (bisection on [0, min N]); the python selftest proves it equal to the scipy 3x3 generalized eigenproblem to 1e-9 for the tee and the asymmetric angle. Check `en1999.6.3.1.tb.<member>` (χ_T from `torsional_curve`, unverified lambda_0 = 0.2, ω_x for a transverse weld).
- **N+M interaction (6.3.3)**: `en1999.6.3.3.int.<member>` for free outlines (Eq. 6.59/6.60, `member_interaction_exponents`, the smaller of the flexural and torsional resistances). The scope check now lists only patch loading as not evaluated; the torsion info check no longer assumes a doubly symmetric section.
- Files: `⚖️checks/🌀️free-outline-stability/🦀️.rs` (new), `🪢️free-outline-torsion` (frames, `LtbOut`, general M_cr), `🔷️outline-checks` (wiring), `🌀️section-solver/🧭️stability/🦀️.rs` + `🧪️tests/🧭️stability` (new), `🌀️section-solver/🦀️.rs`.
