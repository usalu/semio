## 1. Fin plate, splice, base plate on foundation (task 1): design and tooling done, NOT applied
Design (schema-first, two new collections, no new `SteelJoint` fields because every `SteelJoint` field costs ~650 mutation crates and positional SQLite columns):
- `JointPlate` / `jointPlates` (spec `🧰️scripts/en1993-joint-plate-spec.py`): jointId, label, role (finPlate | flangeCover | webCover), plate height/width/thickness/count/fy/fu, bolt class/diameter/hole d0/rows n1/per row n2/pitch/gauge/e1/e2/shear planes, eccentricity z of the bolt line, weld throat/fu/beta_w. A fin plate is one row; a splice is flange cover rows plus a web cover row on the same joint. Demands come from the joint's existing `actions` (shear, tension) and `momentDemand`.
- `FoundationBlock` / `foundationBlocks` (spec `en1993-foundation-block-spec.py`): jointId (a basePlate joint), block length/width/depth, f_ck concrete, grout f_ck and thickness, anchors in tension (count, class, d, A_s, h_ef, edge distance). Gives k_j from A_c1 (EN 1992-1-1 6.7: b2<=3 b1, d2<=3 d1, spread height), beta_j = 2/3 test of 6.2.5(7) (grout f_ck >= 0.2 f_ck and t_g <= 0.2 min(a,b)), F_Rdu <= 3 f_cd A_c0 (V-440 digest); feeds the existing `base_plate_fjd/_c/_fc_rd` and T-stub code.
