## 2. Wall plan positions
Design decision, flagged: the plan geometry is the start point `wallStartXM`, `wallStartYM` (Metre) next to the existing `wallLengthM` and `wallDirectionRad`; the end point is derived `start + l (cos, sin)` (`wall_plan::axis_of`). Storing the end as well would duplicate length and direction and allow contradictions. Plan coordinates share the origin of `centreOfMassXM/YM`.
- Layers (schema-first through the field-adder, which is the SCHEMA-CODEGEN v2 predecessor for rows; its twin generator had not landed for root records): model, snapshot JSON, TS, GraphQL, proto, diff rust/ts/graphql/proto/json, SQLite (sql, rs, ts, tests, fixture), field-meta EN and DE, 579 snapshot fixtures, struct literals, four DSL assets (`wall-start-x-m=0 wall-start-y-m=0`), docstrings with emoji on the model fields.

- Kernel (new module `🧬️schema/🧱️wall-plan/🦀️.rs`, `pub mod wall_plan`): `placed_walls_of` (any member of kind wall with positive length and thickness, masonry and RC), `stiffness_per_modulus_m` (cantilever with bending 4h^3/(E t l^3) and shear h/(G t l), k/E = t / (4 (h/l)^3 + (h/l)/(G/E)), storey height h), `wall_plan_of_storey` (rigid diaphragm 3x3 assembly K = sum k a^T a with a = (cos, sin, xc sin - yc cos); stiffness centre where translation and rotation decouple, Cramer solution; r_x = sqrt(K_theta/K_y), r_y = sqrt(K_theta/K_x) per EN 1998-1 4.2.3.2; oblique walls handled exactly), `plan_metrics`, `stiffness_centre_of` (walls when they give a plan, else the entered centre).
- Applicability: a wall plan exists only if the storey's walls (wall_storey_id empty or equal) are of one material, have non-parallel axes and stiffness in both directions; otherwise the entered `centreOfStiffness*` and storey stiffnesses stand. The wall's own torsional stiffness is neglected.
- Feeds: `planRegularity` (e0x, e0y, r_x, r_y, kx, ky, remedy target cs; explanation EN+DE names the source "from N positioned wall rows"), the accidental torsion `e0` (`storey-forces-and-drift`), and the scene (`respond` e0 for the amplification delta).

A label "Walls of S: centre of stiffness (x, y) m, r_x, r_y" and the stiffness-centre marker move to the derived position. Grips (`🕹️handles`): `start-x`, `start-y` (Translate), `length` (ScalarAlong the wall axis), `direction` (Angular ring about the start); the `cos` grips on the entered stiffness centre are dropped while walls drive it.
