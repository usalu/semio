## 2. Free section outline (`outlineVertices[]`)

- Checks (`⚖️checks/🔷️outline-checks`): `en1999.ref.outline.<section>` (Fail: no outer ring, <3 vertices, non-finite, zero area, self-intersection, hole outside; remedy: parametric kind), `en1999.6.1.outline.props.<section>` (A, centroid, I_y, I_z, I_yz; Warning if unsymmetric), `en1999.6.1.outline.match.<section>` (polygon vs parametric A, 5 %, Warning with remedy `free`), `en1999.ref.vertex.<id>` (unknown section).
