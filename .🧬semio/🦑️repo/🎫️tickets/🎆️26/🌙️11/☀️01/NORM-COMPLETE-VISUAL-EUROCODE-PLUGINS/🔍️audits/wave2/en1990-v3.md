## 3. Wind/thermal non-simultaneity and F*_W cap (task 3)
- Generator: `non_simultaneous_family` (wind: `wind_bridge`, `wind_rail`; thermal: `temperature_bridge`); `Context::wind_thermal_exclusive` (bridge domain and registry `a2.wind_thermal.simultaneous` < 0.5); a scenario holding both families splits into "without thermal" and "without wind" (`Combination::excluded`, shown as `without=…`); `Action::cap` bounds |value| while a traffic-group action acts in the same scenario. The cap is set per member by `apply_wind_cap`: F*_W = F_Wk·(v*/v_b)² with v* from `ndp.en1990.a2.fw.speed_cap` and v_b = `bridgeSls[].basicWindSpeed`; no declared speed or v_b ≤ v* means no cap.
- Oracle mirrors the exclusion (`🔮️oracles/⚖️compliance/🐍️.py`, `FAMILY`, variants per exclusion); the catalogue case `bridge-groups` (E90.26) has wind and thermal, so its blessed numbers may change on the next oracle run.
- Tests: `wind_and_thermal_never_act_together_and_the_wind_cap_applies_with_traffic`, `the_declared_basic_wind_speed_caps_the_member_wind_action_at_the_registry_speed`.
