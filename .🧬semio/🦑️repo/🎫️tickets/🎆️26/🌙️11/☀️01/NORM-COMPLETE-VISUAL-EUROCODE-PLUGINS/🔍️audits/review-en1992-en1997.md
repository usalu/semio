## 1. EN 1992 findings (ranked)

### S1

1. **Stirrup capacity is 10^6 too small at member level.** `C92/🧰️helpers-and-annex-parts/🦀️.rs:157` `shear_capacity_n`: `let asw = asw_per_s * 1.0e-3;`. `asw_per_s()` is m²/m (`📸️snapshot/🦀️.rs:740`); mm²/mm needs `* 1.0e3` (Ø8 2-leg s=150: 0.67 mm²/mm, V_Rd,s(cot θ 2.5) = 295 kN; the code gives 0.295 N). Result: `v_rd_cs ≈ 0`, so `v_limit` at `📋️member-evaluation/🏗️…/🦀️.rs:103` falls back to V_Rd,c and every member with links is judged as if it had none. Fix: factor `1.0e3`. Also `:103`: with links the resistance must be `min(V_Rd,s, V_Rd,max)` (what `shear_capacity_n` already returns); `.max(v_rd_c)` can exceed V_Rd,max and the Python oracle (`🧪️tests/⚖️compliance/🐍️.py:452`) uses `min(v_rd_s, v_rd_max)`. ## 2. EN 1997 findings (ranked)

### S1

1. **Zero or negative capacity passes.** `C97/📇️catalogue/🦀️.rs:140-142` `utilization()` returns `0.0` when `capacity.abs() < EPSILON`, also for demand > 0 (EN 1992's twin returns ∞). Any resistance that evaluates to 0 (bearing with `unwrap_or(0.0)` in `📐️geotechnical-parts:56,175`, sliding, pile) is reported as passed; negative capacity gives a negative utilisation, which also passes. Fix: demand > 0 and capacity ≤ 0 → ∞ (fail), non-finite → fail; unit test with R = 0.
2. **Soil-strength partial factors are not applied to earth pressure and passive resistance in DA1-C2, DA3 and GEO-3.** `C97/📇️catalogue/🧱️earth-pressure:238` RW-06 sliding uses `tan φ_base` without γφ, K from raw φ; `⚓️walls-and-anchors:63-64` RW-07 `horizontal_coefficients(phi, …)` with raw φ and only γG on the pressure and γR,e on the passive part; `earth-pressure` RW-03 `:175-184` likewise; project level `🧱️pile-wall…:190-237` computes E_a, E_p with `phi_w` unfactored (`part_1::earth_pressure_coefficient(phi_w, …)`, `passive_earth_force_n(phi_w, …)`) and only the base friction gets γφ. Under M2 (γφ = γc = 1.25, or DIN 1054 GZ 1C/BS-T/BS-A soil factors) the active pressure must use φ_d = atan(tan φ/γφ), c_d = c/γc. The error is unconservative for exactly the approaches the completion report advertises (DA1-C2, DA3, GEO-3). DA2* (the DE default) is not affected.
3. **Passive wall friction and the curved-surface Kp.** `earth-pressure:89,102` clamp |δ| to φ; EN 1997-1 9.5.1(…) and DIN 4085 limit the passive wall friction to ≤ 2/3 φ for curved failure surfaces `[V]` (80 %). The log-spiral value at δ = φ = 30° is 6.93, the Caquot-Kerisel/EC7 Annex C chart gives about 6.4–6.5 (+8 %, unconservative as the doc comment admits). Cap δ_p at 2/3 φ and keep the cohesion/surcharge terms on separate Kpc/Kpq (`:176` uses √Kp and Kp).
4. **Slope checks.** (a) `📐️geotechnical-parts:302-303` returns `50.0` when the progress callback cancels and `:313` returns 50 when no admissible circle exists: a cancelled or empty search passes with F = 50. Return `None`/cancelled and surface "not evaluated". (b) `:357` `let u = 0.0` with submerged weights γ′ in the driving moment (`:355-360`): with a water table inside the slope the driving force must use the saturated weight and u = γw·h_w (`⛰️slopes:62-63` does this correctly; the two Bishop implementations disagree); script: driving weight in the wet zone is under-estimated by 49 % → F over-estimated `[V]` (90 %). (c) `⛰️slopes:62` surcharge `q` enters the weight unfactored; under GEO-3/DA3 a variable surcharge needs γQ (1.3/1.5), permanent γG = 1.0.

## 3. Cross-cutting points

- Both artifacts have a catalogue layer (formula-correct after the rewrite) and a project/member layer that still carries the older shortcuts. Add member-level golden cases for: links present, prestress decompression, non-trivial combinations, DA1-C2 wall, uplift with groundwater at depth, cancelled slope search.
