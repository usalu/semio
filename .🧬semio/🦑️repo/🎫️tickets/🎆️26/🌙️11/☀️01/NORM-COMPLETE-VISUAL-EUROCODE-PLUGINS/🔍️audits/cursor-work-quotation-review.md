# Cursor Work Quotation Review

RetainedCloneCursor work quote defaults explicitly refuse UnsupportedOwner. At inspection, field, scalar, string, option, vec and both derived record/enum generators provide advance, take and close quotes. Production SharedCloneCursor, entries, dynamic DslValue, paged-list, paged bytes/UTF8/map, tuple, box, array, ordered-map and retained-alias cursors lack these overrides; quote-driven callers therefore refuse them rather than silently inferring CPU. Several test cursors also lack overrides. This is a concrete completeness gap, not a claim all are required by EN1990.

EN1990 persisted snapshot source fields use String, f64/u8/u32, derived records/enums and Vec thereof. External AnnexChoice is a derived fieldless enum; CaseRow, CaseNumber and CaseText are derived records containing String, f64 and Vec of these rows. No array, tuple, map or paged owner was found in those declared snapshot and external row fields. This inspection does not establish generated output or runtime completion.

close_original_quoted_retained_step validates original caller, checks fuel against the explicit quote and all physical axes before invoking; it posts actual turn.work independently and retains primary physical progress, receipt and context refusals. It refuses an exceeded quote after accounting actual effects. Its existing work test exercises zero fuel and physical receipts.

A separate controlled boundary needs caller-level verification: RetirementSelection::step_controlled and close_factory_ticket_controlled do not themselves invoke OriginalRetirementControl::prepare_work or commit_work. In particular close_factory_ticket_inner releases a terminal ticket shell and reports work=1 before any work loan call. If exposed directly to a zero-work control without an outer quote/admission, this permits a physical release; determine whether every real caller independently admits the exact same selected action first. A real zero-fuel recipient law should exercise the terminal ticket and cursor path, require no physical effects and then a paid successful release. No runtime red is asserted here.

RetirementSelection binds the same owner and physical demand, rechecks demand at the admitted copy frontier, and preserves child turn results/work. Factory ticket forwarding maps a successful child step to progress and leaves child error unchanged; source inspection does not establish partial-error span and envelope behavior under a real refusing child. Test a child that reports a nonzero physical retained_progress and actual work with a primary refusal, followed by receipt/context refusal, and verify all fields and measured span survive.
