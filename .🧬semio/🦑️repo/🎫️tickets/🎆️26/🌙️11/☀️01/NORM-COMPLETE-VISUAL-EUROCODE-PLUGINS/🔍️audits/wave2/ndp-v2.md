## 1. Registry (2139 entries, 1950 unverified, 189 verified flags kept)
- `🧰️ndp/wave2_en1995_tables.py`: EN 1995 `ndp.en1995.strength.<class>.<prop>` (36 classes x 12 properties), `kmod.<material>.sc1|sc2|sc3.<duration>` (165) and `kdef.<material>.sc1|sc2|sc3` (48). The per-row `verified` flags of the code became the registry status (C16, C24, C30, GL24h, GL28h, GL32h strength; the kmod/kdef rows flagged true). The doubtful C16/C30 numbers (review E95) stay flagged in the evidence and in worklist section 3. The 30 old spec rows `ndp.en1995.t-01.*` were replaced (their spec value, spec line and evidence moved into the `strength.*` rows, so the conflict table maps 1:1). Code: `⚖️kernel/📚️strength-tables/🦀️.rs` reads everything with `ndp_common/ndp_number_opt`; `strength_row` returns an owned `StrengthRow` (`verified` = all 12 cells verified); callers (`Mat.row`, `properties_for_class`, fire `product_kfi`) adapted.
- EN 1993 (`wave2_en1993_tables.py`): Table 2.1 of EN 1993-1-10 (49), EN 1993-1-2 Table 3.1 k_y/k_p/k_E (39), Table 5.1 bow imperfection (10), `1-1.alpha-cr-min`, `1-8.psi-end-plate`, `1-8.gamma-m5`, `5.pile-driving-limit-ratio`, `5.pile-full-capacity-driving-ratio`; the placeholders are keyed: `v-026.2` = 0.05 (Horne tolerance, evidence says engineering tolerance, not normative), `v-420.2` = text of the group effective lengths. The Rust constants became reads (`alpha_cr_min()`, `psi_end_plate()`, `gamma_m5()`, `horne_tolerance()`, `pile_*_ratio()`, toughness `is_encoded/max_thickness`, fire `table_3_1`, `bow_ratio`); the `clauses` crate got the dependency on `semio-s-plugin-norm-compliance` (Cargo.lock needs `synchronize`).
- EN 1991 (`wave2_en1991_consts.py` + `const_to_fn.py`): configuration factor, member and flame emissivity, combustion factor, mu3 cap of cylindrical roofs, vehicle stiffness, LM1 axle spacing (two copies), rail alphas (8), silo patch factor, crane mu and phi_5, c_pi cases. Stefan-Boltzmann and ambient temperature stay constants (physics).
- EN 1996 (`wave2_en1996_tables.py`): shape factor delta table (30), fire thickness rows (24).
- EN 1998 (`wave2_en1998_tables.py`): `q_max` by system, material and ductility class (25) and the drift limit ratios (3), read in the q-limit, base shear and drift checks.
- Codegen (`🧰️ndp/codegen.py`) now writes one file per Eurocode part (`🗃️table/enXXXX.rs`, each far below the 200 KB guard; total 205,681 bytes, crate cap 254,000) joined at compile time (`const fn join`) into the unchanged sorted `NDP_TABLE`; rows use the compact `c(..)`/`d(..)` constructors with `.at()`, `.ed()`, `.ver()` (lib `🔖️ndp/🦀️.rs`). ## 2. Exact tagging
- EN 1993 members (`🧮️action-resistance-checks`): every builder cites the gammas its resistances use (`GAMMA_M0` for axial, bending, shear, torsion; `M0+M2` for net tension; `M1` for buckling, LTB, interaction, shear buckling (the V_bw check was untagged)). `AnnexParams::for_choice` hides its own reads from the thread log (`record_ndp_reads`), so the first check no longer inherits all five factors.
- EN 1994: `head()` cites `AnnexParams::ndps_of(base)`: none for EN 1990/1991 checks, gamma_C/S for EN 1992, gamma_M0/M1 for EN 1993, the composite part all but gamma_Mf, the bridge part also gamma_Mf. This is exact per standard part, not per data flow of each of the 53 check chains.
- EN 1999: `funnel_ndps(id, part, section)` (serviceability 7.2 cites stress or deflection ratio, fire gamma_M,fi, fatigue gamma_Mf and gamma_Ff, thin wall and shell gamma_M1, connections gamma_M2 (+ gamma_M1 and gamma_Mw for welds), members gamma_M1 and gamma_M2), explicit lists at the member, thin-wall, bolted (edge/pitch distances now cite nothing, they use no factor) and welded sites; the 8.5 distance checks previously carried all eleven ids.
- EN 1996 wall evaluators: the three `NDPS` supersets are replaced by per-check family sets by clause (compression, eccentricity, shear, flexure, concentrated, slenderness, material, openings, fire, durability, bed joint, reinforced, simplified, basement) plus the annex-specific K and shear-cap rows and the gamma_M row of the document's class and situation (`AnnexParams::gamma_m_ndp()`, passed down as `gamma_ndp`). The family-to-clause assignment was made from the clause of each check and the family names, not by tracing data flow: please have the en1996 owner review `load-case-checks` and `fire-durability-and-reinforcement`.
- Reads of the new tables are cited where they happen: `record_ndp_reads` around `q_max_for_system` and `drift_theta` in en1998; en1995 strength reads are logged inside `strength_row` and drained by the existing `ndp_reads()` of the catalogue and assessment builders.
