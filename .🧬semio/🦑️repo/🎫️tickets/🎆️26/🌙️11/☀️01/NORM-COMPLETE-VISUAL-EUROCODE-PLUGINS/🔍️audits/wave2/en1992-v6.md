## 1. Remedies without a single-field passing value
- `member-evaluation/🩹️minimal-remedies`: after the single-field search, a failing check without any applicable remedy gets a JOINT remedy (`Solver::joint`: the secondary planned field is moved by scales 1.25/1.5/2/3 on its grid, the primary is solved for each; pairs in plan order, both directions). - If even that fails: a typed notice = non-applicable `OneOf` remedy on the check subject, `options` = governing field paths, EN + DE "No single change suffices / Keine einzelne Änderung genügt". Anchors do the same (`⚓️anchor-evaluation/🩹️minimal-remedies::notice_of`). Contract: every failing check leaves with an applicable remedy or a notice.
