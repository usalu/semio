## 2. Third-party oracles (`🔮️oracles/📐️third-party/🐍️.py`, new)
- **T-90** (sectionproperties 3.10, composite analysis): E_iA_i and E_iI_i of every part, the exact rigid composite (EI) of the stacked section; γ_i, a_2 (B.5) and (EI)_ef from those library constants; the deflection from a PyNite beam carrying (EI)_ef. T-beam: (EI)_ef 1.296e12 between separate 0.772e12 and rigid 1.881e12 N·mm², efficiency 0.689; three-part box also run. The kernel `gamma_beam` agrees to 1e-9 (Rust test).
- **T-106**: the exact residual of the notional char depth d_char,n = β_n t (`b-2d` × `h-d`, protected top at its original position) from sectionproperties (A_r, I, W_el), p/A_r from the exposed faces, k_mod,fi with the registry divisors. This is the section of the method; v2 drew the d_ef section of T-101 (approximation). Kernel and scene now both use it (the scene residual entity of a T-106 case has the area of `reduced_properties`, test `the_t106_residual_is_the_exact_char_depth_section_of_the_kernel`). Rounded char corners of the actual char line are not modelled (not part of the method).
- **Dynamics (PyNite 3.0)**: `modal` of PyNite is known wrong (mechanics-kernel audit), so the oracle uses inverse iteration on PyNite static solves (load = mass · mode estimate; Rayleigh quotient). Strip of T-63: f1 6.7178 Hz = closed form; girder of T-120: f1 2.2266 Hz and a_single; orthotropic floor as a grillage of 8 × 8 PyNite members: f1 6.98149 Hz against the exact plate formula 6.98161 Hz (2e-5), mode shape = sin·sin to 1e-13, modal mass 720 kg = m·ℓ·b/4.
- Rust test `sectionproperties_and_pynite_agree_with_the_gamma_method_the_residual_section_and_the_dynamics` (schema tests) runs the script and compares kernel functions: `gamma_beam`, `reduced_properties`, `floor_f1`, `bridge_f1`, `single_pedestrian_acceleration`, new `floor_plate_f1`, `floor_plate_mode`, `floor_plate_peak_acceleration`. The path of the existing design-case oracle test (`../../../../🔮️oracles`) was one level short (the oracle dir is 5 levels up from the checks crate); fixed to 5.
- Open: the module is not in the norm oracle manifest (request to shared). No library covers fire charring/CLT fall-off, Miner or the crowd model.
