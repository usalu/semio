## 2. Entity `decks[]` through every layer
- Fields (18): enums `laminationProduct` (solid, glulam), `barProduct` (threaded-bar, smooth-bar); geometry `spanM, deckWidthM, deckDepthM, laminationWidthM, barSpacingM, barDiameterM, plateSideM`; prestress `initialPrestressPa, lossRelaxation, lossCreep, lossMoisture, barResistancePa, shearStressPa, compressionPerpendicularPa, wheelLoadN, wheelContactM`. New unit `stress_pa` (Pa, shown N/mm2). Model, ts/json/graphql/proto twins, diff lanes, outline, duplicate ids, field-meta EN + DE, SQLite (table `en1995_deck`, DDL, Rust and TS codecs, admission, tests), 20 kinds (`insert-deck`, `remove-deck`, 18 `change-deck-*`) in `mut-deck-geometry` and `mut-deck-prestress`, edit rules incl. `move_item: &[]`, taxonomy, harness, oracle manifest, build owners, lock.
- Checks (`⚖️timber/🧱️entities`): `deck_results` runs T-122 through the case bridge (stresses N/mm2, bar area from the bar diameter, plate area from the plate side, h = deck depth, mu from the registry): `prestress_min`, `friction`, `bar_force`, `plate_bearing`, `wheel_spread`. Registry constants `ndp.en1995.deck.{min-prestress,default-loss,friction-coefficient,k-c90}` are read by the kernel and recorded in `ndps`; all stay unverified (so passes end as `unverifiedBasis`). Layout failure `en1995.decks.layout.<id>` (EN 1995-2, Annex A) for non-positive dimensions, prestress, bar resistance or losses outside [0, 1). Remedies: the catalogue's lever remedy (raise `initialPrestressPa`) stays for prestress and friction; `bar_force` gets `barResistancePa`, `plate_bearing` gets `plateSideM` (by the square root of the utilisation), `wheel_spread` none (the lever would worsen them). `bar_area` and `plate_a` have no affine field home (derived from the diameter and side), hence no re-homed remedy there.
- Demo: `entity_demo_document()` has `deck-1` (8 x 4 m, h 250, bars O32 at 0.8 m, plate 300 mm, losses 0.15/0.25/0.1, f_c,90,d 1.54 N/mm2, wheel 100 kN on 400 mm). Hand values: sigma_p,min 0.57375 (u 0.6100), friction u 0.5810, bar force 200 kN of 563 kN (u 0.3553), plate bearing u 0.9620, wheel spread u 0.1804.
- Trace: T-122 is `check` (`en1995.t-122.`) on `decks`. Deck plates (orthotropic plate model D_x, D_y, D_xy, k_grid; Annex A.2 b_ef) have no normative text in the repository: recorded in the note as "partial not-modelled" (EN + DE) with the reason; nothing invented. JSON edited, twin row edited identically, codegen verify ok.
- Oracles: compliance oracle `cat_t122` gained `friction` and `wheel_spread`; `deck_entity_checks` evaluates the entity document independently (ids `en1995.t-122.<key>.decks:<id>:T-122`); the check-outcome oracle marks the three unverified-registry checks. Fixtures `deck-pass`, `deck-fail` (copies of column-pass/fail with one deck) and feature rows pass-34/35 and fail-32..35. Oracle values equal the hand values to 1e-15. Third-party: `📐️third-party` entry `deck-bar` (PyNite tension member: reaction, axial force, stress and elongation of one prestress bar against P = sigma_p,0 s h, P/A and P L/(E A)); `--check` passes. An orthotropic plate strip was not built: PyNite plates are isotropic and the orthotropic stiffnesses are exactly the deck-plate content without normative text.
- Rust tests added (uncompiled): `a_stress_laminated_deck_reproduces_the_hand_values_of_t_122_and_is_re_homed_onto_its_fields`, `a_weak_deck_fails_with_remedies_on_the_deck_fields_and_the_remedies_fix_it`, `a_degenerate_deck_fails_with_a_layout_check_on_the_entity`, `pynite_agrees_with_the_jacking_force_and_the_bar_stress_of_the_typed_deck` (schema compliance tests); scene `a_stress_laminated_deck_is_drawn_with_laminations_prestress_bars_anchor_plates_and_a_wheel_patch`.
