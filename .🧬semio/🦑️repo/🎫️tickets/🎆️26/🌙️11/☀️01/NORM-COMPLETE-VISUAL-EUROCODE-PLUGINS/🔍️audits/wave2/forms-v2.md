## 1. Shared forms (`projection-kit/🗂️design-case/🧊️forms`)
- New `🪵️profiles/🦀️.rs` (generic, no domain knowledge):
  - Extruded profile prism: Member roles `taper`, `apex_angle`, `apex_radius` give the side elevation (`profiles::elevation`, moved from `entities::rows`) as a `SolidShape::Prism` extruded across the width; the apex zone is a tinted prism; angular grips (taper angle, apex angle with the documented zero direction) and a radial grip (apex radius) come through the new `Plan` kind `Curved` (absolute handles in the stored unit of the angle/length input).
  - Laminated plate: Plate and Member roles `plies` (series `t1…tn`) and `ply_dir` (series `o1…on`, >= 0.5 parallel) give a `SolidShape::Laminate` (one `GrainLayer` per ply, cross plies at 90 degrees, grain appearance only); a grip on the top of every ply after the first.
  - Charred section: `Extras.char` = list of outline parts (outline plus holes in section coordinates, layer, colour, opacity, texts) extruded along the member; the reduced outline, char band, front band and a slab are all outlines the artifact computes from its fire kernel (EN 1995 keeps `Span2`/`band` in its kit).
  - Mode overlay: `Extras.mode` = frequency, amplitude, shape `(t, u)` -> ghost `DeformationOverlay` on the body; optional plate grid -> `Surface` grid part with a vertex field `peak * |value|`, surface overlay, legend and note.
  - Composite assembly: `Extras.assembly` = sub-forms (Box, Cylinder, Laminate, Pins with points or grid) with position and optional axes, rotated by the yaw of the case; the first sub-form is the body (id = case id); `Extras.attachments` add sub-forms to the normal parts; `Extras.grips` and `Extras.notes` add grips and labels.
  - `Part` now holds a `Geometry` (solid, surface, fastener), colour channel override, deformation, detail text and layer override; entity kind follows the geometry.
- `🗺️layout/🦀️.rs`: `origins`/`origins_with` pack by bounding box in shelves of at most 60 m, jumping behind every reserved or placed rectangle (terminates), `overlaps`. All six case codes use `forms::origins`, so all six get the new default places.
- `draw_with`, `handles_with`, `extent_with`, `signature_with` take an `Extend` (`&dyn Fn(&CaseRow, Size) -> Extras`); `draw` etc. are the extras-free calls the other five codes keep. `CaseDraw` gained `notes` and `legends`. `aux_is_appearance` (grain, ply_dir) and `dimension_is_scalar` (apex height) are new law hooks.
- Form unit tests (new, toy catalogue entries TP, AX, LP): tapered prism with angle grip, curved prism with apex zone and angle/radius grips, ply stack, charred outlines (U-band, hole, slab), mode ghost and heat map, assembly with yaw/axes/extra grip/note/extent, attachment, dimension laws of the extras, shelf packing around reserved rectangles, and the oracle test below.

## 2. Laws (`test_support`)
- `assert_case_laws` accepts and checks every primitive the forms emit: box, cylinder, wedge, prism (finite, outline area, height), laminate (plies, footprint area), surface grid (rows x columns nodes, overlay one sample per node), pins (finite axis, diameter, non-empty pattern), mode overlay (finite, non-empty, positive frequency), and binding coverage (an opaque part of a bound case carries the utilisation).
- `assert_case_dimension_laws_with` (the old call delegates): signature covers prism coordinates, laminate plies and footprint, surface and pin sizes, overlay displacements; counts that change with an input (nails) compare the common prefix.
- `assert_case_layout_clear`: no two default case rectangles overlap and none touches an obstacle; called with `Layout` poses in the case tests of EN 1990, 1991, 1992, 1995, 1996 and 1997 (EN 1995 also against the rows of the typed entities of the entity demo with the catalogue cases).
