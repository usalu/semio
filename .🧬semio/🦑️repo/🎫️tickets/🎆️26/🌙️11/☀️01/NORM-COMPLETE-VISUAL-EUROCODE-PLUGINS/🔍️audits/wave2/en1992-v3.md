- `🌀️creep-curve`: diagram `<m>/creep-shrinkage`, phi(t, 28 d) and eps_cs(t) in permille over the logarithmic age (functions of the age tracks), markers at 50 years.
- `📳️fatigue-range`: per bar delta sigma_s between the quasi-permanent and the characteristic SLS moment (state II, lever arm to the neutral axis, the section steel stress refers to the lowest bar: an indicative stand-in, the document has no load cycle model), against `fatigue-straight / gamma-s-fat` from the registry; tinted shell per bar (field `fatigue-ratio`, layer `fatigue`) and diagram `<m>/fatigue-range`.
- `🕸️strut-and-tie`: for beams with span <= 3 h the determinate 2-strut-1-tie truss solved by `solve_truss` (ST-04): tie at the effective depth, apex 0.1 h below the top, nodes 0.15 h from the ends, strut width 0.2 h (modelling assumptions, flagged in the code, not normative), load = 2 V_Ed of the governing ULS combination; 3D struts as boxes, tie as a rebar of the required area, forces in a label (layer `strut-and-tie`).
- Tests `🎬️scene/🧪️tests/🔭️design-views`: monotone loss and force balance, track and field in the scene, creep marker equals the Annex B function, fatigue ranges finite and tinted, analytic equilibrium of the truss (tie = R run/rise), 3 entities for a deep beam and none for a slender one.
