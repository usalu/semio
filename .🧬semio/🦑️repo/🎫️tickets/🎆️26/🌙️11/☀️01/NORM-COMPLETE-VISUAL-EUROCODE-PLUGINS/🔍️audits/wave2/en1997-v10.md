## 1. K_FI on geotechnical partial factors: removed
- No spec text is in the repository (`TICKET/📐️specs` holds only targets; no DIN 1054 file). From the standards as known: EN 1990 Annex B3.3 K_FI multiplies the unfavourable action factors of the structural (STR) fundamental combination; EN 1997-1 2.4.7.3 and Annex A fix gamma_G/gamma_Q in Tables A.3/A.4 and differentiate by geotechnical category (2.1), not by RC; DIN 1054 does the same with GK1-3. So the scaling was an assumption without a citation -> removed. UNVERIFIED against the clause text (flagged in the docstring of `DesignApproach::resolve`).
- Code: `resolve`, `annex_params`, `resolve_params` lost the reliability-class parameter; `parse_reliability_class` and the `reliabilityClass` catalogue input are gone; ~30 call sites (project checks, pile/wall/slope/uplift, lateral, catalogue cases/factors/slopes, editor panel, tests) and the Python oracle (`geo.factors(annex, approach, situation)`, `K_FI` table) fixed by a script. Picker labels lost "(K_FI ...)".
- Tests: `reliability_class_does_not_scale_the_geotechnical_partial_factors`, `reliability_class_of_the_document_leaves_every_verdict_unchanged` (RC1/RC3 vs RC2 same utilisations and statuses).
- Kept: snapshot leaf `reliabilityClass` + mutation `change-reliability-class` (document-level, shared by the family) with no effect on en1997. Removing it needs model, 5 twins, diff, SQLite, DSL, 400+ fixtures: owner decision (requests.md).
