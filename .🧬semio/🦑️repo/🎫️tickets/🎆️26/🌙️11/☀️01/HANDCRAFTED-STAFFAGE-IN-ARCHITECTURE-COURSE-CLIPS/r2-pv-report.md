## Resolved
- **OI-51** `🔌️appliances/toaster` and `vacuum-cleaner`: 1:50 pens body 0.35 to 0.18, detail 0.18 to 0.13, texture 0.13 to 0.1, ground 0.5 to 0.35. 1:100 body 0.25 to 0.18, ground 0.35 to 0.25 (the fan-heater family values from ha1). Styles text en and de follows. Checks pass.
- **OI-52** `shower-head`: hose 1:100 is a 0.13 mm line (finest ISO pen, true width 0.12 mm). At 1:200 the hose cannot be dropped: its loop sets the drawn extent (a drop gave extent-height 0.81 vs 1.12 m and extent-width errors, check failed), so it stays a 0.1 mm line (the finest printable stroke, true 0.06 mm), stated in `thinElements` (en and de). 1:50 unchanged (0.24 mm, true).
- **OI-54** Pens. Surveyed all 144 assets (`🧰️pv/pens2.py`): the reference assets (vegetation, humans, components, vehicles) use 0.1 (texture, glazing), 0.15, 0.2 and 0.3 in several hundred places, and `authoring-v2.md` only asks for "0.13-0.18 body" at 1:500, so the ISO 128-20 series is not enforced by the archive rules. No catalog-wide snap. Only single outliers inside my area were snapped: pumpjack 1:50 body 0.22 to 0.25 (styles text too); circulation-pump, gas-boiler, hot-water-cylinder 1:200 ground 0.2 to 0.25 (closes OI-53 partly). Left as they are in the reference family: 0.1 texture/glazing, 0.15 and 0.2/0.3 body/ground series. Not touched (outside my set): dairy-cow 1:200 body 0.26, herring-gull and mallard-duck 1:50 body 0.31, pigeon (0.3, 0.4).
- **OI-57** `operating-table` `depthM` 0.6 to 0.5 (the summary and notes give the 0.5 m IEC class width; no source for 0.6, castors are on the carriage not wider). `tilt-window` summary now says the 1.25 m section height is the 1.2 m sash plus the sill (en and de). `tealight` summary now gives the 40.5 mm overall height with the flame next to the 16 mm cup (en and de).
- **OI-58** Decision, no swap, no compatibility: the checker (`🗂️catalog/🟦️.ts:219`, `:511`) and the manifest define `widthM` as the horizontal extent of the default elevation drawing, so laptop (0.275) and split-ac (0.21) values are right and a swap would break the extent check. - **OI-59** `gas-hob` plan view at 1:50 (10 shapes: plate 0.30 x 0.52 m, burner ring, flame ring, cap, four-rail pan-support frame, knob and pointer) and 1:100 (plate with knob merged, burner disc with flame merged, frame outline). - **OI-56** No change, with evidence. Survey of all 144 assets: 1:500 elevation shape counts are 27 (tower-crane), 22 (rollator), 19 (cyclist, tram, stroller), 14-18 for most humans and 8-9 for the archive's walking-commuter and running-child, so 12-18 is the norm of the handcrafted references. The shapes are bone-bound links (head, torso, three arm links, thigh/shank/shoe per leg), so a union path would lose articulation in every clip. The "1 to 4 closed paths" rule is read as visible silhouettes, not bone shapes. If the owner wants real merging, it needs a rig decision (articulation at 1:500).
