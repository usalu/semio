## Mounting Correction

The boiler helper now treats its position as the floor datum and places its canonical connection origin 0.7 m above it at the same scene metre, in both the installation and comparison panel. Its catalog bracket is 0.64 m above that origin, hence 1.34 m above the floor. The front radiator now uses the exact floor origin. Boiler-connected paths terminate at the placed cast bottom; the comparison paths use the corresponding two stub positions.

Before the source changes, the two new language-neutral cases failed for both boiler floors and for the radiator nudge. Afterward all five primary-energy scale tests passed with independent Manim Rectangle geometry and the catalog bracket transform. The representative post-change still uses actual canonical static poses and was visually inspected with the supply pipe; it shows the raised boiler and pipe contact. It remains a still, omits its text label, and does not establish animation timing.
