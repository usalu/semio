Design notes
- `duct-fan` is seen face-on so the blades read (the symbol it replaces); its real discharge is normal to the drawing plane, so the emitter has a nominal in-plane direction (documented in the assumptions). At 1:200 five blades become three broad ones so the gaps survive printing. Poses also differ in blade phase (0, 18, 36 deg).
- `mvhr-unit` heat recovery is expressed through the two emitters relative to the 21 degC reference room; the flow and the heat scale with the single `fan` operating level (0.5 normal, 1 boost).
- `tealight` is below the print features at 1:200 (0.19 mm): the cup and flame are thickened within the extent tolerance (decisions say by how much) and the flame is merged with its tongue there.
- `shower-head` has no spray shapes by design: the water is shown by the emitters and the warmed head, handle and hose. The hose passes in front of the rail on its way up.
