# Audit of test changes, round 2 (agent wa, read-only)

Method: `git diff HEAD` of all 48 modified or deleted test files under `🎓️teaching/🏛️architecture` (`🧪️*` folders, `*.cases.json`, test data `🔣️*.json`, vitest `🟦️.ts(x)`), plus a read of the untracked test files (about 35, mostly new suites with no HEAD baseline) and cross-reference with `T/r2-*-report.md`, `T/r2-coordinator-rulings.md` and the earlier migration, asset, fix, cleanup and q reports. Paths are shortened: `V` = `⚡️energy/🏗️authoring/🎨️visuals`, `S` = `🧍️staffage`, `E` = `⚡️energy`. Line numbers are in the working tree.

## Verdict
No assertion was weakened without a reason in the ticket. Most exact-to-tolerance changes follow the replacement of procedural glyphs by handcrafted staffage casts (thermal frames carry a halo, casts are not tight bounding boxes). Items that need attention:

1. **T1** The thermal-span guard was narrowed from clip to Scene by agent tr, citing a coordinator ruling that is not in `r2-coordinator-rulings.md`. Record it there.
2. **T2** `export-performance` pinned digest of `radiator-transient/0000.svg` is stale and was not re-pinned. The test still fails, which is the correct behaviour. Re-pin only with a documented reason (the panel-radiator wall removal, OI-34).
3. **T3** The ventilation close-up radiator floor-clearance assertion can no longer distinguish clearance from floor (delta larger than the clearance), and the person height lost its upper bound.
4. **T4** Three vitest cases are skipped on this host when tools are missing. Justified, but coverage is lost here.
5. **T5** `cast-export` has silent `skip()` on missing bun and `skipIf(win32)`.
