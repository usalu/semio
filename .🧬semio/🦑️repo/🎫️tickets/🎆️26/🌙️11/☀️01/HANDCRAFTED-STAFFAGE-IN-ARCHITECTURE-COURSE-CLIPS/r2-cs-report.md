## Style verdict (no outlier needing an asset fix)
- Line weights and pen hierarchy: new humans (standing-adult, walking-commuter, person-cooking, seated-office-worker, sleeping-person) use the same heavy silhouette outline, thinner inner folds and the vertical clothing hatch as cyclist and seated-reader. Appliances follow the bookshelf/heat-pump pattern (outline plus filled navy body, thinner details such as grille slats, dials, rack units). Large machinery (oil-pumpjack, vintage-car-1929, city-car) is thin-lined, as the archive's own diesel-generator and city machinery are at 1:50, so it is consistent, not an outlier.
- Detail per scale tier: server-rack, gas-boiler, hot-water-cylinder, mvhr-unit carry fitting detail at 1:50 and collapse sensibly at 1:100. Tiny items (tealight, room-thermostat, thermostatic-radiator-valve, cooking-pot, expansion-valve) are small but legible symbols, like the archive's small objects.
- Silhouettes and stance: the five humans read as the same hand as the archive (head, neck, hand and shoe shapes, seated pose on the chair, supine sleeper with pillow and blanket). No proportion outliers.
- Hatch and fill: thermal fills and line hatches match the references (hatch only on clothing and cut sections).
- Weak spots, left as authored because they are physically right and the same family as references: the open laptop at 1:50 is 4 mm and reads as a V; gas-hob is a thin slab; chalkboard is a plain frame.

## Thermal palette
- The cast exporter uses `range: { mode: "shared" }` (`🎨️depiction/🟦️.ts:66`): a fixed, documented range for every cast. Surface temperature is -20 to 60 degC, centred on the preset's air temperature (`🌡️thermal/📏️quantities/🟦️.ts:37`, `🖍️palette/🟦️.ts` `scaleOf` + `divergingColour`). In an office at 22 degC a 35 degC laptop is only 13/38 = 0.34 of the warm span, hence pale.
- The scale is therefore not per-cast auto-ranged. That is honest and comparable across casts, which is why a human at 33 to 34 degC is saturated orange and a 35 degC device is pale only through the limits of the ramp.
- Proposal (not implemented): an optional cast field `range` with `{"spanK": n}`, the scene scale centred on the environment air temperature and extending +/- n K. Authored once per scene (the same n for every cast of that scene), so casts in one scene stay mutually comparable while the course gets legibility (for example n = 15 K turns 35 degC at 22 degC into about 0.87 of the warm ramp). It touches the cast schema (JSON and TS parser), `DisplayRange` in contracts, `scaleOf`, the Python manifest and the cast cache key, and changes the key of every thermal cast, so it was not done while about 20 agents are rendering concurrently. `DisplayRange` already has `fixed` and `auto`; `spanK` is a third, environment-relative mode.

## Task 2: cooler drain
- The "drain pipe" is the discharge vane of `split-ac-indoor-unit` in the `start-cooling` clip: it opens to 35 degrees (`🔣️.json` bone `vane`, y 0.015, length 0.125) and its tip sits 0.0626 m under the casing bottom (cast `boundsM.minY` -0.1126 incl. 0.05 m margin). The asset is right; placing the casing on the floor put the open vane in the slab.
- Fix in the placement, `❄️cooling/🎬️clip/⚖️heating-comparison/🐍️.py` `_build_ahu` (~l.198-200) and constant `CAST_MARGIN_M`: after the origin shift the unit is lifted by the vane dip, computed from the cast union bounds. Probe: lift 0.2683 Manim units, equal to 0.0626 m x 4.2857 units per metre, so the open vane tip now touches the slab line and the casing stands 0.27 above it (wall-style mounting).
