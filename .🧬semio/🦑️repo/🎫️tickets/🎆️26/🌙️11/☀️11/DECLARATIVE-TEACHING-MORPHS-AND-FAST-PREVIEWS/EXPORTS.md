## Defects found while mapping

- E-001 `lib.stills` decodes at `container.duration * 0.5` and breaks on the first frame at or after that time: the chosen frame depends on the container duration rounding and decoder timestamps, not on the output frame schedule. Fix: frame index derived from the frame count (see `📤️export/🖼️stills`).
- E-002 `concat_videos`/`excerpt_segment`/`render_presentation` do not look at the profile of their inputs. With the profile folder layout a final job normally cannot reach a preview slot, but explicit paths (joiner, delivery, `media_dir` override) can. Fix: `require_profile`.
- E-003 The delivered course preview constants `854x480`, `15 fps` and crf/preset values live in a ticket contract JSON; the profile JSON already declares them. Fix: size/fps read from the profile.
