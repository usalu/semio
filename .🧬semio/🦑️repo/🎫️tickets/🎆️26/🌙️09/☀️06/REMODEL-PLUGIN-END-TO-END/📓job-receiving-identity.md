# Job receiving identity correction

OriginalJobDecodeOwner retains OriginalDecodeState, which owns persistent construction and work issuers and a detached decoder receipt. receive re-loans those same issuer identities, detaches the continuation even across unwind, and can therefore preserve identity across calls in principle. My earlier statement that a distinct Job frame was necessarily required was too broad.

Current receive checks a cancellation-aware checkpoint before the operation, and with_native_retirement uses a different retirement control without a construction loan. A post-cancellation recovery seam needs the actual same authority under permitted retirement, not a fabricated recipient. Existing admit_original_construction has no retained effect frame of its own.

The sealed-shared recovery test currently reissues fixed physical grant and local work ceiling every loop and drops a foreign refusal without paid retirement; it does not yet prove finite stock or complete error custody. Native owner notified. No execution.
Construction frame identity is the persistent issuer numeric identity, not the address of the recreated turn recipient. The action is retained by the frame and issuer outstanding count.
