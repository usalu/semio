## 1. What was built

### 1.1 The case suite — `E/🏛️bestest/🦀️.rs` (new, mounted as `crate::bestest`)

`Model`: **600, 600FF, 610, 620, 630, 640, 650, 900, 900FF, 910, 920, 930, 940, 950**.

### 1.2 Examples — `A/📚️examples/🏛️bestest-<case>/`

Fourteen new example dirs (`🦀️.rs` + `🟦️.ts` + `🧪️tests/{🦀️.rs,🟦️.ts}` + `🖼️assets/🗣️.dsl.semio`),

`EnergyModelSnapshot::parse_dsl` rejected it with "missing model line" and the mutation case's own
`identity-round-trip` scenario was reading a document that cannot parse. The demo now carries the
case 600 model and its asset is regenerated from it.

`🔋️model.json` — Newell areas, air-to-air U-values from layer stacks plus film resistances, glazing,
ACH, gains — asserted against the standard's own numbers, plus the committed EnergyPlus documents).

A case whose `🔮️energyplus.json` does not exist yet **fails with an explicit message naming the
absent file**. The Rust host has no skip channel ("a missing registration, a panic and an error are
all results — never a silent skip", `🧰️framework/…/🧪️test/🏃️runner/🦀️.rs:220`), and agreeing with
an absent document would be worse than a red.

## 2. Engine defects found and repaired

Authoring the cases against the real kernel surfaced a set of genuine physics and numerics defects.
Each was fixed at the cause, not worked around in the case models.

| # | File:region | Defect | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `E/📍️site` `solar_position` | Azimuth used `cos(acos(sin α))` — i.e. `sin α` — in the denominator instead of `cos α`, and `acos` returns 0…180° only, so the sun could never be west of south. Every afternoon was mirrored onto the morning. | Correct denominator `cos(lat)·cos(alt)`; azimuth reflected to 360−a for positive hour angles. Real equation of time and longitude/time-zone correction added; the caller now passes local hour-of-day instead of the run's absolute hour index. |
| 2 | `E/🌰️kernel` `step_surface` | Exterior-surface heat balance was solved and then **discarded** — the CTF was driven by outdoor AIR temperature — while the absorbed exterior solar was injected straight into the zone air at 70 %, plus another 30 % through a fake "interior absorbed solar" argument. Opaque walls got 100 % of their absorbed solar as an instantaneous zone gain and no sol-air lift at all. | The exterior solve's answer is now the CTF's driving temperature (sol-air), computed with the previous step's conduction and the real Brunt sky temperature; nothing is dumped into the zone. |
| 3 | `E/🌰️kernel` `step_surface` | Sky temperature was `dry_bulb_c + 253.15` — a 273.15 typo that happens to read as a fixed 20 K depression. | `crate::site::sky_temperature_k(dry_bulb, dew_point)`. |
| 4 | `E/🌰️kernel` `step_fenestration` / `Publish` | Window conduction `U·A·(T_out − T_zone)` was accumulated into `zone_envelope_w`, which `Publish` then **subtracted** — so every window conducted with the wrong sign. | Window conduction goes into the zone-air bucket the balance already sums; `zone_envelope_w` is left as the opaque diagnostic it is. |
| 5 | `E/🌰️kernel` `Publish` + `E/🏠️zone_air` `net_sensible_w` | `sensible_gain_w` included `surface_convection_w`, and `ZoneAirBalance::net_sensible_w()` added it again — surface convection counted twice. | `sensible_gain_w` = internal + transmitted solar only; convection reaches the balance once, through its own field. |
| 6 | `E/🏠️zone_air` `bdf3_next_value` | Used `history[1..3]` — i.e. T\_{n−1}, T\_{n−2}, T\_{n−3} — instead of T\_n, T\_{n−1}, T\_{n−2}. The whole integrator lagged one timestep. Its own `bdf3_rate` counterpart used the correct indices, so the two disagreed. | `history[0..2]`, which makes `bdf3_rate` a true inverse. |
| 7 | `E/🌰️kernel` `SystemSubstepStage` | `Predict` pushed a predictor temperature into the BDF3 history and `ApplyIdealLoad` pushed a corrected one — **two** history advances per substep — and the free-float path (no ideal loads) pushed only in `Predict`. | One commit per substep, in `Complete`, with the balance the system stages actually delivered into. |
| 8 | `E/🌰️kernel` `Predict` | The predicted load came from `sensible_gain_w − previous heating + previous cooling`, which omits infiltration entirely and subtracts the previous substep's demand. | A real predictor: free-float the zone with `system = 0`, then invert the same integrator for the power that lands exactly on the active setpoint (`zone_air::required_system_sensible_w`). |
| 9 | `E/🌰️kernel` `Thermostat` stage | Setpoints were `schedule · 24 + 20` (heating) and `schedule · 6 + 24` (cooling) — the schedule value was a normalized fraction with a hard-coded 20–44 °C / 24–30 °C mapping, and no schedule could express 27 °C heating or 10 °C setback. | The schedule value **is** the setpoint in °C. |
| 10 | `E/🎛️controls` `proportional_fraction` | A zero throttle range returned 0 — a dead controller — so an ideal on/off thermostat (what §5.2 specifies) could not be expressed at all. | Zero throttle means full output the moment the error turns positive. |
| 11 | `E/🌰️kernel` `Infiltration` stage | `InfiltrationMethod::WindAndStack` was hard-coded and `stack_height_m`/`discharge_coefficient` were literals; a constant-ACH model could only be smuggled in through `constant_term_coefficient`. | `Infiltration` now carries `method`, `design_flow_ach`, `effective_leakage_area_m2`, `discharge_coefficient`, `stack_height_m`, and the kernel maps it onto `InfiltrationSpec` one-to-one. |
| 12 | `E/🧠️precompute` `Materials` stage | Solar absorptance and emissivity were overwritten by **every** layer, so the innermost layer decided how much sun an exterior wall absorbs. | Taken from layer 0 only, with the outside-first layer order stated (EnergyPlus's own `Construction` convention). |
| 13 | `E/🧠️precompute` `Fenestrations` stage | The host surface conducted through its GROSS area while the window added its own — 21.6 m² of south wall plus 12 m² of window on a 21.6 m² wall. | The window's area is subtracted from the host surface's opaque area. |
| 14 | `E/🌰️kernel` `commit_zone` / `E/🧪️sim` `step_aggregate_facility` | `state.delivered_total` accumulated across the whole run while the facility meters integrated it once per hour — the facility meters grew quadratically with run length. | `delivered_total` is reset at the start of each timestep, where it is a per-timestep roll-up. |
| 15 | `E/☀️solar` | Window shading was `shading_factor(1.0, 0.0, 1.0, sun_alt)` at both call sites — overhang depth hard-coded to zero, so **no shading existed**, and `ShadingSurface` was consumed nowhere. Cases 610/630/910/930 were unreachable. | New `window_shading` / `WindowProjections`: profile-angle overhang and fin shadows on the beam component, a semi-infinite-strip attenuation on the sky-diffuse component. `Fenestration` gained `height_m`, `sill_height_m`, `overhang_depth_m`, `overhang_offset_m`, `fin_depth_m`, `fin_offset_m` — the window-attached analogue of `Shading:Overhang:Projection` / `Shading:Fin:Projection`. |
| 15b | `E/🏢️envelope` `solve_interior_surface_temp` | The inside face temperature was solved with the wrong sign: heat conducting INTO the zone came out as a surface COLDER than the room air. The energy leaving the function was still right (`convection = h·(T_air − T_s)` cancelled the error), so only the reported `SurfaceState::inside_temp_c` was wrong — but it was wrong every hour of every run. | Both the solve and the convection term flipped, so `conduction`, `convection` and the temperature all agree on "positive into the zone". The existing test asserted the inverted direction and was corrected with it. |
| 16 | `E/🔋️model` `Model::validate` | `#[cfg(test)] pub(crate)` — not compiled into production builds, unreachable from any caller, and `Engine::run` never invoked it. | Now `pub` and pure, additionally validating schedule references and fenestration geometry/optics. `bestest::run` calls it before `Engine::run`. |

## 3. Real gaps left open, named rather than papered over

1. **No hourly HVAC power output.** `step_aggregate_zone` registers exactly one time series per zone
   (the zone air temperature) and routes heating/cooling into METERS, which retain a running total
   and a peak but no hourly trace. `hourly.heatingW` / `hourly.coolingW` therefore come back EMPTY
   and the harness reports them as unavailable rather than comparing zeros. Closing it means
   `series` becoming `zones × 3` in `EnergyNumericalCensus::observe` plus a series branch in the
   `Heating`/`Cooling` aggregation stages — a change to the admission census, not a projection
   detail.
2. **`ScheduleSet` still lives on `SimulationConfig`, not only on `Model`.** A peer worker (W-D0)
   added `Model::schedules` and `Model::run_period`, but nothing reads them: the kernel's eleven
   lookup sites, the admission census (`observed_model_bytes`) and the close-step pump all still go
   through `config.schedules`, and `model_is_terminal_empty` does not mention either field. The
   BESTEST cases populate `model.schedules` (so the committed `🔋️model.json` **is** self-contained,
   which is what the contract requires) and `bestest::simulation_config` derives the config's copy
   from it. Finishing the move is W-D0's, not this worker's.
3. **Interior solar never charges thermal mass.** The first-order CTF is driven by the outside face
   only, so transmitted window solar reaches the zone air directly and cannot be stored in the wall
   or floor. This is the structural reason the 900-series (high-mass) results are expected to
   deviate more than the 600-series, and it is why case 960 is not registered.
4. **Diffuse shading is an infinite-strip approximation.** `window_shading`'s diffuse term is the
   exact infinite-strip view factor (`y/√(d²+y²)` for the overhang, the azimuthal twin for the fins,
   derived independently in Python before the Rust was written and pinned by four hand-derived unit
   tests in `E/☀️solar`), not a view-factor integral over the real finite projection. Ground-reflected
   diffuse is not attenuated at all, because the caller's sky model has no separate ground term.
5. **`ShadingSurface` remains inert.** Free-standing site shading polygons are still consumed
   nowhere in the kernel. The BESTEST cases do not need them; a real project would.
6. **`Zone.conditioned` doubles as "use the transient integrator".** `advance_zone_air` branches on
   it: `true` runs BDF3, `false` collapses to an analytical quasi-steady temperature. The free-float
   cases therefore keep `conditioned: true` — they are unconditioned in the HVAC sense but must
   still integrate transiently. The flag needs splitting into two concepts.
7. **`sha256` of the weather file** is computed by the harness (`semio_repo_test_host::sha256_hex`),
   not by the engine — the energy crate has no hasher dependency, so `results_report_json` takes the
   digest as a parameter rather than inventing one.
