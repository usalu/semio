# DIN EN 16798 hygienic outdoor airflow

The German national foreword NA.5.1.1 specifies a minimum of 4 L/s per person for non-residential design under complete mixing. The corresponding European default is Annex B.3.1.1. The minimum applies to the total outdoor airflow. The German foreword also requires adherence to the WHO pollutant values and calls for an adjustment when the air distribution departs from complete mixing. [DIN publication record](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-16798-1/349622591) · [DIN preview with NA.5.1.1](https://www.normsplash.com/Samples/DIN/366818232/DIN-EN-16798-1-2022-de.pdf) · [REHVA technical explanation of the European default](https://www.rehva.eu/rehva-journal/chapter/european-standardisation-on-indoor-air-quality)

For a German low-polluting Category IV office with ten occupants and 10 m² floor area, Method 1 yields (10 × 2.5 + 10 × 0.3) × 3.6 = 100.8 m³/h. The hygienic minimum is 10 × 4 × 3.6 = 144 m³/h. The zone fails at 100 m³/h and passes at 144 m³/h. The AHU capacity for that zone must cover 144 m³/h even when the assigned flow is lower. Capacity sums the larger of the assigned airflow and hygienic minimum per linked zone. The building's model treats all linked design occupancies as simultaneous.
