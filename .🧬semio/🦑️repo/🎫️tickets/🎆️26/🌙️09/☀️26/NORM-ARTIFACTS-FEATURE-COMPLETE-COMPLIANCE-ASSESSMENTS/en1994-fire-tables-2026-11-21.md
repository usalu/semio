# EN 1994 tabulated column fire resistance

## Partially encased columns

Case C94-13 evaluates the verified rows of EN 1994-1-2:2005 Table 4.6. The German and English publications agree on the rows. The case checks section dimensions, reinforcement axis distance, reinforcement ratio and the web-to-flange thickness ratio. It requires the stated material, exposure and member boundary conditions, and checks the column length against the table's applicability limit. [EN 1994-1-2:2005](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/en.1994.1.2.2005.pdf) · [DIN EN 1994-1-2:2010-12 as published in the Lower Saxony government gazette](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80835/Nds._MBl._Nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_Anlagenband_37l.pdf)

The user supplies the ratio of fire design action to ambient member resistance. For this comparison, ambient resistance must use twice the fire buckling length and account for eccentricity. The previous automatic check supplied a ratio of two design actions. The automatic report now explains the needed basis and directs the user to the case. [EN 1994-1-2 §4.1 and §4.2](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/en.1994.1.2.2005.pdf)

The model selects the next conservative tabulated load level. For combinations with incomplete entries the case reports non-applicability. It also checks against the user-supplied ambient reinforcement axis minimum. The German National Annex applies the structural detailing requirements alongside the fire minima. [JRC worked examples](https://eurocodes.jrc.ec.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2022-06/2012_11_WS_fire.pdf) · [DIN National Annex edition](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-1994-1-2-na/132959618)

## Filled hollow sections and fully encased columns

C94-14 covers Table 4.7 for rectangular and circular concrete-filled hollow sections. It uses the next tabulated load level, checks the width or diameter to wall-thickness ratio, and handles R30 rows with zero required reinforcement separately from unavailable fire classes. Reinforced sections require S500. The user confirms the ambient resistance basis, including eccentricity, twice the fire buckling length, a nominal section yield strength of 235 MPa and at most 3% reinforcement counted in ambient resistance. The actual reinforcement ratio is checked against the table and may exceed the amount that can be credited in ambient resistance. [DIN EN 1994-1-2 §4.2.3.4 and Table 4.7](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80835/Nds._MBl._Nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_Anlagenband_37l.pdf#page=188)

C94-15 covers both alternatives of Table 4.4. It evaluates each set of section dimensions, cover to the steel profile and reinforcement axis distance as a unit. It also checks the ambient bar axis distance and the minimum number and diameter of longitudinal bars. The input confirms the standard member exposure, braced frame and ambient resistance and detailing basis. [DIN EN 1994-1-2 §4.2.3.2 and Table 4.4](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80835/Nds._MBl._Nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_Anlagenband_37l.pdf#page=185)

## Beams and columns with concrete used only as insulation

C94-16 and C94-17 each verify the five published fire classes R30, R60, R90, R120 and R180. R30 uses filled web compartments. Higher classes require 25, 30, 40 or 50 mm of concrete cover to the profile as applicable and a surrounding mesh. The mesh checks cover the largest spacing, smallest bar diameter and reinforcement cover on fire-exposed surfaces. The engineer confirms the member detailing and the applicable member and exposure conditions. [Table 4.3](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80835/Nds._MBl._Nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_Anlagenband_37l.pdf#page=184) · [Table 4.5](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80835/Nds._MBl._Nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_Anlagenband_37l.pdf#page=186) · [Clause 5.1](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80835/Nds._MBl._Nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_Anlagenband_37l.pdf#page=205)

## Partially encased composite beams

C94-18 implements the complete Tables 4.1 and 4.2 cells of the first-generation standard. It keeps each width, depth and reinforcement alternative together and uses the next published load band. The reinforcement ratio is derived from the area of added bars and lower flange. The case checks the encasement ratio, S500 where the bars are credited, the yield strength adjustment, slab geometry and the profiled deck coverage or fire-protection rule. It interpolates the axis distances only between two complete Table 4.2 entries and checks the ambient detailing minima alongside them. [EN 1994-1-2:2005 §4.2.2](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/en.1994.1.2.2005.pdf) · [Published German Tables 4.1 and 4.2](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80835/Nds._MBl._Nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_Anlagenband_37l.pdf#page=183)

## Verification and remaining scope

Rust and SQLite matched all 32 published rows and 91 table selections across Tables 4.4, 4.6 and 4.7. A standalone Rust run then evaluated 12 pass, failure and unavailable scenarios using the actual catalogue functions and checked-in showcase inputs. The editor geometry and showcase cases are wired for all three. The two native packages passed all seven targeted tests, including the complete alternative and applicability checks for the three tables.

The 18 insulation and mesh cases matched SQLite and the production Rust implementation. The Rust run now evaluates 33 catalogue cases, including the two checked-in insulation examples and a column outside the length limit.

The beam verification checked 55 Table 4.1 and 16 Table 4.2 cells, including unavailable combinations, along with 55 axis lookups and 14 production design cases. Native compilation of the new beam and insulation cases remains pending.
