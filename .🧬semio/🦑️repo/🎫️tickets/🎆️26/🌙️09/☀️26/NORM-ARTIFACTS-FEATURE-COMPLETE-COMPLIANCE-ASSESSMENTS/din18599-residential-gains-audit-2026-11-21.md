# DIN V 18599 residential internal gains audit

DIN V 18599-10:2018-09 § 5, Table 4 specifies residential internal heat gains, 45 Wh/(m² NGF·d) for single-family houses and 90 Wh/(m² NGF·d) for multifamily houses. The [dena guide](https://www.dena.de/fileadmin/dena/Publikationen/PDFs/2023/LEITFADEN_ENERGETISCHE_GEBAEUDEBILANZIERUNG_NACH_DIN_V_18599.pdf), section 5.1.1 and table 6, identifies buildings with up to two dwellings as single-family houses and confirms these values. [BAFA's formula collection](https://www.bafa.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/DE/Energie/qpeb_formelsammlung.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=7) gives the daily gain equation and values. The [municipal energy documentation](https://www.stadt-wettin-loebejuen.de/de/datei/anzeigen/id/12081%2C1020/anhang_1_eneka.energieplanung_dokumentation_10_2024.pdf) explicitly identifies the 2018 edition and Table 4 for the EFH figure.

For 100 m² NGF, the corresponding average power densities are 45/24 = 1.875 W/m² and 90/24 = 3.75 W/m². For a 31-day month the internal gains are respectively 139.5 and 279 kWh; for 28 days 126 and 252 kWh. These are heat sources, not useful heating, final energy, primary energy or a full compliance result.

Existing balance-model `heating_months` already multiplies editable `ThermalZone.internal_gains_w_m2` by zone area and monthly hours. It never derives that value from Table 4. The example residential zone uses 3.5 W/m². The model records `BuildingCategory` and zone area, but it has no dwelling-unit count and cannot determine EFH versus MFH from category or occupants; attachment is also not a valid substitute. The editor exposes `zones[].internalGainsWM2`; diagrams show gains and monthly heating balance. No automatic residential profile selection was found in the DIN 18599 artifact.

Recommended addition: a typed and explicitly selected residential building-use classification, with a pure 2018-09 Table 4 function yielding daily gain density and average W/m². Its result should feed the existing zone monthly balance for the residential standard profile. Either require dwelling count or select EFH/MFH explicitly; never infer it from occupants, attachment, or the existing editable gain. Expose building-level classification beside building category in editor and gain source/value beside `zones[].internalGainsWM2`; identify calculation in the report and monthly diagram/table. Preserve an explicit custom input path only when an alternative calculation regime permits it, not by silently treating custom gain as the standard profile. Add independent language-agnostic examples for both classifications and 28/31-day periods; test category gating and missing/invalid dwelling count. As inputs and profile selection are absent this is a schema-to-UI feature, not a standalone numerical patch.

Scope: the daily-to-monthly multiplication is a derived arithmetic check from the public daily formula and continuous daily residential profile; it does not validate the remaining DIN V 18599-2 monthly method or the entire 18599 series. The 2025 DIN/TS edition is distinct and must not silently reuse this 2018 selection without verification.
