# EN 1993-1-9 Table 8.10 runway fatigue

C93-18 selects the nominal detail class for the top flange-to-web junction of a crane runway beam. It checks that the wheel-load stress ranges were evaluated at the location required by the selected construction detail and that the user confirmed the detail geometry and stress basis. Where effective full penetration is used, that confirmation includes conformity with EN 1993-1-8.

| Detail | Construction | Category at 2 million cycles | Stress location |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | Rolled I or H profile | 160 | Web |
| 2 | Full-penetration tee-butt weld | 71 | Web |
| 3 | Partial or effective full-penetration tee-butt weld | 36* | Weld throat |
| 4 | Fillet welds | 36* | Weld throat |
| 5 | Tee-profile flange with a full-penetration weld | 71 | Web |
| 6 | Tee-profile flange with partial or effective full penetration | 36* | Weld throat |
| 7 | Tee-profile flange with fillet welds | 36* | Weld throat |

The case applies §8(2) to the supplied unfactored two-million-cycle equivalent vertical compressive stress range. Demand is γFf × ΔσE,2 and capacity is ΔσC / γMf. It applies the separate §8(1) limit of 1.5 × fy to the frequent direct stress range. The asterisk is retained in the worked check and the case uses the published lower class of 36 MPa. The optional Figure 7.3 alternative for asterisked categories requires a different stress-life curve and is outside this equivalent-range case. The input ranges come from structural and fatigue load analysis.

The English standard, printed page 29, provides the seven categories and locations. The 2009 corrigendum does not change Table 8.10. V-509 traces the catalogue entry and V-505 tracks the remaining nominal detail tables. [EN 1993-1-9:2005](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/en.1993.1.9.2005-1.pdf#page=31) [EN 1993-1-9:2005/AC:2009](https://es.scribd.com/document/165048425/EN-1993-1-9-2005-AC2009-EN)

The language-neutral fixture covers all seven rows, inapplicability for incompatible stress locations and an unconfirmed geometry, zero demand, the fatigue boundary and a frequent-load failure. SQLite 3.54.0 reproduced all eleven fixture results using an independent SQL category lookup and numerical checks. The script takes the path to `table-8-10.json` as its argument. The production Rust evaluator, compiled in a standalone harness, passed the same eleven cases. The full workspace-backed test was still waiting on shared workspace preparation during verification.
