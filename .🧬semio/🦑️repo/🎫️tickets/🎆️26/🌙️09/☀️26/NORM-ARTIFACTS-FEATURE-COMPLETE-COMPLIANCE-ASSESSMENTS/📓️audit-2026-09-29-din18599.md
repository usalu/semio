## Blocking

- `din18599.2.cooling-need` (~L1437): limit is `q_c.max(1.0)`, so utilization cannot exceed 1. The check never fails and has no remedy.
- `din18599.1.net-floor-area` (~L1036): status follows `ΣA_zone / A_N`. The remedy is emitted only when the absolute difference exceeds 5% of A_N. A smaller overrun can Fail with no remedy.
- `din18599.geg.ht-prime` and `din18599.2.heating-demand`: U and ΔU_WB remedies are emitted only when those fields are above their reference. An aggregate Fail with every element already at the reference has no remedy.
- `din18599.geg.mean-u.*`: the remedy sets the worst element’s U to the group limit, which does not by itself bring the area-weighted mean under that limit.
- `din18599.7.cooling` (~L1459): when cooling is installed and Q_C,nd exceeds the limit, the only remedy is a window g-value. A building with no windows Fails with no remedy.
