# EN 1998 concrete and steel jackets

EN 1998-3:2005 with the corrigendum now has four editable strengthening cases. The concrete jacket case computes the four A.4.2.2 adjustments to analyst-supplied monolithic capacities. Shear resistance is multiplied by 0.90, yield chord rotation by 1.05, and the yield moment and ultimate chord rotation are carried through. The shear and ultimate rotation results precede the overall Annex A factor. The input confirms full composite action, the axial-load convention, the concrete assumption and the material basis. [EN 1998-3, A.4.1 and A.4.2.2](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/en.1998.3.2005.pdf)

The steel jacket shear case computes the A.21 addition once the elastic basis is confirmed. It uses the design yield strength and supports discrete straps or a continuous plate. The lap-splice case checks a length of at least 1.5 times the splice length, at least two bolt rows on each face perpendicular to the loading direction, and the specified row positions for a splice at the column base. [EN 1998-3, A.4.3.2 and A.4.3.3](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/en.1998.3.2005.pdf)

The FRP case covers A.4.4.2(1)–(8), including wrapped, U-shaped and side-bonded rectangular branches and the wrapped circular column. It uses the effective bond length and configuration-specific effective debonding strength. The total shear capacity is limited by the entered diagonal compression resistance, with the tighter cyclic limit applied to walls and columns with shear span ratio at most 2. The original formula images confirmed the squared strip ratio in A.22 and the nested square root in A.28. Rust and SQLite reproduced all 15 FRP computations. [EN 1998-3, A.4.4.2](https://gaprojekt.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Eurocode-8-Design-of-structures-for-earthquake-resistance.pdf)

The production Rust jacket formulas and SQLite matched all 23 jacket vectors. Native editor integration and Play delivery remain pending. AS-09 keeps cyclic FRP hinge shear, confinement and splice improvement, the jacket interface, other materials and complete structural intervention verification open.
