# EN 1993 fatigue assessment method

EN 1993-1-9:2005 §3 provides damage-tolerant and safe-life assessments. Case C93-32 records the selected method, the assessment of rapid failure following local cracking, the lifetime inspection and maintenance programme with its reference, and the basis for claiming damage tolerance. It checks evidence of load redistribution or assessed EN 1993-1-10 material selection with planned maintenance. A rapid failure route directs the appraisal to safe life. A new case starts undecided. [EN 1993-1-9 §3](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/en.1993.1.9.2005.pdf)
