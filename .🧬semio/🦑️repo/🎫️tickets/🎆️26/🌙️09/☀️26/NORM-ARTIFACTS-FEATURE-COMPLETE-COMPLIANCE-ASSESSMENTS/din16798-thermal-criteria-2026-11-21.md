# DIN EN 16798-1 thermal design criteria

The live seasonal temperature checks compare winter against a heating minimum and summer against a cooling maximum. Tables B.2 and NA.3 describe reference design temperatures for the specified use, typical clothing insulation and activity. EN living rooms have winter minimums of 18 °C and 16 °C in categories III and IV. Comparable seated offices have 19 °C and 18 °C. The narrower table for other dwelling rooms needs an explicit room profile. German applicability depends on the dwelling-unit area. [DIN official preview, Table NA.3](https://www.normsplash.com/Samples/DIN/366818232/DIN-EN-16798-1-2022-de.pdf) · [DIN publication, EN Table B.2 and Figure B.1](https://de.scribd.com/document/1071290161/)

The free-running EN check and diagram use Figure B.1. Category I is −3/+2 K from the central comfort temperature, category II −4/+3 K and category III −5/+4 K. The running mean must exceed 10 °C and stay below 30 °C. This summer and shoulder-season method presumes primarily sedentary occupants who can adapt their clothing and control windows in a building without active cooling. Below the lower limit the remedy raises the operative temperature. The German NA.4.1 requires hourly temperatures and evidence of occupied periods. A running mean cannot supply that assessment.
