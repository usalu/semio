# DIN EN 16798 inspection age

EN 16798-17:2017 §1 excludes mandatory inspection frequency from the scope of the standard and refers to national decisions. CEN/TR 16798-18:2017 §5.2.1.1 gives a five-year default for planning inspections as non-mandatory guidance and lists factors that can change the interval. DIN EN 16798-17:2019-09 added a normative German annex. NA.1 refers to the applicable national energy-saving rules and identifies DIN SPEC 15240 as inspection guidance. The app previously reported three or five years as a mandatory limit attributed to DIN EN 16798-3 §8.1 and suggested resetting the record. The report now retains the entered elapsed years as information with the selected standards context. This keeps a four-year-old central record and a six-year-old decentral record visible without asserting compliance or a legal due date. The legal schedule, on-site measurements, and the formal Part 17 inspection report still require coverage.
