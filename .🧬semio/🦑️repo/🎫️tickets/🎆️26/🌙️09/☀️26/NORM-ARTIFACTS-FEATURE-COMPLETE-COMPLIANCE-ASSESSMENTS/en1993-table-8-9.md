# EN 1993-1-9 Table 8.9 fatigue of open stringers

C93-19 and C93-20 cover both details in Table 8.9. C93-19 uses the direct stress range at the stringer-to-cross-girder connection and selects class 80 MPa when the plate thickness t marked in the detail drawing is at most 12 mm, or 71 MPa above 12 mm. The user confirms the detail geometry and stress location.

C93-20 checks the continuous open stringer connection at the cross-girder web against class 56 MPa. It combines the direct and shear stress ranges for the same governing fatigue load event using Δσeq = ½(Δσ + √(Δσ² + 4Δτ²)). The calculation then applies the supplied damage equivalence and impact factor λΦ₂ and the fatigue partial factors. The case reports the independent frequent direct and shear stress limits, and includes the supplied utilization for the separate EN 1993-2 check between stringers. The two user-entered web stress ranges come from analysis of the net section for the governing load event. For that analysis Table 8.9 gives Δσ = ΔMₛ/Wₙₑₜ,ₛ and Δτ = ΔVₛ/Aw,net,s. EN 1993-2 supplies the bridge load-analysis and damage-equivalence procedure in §9.4 and §9.5.
