# EN 1991 German fire load and reliability

E12.03.1 uses DIN EN 1991-1-2/NA:2015-09 Annex BB.5 to derive the design factors for fire load and heat release. It applies the selected floor-area or representative-average approach from Table BB.3, with an option to take the more adverse probability where required by applicable technical building rules. It includes the applicable response method, automatic suppression and the annual structural target from Tables BB.4 and BB.5. The result reports the annual damaging-fire probability, conditional failure probability, reliability index and both partial factors. [DIN EN 1991-1-2/NA §§BB.5.1–BB.5.3](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/140002/Nds._MBl._Nr._3_2019_vom_24.01.2019_Anlagenband_1-Teil_1.pdf#page=42) · [Technical building rules](https://www.umwelt.niedersachsen.de/download/157798/VVTB_Nds.-MBl__Fassung_Juni_2020.pdf)

E12.03 uses the applicable factor to calculate the design fire load from the classified occupancy data in Table BB.1 or a project survey. The separately identified EN Annex E 80th-percentile rows were checked against the 2002 edition and recorded as verified for the EN basis. Their German registry values remain unset. The German parametric gas temperature still requires the Annex AA model. [German Annex BB](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/140002/Nds._MBl._Nr._3_2019_vom_24.01.2019_Anlagenband_1-Teil_1.pdf) · [EN 1991-1-2:2002 Table E.4](https://www.phd.eng.br/wp-content/uploads/2015/12/en.1991.1.2.2002.pdf)

The production BB.5 evaluator matched SQLite and SciPy on 157 scenarios covering the eight use rows, all suppression cases, brigade response ranges, failure groups, and unavailable conditional cases. The default demo uses the office mean when it exceeds the calculated area-based probability and reports the factors for both design inputs. A separate language-neutral document fixture covered 21 EN and German annex fire outcomes. The full native test run remains pending.
