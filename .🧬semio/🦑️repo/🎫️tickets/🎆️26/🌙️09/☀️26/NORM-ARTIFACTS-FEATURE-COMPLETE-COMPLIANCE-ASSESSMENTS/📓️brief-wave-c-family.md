2. **Real clauses**: every part the family claims is reached by `evaluate()`; every check is a real clause/equation/table implementation with correct clause ids; no hardcoded value stands in for an input; no surrogate/simplified model where the norm gives the method; DE national annex values implemented where they differ from EN recommended values (and selected by the subject's annex field); checks are applicability-gated (NotApplicable with localized reason when the subject has nothing to check), and every check can both pass and fail.

5. **All facets** updated schema-first (Rust, TS, GraphQL, JSON Schema, proto; snapshot, diff, mutations; binary/text grammar leaves; `🔣️.json` is the parity anchor).
6. **Examples**: at least (a) one realistic fully compliant subject and (b) one realistic non-compliant subject with multiple failures across parts; both in `📚️examples` + `🖼️assets` (dsl + pack) and selectable via `setActiveExample`; the default snapshot is a meaningful realistic subject (not tuned-to-pass trivia).
