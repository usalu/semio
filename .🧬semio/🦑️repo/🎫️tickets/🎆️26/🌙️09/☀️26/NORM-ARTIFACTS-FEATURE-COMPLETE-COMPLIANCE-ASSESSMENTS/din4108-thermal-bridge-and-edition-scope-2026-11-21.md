# DIN 4108 thermal-bridge rates and edition coverage

A flat rate of 0.03 W/(m²·K) requires documented category B equivalence for every relevant junction. When the documentation establishes at least category A throughout the envelope, the rate is 0.05 W/(m²·K). A mixture of A and B therefore uses 0.05. The code uses the general 0.10 reference if documentation is absent or a junction remains detailed. The special flat rate for internal insulation with intersecting solid slabs and the extended equivalence method still need separate inputs. The report compares the detailed contribution to this reference and shows the declared category without certifying a junction's geometry. [KfW Technical FAQ §§7.02–7.05](https://www.kfw.de/PDF/Download-Center/F%C3%B6rderprogramme-%28Inlandsf%C3%B6rderung%29/PDF-Dokumente/6000004865_Infoblatt_BEG_TFAQ_Effizienzhaus.pdf)
