# DIN EN 16798 indoor environment and ventilation coverage

The trace maps the current design checks to indoor temperature, comfort, air quality, light and acoustics. The German edition implements EN 16798-1:2019. German heating-system sizing uses DIN EN 12831-1 and its national annex. The comfort inputs and heating-load design temperatures require distinct applicability checks. The full categorisation, occupancy profiles and national settings still require their own verified coverage. [DIN EN 16798-1:2022-03](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-16798-1/349622591)

DIN EN 16798-3:2026-10 adopts EN 16798-3:2025 and supersedes the German 2017 edition. The current checks reach the earlier ventilation model. Applying the 2026 publication requires separate evaluation of revised filtration, heat recovery, leakage and fan performance rules. The 5-2 distribution and generation procedure and the complete Part 17 inspection procedure also require implementation and worked examples. [DIN EN 16798-3:2026-10](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-16798-3/390958579) · [DIN EN 16798-5-2:2017-11](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-16798-5-2/256578235) · [DIN EN 16798-17:2019-09](https://www.dinmedia.de/de/norm/din-en-16798-17/307217372)

DIN EN 16798-1:2022-03 NA.5.1.1 and EN 16798-1:2019 B.3.1.1 establish a hygienic design minimum of 4 L/s per person for occupied non-residential zones under complete mixing. The airflow and system-capacity checks apply this limit using the entered design occupancy. See [the worked cases and source assessment](din16798-hygienic-airflow-2026-11-21.md).

DIN EN 16798-17:2019-09 NA.1 and EN 16798-17:2017 §1 distinguish the inspection method from the nationally set inspection schedule. The report shows the recorded age and the applicable standards context. See [the inspection scope assessment](din16798-inspection-age-2026-11-21.md).

- VE-22 EN 16798-1:2019 Annex B.3.1.5 and B.3.2.5 document the unoccupied operating regime and assess purge or setback separately from occupied airflow. The German applicability remains unverified.
