# DIN V 18599 and GModG monthly renewable electricity

For each month, GModG §23 limits renewable electricity credited toward primary energy to the lesser of qualified electricity generation and eligible electricity demand. The credited services are electric heating, hot water, ventilation, cooling and auxiliary loads. Built-in lighting is included for non-residential buildings. The annual credit is the sum of monthly amounts. KfW technical FAQ 12.04 gives a primary energy factor of 1.8 and defines the calculation of the qualified PV yield. The live balance reuses the actual twelve heating and cooling months. It apportions the available annual constant loads by calendar days, excludes residential lighting from this energy balance, and shows estimated generation, eligible electricity and credited electricity per month. Recorded solar thermal energy is surfaced as heat requiring allocation to thermal services. The primary-energy flow shows its net screening value after the modelled credit.

The statutory PV yield requires the applicable Potsdam radiation tables, Annex B module technology and year, mounting conditions, degradation and relevant shade. The present editable efficiency and area inputs produce an estimate on project climate data. Results that depend on those renewable inputs surface an unverified basis. The annual PV area remedy was removed because increasing the array alone may leave the result above the primary-energy limit once each month reaches its cap.
