# DIN EN 16798 ventilation outside occupied periods

EN 16798-1:2019 separates building emissions during unoccupied periods from sizing during occupancy. The issued non-residential Annex B default has two paths in B.3.1.5. For a shut-off system, provide one ventilated zone volume in the two hours before occupancy. For reduced operation, deliver 0.15 litres per second per square metre to each room. For residential reduced operation, B.3.2.5 gives 0.10 to 0.15 litres per second per square metre according to the dwelling and occupancy. These are Annex B defaults. The design-time provisions still require a stated occupancy schedule and a pollutant basis. [EN 16798-1:2019 §6.3 and Annex B](https://www.scribd.com/document/1060733568/BS-EN-16798-1-2019-2025-09-15-01-30-00-PM)

The DIN-hosted technical report draft gives a different example clearance of two volumes. The issued 2019 product standard governs the Annex B fixtures. The German National Annex was verified separately for occupied ventilation. The available source does not establish the German applicability or numerical requirements for these periods. [CEN/TR 16798-2 draft](https://www.din.de/resource/blob/66392/911886c68e3d0917dc03085dcc35f144/tr-16789-2-draft-data.pdf) [DIN EN 16798-1 edition](https://www.dinmedia.de/en/standard/din-en-16798-1/349622591)
