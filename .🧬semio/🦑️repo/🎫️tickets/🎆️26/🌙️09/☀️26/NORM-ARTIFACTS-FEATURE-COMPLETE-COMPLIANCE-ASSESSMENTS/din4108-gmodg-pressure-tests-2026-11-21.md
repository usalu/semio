# DIN 4108 airtightness pressure tests

GModG §26 permits a measured airtightness rate to feed the annual primary-energy assessment when the pre-completion test follows DIN EN ISO 9972:2018-12 Annex NA and both pressure directions meet the applicable limit. The calculation compares the two recorded airflows at 50 Pa separately. It applies 3.0 or 1.5 h⁻¹ to conditioned air volume, or 4.5 or 2.5 m³/(h·m²) to envelope area when conditioned air volume exceeds 1,500 m³. The area method is an available alternative above that threshold. [GModG §26](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__26.html)

The DIN 4108 catalogue includes four worked checks for a whole-building volume assessment, the envelope-area alternative, separate non-residential zone assessment, and a documented sample of identical exterior-access units. Zone results require the stated §26(4) eligibility. The sample check requires the project team to establish the Annex NB sampling basis. The production evaluator returns a localized applicability result when the test evidence or measurement inputs are missing. A sample's numerical result does not establish that the sampling protocol was followed. [GModG §26(4)–(5)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__26.html)

`check-din4108-pressure-tests.py` verified 23 language-neutral cases against SQLite and the production Rust evaluator. The typed JSON schema was validated against the cases. The native catalogue test exercises both pressure rows and changes one measurement to a failure. The editor projection test checks that the new verification table reaches the catalogue render route. The four catalogue entries can create editable pressure-test rows in a DIN 4108 document. New rows start with an unconfirmed test protocol and blank measurements. A localized inspector exposes the inputs, and the live evaluation tracks each row. A failing measurement carries instructions to seal the leakage paths and repeat the test. The remedy cannot overwrite a recorded reading. The compliant and failing demo documents include measured examples.
