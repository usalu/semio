# GModG primary-energy factors

The catalogue lists all sixteen Annex 4 rows, including the CHP heat entry that requires Method B. The calculation applies the baseline non-renewable factors where the supplied carrier has a direct value. Network heat and plant-specific cases show an unverified basis in the live report. The model also recognises grid electricity for heat pumps in the monthly renewable electricity calculation. The separately qualified conditions for network-specific, biogenic and CHP factors remain open. [GModG §22](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/__22.html) · [Annex 4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/geg/anlage_4.html)

All sixteen rows matched the independent SQLite lookup. The compiled Rust comparison also passed the grid heat pump, district heating and unrecognised carrier cases. Browser validation requires a fresh signed component.
