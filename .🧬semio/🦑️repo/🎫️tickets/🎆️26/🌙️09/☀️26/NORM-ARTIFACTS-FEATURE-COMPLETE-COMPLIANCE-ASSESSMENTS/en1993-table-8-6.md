# EN 1993-1-9 Table 8.6 hollow-section details

The C93-26 catalogue case covers the nine details for hollow-section walls up to 12.5 mm. The result selects the tabulated fatigue category and applies the 45° dividing angle for slotted tubes, the thickness increase above 8 mm where the table permits it, the 10% weld reinforcement limit, the 100 mm attachment width limit, and the 8 mm maximum thickness for the two fillet-welded endplate details. Detail 1 requires a diameter strictly below 200 mm. Fabrication and inspection requirements and applicable stress locations must be confirmed from the project evidence. [Table 8.6](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80810/nds._mbl._nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_anlagenband_37i.pdf#page=283)

For the slotted tube the direct section check is accompanied by the weld-throat shear check from Table 8.5 detail 8, category 80 MPa with m = 5. The frequent shear range has its own §8(1) limit. The inputs are the resulting project stress ranges and the steel yield stress. Member-force stress analysis and the other attachment details in Table 8.4 remain separate open provisions. [Table 8.5](https://www.niedersachsen.de/download/80810/nds._mbl._nr._37_2012_vom_30.10.2012_anlagenband_37i.pdf#page=281)
