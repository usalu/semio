## Live defect: owner-level fail-first law

The product repair belongs in:

- [Drawing retained-owner service](../../../../../../✏️s/🔌️plugins/🖍️draw/🗿️artifacts/🖍️drawing/🏅️standards/🔖️1/🪆️subsets/✳️any/🧬️schema/🧰️owned/🦀️.rs)
- [Retained mutation authority tests](../../../../../../✏️s/🔌️plugins/🖍️draw/🗿️artifacts/🖍️drawing/🏅️standards/🔖️1/🪆️subsets/✳️any/🧬️schema/🧰️owned/🧪️tests/🔬️retained-mutation-authority/🦀️.rs)

The fail-first law is:

> For every source snapshot accepted by the declared document bound and every mutation whose individual owned fields are within their page bound, the mutation candidate must obtain a reservation that accounts for all fixed retained backing before lifecycle work begins. It must either apply and return a terminally retired owner, or reject only when the declared aggregate document bound is actually exceeded; a valid 4,096-byte rename field must not fail because the service double-counts its source/candidate and fixed backing against the unrelated envelope-decode ceiling. A 4,097-byte field must still reject with `drawing-store.mutation-field-capacity` and return the exact source owner unchanged.

This requires one explicit product budget contract rather than merely increasing a constant: define whether the source plus candidate duplication and fixed arena pages consume a mutation-specific aggregate, then size/reserve that aggregate consistently with the maximum accepted Drawing document. The existing aggregate test establishes the intended 4 KiB field boundary ([authority test: 1020-1077](../../../../../../✏️s/🔌️plugins/🖍️draw/🗿️artifacts/🖍️drawing/🏅️standards/🔖️1/🪆️subsets/✳️any/🧬️schema/🧰️owned/🧪️tests/🔬️retained-mutation-authority/🦀️.rs#L1020)); it currently fails before that law is evaluated.

After the exact-boundary law is green, re-enable the seven blocked cases for cancel/stale, false-terminal close, duplicate framing/name, all fourteen variants, reorder rollback, and phase rollback. Their current Drop failure is secondary to the earlier admission rejection, not evidence of a second independent defect.
