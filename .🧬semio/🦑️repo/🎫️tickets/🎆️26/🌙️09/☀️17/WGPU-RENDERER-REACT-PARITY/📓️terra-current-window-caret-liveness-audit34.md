### Required invariant

Persist a presenter predicate such as `awaiting_runtime` that is true *only* for a pending `Render` or `Acknowledge` cursor whose `input_wait` is `InteractionCheckout`. Then park all frame-owner self-requeues while that predicate is true:

```text
continue_frame = !awaiting_runtime
  && (cursor_wake || component_close || text || world3d || settle
      || ready_applies || live_frame_build || presenter_runnable)
```

`component_close` must be gated too. It is not an independent runnable owner while the presenter blocks frame admission. So must `cursor_wake`: it is an input-facing directive, but it cannot admit a frame behind this pending present. `ready_applies` and `live_frame_build` are particularly misleading in this state: they can remain true, but apply pumping/admission cannot advance the checkout.

Do **not** park retirement, ordinary `Pending`, `RuntimeLock`, `SceneIntent`, a retained fault, or acknowledgement work. They need present turns or a diagnostic path. The stall watchdog should reset/exclude only the precise live checkout predicate; it must retain fault detection for the other non-progressing cursors.
