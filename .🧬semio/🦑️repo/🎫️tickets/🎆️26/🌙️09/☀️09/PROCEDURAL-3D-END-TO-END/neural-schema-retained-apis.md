# Neural Schema Retained APIs

- `🧠️neural/⚙️engine/📔️registry/🦀️.rs` owns `SchemaComponent` and `schema_component_info`; its current `construct`, `deconstruct`, `modify`, and `success_output` use cold dictionary construction and clones. `Registry` stores each generated component with its schema and has `OperatorPlanCursor` and `OperatorFinishCursor` for the retained evaluator.
- `🧱️model/⚙️operator-record/🦀️.rs` defines `Operator::step_plan`, `OperatorPlanAdmission`, and `OperatorJob`: budgeted `step`, demand queries, progress, cancellation, paid `close_step`, and `terminal_is_empty`.
- `📖️lookup/🦀️.rs` provides `OriginalDictionaryLookup`: grant-bounded `step`, exact source/root/needle identity, and borrowed `result`; it avoids cloning the source value.
- `📥️input/🦀️.rs` provides `BudgetedOwnedEntry::{new,from_shared_value,from_shared}`, `next_demands`, `step`, `next_close_demands`, `close_step`, and `terminal_is_empty`. It moves original dictionary/entry ownership through `OriginalOwnedEntry`; `BudgetedInputMerge` has similar quote/step/take/close custody for merging roots.
- `🧱️model/📚️dictionary/🦀️.rs` exposes `Dictionary::entry_at_rank`, `value_shared_at_rank`, `original_root_identity`, `iter`, and `into_retirement`. `ValueRetirement::from_dictionary` and retained-clone machinery retire nested owners. `ColdDictionaryBuilder` and synchronous `Dictionary::insert` are explicitly cold-only.
- `🧱️model/🏷️schema-model/🦀️.rs` contains owned `Schema`/`FieldSpec` and `SchemaRef`; `Registry::schema_refs` clones metadata for a cold result. No dedicated borrowed `SchemaComponent` retained job or field-builder was found in the neural engine search. Existing laws for modes and errors are in `🧪️tests/🔬️unit/📚️cardinality-and-schema-component-laws/🦀️.rs`; retained lookup/input ownership laws are in `🧪️tests/🚦️owned-controls/🦀️.rs` and `🧵️retirement/🧪️tests/🧵️retirement/🦀️.rs`.
