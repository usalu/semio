# Exact Through Notch From an Offset Parallel Cylinder

The original `brep.bool.cut` route now publishes an exact through notch when an offset parallel cylinder spans both end planes of the stock and its axis lies strictly outside the stock disk. The retained stock and tool walls share two vertical edges. Each cap joins one surviving stock arc to one concave tool arc. The tool's cylindrical face is flipped for the material shell, while its native UV trim remains a positive parameter interval. The two wall and cap pairs have opposing effective coedges and matching 3D and UV evaluation.

The cap fan is certified by placing its interior point on the line of centers, inside the stock and behind every tangent to the removed tool arc. Its upper bound is the tangent at the trim endpoint closest in angle to either side of the tool chord. A nonempty interval exists for this family when the tool center lies outside the stock and the two circles cross. The funded preview samples the native caps and walls from that same recipe and keeps the native face, edge and vertex labels. Exact Area uses the absolute arc lengths. Exact Volume subtracts the tool segment from the surviving stock segment.

The offset route also returns stock on zero-volume contact or separation, reports an empty result when a larger cutter spans and contains the stock, and trims one axial end when the cutter contains the complete radial footprint. A partially overlapping in-stock tool axis, a sealed offset bore, and a cut that would separate two bodies require their own exact boundary owners.

Three neutral witness cases cover equal and unequal through notches and an offset enclosing tool. All three Manifold-3D tests passed with 59 assertions.
