## Audit findings fixed

- `validate` reported the whole Body: `Brep::validate_sync` (`⚙️engine/🦀️.rs:1309`) now returns `validity_sync(shape)` as the same JSON (`ok`, `issueCount`, `issues`), scoped by reachability, for solids, shells, faces, compounds, etc. The label-reach rule moved from the engine into `queries::validation::issue_reaches` (`✅validation/🦀️.rs:400`); the gate (`validate_gate_sync`) and the new `validity` share it. Test: a flipped face in one box leaves the sibling box `ok`.
- `watertightness_stub_unchecked` and `WatertightnessVerdict::NotChecked` deleted from `📏mass-properties/🦀️.rs` oracle (and its test assertion; no other caller existed). The real verdict is `manifold_report` / `Brep::watertightness_sync`. It skips point (degenerate) edges and compares `forward != flipped` per edge, the validator's own rule.
- **Existing mass integrator was wrong for centroid and inertia of curved solids** (found by writing the oracle first): `integrate_triangle_adaptive` judged convergence by the volume alone, so a cylinder's centroid stayed off its axis by 7.5e-4 and a torus's axial inertia converged to 594.6 instead of 577.37 at any tolerance. Fix in `📏mass-properties/🦀️.rs`: every one of the 14 moment components (11 old + 3 new area-moment components) is refined against its own accumulated absolute magnitude, with the loop's budget (`tol` of that magnitude) shared between triangles by parametric area (`loop_moments` :725, `integrate_triangle_adaptive` :678). Straight UV p-curves are sampled once per pi/4 of `u` in the quadrature path only (`loop_uv_polygon_with`, :1098); the validator's polygon is untouched (changing it globally made a boolean fail validation with `outer-loop-winding-inverted`). Result at tol 1e-6: cylinder 1.0 s, cone 1.2 s, torus 1.0 s (torus was 130 s before). New public helper `solid_mass_with_area_centroid` (:164).

## Gaps and risks

- `validity` runs the whole-body validator and filters by reachability, so one call costs the whole arena; a scoped validator would be the fix.
- `shape_distance` is sampled (12 samples per edge, both ways, edge-to-edge) and then polished by alternating exact projections; exact for convex pairs, only up to sampling for non-convex ones. Pruning uses `face_aabb` lower bounds; the solid `FaceBvh` is not used because scopes are not always solids. Overlapping solids report 0 with a vertex of A as both witness points.
- `bounding_box` is exact for lines, circles, ellipses, spheres and tori (closed-form extrema kept when on the trimmed face) and for ruled cylinder/cone faces; NURBS curves (sampled and refined) and NURBS faces (tessellated at 1e-3 of the diagonal) set `exact = false`.
- Mass: the planar cap of a circular edge is a chord polygon, so cap area and volume are low by about `tol` relative (cylinder 4.5e-7 at tol 1e-6); fast paths stay exact for box and sphere. A compound sums its solids (overlaps are not merged). An open shell reports area only (`volumetric = None`). Principal axes of a repeated eigenvalue are an arbitrary orthonormal pair.
- Mesh: `aspect_ratio` of a polygon is its worst fan triangle; duplicate vertices use a `1e-6` diagonal tolerance; `boundary_loops` unions boundary edges by shared vertex (a pinched boundary is one loop).
- Boolean defect found, not mine: `intersect(sphere r=2, box x in [0,3], y,z in [-3,3])` (cut plane through both poles) returns a sphere face of area 75.35 (1.5 times the whole sphere) and volume 50.24 (3 times the hemisphere), and `validate_body` reports no issue; `volume_sync` agrees, so the face trim itself is wrong. The z-slab hemisphere is correct and is what the test uses.
- `brep::engine` suite: 4 `operation_jobs::tests::*` (lane J, fixture output counts / sampled cancel) failed during my last full engine run; they belong to the in-progress jobs lane. `a_boolean_leaves_both_of_its_input_solids_alive_for_the_next_evaluation` passes (701 s under fleet load).
- No flow node or `BrepKernel` trait method was added; `brep.measure.validate` now answers per shape automatically. Wiring `analyze_sync` into the `analysis` inference (AD4) is lane Y.
