## Remaining user-facing boundaries

- Bevel explicitly rejects any globally nonconvex source and any mesh with any boundary edge, even when the selected edge is an ordinary manifold edge in an otherwise valid open or concave model. See `🧰️framework/🔨️modules/🧊️3d/🥽️mesh/🛠️modeling/📉️bevel-and-decimate/🦀️.rs`: global vertex-against-all-face-plane convexity check and all-halfedge twin check precede selection-specific profile. Its Flow summary accurately says closed convex, so this is a declared limitation rather than misleading registration.
- Bridge source now walks two directed disjoint boundary loops, and the current live implementation advances their relative counts with triangles/quads; unlike the older equal-loop-only report, it no longer has an equal-count rejection. It still explicitly rejects ambiguous/open/intersecting loops and folded or degenerate constructed walls. Its current unequal-loop behavior has not been independently tested in this audit; do not describe it as verified by the older report.
- Loop Cut is a connected quad-strip operation, Knife Cut is one projected face, and Inset is per-face. They are useful focused edits, not arbitrary multi-face cutting or region inset. Bevel's global clipping approach and these operations do not provide a general polygon mesh Boolean, edge slide, boundary-edge extrusion, or subdivision-surface smoothing. No such operations were found among the current Flow 55 or public `MeshModelingOps` signatures inspected; this is a source inventory, not an exhaustive industry feature taxonomy.
- Authored indexed UV unwrap, selected Weld, seam-preserving dissolve, normals/UV-preserving edit paths, Combine assets and GLB sparse metadata are recent implemented work; do not re-report their former blanket refusals. Preservation remains operation/input-specific, with focused rather than full linked verification.
