## Imported Blind Circular Bore

The cold Procedural widget now accepts the wall and selected interior floor of a circular blind bore. The native proof verifies the six stock faces. It authenticates one full circular opening on one stock cap, verifies the selected floor is strictly internal and faces into the bore, and requires the cylinder to share the exact rim edge and opposite effective coedge use with both. This covers either open direction and copied faces whose effective normal reverses the original support frame. Mixed and partial selections, a reversed floor, and foreign members are refused.

The standalone witness passed both blind directions with flipped floor faces and both member orders. It also preserved the one- and two-through-bore checks. Manifold passed four tests and thirty-two assertions covering the same blind dimensions and expected stock. The compiler source preflight passed all four targets at 3,988 registry lines and 1,447 solid-topology lines. Scoped whitespace and both registered fixture paths checked cleanly.

The native test builds each blind geometry with the real Boolean. The registered Procedural test builds the cut, exports it as a shape value, dispatches the widget with a fresh import at fuel 1, rejects one-face selections and checks the restored stock volume and area. Those two linked tests have not run during compiler storage reclaim.

Complete canonical rounded-box fillets, imported single or double through bores, and single blind bores are supported. Retained Flow supports single through or blind bores. Enclosed bores, isolated chamfers, arbitrary surfaces, frame-placed rounded boxes, and partial or mixed selections remain outside these certificates. Authentic rounded boundaries with a noncanonical face or ring ordering still require a correspondence implementation.
