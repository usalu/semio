# Exact Retained Annular Area and Center of Mass

Original `brep.measure.area` and `brep.measure.centerOfMass` now read the exact annular result when a retained construction or cut publishes an annular certificate. This covers coaxial and eccentric through bores, both blind directions, enclosed cylindrical voids, one- and two-shoulder stepped cylinders, and full or partial annular Revolve.

The Area calculation sums the cylindrical wall spans, the exposed planar rings or disks, and the radial closing faces of a partial sweep. Center of Mass integrates the first moment directly in the native frame. For a bore, it subtracts the true axially clipped and laterally displaced cavity. A stepped shape adds the annular shoulder band's moment. The sector formula includes the sweep's angular first moment and uses the signed world frame. All calculations have a fixed number of operations and use the existing measurement routes. The certificate and preview mesh remain separate geometry representations.

The neutral Boolean corpus now states the centroid for all applicable coaxial and eccentric rows. Its independent oracle computes moments from signed tetrahedra of the Manifold-3D mesh. Three.js independently checks the signed world placement and centroid of the full, positive-partial and negative-partial Revolve fixtures. The two oracle suites passed all 12 tests with 378 assertions. Seventeen Rust source tests passed, including the exact Section, annular mass properties and earlier topology and trim cases. The registered original Boolean and Solid fixtures invoke Area, Volume and Center of Mass on the retained outputs. No competing linked test was launched while the official shared-store build was active.

Rust syntax and scoped whitespace checks passed. Native source closure remains 3,999 active Engine lines and 3,998 registry lines. The original Boolean test has 900 active lines and 108,465 bytes. The original Solid test has 2,279 active lines and 233,974 bytes.
