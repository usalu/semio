# One-Shoulder Coaxial Cylinder Fuse

The registered original `brep.bool.fuse` now accepts exactly coaxial cylinders of unequal radii when the smaller cylinder extends beyond one end of the larger cylinder. The inputs can overlap axially or meet across the whole smaller cap. The Boolean proof chooses the larger stock independent of operand order and preserves the signed direction of the extension. A smaller cylinder wholly contained by the larger still uses the existing containment result. Cylinders separated by an axial gap require separate output bodies and remain refused by this single-solid Fuse route. A smaller cylinder extending beyond both ends needs two shoulders and also remains outside this recipe. The exact coaxial check retains the existing requirement that the shared axis be representable by the input frames.
