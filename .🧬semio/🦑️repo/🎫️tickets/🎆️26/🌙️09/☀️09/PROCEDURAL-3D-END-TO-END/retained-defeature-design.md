# Retained Defeature Design Audit

Read-only source inspection, 2026-11-21. Flow declares `brep.solid.defeature` with a solid `geometry` and selected `faces` list, outputting a solid (`🧾️register-operators/🧮️booleans-transforms-and-solid-features/🦀️.rs:267-275`). Its cold `evaluate` reads these and calls `kernel.defeature`; unlike adjacent retained operators, Defeature supplies immediate admission but no retained geometry job (`🧲️features-intersect-evaluate-and-topology/🦀️.rs:85-101`). The cold operation extracts the source and face handles into a private body before `DefeatureJob` (`⚙️engine/⏱️operation-jobs/🦀️.rs:312-319`).

`DefeatureJob` is not a sound general solid repair: it checks membership and a minimum count, then tries sewing only if a removed face has exactly two coplanar kept neighbours, discards any sewing error, and removes selected faces from the outer shell (`🛠️operations/🧵️sew/🦀️.rs:168-224`). The existing box-face test only checks the resulting count is five (`🧪️tests/🔬️heal/🦀️.rs:39-46`), which leaves the box open. The operation-jobs fixture `defeature-hull-smallest-face` checks outputs, face count, volume and area but does not establish closed-manifold repair. Its metrics must not be treated as a geometrically correct defeaturing oracle.

Concrete repair target: undo a single straight edge chamfer on an otherwise unmodified axis-aligned box, with exactly the chamfer face selected. Example: box [0,2]³ with one edge chamfered by 0.5; replacing the chamfer face with extensions of its two neighbouring orthogonal stock planes restores the original box, volume 8, area 24 and bounds [0,0,0]–[2,2,2]. Certify the entire actual source boundary and the selected face membership, its adjacency and the unambiguous corner/stock reconstruction before accepting; reject arbitrary face selections, changed stock or ambiguous topology. Do not reconstruct a bounding box solely from bounds, or remove the face and return an open shell. Validate with a language-agnostic fixture and independent Manifold box oracle, including source immutability, two oppositely oriented coedge uses per edge, cancellation, unfunded progress, invalid or foreign selection, and no premature publication.

There is a plausible funded backbone, not a general ready-made clone: `plan_box_finish` produces a one-edge chamfer boundary from a certified box (`⚙️engine/🧠️native-registry/🧊️solid-publication/🦀️.rs:29-69`); one-edge chamfer is encoded as a `PrismBoundary` (`🏗️solid-transaction/🧰️box-finish/🦀️.rs:23-24`). However publication assigns a generic `PlanarBoundary(None)` provenance to Prism and ChamferedBox (`🏗️solid-transaction/🦀️.rs:685`), so the original stock and selected feature cannot currently be assumed available from provenance. Either certify against live topology incrementally or preserve an explicit trustworthy feature certificate at creation, including a verifiable face identity. Once certified, `plan_solid_primitive` / `plan_solid_boundary`, `step_solid_primitive_topology`, `reserve_solid_primitive` and `publish_solid_primitive` (`🧊️solid-publication/🦀️.rs:16-17,92-99,169-196`) stage and publish an independent repaired solid under grants. For general defeaturing, build a retained, cancellable topology traversal and selection/adjacency certificate; stage intersection and extension of surviving support surfaces, rebuild loops/coedges and validate closed oriented shell before atomic publication, with explicit refusals for unimplemented surface families. The cold `Body::extract` and `DefeatureJob` alone do not supply funded per-item clone and correct healing semantics.

No code changed and no tests run.
