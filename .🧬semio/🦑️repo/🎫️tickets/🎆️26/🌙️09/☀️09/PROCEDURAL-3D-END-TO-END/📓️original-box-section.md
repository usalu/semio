# Original certified Box Section

The retained Section node admits a certified box and finite plane, then clips one stock edge per granted turn. It orders the three to six crossings toward the requested normal and feeds them individually into the existing point-backed polygon face cursor. The completed face enters the live registry when the original output is ready. The response preserves the registered `faces` list and contains one real planar face with independent picking labels.

The route explicitly refuses planes that miss the box, have a zero normal, or come within kernel tolerance of a box vertex. This covers axial and oblique cuts with unambiguous crossings.

The registered Flow fixture verifies the single list member, plane support and winding, world bounds, exact area, preview references, refusal and cancellation with no new live handle. Manifold and Three.js independently verified the reversed axial rectangle, central oblique hexagon and oblique corner triangle with all 27 assertions passing.
