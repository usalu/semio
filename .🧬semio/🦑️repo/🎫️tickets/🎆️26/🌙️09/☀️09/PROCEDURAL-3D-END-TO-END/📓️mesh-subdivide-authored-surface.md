# Retained Subdivision Surface Fidelity

The previous native Subdivide build replaced a submitted vertex normal [0, 0.6, 0.8] with the polygon rebuild normal [0, 0, 1]. The rebuild also omitted raw corner UVs and smooth face shading. The existing generic attribute path followed source provenance but could not restore those raw surface fields.

Subdivide now shares the funded refinement surface cursor. It reads the same source weights as the generated positions. Convex polygon centers accumulate one original corner per event, and concave triangle centers use their bounded weighted provenance. The resulting normal is normalized. An incomplete or zero summed raw normal leaves the generated vertex available for geometric shading fallback. UVs interpolate within their original face and a repeated convex center reuses its completed UV sample. Surviving source corners keep their exact UVs on both sides of a seam. Refined faces inherit their source shading flags and continue through the existing generic face, vertex, corner and seam remap.

The native convex fixture subdivided a smooth quad beside a flat neighbour across an authored seam. It retained the face and point attributes, produced a center UV of [0.5, 0.5] and preserved different UVs on the shared edge. The concave fixture verified weighted centers with matching raw and generic authored normal and UV channels. The two passes performed 47 and 55 funded surface turns and paid 221 and 353 copied bytes. A short backing grant performed no Work, and cancellation after 16 remap events retired 129,154 bytes without publication. The shared Triangulate route also passed native exact-copy and cancellation checks after the refactor.

The Three.js GLTFLoader matched actual native exported geometry, UVs and normals in both cases with 217 assertions. The full portable Mesh suite passed 19 tests with 4,232 assertions. The registered Flow workbench checks Construct to Subdivide, center normals and corner UVs, inherited shading and cancellation. Source parsing and repository source limits passed. The linked Flow build remains storage-gated.
