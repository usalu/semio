# Retained Section of Skew Convex Solids

The Section route now accepts a bounded convex planar solid from its original face and line-edge topology. It verifies one closed shell, reciprocal coedges, native planar faces and support-plane containment of every authored vertex. It then feeds the existing incremental Section edge cursor. The result remains an ordered native polygon suitable for exact face Measure and preview. Open shells, curved trims, nonconvex material, cavity shells, and oversized sections retain their existing typed refusal.

The compact Rust suite exercised both operand orders for two multi-support skew intersections, including reflected stock, and recovered exact planar loops. Manifold independently measured each cross-section. The registered Flow law measures the resulting faces in the normal command path. Full linked Flow execution remains pending.
