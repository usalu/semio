# Retained Planar Face Area

The registered `brep.measure.area` operation now accepts original finite planar face handles, including Section results bounded by a mix of arcs and chords. The cursor reads one authored coedge per granted turn and charges the trim arena when needed. It integrates outer and hole loops in world units and can pause or cancel through the original measurement job. Existing certified areas and polygonal solid volume use their prior entry points.

Area uses the signed endpoint chord contribution and an exact arc correction. For a circular or elliptical arc, the doubled correction is the product of the parameter radii and the sweep minus its sine. For a complete clamped rational quadratic with positive weights and equal endpoint weights, the correction integrates the weighted control triangle. The closed form covers elliptic, parabolic, and hyperbolic arcs, with a convergent series around unit weight. The curve, p-curve, authored parameter range and endpoints must agree. Other rational spans and unsupported curved trims give explicit refusals.
