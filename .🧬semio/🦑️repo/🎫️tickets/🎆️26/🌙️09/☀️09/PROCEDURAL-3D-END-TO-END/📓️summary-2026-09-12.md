## Root causes found and fixed (highlights)

1. **No extension invoke door**: the React host had no `PluginWasmHandle.invoke` and the guest dropped `Event::Request`; the ABI's request/respond seam now exists on both sides, shared by the wgpu bridge.
2. **Contributions push starved** by the `refreshUi` generation race; now an owned publisher unit; a not-contributed tick no longer re-arms.
3. **Tick storms**: `pending_effects` polled a scratch session; the retained session now owns a per-window latch; fault/invocation-in-flight never re-arm.
4. **10 s per hop**: nine infinite CSS animations on `:root` over inherited registered properties forced full-document style recalcs every frame (112 s of 129 s) — moved onto painting elements, `inherits: false`.
5. **Example switch**: `refreshUi` abandoned every pass under the eval chain (livelock) — coalesced; `No example` loaded the hex column.
6. **Long booleans killed the worker**: `evaluate` is now a budgeted resumable job with liveness heartbeats and shard/app recovery.
7. **View-state carriage**: the 248 KB contributions pack rode in the crossing view state against a 64 KiB cap — removed (nobody read it).
8. **Node-graph paint**: `render_frame` discarded its scene; adapter-less pages poisoned the canvas; surface re-keyed per boot; captions were one letter; camera never fitted.
9. **Mesh delivery** cost 5–11 round trips per solid (4 KiB gesture quota) and units-not-time budgets; the preview chain has its own 64 KiB route; `ratio` no longer lies; a kernel scaffold solid broke `Box Shell`.
10. **Dev tooling**: vite wedged on a whole-repo FSEvents stream (source-watch plugin); three plugin-module staging roots drifted (one root + loud staleness); wasm-dev `opt-level` overrides (96 → 58 MB).
