# Retained Linear Distance to Planar Faces

The registered `brep.measure.distance` operation now accepts an original line or linear edge and a finite line-bounded planar face in either order. A line contributes its full support and an edge contributes its authored finite interval. The traversal reads the original outer ring, holes, edge intervals and vertices. It validates each linear boundary against its authored endpoint records and the face plane. Curved boundary members and nonplanar surfaces give explicit refusals in this operand pairing.

The cursor considers the nearest retained line point to the supporting plane when its orthogonal foot lies on the face. It also compares exact distances to every retained boundary edge. This covers parallel lines whose projections cross the face, projected segments contained in the material or a hole, and closest points at edge and vertex limits. It uses one funded coedge step at a time. The shared cursor still publishes points for Closest Point and scalar distances for both retained vertex and linear measurements.

The 12-case neutral corpus covers interior crossings, crossings through a hole, trim exclusion, parallel projections, antiparallel authored orientation, exterior edges and vertices, and zero-width trims. Three.js independently reconstructed the holed face and minimized distance against its triangles. The compact native witness compiled the actual retained cursor and linear solver against cached geometry types. It passed all 12 cases, rejected an authored endpoint/range mismatch, and passed the existing vertex distance, nearest point, planar area, and cuboid regressions.
