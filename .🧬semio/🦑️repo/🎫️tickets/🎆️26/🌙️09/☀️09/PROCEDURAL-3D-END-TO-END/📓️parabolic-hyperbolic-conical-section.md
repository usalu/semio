Original `brep.intersect.section` now covers bounded parabolic and hyperbolic sections of certified right cones and conical frustums. The exact local conic is solved in the signed section frame projected from the apex. Finite cap levels trim the positive material branch into one quadratic arc and chord or two arcs and two chords. Parabolas use polynomial quadratic spans with unit weights. Hyperbolas use positive-weight rational quadratic spans. The native face stores both the world-space curve and its same-parameter UV trim as matching clamped degree-two NURBS. The existing funded planar contour preview accepts these bounded positive-weight trims. Section retains the same `faces` list, outward contour orientation and stable native labels.
