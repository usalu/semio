## Finding: this is a protocol migration, not a rename

The pushed framework (value, job, store, plugin) moved from the count-based close protocol to the grant-based retained-clone protocol. Plugin callers do not need `SnapshotRetirementStep` -> `RetirementStep`; they need a rewrite of every retirement state machine.

Old (what the plugins implement)                          | New (framework at c44e964)
`close_step(&mut self, max_items, max_bytes) -> Result<SnapshotRetirementStep{Pending{released_items,released_bytes},Blocked,Complete}>` | `close_step(&mut self, grant: RetainedCloneGrant) -> Result<RetainedCloneStep{Progress(p),Complete(p)}>` (5-currency grant, receipts must `fit`)
`ArtifactOwnedValueRetirementFactory::retire_owned(&f, v) -> Box<dyn ErasedSnapshotRetirement>` | `retire_owned(&f, v, grant) -> Result<(Box<..>, RetainedCloneProgress), (ValueError, T)>` plus `retirement_birth_bytes`
`InteractiveJob::close_step(max_items, max_bytes) -> JobPayloadCloseStep` | `close_step(RetainedCloneGrant) -> InteractiveJobCloseStep{Pending{progress},Blocked,Complete{progress},Refused{kind,progress}}` plus 4 `next_close_*_demand` methods and `borrow_outcome`
`owned_retirement(v)` | `admit_owned_retirement(v, grant) -> Result<(Box<..>, Progress), (ValueError, T)>`
`ArtifactStoreOneItemPreparation::close_step(grant) -> Result<SnapshotRetirementStep>` | `-> Result<RetainedCloneStep>` plus 4 `next_close_*_demand` methods; factory gains `begin_demand`, `begin(request, grant)` returns `(Box, Progress)`; every field becomes `ManuallyDrop<Option<..>>` with a terminal-witness `Drop`; payload types need `RetireOwned`.
