# 2026-10-10 Play Lanes (G2, G3)

## Root cause

The computed cover (`playActivationLanes`, `🏢️semio-tech/🎡️play/🔨️modules/🧩️runtime/♻️activation/🟦️.ts`) does NOT miss the stdio extensions. It is greedy over the closures of all pane rows and runs on the union `playRuntimeComponentIds()`, so a pane `stdio-png` (closure `{stdio-image, stdio}`, via `depends-on`/`extends`) is a legal candidate and gets picked. Evaluated against the committed manifests (`generateComponentSourceRegistry` plus descriptor `contributes`, the repo's own `runtimeComponentClosure`) it yields 35 lanes including nine stdio family lanes.

The defect was the hand-pinned lists in `📋️project.json`:
- The 28-lane lists predate the stdio family split (extension crates added in `4e36b2b5012`, play `catalog-release` split in `2604f70cac1`) and were never re-pinned.
- The pinning test `🧪️tests/🧪️playactivation/🟦️.ts` could not catch it: its release assertion read `prepare-release`, which since `2604f70cac1` depends only on `catalog-release` (it got `[]`), and every lane test throws `Unknown runtime component cad` while the generated registry holds only 3 plugins (B1).
- `bim` had no pane, so no lane could contain it.

No computation change was needed. Lane lists are now equal to the computation.
