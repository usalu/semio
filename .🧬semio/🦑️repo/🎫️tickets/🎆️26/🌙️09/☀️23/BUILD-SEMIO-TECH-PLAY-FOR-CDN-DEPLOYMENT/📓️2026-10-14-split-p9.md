Facts that drive the cut (norm, measured per facet with `📐️norm-facets.py`):

- 15 norm artifacts share one generated layout (`🏅️standards/🔖️1/🪆️subsets/✳️any/{🧬️schema,🚪️io,✏️editor,👁️viewer,📚️examples}`).
- **The 32-file ceiling binds first.** A mutation leaf is 3 files (`🦀️.rs`, `🔺️diff`, `↩️inverse`; the taxonomy forbids
  inlining them). The 15 artifacts declare **554 leaves** (18–80 each); 311 of them would need package names above the
  48-character law L4 if every leaf were its own crate, and every `insert-X`/`remove-X` pair names the other in its
  inverse, so a pair cannot be split across crates.
- **Embedded inputs bind the bytes.** `🧬️schema/🦀️.rs` embeds all 20 facet leaves (rust/ts/graphql/json/proto ×
  artifact/snapshot/diff/mutations), 300–450 KB per artifact, and its own `rust` leaf is the 150 KB file that also holds
  the compliance computation (en1993: `check_full_steel_structure` and helpers, 2 900 lines).
- **Fixture tests embed 5 files each.** 50–130 template test files per artifact (`✅apply`, `⛔dupe`, `🔬️middle-row`)
  `include_str!` their quintet (before/after/mutation/diff/outcome): 400–830 embedded files per test unit.
- The artifact root (530–2 700 lines) holds the document records, the dialect constants, the 500-line `#[path]` mount tree
  and the declaration; every lower layer names records through `crate::`.
- Mutation leaves name the dispatch enum (`impl MutationKind<S, En1993Mutation>`, inverses build `En1993Mutation::…`),
  the mutations root re-exports the io mutation bridge, and the io text codec names every leaf: leaf ↔ enum ↔ io cycles.

Baseline compile (17:40): `cargo check --lib --tests` of the en1993 workspace fails upstream in peer fleet A
(`semio-framework-ui-theme`: unresolved `ui_contract`), before any norm crate compiles.

## 2. Cut plan

### 2.1 Decisions (norm family, applied to all 15 artifacts by one config-driven tool run)

- **N1 Owner-rooted crates** (brief, P4 D1): every crate is `<owner>/📦️packages/🦀️rust/Cargo.toml` with
  `[lib] path = "../../🦀️.rs"`; the owner's `🦀️.rs` is the crate root, items reach consumers only from their owning crate,
  root re-export barrels (`crate::En1993Snapshot`, `crate::mutations`, `crate::document`, `pub use component::*`) are
  resolved to owners and deleted.
- **N2 Records, dialect and document-schema constants move into the snapshot facet** (`🧬️schema/📸️snapshot/🦀️.rs`, as P2
  decision 3). The artifact root keeps identity, definition, declaration and languages only.
- **N3 Compliance computation moves out of `🧬️schema/🦀️.rs`** into `🧬️schema/💡️inferences/⚖️checks/🦀️.rs` (the place
  iso16757 and vdi3805 already use), so the artifact schema leaf is the schema again and the checks sit with the
  inference that publishes them.
- **N4 Mutation leaves are generic over the dispatch enum** (P2/P4/P5 D2):
  `impl<Op> MutationKind<S, Op> for InsertPile where Op: Mutation<S, Diff = D> + From<RemovePile>`, inverses build
  `Op::from(..)` for exactly the leaves they emit. `#[derive(dsl::Mutations)]` already emits the `From` impls.
- **N5 Mutation groups are package-only group directories** (P2 decision 8): `🧬️mutations/<emoji><section>/` holds the
  group crate root and its package; leaf directories and fixture paths do not move. Groups follow the snapshot sections the
  leaves edit (a `change-*` leaf joins the section of the field it sets), every `insert-X`/`remove-X` pair stays in one
  group, and a group holds at most 10 leaves (≤ 31 files). Crate names `semio-s-artifact-norm-<n>-mut-<section>` (≤ 48).
- **N6 Fixture vectors are data read at test runtime.** The template tests become one shared law runner,
  `semio-s-plugin-norm-test-support` (`📕️norm/🧪️tests/🧰️support`, dev-dependency only), generic over snapshot, mutation and
  diff, which walks `🧪️tests/🧫️fixtures/🧬️mutations/<leaf>/<case>/` through `CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR` and applies the same
  laws per declared `status` (wire witness, committed diff and after-snapshot, inverse restores, inverse-sum law, refusal
  code). Each `-mutations` crate declares one `[[test]]`. No fixture byte enters a compilation unit.
- **N7 Facet leaves are owned by their facet crate** (`SNAPSHOT_LEAVES` in `-model`, `DIFF_LEAVES` in `-diff`,
  `MUTATION_LEAVES` in `-mutations`, `ARTIFACT_LEAVES` + the descriptor in `-schema`).
- **N8 Shared norm code stays shared**: `semio-s-artifact-norm-contract` is itself over the line ceiling and is cut along
  its own folders (`📇️registry/🧬️contract/{🧾️definition,🪶️sqlite,🖥️app-surface}`); nothing is copied into artifacts.
