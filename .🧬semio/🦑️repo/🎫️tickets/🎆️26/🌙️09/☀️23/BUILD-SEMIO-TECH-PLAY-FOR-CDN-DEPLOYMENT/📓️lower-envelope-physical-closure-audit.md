## Canonical Bounded Ledger Design

Keep the ledger itself as the exact owner. Once all entries, reservations and group suffixes are empty, transition to a retained physical close phase which prevents further reservation or mutation. The phase should expose its next actual allocation extent and accept unchanged item/byte grants. No whole-chain extent estimate or accumulated release prepayment is needed.

Release one allocation per turn: a slot backing slice, its empty page wrapper, then an empty directory chunk. A directory chunk must remain retained while it still links another chunk or page. Existing64-slot and512-page constants determine bounded page extents; slot initialized metadata must be disposed before backing reclamation, including any remaining shared visibility leases. Each successful free reports the exact allocation in the same call. Below that extent it retains ownership and reports zero release. The caller admits that demand against its existing maximum allocation authority. Terminal physical close means resident page/directory owners are absent, not merely that live entry count is zero.

FreshVcs arrays and four history lanes should all call this same typed ledger authority; Vcs/envelope structural teardown should retain the authority until it is physically terminal. Resident backing remains excluded from serialized history, and no ambient registry or persisted cursor is introduced.
