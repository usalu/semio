## 3. Release tooling blockers found in fresh-4 (not Rust errors)

1. `os-plugin-describe-rs:build` and every `*:component-dev` fail in `writeCompletedCargoInvocationProvenanceV1` (`🧰️framework/🔨️modules/🏃️process/📦️artifacts/🏗️native-build/🟦️.ts:379`): `FileObservationErrorV1 byte-capacity` because `CurrentPhysicalOwnerV1` was capped at `maxBytes:128*1024*1024` (log: `totalBytes 127555790` when hitting `libsemio_framework_value-*.rmeta`). Decision needed from main/tooling owner: shrink the receipt (dedupe input paths into one table, store `.d` hashes not text) or stream it. My cap edit is necessary but not sufficient; revert if another design is chosen.
