These focused laws complement the all-pane browser proof. Codec-only rows prove command serialization and the declared action bridge, while typed dispatch, history replay, ownership, and retirement rows exercise persistence semantics and cancellation/progress boundaries. No single representative law proves every mutation of a family. Any failing browser interaction should select its exact domain command law and fixture rather than weakening the all-pane requirement.
