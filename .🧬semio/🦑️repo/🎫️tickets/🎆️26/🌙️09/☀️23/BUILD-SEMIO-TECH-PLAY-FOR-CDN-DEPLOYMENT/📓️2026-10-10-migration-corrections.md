# 2026-10-10 Migration corrections (rn-os, appended in order)

## 10:2x Q: is `ArtifactStoreInitializationAuthority::step -> StepOutcome` (plugin `🦀️.rs:~19196`) final?

No. It is a leftover of the old job protocol. `ArtifactStoreInitializationJob::step` implements `InteractiveJob::step`, whose signature is now `fn step<'a>(&'a mut self, cx) -> Result<Option<JobOutcomeBorrow<'a>>, ValueError>` plus `fn borrow_outcome<'a>(&'a self, &'a JobOutcomeDescriptor) -> Result<JobOutcomeView<'a>, ValueError>` (job `🦀️.rs:1289`). rn-os will move the authority trait to the same two methods (`step` and `borrow_outcome`, delegated by the job; the job's own `terminal_handoff` completion uses `JobOutcomeBorrow::admit_complete(cx, None, None)`, its missing-owner fault uses a publication and `admit_fault`). Do NOT write a new `ArtifactStoreInitializationAuthority` impl against `-> StepOutcome` now. Plugins that already have one: leave `step` untouched, migrate everything else, and tell rn-os the file; rn-os updates the trait first, the impls follow mechanically (`StepOutcome::Yield` -> `JobOutcomeBorrow::admit_yield(cx)`, `Complete(..)` -> `admit_complete`, `PreviewReady/Fault(payload)` -> a `RetainedJobPublication` field + `advance_from_source`).
