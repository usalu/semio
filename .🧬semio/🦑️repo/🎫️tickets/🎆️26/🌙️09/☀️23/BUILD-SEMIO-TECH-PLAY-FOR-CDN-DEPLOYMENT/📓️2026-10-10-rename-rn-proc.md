## Debt and shortcuts the compiler/reviewers must know
1. REPLACED (was: counted ladders). `counted_close_*`, the `foreign` closure and `foreign_remains` are gone from `crate::puzzle_job`. Every owner that used to be dropped whole after a one-item receipt now retires through real bounded retirement: `RetireOwned` bundles (`Puzzle3dWindowWorkOwners`, `Puzzle3dPrecomputeWorkOwners`, `Puzzle3dClipboardOwners`, `Puzzle3dInstanceOperationOwner`, `FillRevalidateOwners`, `BrushSuggestionsRunOwners`, `Puzzle2dFillRevalidateOwners`, `Puzzle2dFillRunOwners`, `Puzzle5dWindowWorkOwners`, `Puzzle5dPlannerOwners`) staged once through `WorkClosing<T>` (`ControlledRetirement`), so each close turn pays one bounded step of the quoted demand. The 2d fill placement is no longer drained in one turn: `accept` keeps it in `retiring_placement` and the next turn retires it with `context.retained_grant()`.
2. `FillBuilder::close_turn` keeps its `retire_one_close_owner` ladder gated on one `FILL_BUILDER_OWNER_PAGE_BYTES` release credit; demands quote exactly that.
3. Reserved-job completion echo: the claim "dead" was WRONG and is reverted. The old `CommitOutput` echo of the raw wire IS consumed by the host: `drive_framework_reserved_worker` (`🧰️framework/🛍️products/💻️os/🔨️modules/🔌️plugin/🦀️.rs:~27017`) faults `interactive-job.output-envelope` on a `Complete` whose output is absent while the admitted `raw` is non-empty, or whose output differs from `raw` (`retained_payload_eq_slice`). Restored through the framework's own publication machinery: `crate::puzzle_job::JobOutbox` now owns a `RetainedPayloadBuilder` (stream `CommitOutput`, the same init -> append_original -> seal frontiers `ReservedJobOutcomes` uses, which is `pub(crate)` to the plugin crate) and a new `JobTurn::Prepare(Vec<u8>)` stages the wire. A reserved job returns `Prepare(take(raw))` BEFORE it calls `completion.complete(..)` (the old guard "retained output prepared before completion" is preserved: a build refusal can never follow a delivered completion); once `commit_prepared()` holds, its `JobTurn::Complete` lends the sealed pages as `admit_complete(cx, None, published)`; the close ladder retires publication, then commit pages. Sites: 2d clipboard (import admits no wire), 3d clipboard, 5d clipboard (copy+cut), paste, import. Unit test: `puzzle_job::tests::a_prepared_commit_output_rides_on_the_completion_and_closes_exactly` (empty, short, 1000-byte wires; output equals the wire). Caveat: `testing::drive*` builds a fresh `StepContext` (so a fresh payload ledger) per turn, hence only single-page payloads are valid there; the real session reuses one ledger.
4. Each published fill tick/checkpoint now costs several paying turns (init, append, seal, lend, retire) instead of one.
