During `wrsWb7f`, host `df` reported 22 GiB available; the configured 15 GiB floor leaves about 7 GiB headroom. The mounted compiler image reported 41 GiB used and 29 GiB available out of 70 GiB. Its host backing file `.cache/b/r/fixed.dmg` reported 146,797,104 allocated 512-byte blocks, approximately 70 GiB, with a 70 GiB logical file size. Therefore inner free space is not currently equivalent to host free space. The approximately 29 GiB allocation-minus-inner-used gap is a reclaim candidate, not a guarantee of recoverable bytes or a prediction of future peak.

The supported image reclaim owner checks bloat, requires the exclusive image lease, detaches the idle image, attaches through the trim-capable stack, waits for allocation to settle and detaches. At observation, release site PID 1942 and several other active processes held shared `native-fixed-image` and `repo:compiler-writer` leases on the compiler image. Reclaiming that image now is not a concrete safe action; it must remain governed by its owners. No cleanup or mutation was attempted.

Existing measurements do not bound subsequent physical image writes, other fleet activity, or publication copies, so crossing the host floor cannot be ruled in or out. Continue observing host free space and governed admission; the inner 29 GiB free cannot be treated as host headroom. This observation does not change the earlier unit-cache analysis.
