Follow-up to [cad-react-e2e-2026-09-16.md](./📓️cad-react-e2e-2026-09-16.md): every `🕹️interactions/*.json` statechart under `📚️examples/🖼️assets/🏗️modelDefinitions/*/` runs in the React dev app (`🛠️dev📐️cad⚛️react`, port 6020) through the generic interpreter, paints its preview lane while it runs, and lands its commit in the document as an undoable edit. ## Constraints and open items

1. **64-edit ledger per store** (`ARTIFACT_HISTORY_LEDGER_CAPACITY`, framework-wide, no compaction): with engagement steps coalesced, each interaction costs one config-ledger item (the engagement run) plus the commit's own config snapshot, so a document session saturates after roughly 30 committed interactions; a coalesced edit also keeps every amended snapshot in its `forwards` (the framework's amend semantic), so a very long rubber-band grows one edit. Not cad-local.
2. `Move`/`Rotate`/`Copy`/`Mirror` live in `spatial.shape` only; the Building/Energy/Structure panes offer exactly their model definition's assets (Building: the 11 placements). Typing `Move` in Building answers `Unknown: Move`.
3. The mode choosers `curve.construct` / `surface.construct` / `energy.energy.constructBasePlate` (`interaction.call` composition), `measure.area` (`kernel.query`) and `surface.extrudeCrv` (`assignExtrusionDistance`) still stop at their first state; the 17 `Unsupported` families answer `Unsupported: <action>` in the HUD rather than silently committing.
4. The retained-audit TS oracle (`bun ./📜️script.ts retained-audit`) fails before comparing anything: `🧬️schema/🔣️.json` has no `$defs.CadRetainedJobs` any more — pre-existing, left as is (its literals were aligned to the Rust owner regardless).
5. Harness debt from phase 1 unchanged (registry-less `new_app`, store harness drop contracts, unknown-field codec law).
