Therefore the operation minimum is not a guaranteed safe size for the rebuild phase: admitting at 2,304 MiB based on that field alone would undermine phase booking and may overcommit. See host-capacity/🎫️gate/🟦️.ts:434-448, 🔼️upgrade/🟦️.ts:90-101, and 📏️decision/🟦️.ts:230-250 under 🧰️framework/🔨️modules/🏃️process/🏗️build/🌡️host-capacity.

The queue intentionally raises a barrier after bounded bypasses/time only if its drain calculation predicts that ending local grants permits the head. Calculated drained room adds every ledger row’s observed memory, including quiet, lent, and the head’s own row, and caps at total memory (decision line 205); it does not directly check whether grants can finish independently of the head, nor deduplicate overlapping observed trees. Drainability is an estimate, not a proven liveness bound: a long-lived or dependent grant can maintain the barrier beyond the nominal bypass interval. Quiet/descendant credit can make the estimate optimistic if observations overlap or quiet holders cannot complete independently; this audit does not establish overlap or dependency for the live sample. The later Cargo admission shows the barrier can clear as host observations and queue state change, not why that happened.

Existing tests: 🧪️tests/🟦️.ts:185-224 cover foreign-pressure bypass, bounded local drain and starvation under finite small durations; 🧪️tests/🔼️upgrade/🟦️.ts:19-66 cover replacement accounting and ordering; 🧪️tests/🪂️partial-start/🟦️.ts:12-21 cover fresh fixed request partial starts. These do not by themselves prove a safe partial rebuild reservation or bounded liveness with quiet/overlapping or indefinitely held grants. A targeted pure-decision case should model a scoped rebuild, retained grant, long-lived quiet/dependent holder, and smaller later request, with disjoint and overlapping observations; any design change should first establish independently valid rebuild memory and preserve non-overcommit and fairness. No safe supported autonomous remedy was identified; observe admission and let the owning process complete or its owner decide whether to stop it.
