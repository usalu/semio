# Selected Owner Checkpoint Recovery

Selected discovery stores completed owner facts in the managed graph selection under `inference/selections/<scope>/oc`. The writer uses the existing selected graph lease and optional quota. The checkpoint keys each record by selection scope, admission format, owner name, exact target list and exact input-group list. A stored record is checked for shape, byte extent, digest and owner identity. Before discovery uses it, the existing source-fact hold must succeed and its observations must be retained in the current capture. Failed validation sends that owner through discovery. A newer checkpoint can also repair an owner whose rows in a previously complete admission memo have become stale.

Each pack holds up to 32 owner records into a file of at most 2 MiB. The selection rotates through 256 slots. The first completed owner is flushed immediately. Later batches are flushed at the byte or count threshold, at the next completed owner after three minutes, or at successful completion of discovery. A killed worker can lose its pending batch. Recovery indexes only bounded headers and reads one selected record body at a time. An unfinished pack is written to an optional temporary leaf and the previous complete slot remains available until rename. Existing cache LRU accounts for and can retire both final and temporary leaves.

The complete admission memo remains the first lookup. A valid hit skips checkpoint recovery, and a recovered set of facts goes through the normal admission, graph and authority checks. The graph and authority are then published through the normal sealing path. Workers begin writing checkpoints when they load the new implementation.

All 36 tests passed across the checkpoint, admission memo, inventory, selected fleet exchange and optional cache LRU suites. The checkpoint suite also passed after extending the isolation case to demonstrate that managed retirement removes both checkpoint leaves under the selected writer lease. The tests cover abrupt child termination, exact owner questions, stale repairs, scope and format isolation, malformed and oversized data, a wrapped ring, quota refusal, cancellation, and preservation of complete memo, inventory and selected fleet behavior. The shared JSON fixture is checked against a SQLite query as an independent oracle. The work used disposable test directories under this ticket for all cache writes.
