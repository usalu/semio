# Large Editable Archive Limits

The common current Browser/native folder archive is at most **4,194,304 encoded bytes including framing**; this is an upper bound, not a demonstrated universally retention-safe PDF size. Actual capacity depends on Pack/SPR/member framing, image representation, Store history and mounted allocation/retirement grants. The source PDF length does not determine that size. No larger live end-to-end editable folder archive was established by the inspected source or ticket evidence. The existing exact-4-MiB codec comparison in `native-natural-archive.md` is not a mounted edit/close/reopen proof.

- Wire and persisted representation: `📡️spr/🧵️channel/📦️archive/🧬️schema/🦀️.rs` declares 4 MiB and 1,024 members. `🗜️codec/📚️archive-and-pack-codecs/🦀️.rs` bounds encoded bytes on write/read; `📦️archive/🛬️decode/🦀️.rs` enforces the same input limit. Worker folder write and Rust sync actor validate against it. Browser dev middleware collects a complete body at 4 MiB plus 10 bytes before decode. Local catalog separately allows 8 MiB base64 per field, not an 8 MiB archive.
- Store and mounting: `🔌️plugin/🗃️document/🗄️document-archive-load/🦀️.rs` separately bounds parent Pack + parent SPR + member envelopes by 4 MiB, and sets history file, frame and binary limits to 4 MiB, history JSON to 1 MiB and cumulative history decode allocation to 8 MiB. The recursive genesis projection in `🔌️plugin/🧵️instance/📄️docs/📥️staging/🚪️ingress/🦀️.rs` bounds parent SPR plus projected child envelopes to 4 MiB and 1,024 members. Its check does not include parent Pack in that particular accumulator; the admission check does.
- Other transport: the Browser cold pair in `🏪️store/👷️worker/🧊️cold-pair/🟦️.ts` independently limits Pack + SPR to 4 MiB / 64 pages of 64 KiB. Native staged archive remains format-1 4 MiB with whole-archive reopen. The folder event-log record limit of 16 GiB is an on-disk framing allowance, not an archive admission or safe allocation guarantee.

These are declared framework/transport and resource policy bounds, not an inherent PDF specification limit. PDF snapshot stream data still has contiguous owned vectors; the mounted interactive policy’s 16 MiB per-turn capacity and 1 MiB release ceilings can constrain a single backing even where compressed archive bytes fit. The test in `set-image-u32.md` reports at most 262,144 samples for its contiguous u32 destination under that release grant; it is an operation-specific finding, not a universal document size.

There are useful paged components but no verified paged folder archive spanning these limits. `OriginalSnapshotPackExport` and retained genesis Pack retain canonical Pack pages under a caller maximum; existing `print_document_pack` materializes a Vec and generates SPR, while archive egress/reopen remains bounded and contiguous. `PagedDocumentArchiveDecode` consumes paged commands but produces parent Pack and SPR Vecs and remains subject to archive admission. A complete solution needs bounded snapshot data and lifecycle, paged archive representation and Store projection, authenticated ordered folder append/readback and paged reopening, with matched byte limits and exact integrity/frontier checks. Measure a real high-entropy fixture at each boundary, including cancellation and edit/save/reopen, rather than assuming a new wire maximum alone suffices.
