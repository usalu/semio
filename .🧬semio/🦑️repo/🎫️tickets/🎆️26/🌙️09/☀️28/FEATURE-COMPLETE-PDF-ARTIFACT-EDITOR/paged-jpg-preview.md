# Bounded JPEG Canvas Preview

The staged preview samples one validated baseline JPEG entropy block per turn. It computes inverse transforms only for blocks that contribute to the requested raster and uses the same nearest component lookup and transforms as the PDF editor’s existing JPEG decoder. It writes into caller-owned bounded pages. Canvas publication is gated on the final EOI and exact expected dimensions. The native preview port and paged output remain staged and require binding to the editor’s retained render owner and cache.

The 2,125,603-byte color witness produced a 256 × 256 preview, 196,608 sample bytes in 48 physical 4 KiB pages. Its 49,169 turns read at most 179 original bytes and wrote at most four component samples per turn. The maximum difference versus both Pillow 12.0.0 and full PDF rendering in MuPDF 1.26.7 was three levels per channel. Grayscale restart, Adobe RGB and 1.3 MB metadata controls differed by at most one. Progressive JPEG, clipped entropy, invalid quantization, restart and EOI, stale dimensions and over-limit output all refused with no publication. Cancellation after 1,000 turns also left no published output. The focused probe does not model the guest budget ledger of the renderer.

A second focused probe used the repository’s actual AuthoringWallet, PagedBytes and ControlledRetirement under the mounted ceilings. It compared every retained sampled component with the independently exercised preview cursor, withheld any rendered result until EOI, and released all 216,992 bytes of sample backing. The zero-capacity call performed no work. Cancelling through the funded close path after 1,000 turns withdrew the unpublished preview and returned the full settled capacity. Both measured laws passed. Actual Canvas cache publication and the read-side actor binding remain open.

An odd-sized baseline 4:2:0 color fixture also passed at 113 × 79 source pixels and 67 × 61 display pixels. All 12,261 component slots were written exactly once over 257 turns. Independent Pillow and MuPDF differed from the scoped preview by at most three values per channel.
