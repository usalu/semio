# Retained Browser Actor Lifecycle Audit

The actual published editor and viewer extended logs show the same progression: capture receipt at turn 37, startup idle at turn 83, a six-byte lifecycle receipt on the first close turn (84), then more-work with no further lifecycle receipt through turn 63,999. The probe only sends close after capture acknowledgement and idle; it acknowledges each lifecycle receipt on the next turn and requires a retired receipt, idle, and successful original-source closure. The logs report receipt lengths, not decoded kinds, so the six-byte receipt alone does not establish which close receipt it was or whether the final acknowledgement was processed.

An older viewer trace sampled OwnedStores at close turns 15,802–16,357, with successive hits 46–47 host polls apart; that older run failed original receipt validation at turn 51,366 with a semantic refusal and 18,560 release bytes. The newer 64,000-turn artifact passed that old failure point, but its logs do not locate the remaining rung. These are different artifacts; the old failure cannot be attributed to the newer one.

The mounted-owner close batch can reduce the cost of multiple funded close actions within a turn, subject to the remaining grant, deadline, and progress. This fits the observed repeated Store work, but neither the sampled older trace nor the extended newer logs prove that the batch reaches terminal retirement or resolves every subsequent acknowledgement and source-close rung.

On the newly authenticated publication, record artifact identity and decoded lifecycle receipt kinds and acknowledgement event delivery, then sample the current close rung and per-turn demand, posted receipt, available grant, work and deadline at startup, after close acknowledgement, and at intervals through the prior 64,000-turn boundary. Require the retired receipt acknowledgement, idle, and closeOriginal sourceClosed result. If progress remains in OwnedStores, count actual completed Store actions per host poll and compare to the pre-batch trace; if it advances, capture the next persistent rung and its demand/receipt instead of assuming batching solved the entire lifecycle. The export serializer release ceiling concerns a different operation and is not evidence for this open/close probe.
