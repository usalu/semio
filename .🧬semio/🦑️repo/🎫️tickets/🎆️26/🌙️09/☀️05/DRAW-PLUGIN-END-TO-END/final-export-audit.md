# Draw Export Ownership Audit

- Editor export header allocates the filename, MIME, route and options and moves the captured read into the request without a retained allocation receipt; its work demand only quotes the Work frame. A long title may exceed the turn capacity.
- SVG writer fills pages to Vec capacity rather than the PAGE bound. An allocator may grant capacity greater than PAGE even for try_reserve_exact; the editor rejects a source page larger than PAGE_INPUT (3072), so such a page fails export.
- PDF and SVG writer PAGE is 3072, divisible by three; normal full pages therefore preserve base64 boundaries.

Read-only source review; no builds run.
