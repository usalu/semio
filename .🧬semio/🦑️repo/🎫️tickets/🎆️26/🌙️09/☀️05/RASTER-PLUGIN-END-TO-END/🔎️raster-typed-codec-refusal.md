# Raster Codec Typed Refusal Custody

The live ingress projects Base64 failures through `base64_refusal` in `editor/🎞️reserved-import/🪜️base64/🦀️.rs:7`; all codec variants currently share one literal value error, even though `🧰️framework/🔨️modules/🚪️io/🔤️base64/🦀️.rs:49` includes `InvalidByte { index, byte }`, invalid length, invalid padding and noncanonical trailing bits. PNG's `PngDecodeError` in `framework/🔨️modules/🔲️pixels/📷️png/📥️decode/🦀️.rs:13` distinguishes invalid static message, incomplete and cancelled. Current `png_refusal` projects their messages/kinds but does not expose the enum itself. Both ingress owners hold typed errors privately, which does not provide the caller typed error custody.

The funded source-preserving mechanism is `framework/🔨️modules/🧵️job/⚠️failure/📦️domain/🦀️.rs:7,80–117,147`: a typed owner implements `RetireOwned + Sync + OriginalDomainProjection + Debug` and `OriginalDomainFailure`; `OriginalDomainReservation<T>::prepare` prepays the real frame, `with_body` retains its original error and callback refusal, and `admit_original_domain_failure` transfers the same frame without reallocating. `OriginalWorkFailure<E>::fold` at `job/⚠️failure/🎟️work/🦀️.rs:9` posts actual physical effects and CPU once while retaining primary, receipt and context refusal. Prepare before executing a decoder turn that can fail, leave the source in the prepaid frame if publication is denied, and do not debit its already posted effects a second time. A Raster-owned wrapper must expose every codec variant and its fields in its field projection and have real controlled retirement support; the plain Base64 enums do not currently derive that support. Cancellation should leave the retained reservation for paid close rather than force publication.

The existing physical, same-source, foreign-source, denial, pointer, nested refusal and funded-close law is `job/⚠️failure/📦️domain/🎟️reservation/🧪️tests/🦀️.rs:25–63`. It demonstrates actual original source receipts rather than manufactured authority. The generic reservation is a concrete available seam, not evidence that Raster has wired or tested it.
