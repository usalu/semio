# Original Work Fixture Close Receipts

OriginalWorkFixture.close previously called ArtifactCommandWork.close_step directly. A Work can return unposted close progress because the real ArtifactRetainedCommandJob outer lifecycle posts it. The fixture therefore omitted those effects from its genuine original caller's physical ledger, while other Work implementations that posted internally behaved differently.

A neutral fixture now names three cases: unposted success, already-posted success, and failure after an actual 37-byte release. The authored law creates its payload under original construction, compares the real OriginalJobFixtureCaller cumulative receipt against the allocator observer, and checks exactly three units of reported domain work. The already-posted case detects double charging; the failed-partial case checks the complete original error and released-byte witness. This is an independent physical observer check, rather than an expected receipt generated by the implementation under test.

The helper now uses the same original_lifecycle_step contract as the actual outer job, with the Work's exact declared close demands and terminal witness. It posts only unposted physical effects, preserves failed partial effects and independent work, and borrows original retirement permission without creating another grant or caller. The source and law parse with the pinned registered rustfmt. They have not yet been compiled or executed.
