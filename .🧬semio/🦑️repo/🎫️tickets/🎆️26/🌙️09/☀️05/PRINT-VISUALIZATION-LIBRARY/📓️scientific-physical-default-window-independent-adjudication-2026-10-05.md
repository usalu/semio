## PGF Primary Canvas Scope Contract

Verified direct primary manual: [PGF/TikZ coordinate and canvas transformations, section108.3](https://tikz.dev/base-transformations). Canvas transformations affect coordinates, images, text, shading and line thickness together. They live in pgfscope rather than a plain TeX group, and the scope end restores the backend state. pgflowlevelscope encloses a pgfscope and installs the supplied transformation through pgflowlevel. Coordinate-only transforms do not scale text or widths; this is why they cannot alone provide the promised complete physical viewport effect. PGF does not maintain node/shape coordinates correctly under canvas transformation, so actual PDF paint/glyph positions and body bounds remain necessary independent evidence; internal native probe numbers alone are insufficient. This audit did not execute a transform compiler.

All36 own default option lists omit or explicitly empty domain/range. Shared reset empties both. Thus clearing them in the36 baseline fixture does not mask a nonempty native per-family window.
