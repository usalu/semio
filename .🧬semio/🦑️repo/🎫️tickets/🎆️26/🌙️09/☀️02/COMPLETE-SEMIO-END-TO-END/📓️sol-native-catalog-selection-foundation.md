This is the first source-only planning slice of `📓️terra-multi-provider-verified-catalog-blueprint.md`. Exact plugin/package/version identities and receipt counts select only the requested modeled provider closure. Results are immutable copies; staging exposes no receipt or catalog until every modeled callback and preflight succeeds.

The strict neutral fixture models `native-stdio-v1` and `native-stdio-vcs-v1`, with 26 and 1 declared receipt counts respectively. The independent oracle uses strict AJV and a separate iterative closure/topological algorithm for every outcome, rather than calling the shipped DFS planner to determine truth. Nineteen vectors cover two positive profiles and seventeen denials, including unselected provider output, cancellation before/after the first callback, selected second-provider failure and final registry conflict. Only neutral callback order/count and zero publication are in scope. There are no process factories, raw/core/descriptor bytes, bundle publication or hub activation in this model.
