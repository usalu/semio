# Artifact-Owned Native Publication Capabilities

The original History source law reproduced direct registry calls inside generic Store construction and retained hydration. History’s isolated native law remains an explicit owner-publication contract: both public IO directions refuse before registration and complete after it.

The original Space and Collection runtime laws separately require automatic owner publication during direct Store creation, reload and bounded retained hydration. Their authentic originals passed, so moving publication exclusively to member factories would violate existing runtime requirements. That transient move was corrected before claiming qualification.

ArtifactPack now exposes an explicit publication capability with a neutral default. The five already-declared native registration owners (Space, Collection, Flow, DAG and Run) implement publication with their same concrete authored dialect and codec. Generic Store and hydration invoke that owner capability; they contain no dialect selection or registry call. History has no implicit capability and retains its explicit register_sqlite_snapshot authority.

No original source or runtime assertion was weakened. Replays of History source, Space/Collection lifecycle laws, Flow’s actual member-open law and the owning native routes remain pending.
