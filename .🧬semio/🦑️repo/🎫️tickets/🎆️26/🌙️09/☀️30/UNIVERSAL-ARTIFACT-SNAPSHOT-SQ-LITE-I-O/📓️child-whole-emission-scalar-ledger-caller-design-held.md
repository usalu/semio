# Whole Retained Emission Through the Actual Scalar Ledger

The complete production press join is best expressed as one scalar identity for the entire original ordered emission, rather than one identity per child. This preserves the actual ledger's absolute tick semantics: each tick replaces its complete emission. It also avoids constructing an unbounded contiguous vector of child identities merely to satisfy the existing `ScrubInput::leaves` boundary. The ledger handles one cheap scalar leaf; its original emission remains owned once in the paged registry.

The [schema-first caller contract](📥️inputs/child-paged-complete-family/press-whole-emission-source-contract-held.json) names every real press caller. A complete original emission includes the paged children and artifact/config/window-config lanes. Preview reads only an immutable accepted-ledger identity, borrows its original owner, and visits each original lane in order. Commit extracts that original owning emission before reconciliation consults the now-empty accepted ledger. Its transaction stamps that same original. Abort, window retirement, base/tool replacement and complete runtime close revoke scalar ledger authority before source retirement. Empty child publication filters its ordinal view while preserving every original empty semantic owner.

The generic held registry already permits a complete publication owner as its `C`; its current owning demand uses individual actual paged child owners to isolate admission, pointer identity, monotone reuse and bounded disposal. That demand is not proof of this whole-emission production join. Required caller work remains the actual `ToolMachineRuntime` field and its `settle_press`, all four preview lanes, retained window/abort/freeze paths and close, together with the complete emission owner's typed field closure. All these production edits remain held until their caller family and genuine owning demands are coherent.

This design does not rename a large typed mutation String into source ownership. Typed artifact/config/window-config mutations and delta owners still require their real bounded disposal and controlled clone policies. The original encoded8194 ChildEmit carrier law, Source grouping/inverse/undo/redo, Store transport and required global OpBinary decoder/canonical comparison are separate required joins. No byte or String flattening, cloned original emission, implicit Drop, guessed refund, enlarged grant or blanket global adoption is authorized by the scalar ledger design.
