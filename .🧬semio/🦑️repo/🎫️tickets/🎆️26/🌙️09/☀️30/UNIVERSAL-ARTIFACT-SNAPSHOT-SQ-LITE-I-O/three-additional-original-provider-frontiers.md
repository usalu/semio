## Prioritization

TXT is the smallest new independent ownership defect because it visibly replaces caller control. JSON has substantial semantic schema and named subset behavior but its owner signatures hide unpaid typed construction and ignored encode authority. Deflate additionally needs owned inflater history retirement, so it is larger than Binary and should follow a complete Binary reference. Source discovery is not runtime proof, and pending root selected-native tests do not qualify these three domains.
