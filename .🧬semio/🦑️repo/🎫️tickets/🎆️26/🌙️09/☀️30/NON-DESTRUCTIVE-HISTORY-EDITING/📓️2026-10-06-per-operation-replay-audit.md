## Producer Contract

The next seam separates domain semantic preparation from publication metadata. A domain replay cursor receives an immutable projection alias, mode and fixed operation authority. Each advance borrows the stable original operation and optional replacement payload; it must decode/copy/invert/apply under item and byte grants. It returns an owned candidate projection, inverse result, messages and foreign-step fact. It never invents a live edit id, actor, clock or revision. Report replay keeps forward behavior after an inverse refusal and records Fatal; Merge retains its distinct refusal policy. Prefix preparation can omit inverse construction.

Cancellation transfers the cursor and every prepared result into exact domain retirement, with no semantic work after cancellation and terminal-empty proof before Drop. No live cold fallback is allowed. Generated mutation dispatch should forward authored domain capabilities; adding a poll around synchronous leaf methods is not a producer implementation. Store close-edit/clamping is coordinator-owned and must remain separate from this operation preparation seam.
