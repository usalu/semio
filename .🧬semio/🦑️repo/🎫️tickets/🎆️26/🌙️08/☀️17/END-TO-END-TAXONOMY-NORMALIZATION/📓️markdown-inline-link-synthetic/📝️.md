## Deterministic linear approach

Maintain a monotonic main cursor and the earliest opening bracket since the previous closing bracket or successful match. All opening brackets before the same next closing bracket share that closing boundary; if its destination is invalid, retrying every earlier opening bracket is unnecessary. On failure resume after that closing bracket, **not** after speculative destination/title lookahead: the failed suffix may contain a later viable opening bracket. On success emit the actual destination span and jump to the successful match end, preserving leftmost non-overlapping order.

Cache three forward-only lookaheads: next right-parenthesis-or-whitespace destination delimiter, next nonwhitespace position, and next double quote. A cache can be reused only when its found boundary is at or beyond the new query; otherwise advance from the new query/previous boundary without retreating. Destination starts increase with the main cursor, the next-delimiter function is monotone, and next-nonwhite/next-quote queries derived from it are also monotone. End-of-input is a reusable sentinel. This prevents overlapping failed destinations and title suffixes from triggering repeated scans; precomputed next-boundary arrays are an alternative with explicit linear memory.

The label scan must not introduce CommonMark balancing, escaping, code-fence filtering or angle handling. A label may contain opening brackets and line breaks, but ends at its first closing bracket. A nonempty destination ends at its first right parenthesis or exact ECMAScript whitespace; optional title syntax is whitespace plus a double quote, text to the first subsequent double quote, then an immediate closing parenthesis. Keep definitions/HTML untouched and preserve physical span validation downstream.
