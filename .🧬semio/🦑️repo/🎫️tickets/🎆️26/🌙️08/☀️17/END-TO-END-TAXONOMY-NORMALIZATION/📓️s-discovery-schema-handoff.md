# Complete Discovery Schema Handoff

## Cause and Correction

The normalizer maintains a reduced execution schema and the complete validated discovery schema. New nested Cargo prerequisites inspect entry contracts and ecosystem targets available only in the complete schema. Casting the reduced object to `DiscoveryTaxonomy` concealed missing runtime data and caused valid package authority to fail.

After the package lane corrected its immediate caller, the coordinator replaced the nineteen remaining unsafe casts in the normalizer with `taxonomy.discoverySchema`. No authority rule, schema construction, fixture expectation, or public ignore option changed.

## Test-First Evidence

A language-neutral permanent fixture enumerates eleven discovery authority functions and their required full-schema argument. The independent TypeScript parser locates their actual call expressions in the normalizer. Before the replacement, the packet failed with zero passes, one failure, and three assertions in 3.02 seconds. Afterward it passed one test with twenty-three assertions in 1.404 seconds.

A subsequent absolute-path Bun packet exercised that handoff check, ticket-document authority, artifact facet authority and actual repeated scaffolding, the live forty-leaf README/LICENSE preimages with exact fast-glob parity, and the frozen catalog's projection/canonical/drift/collision matrix. It passed eleven tests with zero failures and 1,287 assertions in 2.99 seconds. Ajv supplies independent schema validation. This focused packet does not claim that all transaction or monorepo gates pass.

The package lifecycle's earlier successful native/Wasm checks and transaction proof are recorded separately in `📓️s-nested-cargo-package-integration.md`; they preceded this mechanical audit and are not represented as a new rerun of all nineteen call sites.

The complete ticket-document packet then passed five tests with zero failures and eighty-nine assertions in 9.31 seconds. Actual transactions covered empty, open, and historical documents through rollback, same-ticket retry, commit, byte/mode preservation, and an empty replan. Negative cases covered occupied destinations, changed sources, counterfeit owners, and VS16-folded collision. The isolated non-generator fixture removes only the unrelated registry `inputDiscovery`; its fixture roots are now retained instead of deleted. Production generator authority remains unchanged.

## Apply Preflight Progress

Before starting the real 236-document batch, inspection found that apply's fresh authority rederivation did not forward its progress callback. The long repository-wide incoming scan could therefore run silently despite the caller requesting progress. A new language-neutral expected event in the existing document fixture first failed during actual rollback with zero passes, one failure, and five assertions in 2.10 seconds.

The rederivation call now forwards only `options.progress`; cancellation, operation sets, and digest comparisons are unchanged. The complete document packet passed five tests with zero failures and ninety-five assertions in 6.88 seconds. Each actual lifecycle observes the preflight incoming-candidate event, independently validated with Ajv, before proceeding through rollback, retry, commit, and empty replanning.
