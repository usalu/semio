## Ordering and Fault Recovery

Builder body-empty means destination pages, reader, current fragment and evidence dependencies have actually detached. It is distinct from whole builder terminal while its source/resident bindings remain. Source detach requires closing authority and all builder-owned range evidence settled; copied completion alone is insufficient. Exact old bindings stay valid after operation revocation.

After source detach, UI clears the builder's source field in a separately granted private transition and advances the same witness phase. The pending parent still holds the original field and proof. Source settlement then drops its last witness/builder alias. UI observes the exact original settled pairing before clearing its parent aliases and declaring domain retirement. Neutral record/cell retirement follows later.

Wrapper faults after actual detach/settle recover through the privately held source binding or the parent's retained original pairing. Mutating replay refuses. For an honestly never-bound or cleanly refused binding, a close-only null-to-held-proof abandonment may use the same exact body-empty witness and original parent slot; it must consume the binding permanently and cannot admit another builder. Ambiguous/foreign proofs refuse without clearing either side.

The source additionally rejects detach while an original Fragment/Release handshake remains outstanding. A never-transferred raw fragment may still be retained solely by the source; the builder proof does not certify its retirement. Successful binding detach sets the existing field-closing flag and preserves raw descendants. The original source's remaining cleanup is separate from refunding the now-detached UI builder record.
