Domain-verb mapping notes:
- change-coefficient: "change" family. Fatal invariant chosen for denom == "0" (a rational with a
  zero denominator is not a representable number) — the closest analogue to the family's "Fatal on
  domain violation" clause; no other domain invariant was evident from the EquationNodeKind enum.
- replace-points / replace-graph: mapped to "edit/replace" family's no-op rule rather than "delete/
  target-missing" since both are whole-collection/whole-value swaps with nothing that can be
  "missing" — matches the contract's shrink-only allowlist spirit even though they aren't literally
  root `change-<artifact>-<field>`/`clear-*` kinds.
- connect-nodes: distinguished Fatal `duplicate-id` (same edge *id* reused) from Warning `no-op`
  (same source/target pair under a *fresh* id — parallel edges forbidden) per the family's own two
  distinct sub-cases.
