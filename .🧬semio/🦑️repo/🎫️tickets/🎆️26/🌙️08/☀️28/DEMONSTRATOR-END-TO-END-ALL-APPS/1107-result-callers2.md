## Design (one rule for all admit_original_tool_* callers)
`OriginalConstructionAdmission` needs a resident home across turns (bind -> construct -> settle). Producers behind `build_tool_job` (167 impls) and direct constructors (about 12 more in plugins) have none, so they do the whole sequence inside the initiating turn:
- `action-bus::admit_original_construction_settled` (generic) and `admit_original_tool_construction_settled` (tool form): loop bind/construct/settle with a fresh local admission; before the construct step it requires fuel, yield state and grant for construct + settle, else constructs nothing and answers `Ok(false)` (caller slots untouched, so a lost local admission is harmless). A residual unfunded settle after a built candidate is a typed OwnershipLimit refusal, never a hang. Failed -> typed invariant error; causes stay in the admission.
- Callers with a real resident owner (ReservedPreparation) hold the admission instead (see below).
