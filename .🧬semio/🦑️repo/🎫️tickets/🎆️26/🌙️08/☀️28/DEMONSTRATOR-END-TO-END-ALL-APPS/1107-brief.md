## ADDENDUM (runtime2) — encode side provider: one primitive `NativeEncodeControl::with_effect`
Overrides the "entry takes an effect param" idea for encode: control-only callers (sqlite snapshot hosts, store codecs, intrinsic encoder, print mutations, generated `__dsl` hosts) get their funded effect from the control they already hold.
- `NativeEncodeControl::with_effect(grant, maximum_work, |effect| ..) -> Result<Option<R>, ValueError>` (value/🛫️encode/🎟️effect; owner port-e ac2ac4bb1b043a97d). `None` = quote not admitted, nothing ran, nothing charged. The control's commit of the receipt's performed work is the single charge; callers report work = performed in their outcome and debit nowhere else. Physical progress keeps the existing `submit_constructed_progress` channel.
- It is THE primitive: `effect_allocated`, `effect_allocated_with_work` (run_closure_effect) and `NativeSnapshotBodyWallet::effect_encoded` are thin layers over the same internal sequence; no second copy. Decode: `receive_effect` (frame, resumable) stays; a one-shot decode `with_effect` of the same signature may be added on the same internals.
- Law: one corpus fixture read by three drivers (encode control, job `with_original_effect`, `AuthoringWallet::run`) asserting identical (admitted, performed), committed == performed <= maximum, unadmitted runs nothing and charges 0, no live action after. Sits beside `original-effect-provider`.
- Summary of drivers: job turn -> `cx.with_original_effect`; native decode -> `receive_effect`; native encode -> `with_effect`; no turn -> `AuthoringWallet`.
