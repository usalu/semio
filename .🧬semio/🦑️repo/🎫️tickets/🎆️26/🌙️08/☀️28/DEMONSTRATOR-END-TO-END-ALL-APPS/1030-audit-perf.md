# 1030 audit: interim and unbounded full copies (lane perf)

Scope: demonstrator pane plugins under `✏️s/🔌️plugins` (procedural, fem 2d/3d, energy, process3d, cad, gis, sourcing, flow, puzzle, playbook). Static audit by grep and reading; costs are estimates (N = elements of the model, passes = full tree walks), not measurements. Paths are abbreviated: `P` = `✏️s/🔌️plugins`, `…` = the standard `🗿️artifacts/<x>/🏅️standards/🔖️1/🪆️subsets/✳️any` prefix.

Legend: FIXED = fixed in this lane (see `1030-result-perf.md`); OPEN = owner lane must act.

## Not found
- process3d, sourcing, gis (map/terrain), cad editor: no `to_value`/`to_reference` round trips in render, panel, inspector or viewer paths; remaining hits are exporters (on demand) or constant-size tables.
- No `from_reference(x.to_reference())` pairs in procedural gen2d/gen3d paths.
