## Step 2 (production faults found at the source; runs W-recvclose-5..7)
- NativeReceiveSlot (🌳️value/🦀️.rs): `close_demands`/`close_work_demand` returned Err(invalid) while the slot was not yet closing, `close_borrowed` never began the child close. Parents (mapped, tuple, set, map, box, sequence) all delegate to the slot, so they raised the "native receiving original type, field or phase is invalid" Err at `close_demands(body).unwrap()`. Now: not closing -> begin_close_demand/work demand, and close_borrowed starts the close first.
- NativeUnitEnumReceiver close_demands returned the selector demand unnested while close_route nests the grant (depth - 1): the selector's begin close was denied forever ("receiver failed its funded close"). Now `nested(selector.close_demands(body)?,0)`.
- Tuple (macro) and refusal-failure receivers dispatched child closes with the parent effect and no admission check; their quote is nested (+1 depth), so a depth-denied step still worked ("denied turn worked"). Now: parent admission check (`native_close_denied`), child gets `effect.nested(child_grant(g,0))` and `reply.progress()`.
- Take-demand mismatch (root of "native take was admitted but published nothing"): array phase 4, map phase 4, set TAKE, sequence phase 9 quoted `fixed(0)` while the child slot take publishes through `native_output_take`, which costs `2*size_of::<Option<T>>()+extra`. A denied take returned Progress, the parent read it as published. Now those phases quote `child.take_demand()`.
- Scalar read demand: `read_copy_bytes` is now `size_of::<Option<Self>>()` (the receiver writes `Some(value)` into its Option output; fixture says optional-u64-placement and the law asserts size_of::<Option<u64>>()).

## Step 3 (clean baseline W-recvclose-9: 13/47; probes removed)
- Lesson: an eprintln! inside a heap-observed region contaminates the allocator observation ((246,78) bytes in every post); all [DEBUG] probes are removed again (drive.rs restored byte-identical to its pre-probe state).
- Root cause of the remaining "original construction action retains an unsettled reply" / "native projection backing changed its original ownership" / "native receiver backing changed original Work scope" reds (probe showed `advance_metadata Bind` failing in `validate_effect`): the shared driver ran nested turns under a fresh `Authorities` + `NativeDecodeControl` per `receive_traced` call and local/close turns under the `EffectBank`'s own Work authority. A receiver's backing/binding is bound to (Work scope, issuer, Source turn), so any second call, or the close after a nested turn, presented a different Work owner. The passing original-crate tests keep ONE `Authorities` + control for the whole life of an owner and take local effects with `control.with_effect`.
- Fix (driver infrastructure, additive): `testing-admission` driven.rs gets a thread-local persistent decoder session per bank (keyed by the bank's Source turn identity, ManuallyDrop so a failed test never panics in a TLS destructor): `session_open`, `session_admit` (cancellation flag), `session_nested`, `session_run` (local funded turn through `Authorities::effect` = `control.with_effect`, debits the bank wallet via new `EffectBank::debit_work`), `session_driven` (= `driven` over the session). receiver-driver.rs uses them for receive/close/funded; `retire`/`close_source` stay on the bank (they close the bank-admitted Source and outputs).
- Driver bug: denied Local turns were run with work cap 1 although the selection needs `work`; a multi-unit action then failed with WorkLimit instead of being denied. Denied turns now use `work.max(1)` / `w.max(1)`.
