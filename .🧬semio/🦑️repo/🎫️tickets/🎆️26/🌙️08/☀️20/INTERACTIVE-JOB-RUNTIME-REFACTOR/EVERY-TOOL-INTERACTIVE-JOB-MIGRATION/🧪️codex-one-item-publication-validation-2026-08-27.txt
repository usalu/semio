STORE-OWNED PREPARED DIGEST API CLOSURE
Exact public API:
- `ArtifactStoreOneItemLiveAuthority::prepared_edit_digest<Mutation: Serialize>(&self,
  edit: &Edit<Mutation>) -> Result<[u8; 32], String>`
- `ArtifactStoreOneItemLiveAuthority::prepare_one_item<P, Mutation: Serialize>(&self,
  edit: Edit<Mutation>, post_snapshot: Arc<P>)
  -> Result<ArtifactStoreOneItemPrepared<P, Mutation>, String>`
- immutable authority getters: `operation()`, `generation()`, `base_revision()`,
  `base_applied_edit_count()`, `next_sequence_number()`, `next_clock()`, and `actor()`
- prepared checkpoint getter: `edit_digest()`

Authority and prepared-envelope fields are private. Apps cannot construct a live authority,
inject cursor/history fields, mutate an accumulator, or construct a raw prepared envelope. The
preferred constructor seals semantic edit/root inputs with Store-derived actor, clock, identity,
and digest metadata. Commit preflight recomputes `prepared_edit_digest` and compares it with the
sealed candidate before any publication mutation.
