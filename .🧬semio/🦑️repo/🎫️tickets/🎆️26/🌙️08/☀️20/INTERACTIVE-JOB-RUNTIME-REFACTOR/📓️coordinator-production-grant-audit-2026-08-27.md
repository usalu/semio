## Confirmed Defects and Work Ownership

Flow's cleanup requires 16,384 bytes to release its retained preview vectors, even when production will only supply 4,096. Flow's preparation clones/prepares/seals in one turn without charging its actual work. The Flow executor owns concrete repair and tests, including the existing 16 KiB value envelope rather than simply reducing functionality.

CAD, Writer, and Sequence also call a complete `prepare_*` function followed by canonical Store sealing in one advance; Sequence's documented envelope is 64 KiB. Their native compilation and actual maximum-envelope preparation/retirement are distinct gates. No runtime admission credit is assigned by this census.

The newly delivered DAG/Space/Sourcing and Home/Animate/FEM3d scalar Config owners use small explicitly bounded envelopes. Larger valid roots remain unfinished, including roots whose unrelated Config fields exceed admission. Such limits are not equivalent to full app interactivity.
