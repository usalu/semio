## One Response Per Existing Dispatch

The worker must own a private response credit for one admitted dispatch before invoking the guest. Exactly one canonical success or fixed fault may settle that credit. Duplicate/reentrant completion cannot post another response. A refused post or result-normalization exception keeps the original guest result, candidate backing and credit serviceable; sending a small fault code does not dispose those roots.

The receiver's original worker slot must retain the event root before any fallible header/body normalization, pending removal or callback. Header-only inspection is bounded to the canonical maximum 23 bytes. Exact worker, activation and transport-request correlation is required before claiming a pending dispatch. Malformed bodies remain under their original raw owner; format validation cannot create ownership.

Wrong-slot or malformed-header roots cannot overwrite a previous emergency root or be attached to an unrelated request. For the canonical credited producer, outstanding admitted credits provide a finite containment bound. Stop further admissions on the first protocol violation, preserve pending roots, and make the fault explicit. Arbitrarily many unsolicited rogue roots cannot be claimed memory-bounded by a finite registry; platform/worker isolation and any unresolved exceptional roots must be reported separately. Do not turn this limitation into an unbounded quarantine list or an ordinary successful-close receipt.

## Custody Is Not Semantic Consumption

A genuinely transferred, entire fixed response backing, privately captured under the exact dispatch and charged to a pre-reserved resident lease, can retain every raw page byte even while semantic decoding is unfinished. This may provide host custody evidence for the existing exact native InputAck. It is not selected-field completion, UI publication ACK, lifecycle ACK, or native retained-source retirement.

Native InputAck still requires the exact outstanding page receipt, unchanged native page, staged acceptance and final callback verdict. Its control response must not recursively create another variable response or implicitly publish the next page. Another page requires separately available host admission. Cancellation/close control capacity must not be exhausted by data admissions; choose the partition within the supplied capacity and test saturation without raising it.

UI's admitted builder is the sole driver of its selected field. It calls the private-bound source continuation with the exact builder, one child step per turn. The content reader observes that selected field; it does not compete to advance it. A null next fragment is retained backpressure, never a fabricated offset or success.
