## Result

The canonical generated launch catalog contains 185 `🛠️dev` configurations. Profile suffix discovery finds:

| Profile | Launches |

| React | 51 |

Normalizing the React, WGPU Wasm, and WGPU native suffixes produces 51 product groups. Forty-two groups expose the complete three-profile set. Nine groups expose a smaller declared surface:

- six Mitbestand products are React-only: generator, verfolgen, bearbeiten, koordinator, aggregator, and aussuchen;
- the two explicit multi-user `s` launches expose React and WGPU Wasm but no separate per-user native launch; and
- the Puzzle 5D capsule dream fixture exposes React and WGPU Wasm but no native launch.

Compose contributes 11 launch rows across desktop, hub, engine/MCP, sketchpad/play, GQL, 3dm, query Wasm/build, and Storybook surfaces. It remains in the final all-app contract because the developer's later all-app instruction supersedes the original plan's Compose exclusion.

## Acceptance meaning

This census proves launch registration only. A missing profile is not invented when a product does not expose it; every exposed profile must be executed. The final runtime matrix therefore has two joins:

1. all 101 descriptor declaration contexts, representing 94 unique app identities; and
2. all 185 declared development launch surfaces, including the 42 complete React/Wasm/native groups and every explicitly narrower or non-app product surface.

Each join row remains RED until startup, bounded representative work, preview/progress, cancellation, freshness, device loss, deterministic replay, exact terminal handback, English/German accessibility, and exposed device behavior are observed through the real entry point. A successful process spawn without an interactive operation ledger is insufficient.
