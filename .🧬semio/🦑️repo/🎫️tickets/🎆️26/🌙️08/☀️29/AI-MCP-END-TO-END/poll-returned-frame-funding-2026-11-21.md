`dispatch_operation_step` settles its actual slot-zero Context checkout and execution receipt before returning. `original_operation_drive_grant` recomputes the next ordinary retained ceiling through this same checkout policy. The ceiling intersects the configured drive cap with the treasury after subtracting observed guest effects, escrow, the held guest subloan, and protected operation cleanup. The treasury debits completed receipts. Poll cannot reconcile the retained guest loan before its current Core call finishes and exposes the real frozen receipt. A returned-frame atomic demand above this ceiling therefore cannot fit another ordinary turn at that frontier.
