# Detached bitmap inference over edited history

Bitmap has native producers for its persisted operations and their semantic decisions. Its vocabulary implements `original_converted_operation_receiving!(BitmapMutation)`, and `BitmapMutation` implements `RetainedMutation<BitmapSnapshot>`. The mounted editor installs a `PagedOneItemPreparationFactory` and the defining operation and retirement catalog in `build_document_store_owners`.

`OriginalArtifactPairReceiving<BitmapSnapshotDsl>` verifies and transfers Pack genesis and SPR history as separate original owners. `RetainedPersistedDocumentHydration::from_decoded_pack` can then hydrate an `Envelope`, including persisted edit metadata, native operation decoding, the history cursor, transitions and conflicts. The `Store` target currently uses `EditReplay::new_counted` and `EditReplay::step` with a deadline callback. It does not use the installed original semantic fold for that phase.

The mounted archive route takes the hydrated envelope through `begin_persisted_document_store_replacement` and the app's `ArtifactStoreReplayInitializer`. Its `NativeInitializationFold` reads retained ledger operations with their original identities and invokes the installed domain producer. The archive interface publishes through a live instance and an existing replacement slot. The resulting Store exposes its live projection through `snapshot_ref` and `snapshot_read_original` and has a funded document Store disposer.

The cold bitmap job needs a detached orchestration of those producers. It must share the bitmap owner catalog with the editor without creating a crate dependency cycle, transfer the verified pair into original envelope hydration, feed that envelope to the paid initializer, and retain the resulting Store until the worker has received its live snapshot and the Store has fully closed. The current executable therefore returns an explicit typed refusal for history requiring replay. It never solves the genesis image on behalf of an edited document.
