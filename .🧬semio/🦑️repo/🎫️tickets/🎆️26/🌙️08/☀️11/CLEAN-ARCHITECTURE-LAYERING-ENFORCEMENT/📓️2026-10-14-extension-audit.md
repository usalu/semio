## False Positive Controls

Specific deployment/composition may depend on concrete plugins: its purpose is to choose installed extensions. Neutral framework may expose plugin-neutral descriptor and admission APIs. A foreign-format identifier in an IO serializer path is not itself a forbidden import. Sibling artifact serialization in Trinity may be legitimate artifact dependency if explicitly declared; deletion tests should check the selected parent/child contract rather than prohibit every shared artifact edge mechanically. Concrete sibling-plugin types exported from an intrinsic artifact and source/build inventories physically owned by neutral framework are stronger evidence than identifier-name matches.
