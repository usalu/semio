# Pack Transport Capture Admission

## Canonical Ownership

Root approved a distinct external transport machine cause. PackCauseKind is the exhaustive union of Refusal(ValueRefusalKind) and Transport(PackTransportCategory). The source-authored transport categories are NativeIo, HttpRequest, and HttpBody. The owning provider supplies a category for the actual operation and an explicit Never or Transient retry disposition. A category is never recovered from error text, a generic std::io kind, or a Value refusal default.

Already typed providers continue to retain their exact original ValueError through the typed Io variant. That preserves their semantic refusal kind, path and original owned string identity. External providers require a separate TransportFailure variant and an owned concrete source. refusal_kind returns None for that variant; cause_kind returns its explicit Transport category. No additional Value refusal kind or controlled refusal codec revision is introduced.

## Capture Cost And Rollback

Capture must not stringify or clone the original external Error. The original E moves into one first-party fallible shared storage cell with an atomic reference count. Clone retains that same cell without allocating or rebuilding E. The private vtable permits standard Error/Display access and correct concrete destruction, without exposing std::io or ureq-specific classes in public Pack signatures. The public capture boundary accepts only standard-library Error bounds and first-party category/retry/admission types.

The first-party cell layout is computed from E and the actual control header, including alignment and padding, before any allocation. A stable Rust implementation can reserve one typed cell through Vec::try_reserve_exact, then observe capacity times cell size as the actual physical allocation witness before moving E. The one-cell requested size and actual capacity charge remain distinct; if the allocator grants extra capacity, the actual charge must pass admission or the unused reserve is released. The initialized Vec storage becomes the private shared cell, with its exact capacity retained for final reconstruction/destruction. This avoids a dependency on unstable fallible Arc or allocator APIs. Capture must not use infallible Arc::new, an untracked boxed trait object, a separately allocated display String, or a second hidden ownership cell. Ordinary Clone performs no allocation. The allocation primitive must be privately replaceable in tests so a reserve failure is deterministic.

Admission is two-phase. First snapshot the caller's committed bytes, check the exact requested layout against remaining credit, and invoke the cancellation/admission boundary before allocating. Next allocate fallibly while the original E stays outside the cell, and permit the post-reserve observer to refuse before moving E. After all fallible boundaries succeed, move E, initialize the cell, commit the exact byte charge and publish without another fallible step. On canceled admission, insufficient ownership credit, layout overflow or physical allocation refusal, leave committed bytes at the snapshot and return the original E together with a borrowed, source-authored refusal kind/reason and the actual zero retained allocated-byte witness. If the observer refuses after physical reserve, free the still-uninitialized cell, restore the admission snapshot and return E unchanged; the transient physical allocation witness remains separately observable. No partially initialized cell or orphaned cause may escape.

The existing NativeEncodeControl/NativeDecodeControl charge methods do not currently support restoring a failed post-charge allocation. Their allocate_vec implementations charge before try_reserve_exact. Therefore capture cannot claim rollback by calling charge and silently inheriting that behavior. A proper first-party reservation/commit boundary or equivalent caller-owned admission transaction must precede capture implementation; lower controls remain unchanged until their source hold and authorized taxonomy are clear.

The original concrete source may already own library/system storage before capture. Moving E does not create or copy that storage. Its existing owner remains responsible for any prior admission. Wrapper admission proves the new first-party cell allocation only; sizeof(E) does not pretend to measure opaque external heap ownership. Provider ownership and inherited allocation witnesses must be documented explicitly before claiming a total process allocation bound.
