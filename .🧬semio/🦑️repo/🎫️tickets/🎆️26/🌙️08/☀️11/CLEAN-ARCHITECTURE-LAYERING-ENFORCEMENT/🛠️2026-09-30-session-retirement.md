# Geometry Session Family Retirement

The retained close contract seals each authority, preserves sibling geometry claims, waits for active native readers, and drains native resources with explicit structural-item and payload-byte grants. Cancellation pauses the retained cursor without reopening the authority or releasing its resources. A zero item or byte grant returns Blocked and does no work. Positive grants debit buffers incrementally; a buffer is physically freed only after its allocation byte credit is fully drawn down.
