# Owner discovery exclusion proposal

Actual whole12 progress shows the owner scan traversing standards and guest/oracle trees before an invalid Native owner manifest can refuse. The current controlled owner discovery consumes owner-manifests but ignores the same admitted exclude-patterns; current member discovery already consumes them. This separate proposal applies that exact authored exclusion authority before admitting a directory or Cargo manifest to owner discovery. It preserves all pattern/control budgets and existing member law rosters.

Schema-first neutral inputs include malformed Cargo manifests inside excluded standards branches, valid General and Specific owners, and unchanged full discovery controls. They require excluded manifests to remain unobserved. The proposal is authored only, with its full current draft preimage retained. It has not been admitted or executed, and the active whole12 producer and snapshot remain untouched.
