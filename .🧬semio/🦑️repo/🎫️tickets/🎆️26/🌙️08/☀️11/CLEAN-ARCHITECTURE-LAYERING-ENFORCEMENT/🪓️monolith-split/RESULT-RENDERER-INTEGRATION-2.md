## 8. What this evidence does not cover

* rustc never type-checked these crates. The oracle stops at name resolution: field and method existence in bodies, privacy of methods and fields, generic bounds, borrowck, trait-method-in-scope (the Shell imports `FromValue` and `ToValue` by a token hint only), `&mut &mut T` coercions of the WP5 bundles, the edition-2021 temporaries of the two hoisted blocks, and the orphan rules of the trait impls that moved with their types.
* Visibility is `pub` everywhere in the flipped crates (mechanical widening); `private_interfaces` is warn-level. Narrowing needs a real compile that shows which names no other crate uses.
* The moved tests were never run; 32 tests still sit in the monolith.
* Residue: about 15 production items with no crate user (the wasm exports `semio_wgpu_set_hub_env`, `…host_window_blur`, `…set_host_storage`, `…set_agent_bridge_config`, the theme resolvers `theme_from_document` and `apply_theme_document_metrics` (partly moved), `resolve_theme_for_ids` family) stay in the old chunk files; the parent still carries `SHELL_SOURCE`, the 32 test mounts and the import block its remaining children use.
