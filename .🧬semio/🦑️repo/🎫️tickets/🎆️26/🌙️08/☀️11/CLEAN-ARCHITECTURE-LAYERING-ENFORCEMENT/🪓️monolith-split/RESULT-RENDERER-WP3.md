## 0. Verdict

WP3 is **staged, not flipped**. Inside the one compilation unit the WP3 sources now have the exact item shapes of their 13 target crates: the residual back edges are inverted (I1, I2 with a narrowed trait, I3), every 5.2 item move that starts or ends in a WP3 file is applied (the cross-package ones as `📥️lifted` files), `test-support` is implemented, and the crate map, the staged census and the item-level edge graph prove each crate fits its budget with a clean DAG. The hub `R/📥️mounts/🧵️wp3-frame-core/🦀️.rs` is **unchanged**: no `extern crate … as …;` unit was applied and no crate library root was filled.

Why no flip (same finding as `RESULT-RENDERER-WP4.md`): every WP3 crate sits above the WP1/WP2/WP4/WP5 skeletons, which are still empty (only `mailbox_core` was flipped, by WP1). A WP3 crate that names `crate::scenes::…` or `ShellState` cannot resolve it from an empty skeleton, and flipping the hub now would also turn every peer's WP0-style differential red (`extern crate` becomes an empty module in their sandbox). The flip is therefore a mechanical last step, listed in section 8, and everything before it is verified.

**Compiles are refused** for image headroom on every launcher call (`Insufficient physical compiler initial headroom`, `🗑️generated/rwp3-cargo-test-support-1.log`), as the coordinator announced; nothing below claims a compile. Evidence is static: the patched full-resolution differential, census, TS oracle, rustfmt parse, path/region/orphan/definition scans, losslessness.

## 9. Residual risks

* No compiler saw any of this. The differential covers name resolution only; field/method privacy and type errors (generic `RuntimeMailbox<R>` bounds, `Send` of the spawned futures, `&mut *runtime` re-borrows, trait method resolution of `interaction()` against the `Deref` of `AppRuntime`) are unchecked. Highest-risk spots to compile first: `presentation-raster` struct/Drop impls with `<R>`, `RuntimeApply::apply_step<R>`, `RuntimeMailbox::submit_interaction`, `AppPresenter::close_world3d_dynamic_step`.
* The singleton hazard of the lifts (section 1 item 6) is real until the destination owners remove one of the two mounts.
* `AppInteractionState` in `frame-actions` makes that crate the home of pointer/keyboard handling; the name is now narrower than its content (cohesion note for the flip: a `frame-interaction` rename would be cleaner but needs a WP0 registry row).
* The test closure of `ui-engine` is over budget until the companion carries the command-wiring tests; companions are intentionally still skeletons: their test files `use super::*` of the monolith root and cannot be mounted from a companion library root before the flip.
