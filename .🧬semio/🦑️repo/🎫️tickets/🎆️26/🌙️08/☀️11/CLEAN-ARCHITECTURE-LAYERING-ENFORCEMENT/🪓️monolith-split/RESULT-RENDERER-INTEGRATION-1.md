## 5. Decisions

1. **Facades keep the element trees' files in place** (pets/canvas/WP2 precedent): the crate root mounts them with `#[path]`; the old parent file becomes the facade with `use` of the crate modules. Multi-module crates are named in the hubs with `use`/`pub use`; single-module crates (`asset-authority`, `asset-decode` globs) with `extern crate … as …;`.
2. **The two Interpreter laws that name the monolith stay in the monolith** (test hosts), not in the `ui-engine-tests` companion: that companion cannot name `AppInteractionState` before `shell-state` exists.
3. **`testkit` instead of dropping the mocks**: the alternative (laws running the real clipboard path in the monolith test unit) fails to compile, and a crate that stays on plain `cfg(test)` would silently lose its per-thread cell in that unit.
4. **No behaviour edits**: the only code written is the `read_os_clipboard` mock reader (three existing call sites named it), the `read` visibility and `pub` widening, imports and aliases.

## 7. Residual risks

1. **`cfg(test)` crosses crate boundaries in the other real crates.** `testkit` exists for four crates only. `scene-state` (per-thread `SceneCell`), `engine-registry`, `program-bridge`, `kernel-runtime`, `kernel-protocol` and others still carry plain `cfg(test)` variants (about 100 attributes in 17 further crates, counted by `testkitify.py`) that are off when the monolith test unit builds them as dependencies: the per-thread cells become process-wide statics and mocks disappear. Applying `testkitify.py` is mechanical, but each crate with `[dev-dependencies]` that its `cfg(test)` code names needs those dependencies reachable from the monolith test build; I did not roll it out blindly.
2. **Test closures of the WP2 crates** do not resolve: `base1` (pre-edit) already shows 460 resolution rows in `--test` (231 in `🔬️wgpu-canvas2d`, 73 in `🔬️wgpu-raster-frame-cost`, …): the laws still `use super::*` against the old Scenes namespace. This pass adds none (s7: 4 rows against 460; the 22 rows it removed are the hand-offs).
3. **The frame-core crates stay in the monolith** (section 2). The first of them to flip needs `shell-state`; WP5's views and the WP4 tier-1 carve are the gate.
4. **Visibility** of the three new crate trees was widened mechanically (`pub(crate)`/`pub(super)` to `pub`); `private_interfaces` is warn-level, so a narrower surface is a clean-up after a real compile.
5. **Type-level blind spot of the oracle**: field and method privacy, generic bounds and borrowck are not checked (the definition scan covers private members by name; its mentions in section 6.3 were read). The wasm export attributes of `ui-engine-dump` (`dump*`) are referenced by the facade's `pub use` under `cfg(wasm32)`, which keeps them reachable; not link-tested.
6. **Policy audit is stale beyond this pass** (section 6.4); `verify interactivity` stays red for reasons older than the partition.
7. **Peers**: WP5 edits the Shell tree; the full differential restores only the 62 files this pass touched in the baseline, so a WP5 edit during the run is equal in both sides.
