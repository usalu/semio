setDoNotCheckNewlyAddedConstraints 
Internal Use Only.

Sets a global flag not to check constraints that are being added. This optimization is primarily intended for the scenario when multiple constraints are being added one-by-one and the constraints are known to be valid. The constraint group is still evaluated after the constraints have been added. To suppress this evaluation, temporarily set the status of the constraint group to kSuppressedAssocStatus to prevent its evaluation, and un-suppress the constraint group after all constraints have been added.
