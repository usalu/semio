IMPORTANT DEVIATION from the generic wave-2 brief: `MathProjection` does NOT derive
`#[derive(dsl::DslDocument)]` directly — it has a hand-written `impl vcs::DocumentDsl for
MathProjection` that delegates to `MathProjectionDsl` (which does derive `dsl::DslDocument`;
its `edges` field needs `MathEdgeDsl`/`dsl::Wire` folding that a plain-derive on `MathProjection`
itself can't express). Because of this, `MathProjection` also did NOT automatically get
`vcs::DocumentPack` from dsl_derive's blanket impl (that only lands on `MathProjectionDsl`).
Added a hand-written `impl vcs::DocumentPack for MathProjection` right beside the existing manual
`DocumentDsl` impl (`//#region 🔖️Dsl`), delegating through the same `math_projection_to_dsl`/
`math_projection_from_dsl` conversion functions the DSL impl already uses:
  - `encode_pack_with` -> `math_projection_to_dsl(self).encode_pack_with(options)`
  - `decode_pack_with` -> `MathProjectionDsl::decode_pack_with(...)` then
    `math_projection_from_dsl(...).map_err(|m| vcs::text_error_to_pack_error(vcs::TextError::new(m, vcs::TextSpan::at(1,1))))`
    (mirrors the existing `parse_dsl`'s error-mapping exactly).

Registered the codec in `register_mathematical_exports()` (was an empty `fn register_mathematical_exports() {}`
stub referenced by `setup:` in the `semio_plugin!` macro invocation — same pattern as
note/imperative/animate/fem/lowpoly/layout/cad/architect/draw/gis/flow/infinite-board-dag plugins):
  semio_framework_plugin::plugin_runtime::register_document_codec_for_app::<MathematicalPlayApp>("semio.mathematical/v1");
