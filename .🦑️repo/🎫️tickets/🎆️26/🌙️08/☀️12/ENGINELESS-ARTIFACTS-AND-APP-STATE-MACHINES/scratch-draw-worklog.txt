================================================================================
Confirmed the hazard was real: the artifact root
✏️s/🔌️plugins/🖍️draw/🗿️artifacts/🖍️draw/🦀️component.rs already carried a

`&'static [&'static ComposerEntry]` (a `.iter().collect()` view) — a DIFFERENT
type from the real engine `io_registry::entries() -> &'static [ComposerEntry]`.

  - root `declaration()`  `.composers(...)` re-qualified from
      crate::artifacts::draw::standards::v1::engine::io_registry::entries()
    to
      crate::artifacts::draw::standards::v1::subsets::any::io::io_registry::entries()
  - root shadow module's own alias re-qualified from
      use crate::artifacts::draw::standards::v1::engine::io_registry as v1;
    to
      use crate::artifacts::draw::standards::v1::subsets::any::io::io_registry as v1;
  - the real io_registry's internal `crate::artifacts::draw::engine::
    draw_document_to_semio_drawing(...)` call re-qualified to
    `crate::artifacts::draw::io::draw_document_to_semio_drawing(...)`.
No unqualified `io_registry::entries()` call was left anywhere, so nothing could
silently rebind to the shadow.
