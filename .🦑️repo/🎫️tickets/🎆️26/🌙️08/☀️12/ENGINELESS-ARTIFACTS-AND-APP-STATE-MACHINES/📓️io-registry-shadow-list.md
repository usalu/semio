# `io_registry` shadow census — artifact roots carrying `declaration()`

Requested by SMO (#2545) as a durable record rather than a broadcast. Generated by scanning every
`✏️s/🔌️plugins/*/🗿️artifacts/*/🦀️component.rs` that defines `fn declaration`.

## Why this matters

A relocated **unqualified** `io_registry::entries()` binds to the artifact-root wrapper, whose
`entries()` returns `&'static [&'static ComposerEntry]` — a `.iter().collect()` view — while the
engine's returns `&'static [ComposerEntry]`. The bare call does not fail: it silently registers a
different thing. **No gate catches it**: not `--all-targets`, not `--keep-going`, not
`RUSTC_WRAPPER=""`, not `Finished`+exit 0, not law tests. Only reading the call site.

## Rule

> When relocating code, **every unqualified path in the moved body is a hazard until proven
> otherwise.** Qualify it, or prove no shadow exists in the destination scope.

Corollary (APA #2549, learned the hard way): **a stopped pass does not leave nothing behind.**
Halting a pass mid-flight still stranded two `pub fn pilot_languages`. Re-derive invariants after aborting.
