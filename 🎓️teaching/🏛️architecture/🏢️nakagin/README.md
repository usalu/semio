# Nakagin Teaching Reconstruction · Nakagin-Lehrrekonstruktion

## English

A shared schema-first reconstruction for the Grasshopper and Excel clips. It combines documented floor counts, six geometric door/window families from repository CAD, the original 1973 drawings and an explicit set of reconstruction assumptions. It is not a complete measured replica.

The A606 survey inventories **A 76 / B 64 capsules**, total 140. A floors 3–13 contain **2, 7, 7, 7, 8, 8, 7, 8, 8, 7, 7**; B floors 3–11 contain **2, 8, 8, 7, 8, 8, 7, 8, 8**. The JSON authors every floor, attachment group and item independently. The tree remains BUILDING / TOWER / STORY / ATTACHMENT FACADE, with 75 ragged branches: 65 pairs and 10 single capsules. Each slot also records its actual floor, three-phase sublevel, geometric family, centre, orientation and door port.

A is the taller left core; B is the shorter right core. Compact end- and side-entry capsules wrap the cores in three height phases. Source CAD provides placement frames. Registered measured scans support A floor-3 N/J and W/Slash positions and B’s mirrored phase ordering. All 140 visible window-bearing walls have positive circular-rim evidence; 33 physical family/pose records were corrected while retaining identity, floor, phase and count. A4, A5 and A13 each have seven distinct positive positions, exhausting their documented seven-capsule counts and excluding S0. Hidden entry hand remains separately inferred. The original section supports a 2.80 m pitch, A roof 42.14 m and A head 11.36 m. Podium levels 0 / 4.00 / 7.72 m and B roof/head 36.62 / 10.83 m remain derived working datums; separate coping is not the capsule placement datum. Each core uses its explicit sourced or derived roof datum, independently validated against its highest capsule crown; capital_base returns that datum.

The physical casing measures 2.60 × 4.20 × 2.50 m from actual native triangle surfaces and preserves the authored attachment centres. Its local bounds are X ±1.30 m and Y −0.10 to 4.10 m. The nominal 2.50 × 4.00 × 2.50 m values remain separate program/energy controls. The usable interior is 2.30 × 3.80 × 2.10 m above the 0.20 m finish datum; explicit usable-dimension overrides are honored. M+ publishes A806 overall dimensions 255 × 270 × 420 cm without axis labels: the JSON retains their order and does not infer width or height. Every canonical porthole is 1.3 m. A–D are explicitly proposed program/energy studies, independent from the six physical families Slash, Backslash, J, L, P and S.

### Geometry API

NamedPart carries a unique key, semantic role, closed Solid and renderer-neutral material; Assembly holds immutable parts. No drawing engine or third-party runtime dependency enters this API.

- capsule_assembly(t, detail, layout, interior) builds real end- or long-wall apertures, a sourced 1.2 mm steel skin, separate linings and truss, rim/frame/glass, handed entrance and four fasteners, service rails/pipes and thresholds at the actual casing/core ports. Rounded geometry includes all twelve edges and eight spherical corners. A finish slab supports the furniture at the entry level. Portholes use 48 sectors at full detail and 16 in exterior views. Physical quarter fillets default to four/two segments respectively; an explicit dictionary corner_segments override controls finer sampling. Nominal study tessellation is kept separate. Exterior assemblies omit enclosed furniture.
- capsule_casing and capsule_interior expose distinct physical and usable bounds. capsule_parameters_from_casing converts physical controls once, rejecting ambiguous double conversion. capsule_entry and capsule_window expose the actual entry plane and glazing midplane; capsule_interior_profile returns the exact faceted clear-lining polygon.
- The default interior=reference shows a front floor bed and four drawers, molded wetroom enclosure with curved door, tub, washbasin/toilet, built-in media cabinet, TV, audio, phone, folding desk, lighting and detector. Thirty-nine explicitly inferred discrete supports connect sanitary ware, taps, plumbing, media equipment, grilles, shelving and telephone wiring. Floor-level joinery follows the rounded lining instead of protruding into it. Handed geometry leaves the entrance approach open. interior=study selects the proposed A–D fitting programs instead; every primary footprint uses its authored width, length and count, with checked entrance clearance and non-overlap.
- capsule_window_unit(t, layout, state, detail) builds the original fixed-glass/folding-fan hardware and the separately documented A904 inner-sash reference. The primary construction detail establishes the 6 mm fixed pane and numbered hardware dimensions; the observed sash installation date and prevalence across all capsules are unproven. WindowUnitState controls sash angle, fan fraction and rotation. Furnished reference views keep the sash closed; standalone mechanism inspections may open it, without claiming clearance from movable pillows.
- The elongated wall lamp is separate from the ceiling light. Its inferred housing, diffuser and tube sit above the actual ventilation grille, below the usable ceiling, with checked real-material contacts.
- window_unit_axon renders the same standalone source assembly at an explicit WindowUnitState, with named parts and source geometry retained for inspection.
- capsule_axon and capsule_views accept all six physical layouts and explicit glass_opacity. Transparent detailed inspections retain the actual lining, fitout and devices behind the panes. Whole-building overview glazing uses a reflective style while retaining every source exterior part.
- slot_assembly(slot, detail) returns the actual family already placed in world coordinates. capsule_assembly returns local geometry; place_assembly maps any assembly through a Plane.
- core_assembly builds actual entry and bridge openings, local staggered sill landings, a central enclosed lift and three turning flights per regular storey. The phase-0 U-shaped lobby serves four ports, phase 1 the north-west pair, and phase 2 the south-west pair; B mirrors this topology across X, as corroborated by the primary compound plan and measured regular-floor phases. Lift doors open onto phase-0 support once per logical floor. The sparse third floor uses its actual phase-specific ports; ground and office thresholds join local phase-0 support. A separate east return reaches each source-height roof. Walkway widths and stair construction are reconstruction assumptions. Partial-height overrides clip all higher geometry.
- capital_assembly builds a tall diagonal metal enclosure with four closed metal sides, internal bracing and water tank. Height and overhang remain parametric.
- podium_assembly uses the irregular source-CAD roof extent, recessed ground-level pilotis and opaque upper cladding, narrow high windows and curved fascia returns on every edge. All slabs are pierced around both cores. foundation_assembly adds basement, connected three-flight turning stairs, shaft continuation, ring footings and lift pits.
- structure_assembly combines the structure and bridges at actual floors 6, 9 and 12. building_assembly includes all 140 capsules. Optional progress and cancelled callbacks report AssemblyProgress events and raise AssemblyCancelled safely at component boundaries.
- Envelope functions capsule_solid, core_solid, capital_solid and podium_solid remain useful for bounds and quantities; they are distinct from material-layer assemblies. capsule_solid is the nominal study envelope; slot_solid conservatively bounds the physical casing and carries the actual family glazing midplane and handed attachment bolt metadata.

### Files and Checks

The square core contains four triangular corner piers and a separate clipped central compound with west service compartments and an east lift chamber; tower B mirrors this arrangement across X. The primary drawing supplies this topology, while the 2.4 × 2.2 m compound, .24 m chamfers and 1.15 m pier legs remain inferred raster proportions. A .95 × 1.1 m reference lift cabin is parked at ground, with walls, doors, handrail and controls. Its explicit display supports do not assert a historical drive, guide or counterweight mechanism. Ground and office doors pierce the south core wall at their authored podium levels. Basement divisions reproduce section-visible spatial subdivision with assumed plan positions and unverified equipment identities.

The dedicated core cases and NumPy tests are in 🧪️cases/🗼️core/🔣️.json and 🧪️tests/🗼️core/🐍️.py. The independent circulation suite in 🧪️tests/🚶️circulation/🐍️.py preserves actual convex support polygons, validates every support edge with NumPy clipping and NetworkX, and checks continuous .6 m body strips (including oriented diagonal corner routes) against every physical structural mesh and capsule envelope. Geometry consistency does not authenticate every historical dimension.

Verification covers all six closed positive-volume families, all 140 world placements and entry planes against Rhino, every complete door approach, stair headroom, top entry heads, capsule/core/capsule/bridge/podium clearances, shaft penetrations, basement connections, parametric controls and progress/cancellation. Die jeweils sieben positiv belegten Positionen in A4, A5 und A13 schöpfen die dokumentierte Anzahl aus und schließen S0 aus. Verdeckte Türhändigkeit bleibt separat angenommen. Die physische Außenhülle misst nach tatsächlichen nativen Dreiecksflächen 2,60 × 4,20 × 2,50 m und behält die hinterlegten Anschlusszentren.
